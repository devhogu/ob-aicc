AI Lab · Experiments with AI scenarios

The AI Lab runs continuously on the Bank's own infrastructure, isolated from its production systems. A scenario can enter at any time as an Experiment; data arrives as read-only extracts from the Bank's systems and never leaves the Bank; the AI-agent workflow is built, measured, and then accepted, turned into a Proposal for delivery, or rejected. The Lab is where the Bank tries generative AI before it commits to it.

How people and AI agents work together

People steer: they set the intent and carry accountability. AI agents gather signals, analyze, reason, execute, and learn. The two work in parallel, joined by one intent loop and a trust perimeter that is not negotiable.

◆ System A

People

Authority · Judgment · Intent

  1. A.1 Strategic directionSets the intent, the risk appetite, and the boundary of acceptable action.
  2. A.2 Ethics & regulationOwns the relationship with regulators, customers, and the public.
  3. A.3 Final decision rightsKeeps authority over high-stakes, irreversible, and reputational decisions.
  4. A.4 AccountabilityAnswers to the Board, the regulator, the shareholder, the customer.
  5. A.5 Review & recalibrationChecks the Bank against its purpose; resets the intent when the context changes.

● System B

AI agents

Monitoring · Analysis · Execution · Learning

  1. B.1 Continuous monitoringWatches signals on customers, markets, risk, operations, and staff in real time.
  2. B.2 Multi-angle analysisWeighs decisions across competing factors — risk, return, customer, conduct.
  3. B.3 Options for decisionPresents worked-out options with confidence, trade-offs, and sources.
  4. B.4 Execution & automationCarries out authorized actions and routine work, and keeps the audit trail.
  5. B.5 Learning from resultsFeeds results back into models; flags drift; proposes adjustments to people.
What this means

Decision rights stay with people. AI agents make decisions faster, wider in scope, and better evidenced. The Bank does not hand over judgment; it equips it.

The intent loop

How people and AI agents work together: intent → insight → action → audit, continuously

Intent

People set the outcome, the constraints, the risk appetite, and the ethical limits. Direction, not instructions.

Insight

AI agents analyze the data from several angles and present options for decision, with confidence and trade-offs.

Action

People authorize. AI agents execute or hand over. Every step has an owner and limits, and can be reversed where possible.

Audit

Results are logged, evaluated, challenged if needed, and used to sharpen the next intent. Each turn makes the loop more precise.

Lab practice maturity

How far good practice is applied today in each control area of the Lab, as a percentage of what the Bank expects of a production system. The figures are a self-assessment by AICC, reviewed with the guardrails each quarter. Lower figures are expected where a practice matters only once a result moves toward production; the guardrails are required at every level. Point at a figure to see what it means and why it is where it is.

Governance & decisions
Decision rights The sponsor decides the result; the AICC Lead assigns the Risk Tier Who decides is defined, but approvals at each step are not yet recorded in one place, and the Lab has no regular decision forum. Recording each approval in the Experiment's Solution Definition would raise it.
Prohibitions A fixed list: no personal data leaves the Lab, nothing is written back to production The list exists and isolation enforces most of it, but compliance is not yet tested on a schedule. A periodic check with recorded evidence would raise it.
Resolving disagreements Not formalized; case by case There is no agreed way to settle a dispute between the sponsor, the team, and the control functions. A short escalation rule up to the AICC Lead and Steering would move it out of this band.
Risk & controls
Risk classification Lab risk register, categorized by type Risks are recorded for the Lab as a whole, but not every Experiment is assessed on its own or given a Risk Tier before the build. Assessing each Experiment at the scope step would raise it.
Output integrity Drift, bias, and grounding checks during the build Checks run on an evaluation set during every build. What is missing for production is an independent review of the results and monitoring after the build.
Lab isolation Separate network segment, key management, no access to production Isolation is built into the environment and checked at setup. A periodic penetration test with recorded results would close the remaining gap.
Personal data protection Masking, redaction, and extract rules Personal data is masked or removed before it enters. The sign-off with the data-protection Control Function Contact is not yet recorded for every extract.
Failure handling Basic rollback and alerting A failed run can be rolled back and raises an alert, but there is no incident procedure or named responder. A short runbook with an owner would raise it.
Operations & processes
Scenario selection Prioritized backlog of scenarios Scenarios come from a prioritized backlog. The criteria for choosing the next one are not yet written down.
Workflow mapping Service workflow documented for each scenario Each scenario has its workflow described, but not to a common template, so results are hard to compare.
Experiment lifecycle Choose → build → validate Only the middle of the lifecycle is defined. The time-box, the review at its end, and the closing with an Outcome Report are not yet applied. The flow above sets them; applying it would raise this.
Path to delivery Informal handover to production delivery A good result is handed over by agreement, without a Proposal or a handover package. Turning each accepted result into a Proposal would raise it.
Data & analytics
Data pipeline Extract pipeline running; extracts made per scenario The read-only pipeline runs and is reused. Each source does not yet have a named owner on record.
Data quality Quality checked at extract; correctness confirmed Quality is checked when data is extracted. The checks are not yet automated or reported in a standard way.
Data privacy Masking and anonymization of sensitive fields Sensitive fields are masked or anonymized by rule. A periodic re-identification test would close the gap.
Analytics correctness Analytics checked against reference data Results are compared with reference data where it exists. Reference sets are not available for every scenario.
Measurement
Performance metrics (KPIs) KPIs tracked for each scenario KPIs are tracked, but the baseline before the change is not always measured, so the gain is hard to prove.
Hypothesis targets Target set for each scenario's hypothesis Each Experiment states its hypothesis and target. Targets are not yet reviewed by the sponsor before the build.
Workflow metrics Functional and integration metrics recorded Functional and integration metrics are recorded, but there are no agreed thresholds that pass or fail a run.
Acceptance criteria Acceptance defined for each scenario Criteria are defined, but often late in the work and not signed by the sponsor. Agreeing them at the scope step would raise it.
