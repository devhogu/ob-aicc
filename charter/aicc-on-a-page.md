# AICC on a page

**What AICC is.** The AI Competence Center of the Bank is an internal consulting and innovation lab. It explores, trials, and proves the use of AI with the
functions, delivers the outcome with its evidence, and proposes what the Bank should adopt at scale. It shapes the AI adoption strategy from what it proves.

**Whom it serves.** The functions of the Bank. A function is the client of an Engagement, its Domain Owner represents it, and a Domain Expert of the function works with AICC.

**What it offers.** Studies, proofs, delivery, and support, at a support level chosen for each Engagement. It delivers Solutions of three types: an Experiment that ends
in a Proposal, a Product built for one consumer, or a Service that AICC runs.

**How it commits.** In a Service Agreement for each Engagement: AICC commits to the capacity and the outcome, and the function commits to nothing. An Outcome Report ends the Engagement.

**How the work flows.** A business need becomes an Initiative, with a business case. An Initiative delivers Solutions. A Solution is delivered through Epics and Features
in monthly Iterations (IT) and quarterly Program Increments (PI). Every item is in one of thirteen states, and the product owner accepts the delivered outcome.

**How it decides.** The person doing the work decides on the facts. A decision goes to the AICC Lead, or to the Executive Sponsor, only when it crosses Domains, cannot be reversed, exceeds a guardrail, accepts a risk, or concerns a Risk Tier 3 Solution. The Control Functions validate and may stop, and nobody overrides them.

**How it is controlled.** Three Risk Tiers set the checks. The Steering meets monthly and quarterly. Internal audit has read access and gives assurance only.

**Where it is recorded.** The charter holds the rules. Jira and Confluence run the live work. The Registry holds the records for audit, and the Portfolio holds the catalog of Solutions.
