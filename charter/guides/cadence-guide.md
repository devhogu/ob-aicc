# Guide: Cadence

## 1. Purpose and when it applies

This guide explains the rhythm of AICC and what each beat leaves on record. Work runs in Program Increments (PI) of one quarter. A PI has three Iterations, each one calendar month of four or five whole weeks, and the last week of the third Iteration is the Innovation and Planning (IP) week. Every week starts with planning and ends with review. The Cadence workflow shows the general flow without dates, and the Calendar holds the dates with the blocked and gray days.

## 2. The beats and what each is for

| Beat | Events | What it controls | What is left on record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day | Daily Stand-up | Progress and blockers | Nothing; blockers go to the work item |
| Week | Weekly Planning, Weekly Review | The flow, the Limits on Work in Progress, and the Dependencies; the care of the funnel and the Portfolio Backlog | The current boards and Dashboard, and the notes of what changed |
| Iteration | Iteration Planning, Iteration Review and Demo, Iteration Retrospective, monthly Steering | The month's work, acceptance, the gate decisions that are due, the sample of the Decisions of the AICC Lead, and the open items | The Steering Summary; the acceptances; the Registry Snapshot at the Iteration close |
| PI | PI Review and Demo, Inspect and Adapt, PI Planning, quarterly Steering | The quarter's value; the decision on each Active Initiative; the quarterly risk check; direction | The Quarterly Report; the Registry Snapshot at the PI close; the Steering Summary |

## 3. The loops on the beats

The control loops of the Operating Model 6 and the portfolio loops of the Portfolio Management Model 4 run on these beats and add no meeting. The Weekly Review is the operating loop and the backlog care loop. The monthly Steering is the control loop and the portfolio sync. The quarterly Steering is the assurance loop and the portfolio review, and the first quarterly Steering of the year is the direction loop and the strategic loop.

## 4. A month and a quarter in practice

A month starts with Iteration Planning, in which the Team selects the Features for the month. Each Monday and Friday it plans and reviews the week. The last week is the review week: the Iteration Review and Demo, the retrospective, and the monthly Steering, on a day fixed with the people outside AICC. The Registry Snapshot is taken at the close. In the IP week of a quarter, the PI Review and Demo shows what the quarter delivered, Inspect and Adapt solves the main problems, Innovation gives time to learn, the PI Planning sets the intent and direction of the next PI, and the quarterly Steering decides on each Active Initiative, takes the quarterly risk check, and confirms the Maturity Level. The first quarterly Steering of the year also sets the Strategic Priorities, the Envelopes, and the Guardrails. The AICC Lead writes the Quarterly Report, and the Executive Sponsor approves and issues the report to the Board Committee.

## 5. When the calendar is not clean

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| An event falls on a blocked or gray day | It moves to the working day before, never after |
| The IP week is blocked or gray | It is the week before |
| An event is missed | It is not held later, and its intent is covered at the next event |
| The Team has up to three people | Light mode applies: one weekly session, and fewer events |

## 6. What an auditor will ask, and the record that answers

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Did the monthly and quarterly reviews happen? | The Steering Summaries, one for each Steering |
| What was the state of the work at a given date? | The Registry Snapshot of that Iteration or PI close |
| What was reported to the Board Committee, and who approved it? | The Quarterly Report and its issuance block |
