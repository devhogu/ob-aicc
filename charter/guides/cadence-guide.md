# Guide: Cadence

## 1. Purpose and when it applies

This guide explains the rhythm of AICC and what each beat leaves on record. Work runs in Program Increments (PI) of one quarter. A PI has three
Iterations (IT), each one calendar month of four or five whole weeks, and the last week of the third IT is the Innovation and Planning (IP) week. Every week
starts with planning and ends with review. The Cadence workflow shows the general flow without dates, and the Calendar holds the dates with the blocked and
gray days.

## 2. The beats and what each is for

| Beat | Events | What it controls | What is left on record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day | Daily Stand-up | Progress and blockers | Nothing; blockers go to the work item |
| Week | Weekly Planning, Weekly Review | The flow, the Limits on Work in Progress, and the Dependencies | The current boards, and the notes of what changed |
| IT | IT Planning, IT Review and Demo, IT Retrospective, monthly Steering | The month's work, acceptance, and the tactical decisions | The Steering Summary; the acceptances; the Registry Snapshot at the IT close |
| PI | PI Review and Demo, Inspect and Adapt, PI Planning, quarterly Steering | The quarter's value, risks, and direction | The Quarterly Report; the Registry Snapshot at the PI close; the Steering Summary |

## 3. A month and a quarter in practice

A month starts with IT Planning, in which the Team selects the Features for the month. Each Monday and Friday it plans and reviews the week. The last
week is the review week: the IT Review and Demo, the retrospective, and the monthly Steering, on a day fixed with the people outside AICC. The
Registry Snapshot is taken at the close. In the IP week of a quarter, the PI Review and Demo shows what the quarter delivered, Inspect and Adapt solves the
main problems, Innovation gives time to learn, the PI Planning sets the intent and direction of the next PI, and the quarterly Steering confirms the
priorities and funding and the Maturity Level. The AICC Lead writes the Quarterly Report, and the Executive Sponsor approves and issues the report to the
Board Committee.

## 4. When the calendar is not clean

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| An event falls on a blocked or gray day | It moves to the working day before, never after |
| The IP week is blocked or gray | It is the week before |
| An event is missed | It is not held later, and its intent is covered at the next event |
| The Team has up to three people | Light mode applies: one weekly session, and fewer events |

## 5. What an auditor will ask, and the record that answers

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Did the monthly and quarterly reviews happen? | The Steering Summaries, one for each Steering |
| What was the state of the work at a given date? | The Registry Snapshot of that IT or PI close |
| What was reported to the Board Committee, and who approved it? | The Quarterly Report and its issuance block |
