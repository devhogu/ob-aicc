# Charter

The charter is the static body of knowledge of AICC: how the unit is governed and how it operates. It is the source of truth for what
AICC is, what it produces, and how it keeps its records. It holds no figures of the Bank, no data, no documents of the functions, and
no code, and it names no persons or bodies: it names Roles and responsibilities, and the Registry associates them with real people and assignments. A bracketed comment marks an open place and states what is expected there. Start with [AICC on a page](aicc-on-a-page.md).
