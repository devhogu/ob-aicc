# Control Sign-Off

**Template.** The decision of a Control Function Contact: a validation, a stop, a provider check, an Exception, or the clearance of a business case. The check of a Risk Tier 1 Solution is not a sign-off: it is noted in the AI Registry. An Exception to a requirement set by AICC alone is a Decision Record. It is a simple sign-off: the decision, its date, and its scope. It carries no figures of the Bank, no data, and no code.
