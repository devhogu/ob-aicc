## 1. Hypothesis

For one Customer Service team handling repeat contacts about unresolved issues, a case-resolution assistant assembles a verified, source-linked history and proposes a next step from the process function's approved action list. Compared with reconstructing the case manually across systems, this should reduce avoidable repeat contacts and handling effort. The employee reviews every output, decides and acts.

This is distinct from INI-006's discovery of service inefficiencies across the Bank. Intake must confirm the requester, size of the problem, catalog overlap and strategic relevance; entering the Funnel does not record that screening as completed.

## 3. Scope and the minimum viable product

One journey, one queue, one Customer Service team. The choice among payment issue resolution, card dispute progress support and onboarding/KYC progress support is made at Scoping with the Domain Owner and process function. None is selected by this registration.

1. Select the journey and queue, define eligibility and repeat/unresolved terms, and confirm baseline sources during Discovery.
2. Obtain approved read-only historical extracts in the Lab, assess personal data and reconstruct/label cases with Domain Experts.
3. Lock the Evaluation set and agree the status vocabulary and permitted action list with the process function.
4. Build the context service, assistant and employee workspace; compare context-only and assisted views on unseen past cases. End the Experiment with an Outcome Report.
5. Obtain required validation, Team final acceptance and Bank change management before first users; train one team and run the Service within its approved boundary.
6. Compare live results with the existing process and obtain the recorded decision after the MVP.

Minimum viable product: The narrow two-phase employee-assist pilot described in the supplied proposal, ending with evidence for the MVP decision. Phase time-boxes, costs and observation period are not set. Later Capabilities are defined only after a recorded continue decision.

Out of scope: Model-held tools or writes; autonomous customer messages; moving or correcting funds; regulated credit, pricing, eligibility, fraud, AML, sanctions, reimbursement or compensation decisions; voice transcription; general customer 360, a customer chatbot or enterprise-wide knowledge base; unrelated reuse of customer data.

## 5. Risks, dependencies, and Risk Tier

Expected Risk Tier: Risk Tier 2, because customer personal data informs employee advice. This is expected, not assigned or cleared. The assistant holds no tools and writes nothing; each write is an employee action. Model-held writes would raise the Tier.

Control Functions to clear the business case: Model risk, information security, data protection, compliance and legal. While the AICC Lead builds, the Executive Sponsor approves the Solution Definition and assigns its Risk Tier; testing/checking must be by someone other than the builder, under Operating Model 4.4.

Dependencies: DEP-014 applies to clearance and validation. The proposal shares source needs with INI-006 through DEP-008 and DEP-009; those remain Open and do not establish access or permission for this project. Candidates to register at intake include the process function and Domain Experts, approved procedures/action list, source-owner extracts/live reads, IT identity/desktop/integration/environment/change support, Platform Owner hosting/gateway/logging/monitoring and source-owner baseline reports. Their project-specific identifiers, owners and needed-by dates are not yet recorded.

Risks include missing read interfaces/customer keys/timestamps, interpretation quality across Russian/Kyrgyz/mixed text, insufficient eligible volume and unavailable business/test capacity. Narrow scope, approved extracts or manual handling are the proposal's fallbacks; no fallback is marked implemented.
