## 1. Facts

The owner requested shared names across languages, with English meanings and Russian explanations. The initial reference identified an existing contradiction: Vocabulary and Style 3.3 and 3.8 excluded terms from external frameworks and listed common terms such as KPI, SLA and WIP limit as not used. The owner clarified that established business terminology belongs in ordinary corpus usage and instructed reconciliation.

## 2. Options

1. Preserve the former terminology exclusions and explain the common words only as outside references.
2. Prefer established industry terms, define their meanings and their AICC application, and retain local names only where a real distinction requires them.

## 3. Decision

The second option is adopted.

1. Vocabulary and Style revision 3.0 and Document Catalog revision 3.0 are the active reconciled wording in both languages. Portfolio Management Model correction revision 2.2 clarifies the Jira Epic mapping. This is a change after approved corpus baseline edition 2.2; the original baseline decision remains in the history.
2. Related terms explain relationships and distinctions, rather than prohibiting common words. Metric, KPI, SLA, WIP, WIP limit, business case and value stream may be used with their proper meanings. The new definitions do not designate particular metrics as KPIs or assert that a particular SLA has been agreed.
3. An SLA may be internal and may form part of a Service Agreement. Terminology alone introduces no guarantee, penalty or additional commitment. WIP retains the applicable measurement boundary; WIP limit names the existing limit without changing its amount. Business case identifies the justification; Initiative Brief identifies its AICC record. A value stream and a workflow can describe related views of the same work.
4. Shared Business and Technology Terminology is a companion reference, not a tenth governing document. The owner-selected names and the White recommendations provide the naming reference; gray entries remain choices to evaluate in context. The first edition is English with Russian explanations, as requested. Its portal route identifies English content when viewed through Russian navigation.
5. Reconcile directly affected rules, definitions, source metadata and generated pages now. The comprehensive rewrite of existing Russian prose using all selected shared forms is a subsequent pass. Historical decisions, exact identifiers and quoted titles retain their identity.

## 4. Authority and limits

This record captures the corpus owner's instruction. It does not assert a Steering meeting, a separate signature, a legal review or a decision by an unnamed officer. Service commitments, financial authority, measurement populations, work hierarchy, cadence, Risk Tiers and release gates remain as recorded in the governing documents.
