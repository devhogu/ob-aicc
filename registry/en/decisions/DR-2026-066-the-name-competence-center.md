## 1. Facts

The acronym AICC was used as the name of the unit in the text of the corpus, the Records and the portal, and the portal also called one of its sections "Center". A reader met three names for one thing.

## 2. Decision

1. In reader-facing text the unit is named the Competence Center (Russian: «Центр компетенций»); in a title, and in the title of a document, it is the AI Competence Center («Центр компетенций по AI»).
2. The role AICC Lead is named the Competence Center Lead («руководитель Центра компетенций»).
3. AICC stays only as a code: paths and addresses, document identifiers such as AICC-TPL-02, file names, and other identifiers.
4. Closed evidence records are not rewritten (Operating Model 7.4); records made from today use the new name.

## 3. Authority and limits

This record captures the instruction of the Competence Center Lead as the owner of the documents and the Records. It changes names only: no role, duty, right, decision, state, or commitment changes.

## 4. Instruction and evidence

The instruction in the project conversation on 2026-10-07:

> lets keep aicc as a code - for html path, for document codes, for things which are not so visible and jump into the face. those are Competence Center and with title, we just add AI but for the rest we just say Competence Center
