## 1. Facts

The English source has been reconciled across the charter, the explanatory portal pages, the Registry, and the Portfolio. All nine governing documents and fourteen Templates are active. The four Standing Initiative Briefs were entered as Proposed because their establishment approval was not yet recorded. The baseline owner has now approved and instructed their reconciliation and a settled baseline for translation.

## 2. Options

1. Retain a provisional baseline with the Standing Initiative establishment left open.
2. Establish the approved baseline, settle the Briefs and initial operating limits, and maintain subsequent work through the charter's normal decisions and records.

## 3. Decision

The second option is adopted.

1. English source edition 2.2 is the approved baseline for translation. The charter has no outstanding editorial finding or baseline approval action. Each document and Template retains its own current revision; approval of the source set does not renumber unchanged documents.
2. INI-009, INI-010, INI-011, and INI-012 are Approved Standing Initiatives. Their Briefs have no open sections. They become Active when their first approved run-rate Feature is pulled. Their quarterly review and subsequent decisions belong to the Executive Sponsor under Business Model 4.9.
3. The Standing Initiatives share the existing AICC Team capacity. No additional funding, headcount, or fixed allocation to a service area is established. Admission follows the ranked Program Backlog at the Weekly Review.
4. The initial Team limit is one Feature in progress across ordinary and run-rate delivery combined. Active, Completed, Review, and Waiting after work starts share that limit. Ready holds at most two Features. A Capability has a separate limit of one in progress and one Ready, because it groups Features rather than adding delivery capacity. Every lane and Domain uses these shared limits. The portfolio has a shared limit of one Active ordinary Initiative across MVP and Implementation; Standing Initiatives are excluded as the charter requires. The boards record the limits and the normal cadence reviews them.
5. Each Standing Initiative starts with zero admitted and accepted Features. Rates and elapsed times have no observations at establishment. Its initial operating targets are acceptance within the admitting Iteration and complete acceptance evidence for every accepted Feature. A Feature states the business benefit and the evidence required for its own result.
6. RI-008 is Closed and DEP-015 is Met. The twelve Package Definitions are accepted as the baseline descriptions of the catalog; their availability statuses continue to describe the material that actually exists.
7. The English-to-Russian work starts from the file set in `portal/translation-source.json`. It preserves the source revisions, the strength of each obligation, the defined terms, the decision rights, and the recorded state of the work. Subsequent English changes are dated and reconciled with their translations.

This is approval of the baseline and its initial configuration. Operational records continue to report actual events. The existing Service Agreement deficiency, appointments, applicability confirmations, provider checks, and per-use approvals remain subject to their recorded actions and the normal gates of the charter. This decision does not state that an unrecorded appointment, signature, legal confirmation, check, or delivery has taken place.

## 4. Conflicts and advice

The initial limits reflect the one-person AICC Team recorded in the Appointments Record. Existing separation-of-duties requirements and the independent decisions of the Control Functions remain in force. The evidence for this baseline approval is the owner's instruction; no separate Steering meeting or signature by a named officer is asserted.

## Language clarification, 2026-10-03

The baseline owner's subsequent instruction removes the designation of English as authoritative. English identifies the language in which the initial baseline was prepared; approval of that baseline does not establish language precedence. Changes may originate in either language. Differences are reconciled against the recorded decisions and change history to establish which version has drifted. This clarification does not reopen the baseline approval or change the operational decisions above.
