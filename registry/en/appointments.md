# Appointments

This is the Appointments Record, in the form of the Appointments Record Template. It maps the Roles of the charter to real people. It is the Record that names the Holders of the Roles, and it holds their names and posts, and their declarations, consents, and access, and no other personal data. Part C is append-only. One person may hold several Roles; each is in its own row. The names live here and not in the charter.
