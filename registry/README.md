# Registry

The Registry holds the Records of AICC: the process records of the work in progress and delivered, the decisions, the proposals, and the governance records. It holds no figures of the Bank, no data, and no code. They change continuously and are kept by whoever does the work. The AICC Lead is accountable for all of them. The history of the repository is the audit trail.

| Record | Where | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Portfolio Backlog | [portfolio-backlog.md](portfolio-backlog.md) | The ranked Initiatives |
| Program Backlog | [program-backlog.md](program-backlog.md) | The ranked Epics and Features |
| Kanban boards | [board.md](board.md) | The Portfolio Kanban and the Program Kanban, by state, with lanes and Limits on Work in Progress |
| Roadmap | [roadmap.md](roadmap.md) | The three-month Roadmap by Program Increment, and the Milestones |
| Calendar | [calendar.md](calendar.md) | Program Increments, Iterations, weeks, and the blocked and gray days |

Identifiers: PRI-n priority, INI-nnn Initiative, SOL-nnn Solution (in the Portfolio), EP-nnn Epic, FT-nnn Feature, DEP-nnn Dependency, MS-nnn Milestone, DR-yyyy-nnn Decision, RI-nnn risk or issue, ARC-nnn and PLT-nnn standards.

The solutions and services that AICC defines and tries are in the Portfolio (`portfolio/`). Earlier versions of the Records are in `wiki/archive/records-v1/`.
