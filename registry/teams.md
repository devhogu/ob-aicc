# Teams

The Teams that deliver. A Team is a Solution Engineer with the Domain Expert and the Domain Owner, who is the product owner. One person may be in several Teams. Capacity is in days for each Iteration.

| Team | Members | Product owner | Domain | Capacity per Iteration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AICC Team | Timur Alimbayev (AICC Lead and Solution Engineer) | Executive Sponsor, for enabling work | AICC | [ Days available per IT after leave and other duties, stated by the AICC Lead before a Service Agreement is issued ] |
| FP&A Team | Timur Alimbayev (Solution Engineer); [ the Domain Expert, named by the Domain Owner ] | Ademi Moldogazieva | FP&A | [ Days committed in the Service Agreement ] |
| Compliance, HR, legal, finance, accounting | Timur Alimbayev (Solution Engineer); [ the Domain Experts, named by the Domain Owners ] | The head of each function | Functions | [ Days committed in the Service Agreements ] |
| Commercial front office, retail, commercial sales | Timur Alimbayev (Solution Engineer); [ the Domain Experts, named by the Domain Owners ] | The heads of the functions | Front office | [ Days committed in the Service Agreements ] |
| Retail credit | Timur Alimbayev (Solution Engineer); [ the Domain Expert, named by the Domain Owner ] | The head of the retail credit function | Retail credit | [ Days committed in the Service Agreements ] |
