# Risks and Issues

One line for each risk, issue, deficiency, AI Incident, Exception, and Finding. Type: Risk, Issue, Deficiency, Incident, Exception, or Finding. Severity: for an Incident, as the incident management of the Bank classifies it; for a Risk, Issue, or Finding Blocker, Major, or Minor. Expires: the date on which an Exception expires (AI Policy 6.1), and empty for any other type. Due or review: the date or the event at which the action is due or the item is reviewed. The Executive Sponsor reviews the open Exceptions each month, and an Exception that has expired is decided at once.
