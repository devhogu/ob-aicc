# DR-2026-022 The Cadence is a general flow without dates

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead. The Calendar is the dated timeline. The Cadence is the general flow of the events, by Program Increment, Iteration, and week, with no dates. It assumes a clean calendar, with Monday and Friday as the planning and review days of every week. It is the template and the guidance for a dated calendar of events, built later for a rolling two quarters.

## Decision

1. Every week starts with Weekly Planning on Monday and ends with Weekly Review on Friday. The Daily Stand-up is assumed and not marked.
2. An Iteration starts with Iteration Planning in W1, and its last week is the review week: Iteration Review and Demo, Retrospective, and the monthly Steering.
3. The last week of the third Iteration of a Program Increment is the Innovation and Planning week: PI Review and Demo, Inspect and Adapt, innovation, PI Planning, and the quarterly Steering, in this order.
4. The dated calendar of events for the current and the next Program Increment is built at PI Planning from this flow and the Calendar, and is not built yet.

## Revisit

At the first PI Planning, in the Innovation and Planning week of 2026-PIQ4.
