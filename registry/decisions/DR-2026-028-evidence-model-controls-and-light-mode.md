# DR-2026-028 The evidence model, the controls, and the light mode

*Written before the Decision Record Template (DR-2026-029), in its own form.*

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead, after the shape review. All thirteen states are kept.

## Decision

1. The Operating Model 9 states the evidence model once. The working state (backlogs, boards, Roadmap, Calendar, Dependency Map, Dashboard, Teams, Program Increment folder) is held in the Registry until a cutover Decision of the AICC Lead, and then in Jira and Confluence. The evidence records are always in the Registry as closed and dated extracts. Jira, Confluence, and Service Management are not an evidence store.
2. The Registry is kept in a repository with a protected main branch and restricted visibility, with its history not rewritten. Records are retained for the period that the Bank requires for their type. Personal data is limited to names and posts.
3. The Operating Model 10 lists the controls an auditor can test, with their rule, owner, timing, evidence record, and Template. Templates not yet in the Document Catalog are marked "to be added": Decision Record, Steering Summary, Registry Snapshot, AI Incident Review, and Assignment Map.
4. While the Team has up to three people, AICC runs in light mode (Operating Model 7.5): one weekly session, the retrospective and the monthly Steering held in the IT Review and Demo, Inspect and Adapt held in the PI Review and Demo, optional Daily Stand-up, Backlog Refinement, and Innovation, and a smaller use of the states and Stages.
5. The Registry README lists the Records by class: working state, living records, and evidence records.

## Revisit

At the first PI Planning, in the IP week of 2026-PIQ4.
