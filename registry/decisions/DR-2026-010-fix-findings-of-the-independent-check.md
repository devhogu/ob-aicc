# DR-2026-010 Fix the findings of the independent check

Date: 2026-09-30. Decided by the AICC Lead. Basis: the independent checks in portfolio/assessments/2026-09-30-3/ (consistency and coverage).

## Decisions

1. Separation: no one validates or checks work they built; the owner accepts and releases but does not validate. The release is decided by the Domain Owner for Risk Tier 1 and 2 and by the Executive Sponsor for Risk Tier 3. The AICC Engineer does not release.
2. Until AICC has a second engineer, the Tier 1 check is done by a named engineer of the technology function or the Domain.
3. Until the Contact of model risk is named, the AICC Lead may assign Risk Tier 1 to a Use Case whose attributes all indicate it; anything else waits. Until a Control Function names its Contact, nothing that needs it proceeds.
4. Validation comes before a Tier 2 or 3 Pilot. A change of model, provider, data, or scope returns the Use Case to Discovery.
5. Internal audit gives assurance only. It does not validate, release, or stop, and it has read access to every Record.
6. The AICC Lead prepares the report to the Board Committee each quarter, and the Executive Sponsor approves and issues it.
7. The documents are checked when their meaning changes and once a year, by a person the Executive Sponsor names.
8. Restored in one line each: training before use, Community of Practice, knowledge sources, deputies, record retention, yearly review triggers, Measures with baselines.
9. Not restored, on purpose: secretaries, quorums, separate Forums, Readiness Levels.
