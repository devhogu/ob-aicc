# DR-2026-024 Hierarchy, states, and offering types

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead, on the direction that AICC works as a SAFe-inspired lab and keeps Jira statuses clean.

## Decision

1. The levels are the Strategic Priority, the Initiative (a business program in the Portfolio Backlog), the Solution (a solution or service with a type, a Risk Tier, and a receiver), the Epic (a capability in the Program Backlog, over one or more Program Increments), the Feature (closes within one Program Increment), and the Work Item. The Use Case is replaced by the Solution.
2. There are three backlogs: the Portfolio Backlog (Initiatives), the Program Backlog or PI Backlog (Epics and Features), and the IT Backlog (the Features in work, on a Kanban with priority lanes).
3. Every item is in one of thirteen states: proposed, discovery, approved, active, waiting, deferred, completed, review, accepted, closed, pivoted, rejected, cancelled. A Stage is a phase of the work inside discovery or active.
4. A Solution has one type: Service (AICC owns its life cycle, with a business case with run cost and a sunset), Product (built for one consumer, supported on demand), or Experiment (time-boxed, ends in a Proposal and a Handoff accepted by the receiver). The receiver is named in the Solution Definition.
5. A Feature that cannot close within its Program Increment is split. A Feature is approved only when its Dependencies are known.
6. AICC oversees and reports on the Adoption of Solutions that others deliver. The AI adoption strategy is a series of Proposals.
7. Jira carries five statuses (Backlog, Ready, Active, Review, Done), a flag for waiting, and a resolution (Closed, Pivoted, Rejected, Cancelled). The business states are in the charter and the Registry.
8. The Use Case Card is replaced by the Solution Definition Template. The Registry's Use Case Cards became Features of INI-001, and the FP&A pipeline became SOL-001 in the Portfolio.

## Supersedes

The Stages Intake, Discovery, Pilot, Scale, Operate, and Retire of the Operating Model, and the single Program Backlog of Initiatives and Use Cases.

## Revisit

At the first PI Planning, in the IP week of 2026-PIQ4.
