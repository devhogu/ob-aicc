# DR-2026-035 The Control Matrix

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Facts

The controls table in the Operating Model 10 gives the rule, the owner, and the evidence record of each control, but not how a control is tested, where its latest evidence is, or whether it has operated yet.

## Decision

1. Each control in the Operating Model 10 has a reference C-01 to C-26.
2. The charter holds what does not change: the rule, owner, and timing of each control in the Operating Model 10, and its objective, type, and test in the Unit governance guide 7. The Registry keeps the Control Matrix, a living record with the latest evidence and the status of each control, and the populations for sampling.
3. The AICC Lead updates it at each monthly Steering and at each Registry Snapshot.

## Revisit

When internal audit has reviewed the Matrix.

*Note of 2026-10-02: the citations above are stale. The controls are in the Operating Model 8, not 10, and there are 32 of them, C-01 to C-32 (DR-2026-042), not C-01 to C-26. The record is otherwise as it was decided.*
