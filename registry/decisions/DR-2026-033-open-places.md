# DR-2026-033 Open places carry a bracketed comment

*In its own short form, not the Decision Record Template.*

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Decision

1. The charter names Roles and responsibilities only. It holds no name of a person and no body that exists in the Bank. Real names, bodies, dates, and references are entered in the records of the Registry, such as the Appointments Record and the Appointments.
2. Each open place carries a bracketed comment, written as "[ ... ]", that states what is expected there and why. A place is open until the comment is replaced by the entry.
3. The open places are in the Organization guide 8, the Collaboration Tooling workflow, the Appointments, the Priorities, the Teams, the Portfolio Backlog, the Initiative Briefs, the Board, and the Solution Definition SOL-001.

4. The Assignment Map of DR-2026-029 is the Appointments Record.

## Revisit

When the entries are made.
