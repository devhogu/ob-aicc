# DR-2026-044 The Portfolio Management Model

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Facts

The Solution Lifecycle Model held three layers in one document: the portfolio, the program, and the iteration. The portfolio layer decides business and commercial matters, which is investment, priority, and business cases. The program and the iteration run a cadence of events. The two are different processes with different decision makers.

## Options

1. Keep the three layers in one document.
2. Separate the portfolio layer into its own model and leave the program and the iteration in the Solution Lifecycle Model.

## Decision

1. The Portfolio Management Model (AICC-ORG-02) is the Lean Portfolio Management of AICC. It draws on the Lean Portfolio Management of the Scaled Agile Framework, on ISO 21504, and on the Standard for Portfolio Management of the Project Management Institute. The Solution Lifecycle Model becomes AICC-ORG-03.
2. The Strategic Priorities are the strategic themes. An Initiative moves through the portfolio Kanban: funnel (Proposed), Reviewing (Discovery: Scoping), Analyzing (Discovery: Business case), Portfolio Backlog (Approved), MVP and Implementation (Active), and Done (Review, Accepted, Closed). At the end of the MVP the approver decides to persevere, pivot, or stop. A pivot is a Pivoted Initiative and a new linked one, and a stop is Cancelled.
3. The business case is the Initiative Brief, a one-page lean business case. It meets criteria that follow the five questions of the five case model: strategic, economic, financial, delivery, and management. The Control Function Contacts are consulted on a business case that expects Risk Tier 2 or 3.
4. The Initiatives are ranked by the weighted shortest job first method, from the scores that AICC already keeps.
5. Investment and priority are set at the level of the Initiative. A Capability is an Epic in Jira only, and is called a Capability everywhere else. The Program Backlog groups the Capabilities under their Initiatives.
6. The portfolio adds no meeting: the monthly Steering is the portfolio sync, and the quarterly Steering is the portfolio review.
7. The Document Catalog allows nine documents.

## Revisit

When the Solution Lifecycle Model is reshaped as the program execution model.
