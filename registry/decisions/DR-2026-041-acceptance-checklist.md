# DR-2026-041 The Acceptance Checklist

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Facts

The handover of a ready Solution to a Domain depended on several records: the check in the AI Registry, a Control Sign-Off for each Control Function, and the release in the Solution Definition. The Domain, the compliance function, and the others who carry the main questions had no single record that showed what each confirmed at the handover.

## Decision

1. When AICC hands a Solution to a Domain as ready for use at scale, before its release beyond the first users, the AICC Lead completes the Acceptance Checklist (Template AICC-TPL-13). It is not used during development or trials, where the check and the validation apply.
2. It lists, for each party, the items that the party confirms within its remit and signs: the Domain Owner, the Solution Engineer, the AICC Lead, the Checker, the Control Functions (model risk, compliance, information security, data protection, and legal), and the IT function that operates the Solution with the Platform Owner. An item that is not met stops the release. The Domain Owner receives the checklist signed and signs the acceptance of the package.
3. The rules of the check and the validation are unchanged. A Domain adopts a Solution of Risk Tier 1 or 2 on its own risk, within the AI Risk Appetite Statement. A Solution of Risk Tier 3, which is of high impact and risk, is adopted only with the signature of the Executive Sponsor, who accepts the risk and releases it.
4. Operating Model 5.2(d) reads: a Decision goes to a higher level when it accepts a risk beyond the AI Risk Appetite Statement, or concerns a Risk Tier 3 Solution. A risk accepted within the appetite stays with the Domain.
5. The checklist adds no approval of its own. The decisions stay with the parties under the AI Policy, and the evidence is kept in the Registry as `checklists/ACL-nnn.md`.

## Revisit

At the first Solution that reaches deployment.
