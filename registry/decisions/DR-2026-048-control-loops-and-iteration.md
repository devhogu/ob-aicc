# DR-2026-048 The control loops, the full word Iteration, and the alignment of the pack

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Decision

1. The Operating Model section 6 is the five control loops of the unit: direction (yearly), assurance (quarterly), control (monthly), operating (weekly), and the event loop. Each is a Plan, Do, Check, Act cycle with its forum, its decider, its interfaces to the loop above and below, and its records. They run on the events of the Cadence, with the four portfolio loops of the Portfolio Management Model, and add no meeting.
2. Iteration is written in full everywhere. IT means information technology only. The short code of an Iteration is I01 to I12, and the files of the Iterations of 2026-PIQ4 are I10, I11, and I12.
3. The workflows, guides, templates, and registry records follow the Portfolio Management Model and the control loops: the portfolio Kanban steps, the clearance of the Control Function Contacts, the decision after the MVP, Rejected as a decision on the merits and Cancelled as a withdrawal, the quarterly risk check at the quarterly Steering, and the priorities and funding set once a year.
4. The legacy flow of the portfolio in the service delivery workflow, the horizon table of the unit governance workflow, and the interim approval wording are removed.

## Revisit

When the Solution Lifecycle Model is reshaped as the program execution model.
