# DR-2026-015 Fixes from the acceptance assessment

Date: 2026-09-30. Decided by the AICC Lead. Basis: portfolio/assessments/2026-09-30-5/ (documents, and end to end).

## Decisions

1. First activation needs the ten questions, sets the next whole revision, and a later change is a new draft that does not replace the active text until it is activated. The activation binds the Bank; an Entity takes part by its own recorded decision.
2. Appointments missing at activation are made within 60 days, with acting Holders named by the Executive Sponsor meanwhile. They do not block activation.
3. Existing AI uses are tolerated for 90 days only for public or unclassified data. Data of a class with no approved Solution is not used in any Stage until data protection and model risk clear it.
4. The Executive Sponsor approves AI output published to investors, lenders, regulators, or the Board, with a record of the review and approval for each edition, and may name a delegate.
5. The AI Risk Appetite Statement is approved by the Executive Sponsor and noted by the Board Committee in its first report. Until the Board Committee is named, the report goes to the chair of the Board.
6. A validation may state what a Solution shall not be used for. For the mortgage analysis: no output on an individual application, and the AI contribution and the decision of the person are recorded.
7. The AI Registry holds models, providers, and assistants as well as Use Cases, with the date of the provider check.
8. Staffing gaps are tracked as risks (RI-008, RI-009, RI-011, RI-016, RI-019) and are for the Executive Sponsor.
