# DR-2026-023 Short forms in the cadence

*Written before the Decision Record Template (DR-2026-029), in its own form.*

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead. The cadence uses the short forms PI (Program Increment), IT (Iteration), and IP (Innovation and Planning), so that the flow is easy to read and to track through the year.

## Decision

1. The short forms are PI, IT, IP, and the names PIQ1 to PIQ4, IT01 to IT12, and W1 to W5. The events are IT Planning, IT Review and Demo, IT Retrospective, and the IT Backlog, with the PI events unchanged.
2. The Cadence ends with a vocabulary of the short forms, the loops, and the events with their intent. It holds the control flow only. What is done inside each event is described later in a separate artifact, the workflows.

## Revisit

At the first PI Planning, in the IP week of 2026-PIQ4.
