# DR-2026-029 The evidence Templates

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead, after the shape review and the re-evaluation of the charter.

## Decision

1. Twelve Templates give the form of the records that the workflows say are kept in the Registry as evidence: Initiative Brief, Service Agreement, Solution Definition, Control Sign-Off, Decision Record, Steering Summary, Outcome Report, AI Incident Review, Registry Snapshot, Quarterly Report, Assignment Map, and Proposal. The Document Catalog lists them in the order of use, with their identifiers unchanged.
2. The Steering Summary replaces the Notes. The notes of the other events stay in Confluence or in the work items.
3. The Control Sign-Off is one simple decision with a date and a scope, for a validation, a stop, a provider check, or an Exception.
4. Each target is stated once, in the Initiative Brief. The Service Agreement, the Solution Definition, and the Outcome Report point to it.
5. The Assignment Map maps the Roles to people, with the append-only appointment log, the declarations, the access, and the delegations of the Executive Sponsor. The charter holds the Roles, and the Map is the only record that names people.
