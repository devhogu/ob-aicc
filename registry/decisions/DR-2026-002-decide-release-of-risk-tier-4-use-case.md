## 2. Context

Risk Tier Policy 4.1 requires a decision of the AI Steering Committee before release of a Tier 4 Use Case. No Decision Category, Decision Rights row, agenda item, or Scale exit carries it. Finding: F-009, ISS-001.

## 3. Options and criteria

1. Domain Owner decides after validation (as now).
2. The AI Steering Committee decides, after validation by the Control Functions.

Criteria: one accountable Role, consistency with higher documents, proportionality to a unit run by one person.

## 4. Decision

The AI Steering Committee decides the release of a Risk Tier 4 Use Case after validation. The Portfolio Decision Category, the Decision Rights (accountable: AI Steering Committee), the AI Steering Committee agenda Template, and the Scale exit of Portfolio Management are corrected in Iteration 3.

## 5. Rationale

The highest Risk Tier needs collective accountability, and the Risk Tier Policy already requires it.

## 6. Risks accepted

The AICC Lead decides as drafter and owner before an Executive Sponsor is appointed. The Executive Sponsor may revisit the Decision at activation.
