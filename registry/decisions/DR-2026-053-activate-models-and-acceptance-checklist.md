## 1. Facts

- The Portfolio Management Model (AICC-ORG-02) was created on 2026-10-01 (DR-2026-044) and is at revision 1.9. The Solution Lifecycle Model (AICC-ORG-03) was created on 2026-10-01 (DR-2026-040) and is at revision 2.9. Template AICC-TPL-13 Acceptance Checklist was created on 2026-10-01 (DR-2026-041) and is at revision 2.3.
- The three carry the status active in their metadata block since they were created, and no Decision Log entry records their activation. DR-2026-018 activated six documents and five Templates, and DR-2026-034 activated the Business Model and twelve Templates.
- The Document Catalog 4.1 requires the AICC Lead to activate every document and Template, to record the date (for a Template, in the Decision Log entry, because it has no change log), and to enter the Decision in the Decision Log. The Document Catalog 7.1 requires the ten questions before activation, and 7.3 makes that check the control C-04.

## 2. Options

1. Leave the status active and enter no Decision.
2. Set the three back to draft until they are checked.
3. Answer the ten questions, record the result, and activate them from the date of this Decision.

## 3. Decision

The AICC Lead activates the Portfolio Management Model at revision 1.9, the Solution Lifecycle Model at revision 2.9, and Template AICC-TPL-13 Acceptance Checklist at revision 2.3, from 2026-10-02, the date of this Decision. They carried the status active before that date, and this Decision records the activation that the Document Catalog 4.1 requires. The activation binds the Bank.
