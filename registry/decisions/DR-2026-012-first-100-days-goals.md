# DR-2026-012 Goals of the first 100 days, September to December 2026

Date: September 2026 (reconstructed; the exact date was not recorded). Decided by the Executive Sponsor (Simen Munter); recorded by the AICC Lead on the AICC Lead's report of the meeting. It is recorded as reported; no further proof is kept. The wording and the scope are restated in DR-2026-013.

## Decision

The focus of AICC for the first 100 days is as follows.

1. INI-002: identify and present the current service architecture landscape, from commercial portfolio management to IT architecture and infrastructure, to see where AI adoption can start with least disturbance and where it has started.
2. INI-003: work with compliance, HR, legal, and finance to find opportunities to use generative AI in daily work, on the routine mechanical tasks of files and reports.
3. INI-004: work with the FP&A analytics function to bring its AI-generated reports to Board visibility, with a portal and an agentic pipeline for consistent monthly and quarterly reporting of financial metrics to investors.
4. INI-005: introduce HR, compliance, and accounting to AI and train them on the job.
5. INI-006: a larger exploration of the commercial front office: gather customer experience intelligence in one place and analyze it.
6. INI-007: for retail credit lines, loans, and mortgages, consolidate and analyze why mortgages were rejected, looking into the reasoning (a person always decides on a mortgage), build a knowledge base, and improve routine daily work.
7. INI-008: build up the legal knowledge base.

## Stage of the work

The goals are being scoped with each function. No architecture or Solution has been produced yet. A Risk Tier is assigned to each Use Case when a proposal takes shape, and the checks against the controls come before any Pilot.

## Revisit

At the quarterly Steering that assesses the results of the quarter, January 2027.
