# DR-2026-020 Agile cadence and Records aligned with SAFe and Kanban

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead, on the direction to structure the flow, cadence, calendar, backlogs, roadmap, dependency map, and dashboard as the Scaled Agile Framework and Kanban would. Only the scaffolding and the intent of the events are in place now, not the process of the Iteration or Program level.

## Decision

1. Work runs in Program Increments of one quarter: six Iterations of two weeks and one Innovation and Planning week at the end. The first is 2026Q4, from 2026-10-05.
2. The events of each loop and their intent are in Operating Model 7.1: Daily Stand-up, Backlog Refinement, Iteration Planning, Iteration Review and Demo, Iteration Retrospective, PI Review and Demo, Inspect and Adapt, PI Planning, innovation, and the Steering, monthly and quarterly.
3. One Program Backlog flows through the Program Kanban. Teams commit Work Items to an Iteration Backlog at each Iteration Planning. The Roadmap shows three months by Program Increment.
4. New Records: Calendar, Teams, Program Kanban, Dependency Map, Dashboard, and a folder for each Program Increment with its PI Objectives, Iterations, and Innovation and Planning week results.
5. The goals of the first 100 days are the PI Objectives of 2026Q4.
6. The Vocabulary replaces the Sync and Demo, the Quarterly Review, and the Objective with the SAFe terms. The wiki records the mapping.

## Revisit

At the first PI Planning, in the Innovation and Planning week of 2026Q4.
