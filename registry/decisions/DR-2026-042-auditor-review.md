# DR-2026-042 Changes from the auditor's reading of the two models

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Facts

An independent reader took the Operating Model and the Solution Lifecycle Model as an internal auditor would, and filtered what an auditor needs to plan and test.

## Decision

1. The AICC Lead shall not Check, validate, accept, or release a Solution that the AICC Lead built. The Executive Sponsor accepts and releases it. The last sentence of Operating Model 4.6 follows this.
2. Operating Model 4.6 is split into 4.6 (appointers), 4.7 (deputies and delegation, which excludes the decisions under 5.4 and 5.7), and 4.8 (transition and recording).
3. A stop by a Control Function is final. Only a suspension is lifted, by the person who suspended it. Any Control Function Contact may suspend a Solution.
4. The monthly Steering reviews a sample of at least three Decisions of the AICC Lead, chosen by the Executive Sponsor. The quarterly risk check has a stated content.
5. Only the AICC Lead and the named deputy merge to the main branch of the Registry. A closed evidence record is corrected by a new dated entry. The keepers compare access with the Appointments Record each quarter.
6. A control that did not operate, and each audit or supervisory finding, is entered in Risks and Issues with an owner and a due date, and the monthly Steering reviews it until closed (Operating Model 8.2).
7. The Solution Lifecycle Model adds the change to a released Solution (7.3), the review of live Solutions (7.4), and the retirement of a Solution (7.5). A deployment to production follows the change management of the Bank.
8. The controls table is tightened: the benefit confirmation joins C-10, the sample joins C-05, C-03 is the yearly review, C-22 is owned by the Executive Sponsor and tested at each release, and C-26 to C-32 are added. The Unit governance guide and the Control Matrix follow.
9. A Solution whose Risk Tier rises to 3 waits for the release of the Executive Sponsor (AI Policy 3.4).

## Not done, as overbuilt for a unit of this size

A separate risk assessment section, a competence clause for every Holder, a deputy-access rule, and detail on environments and rollback beyond the change management of the Bank.

## Revisit

At the first independent audit of the unit.
