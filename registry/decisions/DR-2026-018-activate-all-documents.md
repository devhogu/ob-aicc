# DR-2026-018 The AICC Lead activates all documents

*Written before the Decision Record Template (DR-2026-029), in its own form.*

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead, who owns the AICC Charter and is responsible for AICC. AICC works in an agile way and does not keep a chain of approvals for its own documents.

## Decision

1. The AICC Lead activates every document and Template. The six documents (Statement of Intent, AICC Charter, Operating Model, AI Policy, Vocabulary and Style, Document Catalog) and the five Templates are active from 2026-10-01.
2. The Executive Sponsor no longer activates documents, and no independent check is needed before activation. The AICC Lead checks the documents with the ten questions, and a second person may check when the AICC Lead or the Executive Sponsor asks.
3. The AICC Lead owns the AI Risk Appetite Statement. The Board Committee notes it. A risk beyond it is accepted only by the Executive Sponsor.
4. The Executive Sponsor still approves AI output published to investors, lenders, regulators, or the Board (DR-2026-014), the Strategic Priorities, the funding, and a Risk Tier 3 release.

## Supersedes

DR-2026-004 (activation rule), and the activation and appetite parts of DR-2026-015.
