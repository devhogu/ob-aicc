# DR-2026-031 The commercial controls and the remaining findings

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead, after the re-evaluation of the charter and the shape review.

## Decision

1. Controls on the commitment (Business Model 7): a capacity ceiling for Service Agreements; intake criteria for an Engagement; the Domain Owner confirms the benefit; a quarterly completeness check of the Outcome Reports; the accepted limit that the AICC Lead issues, delivers, and writes, with a monthly sample by the Steering; the Executive Sponsor owns the budget of AICC. The support of a Service is taken in Service Management, with targets and not guarantees.
2. The Operating Model lists the events and leaves their intent to the Cadence. The rules for moved or missed events are in the Operating Model 7.5. The AI Steering Committee has its terms (7.6). The PI Planning proposes the Roadmap and the quarterly Steering confirms it.
3. An Initiative that spans Domains is approved by the Executive Sponsor. Until the Guardrails are set, the Executive Sponsor approves any commitment.
4. The Risk Tier is confirmed at every validation. The AICC Lead leads the review of an AI Incident.
5. An Adopted Solution is recorded as a Solution Definition marked as such, and a Proposal has its own folder in the Registry.
6. Transition: the Service Agreements of the running Initiatives are to be issued by the end of IT10 2026, and work already accepted needs no Outcome Report.
7. The workflows no longer repeat the deciders, the separation rules, or the life of a document: they point to the documents.

## Revisit

At the first PI Planning, in the IP week of 2026-PIQ4.
