# DR-2026-027 Join the Engagement to the Operating Model, and the small fixes of the shape review

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead, after the shape review (registry/assessments/2026-10-01-3). All thirteen states are kept.

## Decision

1. An Engagement is an Initiative that has a client function. One Service Agreement is issued for each client function. The client of enabling work is the Executive Sponsor.
2. The Service Agreement is issued when the study starts and amended when the business case is approved. It has no states of its own: the state of the Engagement is the state of its Initiative. The Agreement Log is merged into the Portfolio Backlog, which shows the client function, phases, support level, Service Agreement, and Outcome Report.
3. The mandate and the appointments carry a decision reference in the Appointments Record, entered within five working days of each change. The Executive Sponsor may delegate in writing, for a scope and a period.
4. Word fixes: Handover replaces Handoff, the lane Incident is renamed Urgent, "full service" is "run by AICC", "client contact" is retired, "Adoption" is "Adopted Solution", and "Phase" is defined. The check-in at each IT replaces the review of the agreement.
5. The entry: a charter README with a reading path, and "AICC on a page".
6. The Cadence keeps no glossary of its own: the Vocabulary is the one source.
