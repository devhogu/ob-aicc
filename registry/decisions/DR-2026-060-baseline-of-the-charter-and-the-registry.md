## 1. Facts

- The nine documents of the charter are at revision 1.0: the Statement of Intent, the AICC Charter, the Operating Model, the AI Policy, the Business Model, the Portfolio Management Model, the Solution Lifecycle Model, the Vocabulary, and the Document Catalog.
- The 13 Templates are at revision 1.0.
- The documents, the Templates, the workflows, and the guides are the baseline of the unit as of 2026-10-02.
- The Registry holds the live records at their baseline state.
- The check with the ten questions of the Document Catalog 7.1 was made on each document and each Template, and every question is met.
- The unit was organized on 2026-09-02: the appointments were made, the Strategic Priorities were set, and the first Steering was held, and all were agreed (DR-2026-061); no proposal for a budget has been made to the management, so no Investment Envelope is decided; AICC works on Kanban and controls its load by the Limit on Work in Progress.

## 2. Options

1. Activate the documents and the Templates as a clean baseline, and start the Records of the Registry from their baseline state.
2. Hold the activation until the missing appointments are made.

## 3. Decision

The AICC Lead takes the first option, and decides the following.

1. D1: The AICC Lead activates the nine documents and the 13 Templates at revision 1.0, after the check with the ten questions of the Document Catalog 7.1, all met.
2. D2: The baseline is accepted as it is.
3. D3: The Initiative that built the charter is complete with the baseline.
4. D4: The Records of the Registry start at their baseline state: the Decision Log with this Decision and DR-2026-061, the Control Matrix at the status of each control at the baseline, and the Risks and Issues Record with the three accepted limits and the two Issues that the baseline carries (C-08 as RI-004, and the missing appointments as RI-005).
5. D5: The appointments entered in the Appointments Record are those of 2026-09-02 (DR-2026-061). The missing appointments are tracked in RI-005 and are made by 2026-12-01 (Operating Model 4.8).
6. D6: The first events are as the Calendar states: the first monthly Steering, the quarterly Steering of the IP week of 2026-PIQ4, and the yearly Steering of December 2026.

## 4. Conflicts and advice

The AICC Lead builds and decides the baseline. This is an accepted limit (Business Model 7.5, Solution Lifecycle Model 7.3(d)). The Executive Sponsor is informed of the baseline (Document Catalog 4.2). No record exists of the agreement of the Executive Sponsor.

## 5. Where it was decided

This Record; the Decision Log; the baseline rows of the change logs of the nine documents.
