# DR-2026-046 The portfolio loops as figures with a practical reading

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Decision

1. The Portfolio Management Model carries eight figures, each with a practical reading under it: the Roles across the flow of an Initiative, the portfolio Kanban, the gate of a step, the states of an Initiative, the pull from the Portfolio Backlog, the funding loop, the probe loop, and the levels of the work.
2. The table of the steps of the Kanban gains a Record column, which names the record that each step leaves.
3. A figure illustrates and states no rule of its own. The practical reading says how the clauses are applied and adds no rule.

## Revisit

At the first business case that is approved.
