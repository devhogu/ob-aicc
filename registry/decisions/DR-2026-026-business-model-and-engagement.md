# DR-2026-026 The Business Model and the Engagement workflow

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead. AICC works as an internal consulting and innovation lab, with its own commitments and its own record of value and capacity.

## Decision

1. A new document, the Business Model, states what AICC is, whom it serves, what it offers, how it commits, and how it tracks value and capacity. The Charter points to it for what AICC offers.
2. A new top-level workflow, Engagement, shows how AICC and the rest of the Bank work together, from the first contact to the follow-on. It sits on top of the service delivery and unit governance workflows.
3. AICC commits to each Engagement in a Service Agreement: a commitment (phases, support level, scope, deliverables and definition of done, capacity per IT, outcome targets, Assumptions, review, end) and a working agreement. It is issued by the AICC Lead, with no signature chain. AICC commits and the function commits to nothing.
4. An Engagement ends with an Outcome Report. The Agreement Log records every Engagement.
5. Support after delivery is chosen for each Engagement: none, on demand, at agreed response targets, or full service.
6. AICC tracks the capacity it commits and uses, in days, and the benefit that the function claims and confirms, by reference. It does not charge the functions.
7. The Portfolio also keeps the reusable assets and lessons of each Engagement.
8. The Initiative Brief is the business case in the form of the SAFe lean business case, kept as a file for approved and in-progress Initiatives. The volatile detail of trials stays in Jira and Confluence.
