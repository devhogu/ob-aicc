## 2. Context

Eight Forums, seven Registers, 23 Templates, and nine services are active on paper. The Level 1 limits (five Forums, four Registers, ten Templates) cannot be met. Finding: F-012, ISS-008.

## 3. Options and criteria

1. All active at once.
2. Core set of eleven documents; three Forums (AI Steering Committee, Replenishment, Delivery Review); the other constructs dormant until their Maturity Level.

Criteria: one accountable Role, consistency with higher documents, proportionality to a unit run by one person.

## 4. Decision

At Maturity Level 1 the core set is the Statement of Intent, AICC Charter, Operating Model, Roles, Decision Rights, Governance Forums, Decision Management, Portfolio Management, Risk Tier Policy, Document Catalog, and Vocabulary and Style. The active Forums are the AI Steering Committee, Replenishment, and Delivery Review. Every other Forum, Register, Template, and service is marked dormant with its Maturity Level in Iteration 5. The Evolution Plan holds the schedule.

## 5. Rationale

Proportionality: one person holds all Roles, so the active set must be what one person can run.

## 6. Risks accepted

The AICC Lead decides as drafter and owner before an Executive Sponsor is appointed. The Executive Sponsor may revisit the Decision at activation.
