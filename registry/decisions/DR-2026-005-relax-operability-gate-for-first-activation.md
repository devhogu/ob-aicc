## 2. Context

Corpus Assessment D-8 requires Holders and prior use of Templates for R3, and R3 is required for activation. Both can exist only after activation. Finding: F-049, ISS-033.

## 3. Options and criteria

1. Keep D-8 as written.
2. For first activation, score D-8 on the design (Roles defined, Templates complete, procedure written) and require the Holders and first use as conditions of Operate.

Criteria: one accountable Role, consistency with higher documents, proportionality to a unit run by one person.

## 4. Decision

For the first activation, D-8 is scored on whether the Roles, Templates, and procedures are defined and complete. Holders in the Register of Appointments and first use of a Template are conditions for Readiness Level R5 Operating, not for R3. The Corpus Assessment is corrected in Iteration 5.

## 5. Rationale

It removes the circular dependency and keeps the test for operation where it belongs.

## 6. Risks accepted

The AICC Lead decides as drafter and owner before an Executive Sponsor is appointed. The Executive Sponsor may revisit the Decision at activation.
