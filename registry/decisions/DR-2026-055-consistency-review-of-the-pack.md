## 1. Facts

- On 2026-10-02 the nine documents of the charter were read together, with the Records of the Registry, for consistency, after the independent findings and the end-to-end evaluation (DR-2026-052). The documents carry the result in a change-log row that cites this Decision.
- The reading found statements that disagreed, or that left a decider unnamed: where a wait is shown, which Steering runs in the month of the IP week, who decides the AI Risk Appetite Statement, who ranks the backlogs and sets the limit, who approves what the AICC Lead built, what a higher Risk Tier does to a business case, who pays the run of a Solution, and who names the Executive Sponsor and the AI Steering Committee.

## 2. Options

1. Leave the differences and settle them as the cases arise.
2. Decide each of them once, and align the documents and the Records to the decision.

## 3. Decision

The AICC Lead takes the second option, and decides the following.

1. D1: Waiting is a state, shown as a flag on the boards. A suspension is a flag on a Solution and not a state.
2. D2: In the month that holds the IP week the quarterly Steering is also that month's Steering. There is no separate monthly Steering that month, and the quarterly Steering carries the monthly control loop.
3. D3: The IP week is the last week of the third Iteration, except where the Calendar places it earlier and keeps the year-end week free: I12 has five weeks, the IP week is I12W4, and I12W5 is the year-end week with no events. An event that falls on a blocked or gray day moves to the working day before it. Innovation is optional and is dropped first when days are lost.
4. D4: The Executive Sponsor decides the AI Risk Appetite Statement. The AICC Lead owns and reviews it and activates the change on that decision.
5. D5: The controls stay at 32, C-01 to C-32. Controls are extended and none is added: C-01, C-03, C-06, C-07, C-09, C-12, C-15, C-16, C-17, C-20, and C-21.
6. D6: The AICC Lead ranks the Program Backlog and the Portfolio Backlog and sets the limit on the Active Initiatives, within the mix of Initiatives that the Executive Sponsor sets.
7. D7: For a Solution that the AICC Lead built, the Executive Sponsor approves the Solution Definition, assigns the Risk Tier, and approves its use for a data class.
8. D8: A Risk Tier assigned that is higher than the one that the business case expected, or that the Control Function Contacts cleared, returns the business case to the Contacts for clearance.
9. D9: AICC supplies capacity and does not charge for it. The Domain pays the run, the licenses, and the provider costs of its Solutions from its Envelope.
10. D10: The Board names the Executive Sponsor, and the Executive Sponsor names the members of the AI Steering Committee.

## 4. Conflicts and advice

None declared. D3, D4, D7, and D10 give duties to the Executive Sponsor, and no record exists of the agreement of the Executive Sponsor. The AICC Lead tells the Executive Sponsor of the change (Document Catalog 4.2). D4 changes the part of DR-2026-018 that gave the AI Risk Appetite Statement to the AICC Lead; the Executive Sponsor has not yet decided the Statement (RI-032).
