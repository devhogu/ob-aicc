## 2. Context

Statement of Intent 6.2 and 6.3 require every AI to be tested for bias and individuals to be told that they interact with AI. The Risk Tier Policy does not require these for Tiers 1 and 2. The Statement prevails over the Policy. Finding: F-039, ISS-024.

## 3. Options and criteria

1. Raise the Tier 1 and 2 requirements in the Policy.
2. Qualify 6.2 and 6.3 of the Statement by Risk Tier, in line with the Policy.

Criteria: one accountable Role, consistency with higher documents, proportionality to a unit run by one person.

## 4. Decision

Statement of Intent 6.2 and 6.3 are qualified: the testing for bias and the disclosure apply to the extent that the Risk Tier Policy requires for the Risk Tier of the Use Case. The Statement and the Policy are corrected in Iteration 3. The Executive Sponsor confirms at activation of the Statement.

## 5. Rationale

Requirements proportional to risk. Testing every internal productivity use for bias is a cost that the risk does not justify in a unit of one person.

## 6. Risks accepted

The AICC Lead decides as drafter and owner before an Executive Sponsor is appointed. The Executive Sponsor and the Control Functions may revisit the Decision at activation.
