# DR-2026-017 AICC assigns the Risk Tier

*Written before the Decision Record Template (DR-2026-029), in its own form.*

Date: 2026-09-30. Decided by the AICC Lead, on the direction that AICC assigns the categories and informs the function heads.

## Decision

1. The AICC Lead assigns the Risk Tier at Intake, using the attributes in the AI Policy, and tells the Domain Owner the Risk Tier assigned.
2. A Control Function Contact may raise the Risk Tier within its remit, and the Contact of model risk alone may lower it.
3. The validation of Risk Tier 2 and 3 by the Control Function Contacts is unchanged.
4. The Risk Tier no longer waits for a Control Function Contact, so an unnamed Contact delays validation only.

## Revisit

2026-12-31.

Note of 2026-10-01: the AI Policy 3.2 states that the Risk Tier is assigned when the Solution is defined. "At Intake" in item 1 means that the assignment is made when the Solution enters the definition.
