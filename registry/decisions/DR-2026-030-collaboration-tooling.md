# DR-2026-030 The collaboration tooling

*In its own short form, not the Decision Record Template.*

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Decision

1. The charter lists the collaboration tooling and the portals: the Jira project, Service Management (the Bank's IT tool, which AICC joins and uses to host its own services and support), Confluence, the supporting knowledge folder, the corporate share, the AICC portal, the operating portal, and the Service portals. The links are added when the tools are deployed.
2. Jira is a simple business and project management tool and holds no code and no data. Confluence holds the technical guidance and the ways of working in Jira, the architecture repository, and the dashboards. The charter is the organizational governance, and the tools adapt to its workflows.
3. The AICC portal is static and links to the evidence records of the Registry on the corporate share. The operating portal holds non-sensitive information, is static first and later a CMS with a knowledge-base backend, and is updated when new content is available.
4. Tickets in Service Management follow the classification of the Bank, and the records of AICC point to their keys.

## Revisit

When the tools are deployed.
