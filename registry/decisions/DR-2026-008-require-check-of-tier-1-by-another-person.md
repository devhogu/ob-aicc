## 2. Context

Risk Tier Policy 4.1 gives the validation of Tier 1 "By the owner". Roles 8.1 and Statement of Intent 7.3 state that no person validates their own work. Finding: F-038, ISS-004.

## 3. Options and criteria

1. Leave Tier 1 without validation, covered by the AI Use Policy.
2. A check by a person other than the owner, recorded in the AI Registry.

Criteria: one accountable Role, consistency with higher documents, proportionality to a unit run by one person.

## 4. Decision

For Risk Tier 1 the validation is a check by a person other than the owner of the Use Case, recorded in the AI Registry. The Control Function Contact is not required for Tier 1. The Risk Tier Policy is corrected in Iteration 3.

## 5. Rationale

It keeps the separation rule with the lightest possible control.

## 6. Risks accepted

The AICC Lead decides as drafter and owner before an Executive Sponsor is appointed. The Executive Sponsor and the Control Functions may revisit the Decision at activation.
