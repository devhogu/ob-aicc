## 1. Hypothesis

If knowledge bases are built on one common approach across functions, then answers are consistent, cited, and reusable, and each function keeps its knowledge current through a named owner.

## 2. Problem or opportunity, and scope

**Problem or opportunity.** Each function holds knowledge that its people search for or ask about every day, in documents that are not governed as sources. Answers differ, and knowledge leaves with people.

**AICC does.**

1. Agree a common approach for every knowledge base: its sources, a named owner and a review date for each source, access by data class, and answers that cite their source (Standards ARC-001).
2. List with each function the knowledge that its people need daily. Candidates: legal (policies, contracts, regulations), HR (policies and procedures), the commercial functions (products and services), retail credit (credit policies and procedures), and compliance.
3. Rank the candidates by use and feasibility.
4. Build the first knowledge base with legal, and start the second.
5. Record what is learned for the next bases.

**Out of scope.** Publishing knowledge outside the Bank; sources without an owner.

## 3. Outcome, Measures, cost, and risk

**Outcome by 31 December 2026.**

- The common approach.
- A ranked list of knowledge bases by function.
- The first knowledge base, for legal, in use with owned and dated sources.
- The second knowledge base, started.

**Measures.** Sources with an owner and a review date; Questions answered with a cited source; People using the knowledge base. Baselines and targets are set with the Domain Owner by 31 October 2026. They are figures of the Bank, so they are kept in the source system of the function, and this brief points to them.

**Cost.** The time of the AICC Lead. No other cost is stated.

**Risk.** Confidential documents are involved: access follows the data class. Risk Tier 2 is expected.

## 4. Decision and acceptance

Confirmed as a goal of the first 100 days by the Executive Sponsor (DR-2026-012), with the wording and scope restated in DR-2026-013. The business case is approved when scoping is complete.

Acceptance on delivery: The head of each function, for its knowledge base; the Executive Sponsor for the common approach. Acceptance closes the item.
