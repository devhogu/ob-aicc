## 1. Hypothesis

If AICC holds its way of working in a short set of documents that its people can follow, then the documents can be used as written, and the check of the AICC Lead with the ten questions of the Document Catalog finds no open Blocker or Major Finding. An independent check is optional.

## 2. Business outcomes and leading indicators

| Business outcome | Leading indicator | Where the figures live | Date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The documents, the Templates, and the Records are active and used as written | Open Blocker or Major Findings on the documents, target none | Risks and Issues | [ The date of the check of the AICC Lead ] |

## 3. Scope and the minimum viable product

In scope: the documents, the workflows, the guides, the Templates, and the Records of AICC.

Out of scope: the Solutions of the Domains.

Minimum viable product: the documents active and checked by the AICC Lead with the ten questions; an independent check is optional. Its Features are in the folder `features/`.

## 4. Cost, capacity, and value

Cost: the time of the AICC Lead. No other cost is stated.

Capacity: the AICC Lead alone, within the capacity of the AICC Team (Teams).

Value: a unit that an auditor and HR can test, and that its people can follow.

## 5. Risks, dependencies, and Risk Tier

Risk: one person writes, owns, and checks the documents. An independent check is optional (DR-2026-018), and a second person may check when the AICC Lead or the Executive Sponsor asks.

Expected Risk Tier: none, because the work builds no AI Solution (Portfolio Management Model 8.2).

Control Functions to clear the business case: none.

Dependencies: none outside AICC. The AICC Lead checks the documents with the ten questions, and the independent check (FT-005) is Deferred.

## 6. Decision and acceptance

Business case: the charter is in development mode, and the work runs under the AICC Lead as an Exception to the approval of the business case, decided by the AICC Lead (DR-2026-054, RI-031). The Executive Sponsor has not yet approved the business case. The Executive Sponsor, as the client of enabling work, approves it and ratifies the Exception when the charter is baselined [ with the date and the Decision Record ].

Acceptance on delivery: the Executive Sponsor, because it is enabling work of AICC. Acceptance closes the item.
