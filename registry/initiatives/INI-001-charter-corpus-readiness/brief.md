## 1. Hypothesis

If AICC holds its way of working in six short documents that its people can follow, then the documents can be activated and used, measured by an independent check with no open Blocker or Major Finding.

## 2. Problem or opportunity, and minimum scope

The first corpus of 25 documents was too heavy for a team of a few engineers, and an independent assessment found 72 findings. The corpus was simplified to six documents on 2026-09-30 (DR-2026-009). Scope: the six documents, five Templates, and the Records. Excluded: the Solutions of the Domains.

## 3. Benefit, cost, and risk

| Benefit | Measure | Baseline | Target | Date |

| Documents ready to activate | Open Blocker or Major Findings on the six documents | not checked | 0 | FT-005, optional |
| Light process | Documents; Roles; meetings | 25; 13; 8 | 6; 7; 3 | 2026-09-30 |

Cost: the time of the AICC Lead. Risk: one person wrote and owns the documents, so an independent person checks them. Dependency: none. The documents were activated by the AICC Lead (DR-2026-018).
