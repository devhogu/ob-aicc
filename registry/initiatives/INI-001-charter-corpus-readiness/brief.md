## 1. Hypothesis

If AICC holds its way of working in a short set of documents that its people can follow, then the documents can be used as written, and an independent check finds no open Blocker or Major Finding.

## 2. Business outcomes and leading indicators

| Business outcome | Leading indicator | Where the figures live | Date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The documents, the Templates, and the Records are active and used as written | Open Blocker or Major Findings on the documents, target none | Risks and Issues | [ The date of the independent check ] |

## 3. Scope and the minimum viable product

In scope: the documents, the workflows, the guides, the Templates, and the Records of AICC.

Out of scope: the Solutions of the Domains.

Minimum viable product: the documents active and checked by a person who did not write them. Its Features are in the folder `features/`.

## 4. Cost, capacity, and value

Cost: the time of the AICC Lead. No other cost is stated.

Capacity: the AICC Lead alone, within the capacity of the AICC Team (Teams).

Value: a unit that an auditor and HR can test, and that its people can follow.

## 5. Risks, dependencies, and Risk Tier

Risk: one person writes and owns the documents, so a person who did not write them checks them.

Expected Risk Tier: none, because the work builds no AI Solution (Operating Model 6.2).

Control Functions to consult: none.

Dependencies: an independent person to check the documents [ named by the Executive Sponsor ].
