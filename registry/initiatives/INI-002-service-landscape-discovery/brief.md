## 1. Hypothesis

If AICC consolidates the knowledge and the artifacts that the functions and service owners already hold into one map of the service landscape, then the Bank can see where AI adoption can start with least disturbance and where adoption has already started.

## 2. Problem or opportunity, and scope

**Problem or opportunity.** Knowledge of the services of the Bank is spread across the functions. No single view connects the commercial portfolio to the processes, applications, and infrastructure that deliver it. Without one, adoption starts wherever someone asks first.

**AICC does.**

1. Agree with enterprise architecture a common way to describe a service, in layers: commercial portfolio, services, processes, organization, applications and data, and infrastructure.
2. Work with each function head and service owner to collect the knowledge and the artifacts that they already hold (documents, diagrams, registers), and not to create new ones.
3. Consolidate them in the EA repository, and publish a portal that projects the landscape from commercial portfolio management down to IT architecture and infrastructure.
4. Review each part of the map with its owner for correctness.
5. Mark on the map where AI is already in use, and the routine, manual, or document-heavy work where adoption could start with least disturbance.
6. Turn the marked areas into ranked candidates for the Backlog.

**Out of scope.** Any change to services, processes, systems, or architecture. The map describes the current state only.

## 3. Outcome, Measures, cost, and risk

**Outcome by 31 December 2026.**

- The landscape portal, covering the functions and services visited and reviewed by their owners.
- A list of where AI is used today.
- A ranked list of candidates for AI adoption.

**Measures.** Functions and services mapped; share reviewed by their owners; AI uses found; Candidates ranked. Baselines and targets are set with the Domain Owner by 31 October 2026. They are figures of the Bank, so they are kept in the source system of the function, and this brief points to them.

**Cost.** The time of the AICC Lead. No other cost is stated.

**Risk.** No Solution is built, so no Risk Tier applies. The portal shows internal information, so its access is confirmed with information security (RI-018).
