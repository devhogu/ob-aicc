## 1. Hypothesis

If AICC and the function discuss in full what AI can do for credit lines, loans, and mortgages and how to do it, then the function can start with the work that is most useful and controllable, beginning with the analysis of why mortgages are rejected.

## 2. Problem or opportunity, and scope

**Problem or opportunity.** The function holds knowledge and does routine work across credit lines, loans, and mortgages, and it wants to understand the reasoning behind rejected mortgages. AICC has not yet worked through these with the function.

**AICC does.**

1. Hold working sessions with the function on credit lines, loans, and mortgages end to end: the process, the routine work, the knowledge it relies on, and its pain points.
2. Identify and rank what AI can do and how, including the routine work of the day and the knowledge base (INI-008).
3. Discover the analysis of mortgage rejections: what rejection data and reasons exist, where, and in what form; the questions the function wants answered; how AI can consolidate and analyze the reasoning behind rejections.
4. Define the approach, the data, the output, and the controls, with the Control Function Contacts.
5. Run a first analysis where the data is available and the controls permit.
6. Agree the next steps with the function.

**Out of scope.** Any decision or recommendation on an individual application; any change to credit policy; model-based scoring. A person always decides on a mortgage, and AI only consolidates and analyzes.

## 3. Outcome, Measures, cost, and risk

**Outcome by 31 December 2026.**

- The discussions with the function, completed and documented.
- A ranked list of opportunities for the function.
- The approach to the analysis of mortgage rejections, agreed: data, method, and controls.
- A first analysis, delivered where permitted.

**Measures.** Sessions held and documented; Opportunities ranked; Approach agreed with the function. Baselines and targets are set with the Domain Owner by 31 October 2026. They are figures of the Bank, so they are kept in the source system of the function, and this brief points to them.

**Cost.** The time of the AICC Lead. No other cost is stated.

**Risk.** Customer and credit data is involved: Risk Tier 2 is expected. Data protection and model risk check before any analysis of real customer data (RI-014). Conditions of any validation: the analysis gives no output on an individual application, and the AI contribution and the decision of the person are recorded (Standards ARC-004).

## 4. Decision and acceptance

Confirmed as a goal of the first 100 days by the Executive Sponsor (DR-2026-012), with the wording and scope restated in DR-2026-013. The business case is approved when scoping is complete.

Acceptance on delivery: The head of the retail credit function. Acceptance closes the item.
