## 1. Hypothesis

If AICC and the function discuss in full what AI can do for credit lines, loans, and mortgages and how to do it, then the function can start with the work that is most useful and controllable, beginning with the analysis of why mortgages are rejected.

Today the function holds knowledge and does routine work across credit lines, loans, and mortgages, and it wants to understand the reasoning behind rejected mortgages. AICC has not yet worked through these with the function.

## 3. Scope and the minimum viable product

The steps below are the plan of the MVP. They start when the business case is approved and cleared and the Initiative is taken from the Portfolio Backlog into work (Portfolio Management Model 5).

AICC does the following.

1. Hold working sessions with the function on credit lines, loans, and mortgages end to end: the process, the routine work, the knowledge it relies on, and its pain points.
2. Identify and rank what AI can do and how, including the routine work of the day and the knowledge base (INI-008).
3. Discover the analysis of mortgage rejections: what rejection data and reasons exist, where, and in what form; the questions the function wants answered; how AI can consolidate and analyze the reasoning behind rejections.
4. Define the approach, the data, the output, and the controls, with the Control Function Contacts.
5. Run a first analysis where the data is available and the controls permit.
6. Agree the next steps with the function.

Out of scope: Any decision or recommendation on an individual application; any change to credit policy; model-based scoring. A person always decides on a mortgage, and AI only consolidates and analyzes.

Minimum viable product: The approach to the analysis of mortgage rejections, agreed, and a first analysis where the data and the controls permit.

## 5. Risks, dependencies, and Risk Tier

Risk: Customer and credit data is involved. Data protection and model risk check before any analysis of real customer data (RI-014). The conditions of any validation are that the analysis gives no output on an individual application, and that the AI contribution and the decision of the person are recorded (Standards ARC-004).

Expected Risk Tier: Risk Tier 2 is expected.

Control Functions to clear the business case: Data protection, model risk, information security, and compliance.

Dependencies: DEP-004 (named Domain Owners and Domain Experts), DEP-010 (the common approach for knowledge bases from INI-008), DEP-011 (access to mortgage rejection data and its data class; the controls), DEP-014 (validation by the Control Function Contacts).
