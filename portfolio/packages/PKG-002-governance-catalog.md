## 1. What it is and what problem it answers

The control loops, the catalog of controls, the records and evidence structure, and the registry layout, ready to map to a unit.

## 2. What it contains

- [Operating Model](../../charter/documents/operating-model.md), sections 6 to 8: control loops, records, and the 32 controls.
- [Unit governance guide](../../charter/guides/unit-governance-guide.md): control objectives and tests.
- [Registry structure](../../registry/README.md): the division between working state, living records, and evidence.
- [Record templates](../../charter/templates/README.md): the forms to start new records.

## 3. How a function re-deploys it

1. The receiving owner maps each relevant control to the mandate and risks of its unit.
2. The owner names the responsible Roles, the trigger, the evidence, and the review for each control.
3. The keeper starts empty records from the templates and the Registry structure. AICC control statuses, decisions, appointments, and findings are not copied as facts of the receiving unit.
4. The owner reviews the mapping and records its activation under the governance of the receiving unit; each control is then evidenced when its trigger occurs.

## 4. Limits and risks

The catalog is a reusable control structure, not a certification that a control operates. Applicability, ownership, evidence, and status must be established separately for the receiving unit.
