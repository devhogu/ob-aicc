## 1. The need and the outcome

An agentic pipeline that collects the financial metrics from their governed sources, calculates them, drafts the monthly and quarterly
editions, and publishes them to the Board portal. FP&A produces these reports today with AI and some manual steps. The outcome is
consistent reporting with fewer manual steps and one delivery channel.

## 2. Scope and capabilities

To be defined with the Domain Owner: the standard set of metrics, the monthly and quarterly editions, the Board portal, and the
pipeline. Excluded: other investor communications, changes to the methods of FP&A or to accounting systems.

## 3. Architecture and data

Financial data, not personal data, from sources with an owner and a review date. The pipeline reads the sources and has no right to
change them. Publishing is a human act after the approval of the Executive Sponsor. The output reaches the Board and may reach
investors, so the Control Function Contact of compliance confirms the disclosure rules before the first issue. The AI influences the
content of a report and no decision on a person.

## 4. Risk Tier

Risk Tier 2 is expected. The AICC Lead assigns it when the Solution is defined and tells the Domain Owner. A Control Function Contact
may raise it.
