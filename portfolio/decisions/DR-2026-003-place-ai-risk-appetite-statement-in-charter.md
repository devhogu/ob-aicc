## 2. Context

The Statement of Intent requires an AI Risk Appetite Statement. Its carrier, the AICC Charter, is a stub. Finding: F-007, ISS-006.

## 3. Options and criteria

1. A separate document with its own Catalog row.
2. A section of the AICC Charter.

Criteria: one accountable Role, consistency with higher documents, proportionality to a unit run by one person.

## 4. Decision

The AI Risk Appetite Statement is a section of the AICC Charter. The Executive Sponsor approves it with the Charter. The Charter is written in Iteration 4.

## 5. Rationale

It keeps the number of documents down and binds with the Charter.

## 6. Risks accepted

The AICC Lead decides as drafter and owner before an Executive Sponsor is appointed. The Executive Sponsor may revisit the Decision at activation.
