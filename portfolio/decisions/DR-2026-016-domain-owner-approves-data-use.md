# DR-2026-016 The Domain Owner approves the use of a Solution for a data class

Date: 2026-09-30. Decided by the AICC Lead, on the direction of the Executive Sponsor's goals: the head of the function is the product owner and is responsible for approvals in functional use. AICC does the technical work.

## Decision

1. The Domain Owner approves the use of a Solution in the Domain for a data class, and is responsible for obtaining any approval that the rules of the Bank require. AICC provides the technical means and records the approval in the AI Registry.
2. The AICC Lead approves the use of a Solution in AICC.
3. Existing uses are tolerated until the Domain Owner has approved or stopped them, and for no longer than 90 days after activation.
4. The validation by the Control Function Contacts for Risk Tier 2 and 3 is unchanged.

2026-12-31.
