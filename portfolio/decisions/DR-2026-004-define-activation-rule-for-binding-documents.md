## 2. Context

Catalog 6.1 lists the documents that bind persons outside AICC by name. The Operating Model, Roles, Decision Rights, Governance Forums, Decision Management, and Portfolio Management also allocate duties to Domain Owners, Control Functions, and the Executive Sponsor, and are activated by the AICC Lead. Finding: F-042, ISS-026.

## 3. Options and criteria

1. Keep the list.
2. Define the rule: a document that allocates duties or authority to persons outside AICC is activated by the Executive Sponsor; all others by the AICC Lead.

Criteria: one accountable Role, consistency with higher documents, proportionality to a unit run by one person.

## 4. Decision

A document that allocates duties or authority to persons outside AICC is activated by the Executive Sponsor. All other documents are activated by the AICC Lead. The Operating Model, Roles, Decision Rights, Governance Forums, Decision Management, and Portfolio Management are therefore activated by the Executive Sponsor. The Catalog is corrected in Iteration 3. Until an Executive Sponsor is appointed, the text of this Decision is the direction for drafting only.

## 5. Rationale

A rule does not go stale when documents are added, and first-line AICC does not bind other functions by itself.

## 6. Risks accepted

The AICC Lead decides as drafter and owner before an Executive Sponsor is appointed. The Executive Sponsor may revisit the Decision at activation.
