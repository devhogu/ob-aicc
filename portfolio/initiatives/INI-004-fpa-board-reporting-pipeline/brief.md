## 1. Hypothesis

If AI-generated FP&A reports reach the Board through a portal and a simplified agentic pipeline, then financial metrics are reported to investors and the Board consistently each month and each quarter, without manual steps.

## 2. Problem or opportunity, and minimum scope

FP&A already produces reports with AI. Build the portal with them and set up the agentic pipeline for monthly and quarterly reporting of financial metrics. Provides a consistent delivery channel.
