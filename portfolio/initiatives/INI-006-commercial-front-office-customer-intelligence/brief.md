## 1. Hypothesis

If customer experience information (support requests, issues, challenges) is gathered in one place and analyzed, then the inefficiencies of the front office become visible and can be removed.

## 2. Problem or opportunity, and minimum scope

A larger exploration of the commercial front end: sort out the inefficiencies, then gather customer experience intelligence in one place and analyze it.
