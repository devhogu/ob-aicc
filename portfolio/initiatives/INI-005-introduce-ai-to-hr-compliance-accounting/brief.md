## 1. Hypothesis

If HR, compliance, and accounting are introduced to AI and trained on the job, then they use it in daily operations, measured by use and time released.

## 2. Problem or opportunity, and minimum scope

These functions do not yet use even basic AI assistants. Introduce them and train them on the job for daily operations.

## 3. Benefit, cost, and risk

| Benefit | Measure | Baseline | Target | Date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Number of people trained and using an approved Solution weekly | to be set with the Domain Owner | to be set | to be set | end of quarter 0 |

Cost: the time of the AICC Lead, with no other cost stated. Scope is being defined with the function; no Solution exists yet. A Risk Tier is assigned to each Use Case at Intake when a proposal takes shape, and the checks against the controls come before the Pilot.

Approved as a goal of the first 100 days (September to December 2026) by the Executive Sponsor. Decision Log entry DR-2026-012.
