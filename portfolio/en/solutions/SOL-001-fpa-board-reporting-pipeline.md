## 1. The need and the outcome

An agentic pipeline that collects the financial metrics from their governed sources, calculates them, drafts the monthly and quarterly editions, and publishes them to the Board portal. FP&A produces these reports today with AI and some manual steps. The outcome is consistent reporting with fewer manual steps and one delivery channel.

## 2. Scope and capabilities

The scope covers the standard set of metrics, the monthly and quarterly editions, the Board portal, and the pipeline. Excluded: other investor communications, changes to the methods of FP&A or to accounting systems.

## 3. Architecture and data

Financial data, not personal data, from sources with an owner and a review date. The pipeline reads the sources and has no right to change them. Publishing is a human act after the approval of the Executive Sponsor. The output reaches the Board and may reach investors, so the Control Function Contact of compliance confirms the disclosure rules before the first issue. The AI influences the content of a report and no decision on a person.

## 4. Risk Tier

The Competence Center Lead builds the Solution, so the Executive Sponsor assigns the Risk Tier when the Solution is defined and approves its use for a data class (Operating Model 4.4), and the Competence Center Lead tells the Domain Owner.
