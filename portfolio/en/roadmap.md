# Roadmap

The Roadmap shows three horizons: the current Program Increment as intent and direction, the next as planned, and the period beyond as indicative. The Program Increments and their dates are in the Calendar. The PI Objectives of the current Program Increment are in pi/2026-PIQ4/objectives.md. The Roadmap is revised at each PI Planning. The Milestones are placed by Iteration on the Program Board (dependencies.md). A Milestone is At risk when a Feature or a Dependency that it needs is At risk (Solution Lifecycle Model 4.3).

## Roadmap by Program Increment

| Program Increment | Status | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-PIQ4 | Intent and direction | The six goals of the first 100 days (INI-002, INI-006, INI-004, INI-003, INI-007, INI-008) |
| 2027-PIQ1 | Planned | The Solutions that come from the goals; the AI Registry complete; the first deployments; Maturity Level 1 |
| 2027-PIQ2 and later | Indicative | Scale of the first Solutions; Maturity Level 2 for the first Strategic Priorities |
