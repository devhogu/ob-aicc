## 1. Summary

The corpus is **not ready for approval**. Of 27 items assessed, 8 are stubs (R1), 14 are drafted (R2), and 5 are defined (R3). None is approved or operating. One document that the Statement requires, the AI Risk Appetite Statement, does not exist (R0).

The structure is sound: every requirement of the Operating Model has a carrier, the vocabulary audit is clean, the RACI has one accountable Role per row, and every Stage exit has a Decider that matches the Decision Rights. The defects are of four kinds.

1. **Contradictions that block approval.** Ownership of the Templates differs across four documents (F-011), and the release of a Risk Tier 4 Use Case has no Decision Category (F-009).
2. **Missing content.** Eight stubs, and one ghost, the AI Risk Appetite Statement (F-010, F-007).
3. **Overengineering.** All Forums, Registers, Templates, and services are active on paper while no Role has a Holder (F-012, F-033, F-017).
4. **Drift.** The published Statement is the superseded v0.1 (F-004).

Recommendation: close Waves 1 and 2, which are conformance work and a few decisions. By the scoring rules that raises the documents at R3 from 5 to 15 (Vocabulary and Style; Roles and Responsibilities; Decision Rights; Governance Forums; Document Control; Decision Management; Portfolio Management; Registers; Artifact Standards; Risk Tier Policy join them). Then write the stubs, in the order in section 8, and the activation schedule.

**Independence.** The Corpus Assessment requires that a document is not assessed by its author. The documents and this first Assessment were prepared by the same author. The Assessment is to be repeated by the assessor Role once it has a Holder, and the results compared.
