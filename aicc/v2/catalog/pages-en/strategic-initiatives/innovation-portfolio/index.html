Innovation portfolio

The innovation portfolio is the Bank's structured mechanism for developing and commercializing new capabilities and revenue streams — covering idea sourcing, hypothesis-driven experimentation, MVP development, stage-gate review, and the scaling of commercially validated innovations into production. Banks face fintech disruption from well-funded competitors and must maintain an innovation pipeline that is both disciplined (stage-gated, capital-efficient) and fast (capable of matching fintech iteration velocity). Without a portfolio view, innovation investment is episodic and difficult to justify at board level. The opportunity for generative AI is to accelerate experiment analysis, automate stage-gate synthesis, and give portfolio leadership continuous visibility into pipeline health and P&L contribution — shifting the innovation portfolio from a governance burden to a strategic asset with real-time performance accountability.

Idea sourcing & stage-gate

The structured process for capturing, evaluating, and progressing innovation ideas from initial submission through stage-gate reviews to approved experimentation. Stage-gate discipline — with defined criteria at each gate (commercial potential, feasibility, strategic fit, resource availability) — prevents premature capital commitment to unvalidated ideas. Stage-gate reviews consume significant innovation board and sponsor time; the quality of review materials determines the quality of the go/no-go decision.

Stage-Gate Review Synthesis

The AI agent reads experiment data, hypothesis validation outputs, feasibility assessments, and resource estimates for each idea at the gate, generating the review pack with pre-populated scoring against each gate criterion and evidence-gap flags where the scoring basis is insufficient. Innovation board review time concentrates on strategic fit and risk judgments; data assembly and criterion mapping are automated. Gate criteria are applied on a consistent basis across all experiments, enabling reliable portfolio-level comparison.

S
Problem to solve

Stage-gate review packs are assembled by innovation leads from experiment data, feasibility assessments, and commercial evaluations, with drafting taking one to two weeks per gate review. Gate criteria are applied inconsistently because different authors interpret the rubric differently, making portfolio-level comparison unreliable. The innovation board's review time is absorbed in reconciling inconsistent scoring rather than in strategic judgment.

Solution

The AI agent reads experiment data, hypothesis validation outputs, feasibility assessments, and resource estimates for each idea at the gate. It generates the review pack with pre-populated scoring against each gate criterion and flags where evidence is insufficient to support a scoring recommendation. The innovation lead reviews the pack, and the innovation board focuses its review on strategic fit and risk judgments; data assembly and criterion mapping are completed before the review session.

Scaling decision

The investment and operational decision to take a validated MVP into full production — specifying the investment required, the expected P&L trajectory at different adoption curves, the operational risk of the scaling approach, and the milestone-based funding structure. Scaling decisions are the highest-stakes investment decisions in the innovation portfolio; under-resourcing a scaling innovation destroys the value demonstrated in the MVP. The decision requires modeling adoption curve uncertainty and comparing the scaling option against alternative investment uses.

It produces a scaling business case with P&L projections under three adoption curve scenarios, the operational investment requirements for each, and benchmarks from comparable prior scaling exercises. The innovation board and the CFO review the modeled brief before the scaling decision is presented to ExCo.

MVP design & hypothesis testing

Design and execution of minimum viable products and the structured experiments that test the core commercial and technical hypotheses underlying each innovation. Hypothesis-driven experimentation discipline — specifying the hypothesis, metric, test design, and decision threshold before running the experiment — is the primary quality control mechanism in the pipeline. Weak hypothesis design is the leading cause of inconclusive experiment results that consume resources without generating clear go/no-go signals.

Innovation leads revise and resubmit; the quality bar is applied consistently across all experiments in the portfolio regardless of the originating team. Inconclusive results from under-specified hypotheses are reduced before they consume experiment budget.

S

Hypothesis quality review is bottlenecked on experienced innovation leads whose availability limits the throughput of well-specified experiments entering the pipeline.

Solution

The AI agent reads the submitted hypothesis specification and evaluates it against a quality rubric covering prediction specificity, metric definition, sample size adequacy, confound identification, and decision threshold clarity. It surfaces specific improvement recommendations before the experiment is approved; experienced innovation leads confirm that they are valid. Innovation leads revise and resubmit; the consistent quality bar reduces inconclusive results and the associated experiment re-cycle cost across the portfolio.

Acceptance criteria

Experiment Sample Design Optimization Brief

The AI agent reads an experiment's hypothesis, the target customer segment characteristics from the Bank's data platform, and the effect size required for a decisive result, and produces a sample design brief specifying the minimum sample size, recommended control and treatment split, and optimal run duration given the expected traffic. Innovation teams run experiments with statistically adequate designs rather than arbitrary sample sizes that produce underpowered results.

S
Problem to solve

Experiment sample sizes are set by innovation teams based on available customer volume and intuition rather than statistical power requirements. Underpowered experiments produce inconclusive results that cannot confirm or refute the hypothesis at any confidence level, wasting the experiment budget and the run period. The innovation function does not know whether an experiment failed to produce a signal or simply was not designed to detect the effect size that matters.

Solution

The AI agent reads the hypothesis, target segment characteristics, and required effect size for business significance. It produces a sample design brief specifying the minimum sample size for the target power level, the recommended control/treatment split, and optimal duration. Innovation teams submit the brief alongside the hypothesis; underpowered designs are corrected before the experiment is launched.

Acceptance criteria

Experiments are launched with statistically adequate sample designs that can produce decisive results.

Adoption

The AI agent produces sample design briefs for ≥90% of experiment submissions within 12 months of go-live.

Acceptance

≥80% of sample design recommendations accepted by innovation teams without material revision.

Cycle

Portfolio rebalancing

Ongoing optimization of the innovation portfolio composition — balancing early-stage experimentation, mid-stage MVP development, and late-stage scaling — against available investment capital and strategic priority alignment. Portfolio rebalancing requires continuous visibility into the pipeline value at each stage, the expected capital requirement of scaling candidates, and the strategic contribution of current experiments to declared innovation themes. Without rebalancing, portfolios cluster at early stages (low conversion to scale) or at late stages (insufficient pipeline replenishment).

Portfolio Kill Recommendation Brief

The AI agent reads the performance and stage-gate history of each innovation in the pipeline and flags candidates for kill or indefinite deferral: innovations that have missed consecutive gate criteria, MVPs with hypothesis confirmation below the threshold after the full test run, and scaling innovations running persistently behind the adoption curve. The innovation board reviews the kill recommendations and releases the capital for redeployment rather than allowing underperforming innovations to absorb resources without explicit continuation approval.

S
Problem to solve

Innovation portfolios accumulate underperforming candidates because there is no systematic trigger for the kill decision. Innovations miss gate criteria and are deferred rather than stopped; MVPs produce inconclusive results and are given extended test runs rather than resolved. The portfolio becomes cluttered with zombie innovations consuming management time and budget without clear commercial trajectory.

Solution

The AI agent reads stage-gate history, hypothesis confirmation rates, and adoption trajectories for all portfolio candidates. It produces a kill recommendation brief flagging candidates for review with the specific criterion breach and estimated capital freed on kill. The innovation board reviews and makes explicit continue-or-kill decisions; dormant innovations are not passively retained.

Acceptance criteria

Underperforming innovations receive an explicit kill-or-continue decision rather than passive deferral.

Adoption

The AI agent produces kill recommendation briefs for ≥90% of gate cycles within 12 months of go-live.

Acceptance

≥80% of kill recommendations confirmed as appropriate by the innovation board; false positives ≤15%.

Integrating innovation P&L into the Bank's financial reporting structure requires combining business-line revenue data with innovation cost allocations.

Acceptance criteria

Innovation P&L reporting to the board reflects net incremental contribution, not gross revenue claims.

Adoption

The AI agent produces revenue attribution briefs quarterly for ≥90% of scaled innovations within 12 months of go-live.

Acceptance

≥80% of attribution methodologies confirmed as appropriate by the CFO and innovation board.

Cycle

Variance between gross and net innovation revenue attribution is surfaced and tracked each quarter; over-reporting rate decreases by ≥50% within 18 months.
