## Briefly {#short}

In Kazakhstan, two bodies look after the financial market. The National Bank of the Republic of Kazakhstan is responsible for monetary policy and the payment system and runs a regulatory sandbox for digital asset projects. The Agency of the Republic of Kazakhstan for Regulation and Development of the Financial Market (ARDFM; <span lang="ru">Агентство по регулированию и развитию финансового рынка</span>) was created by a presidential decree of 11 November 2019 and has operated since 1 January 2020: it regulates and supervises banks and other financial organizations and protects consumers of financial services. As far as we know, there is no dedicated binding document on AI for the financial market yet.

## What it means in practice {#practice}

These bodies are not our regulator: our banking supervisor is the National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic. But Kazakhstan is the neighboring market with the most developed fintech in the region, and the way its regulators approach AI is a good indication of where requirements may head for us too.

Three things are worth following: what AI rules the ARDFM is preparing, how it sorts uses of AI by risk, and which projects go through the sandbox. If we discuss a joint project with a Kazakh bank or fintech company, their requirements for technology, data, and suppliers will be part of the conversation.

For an employee, the takeaway is the same as ours: credit scoring, fraud prevention, and customer communication are areas where an AI mistake is especially costly, so they need a human check and a clear explanation of the decision.

## Key ideas {#ideas}

- **Two regulators.** The National Bank runs monetary policy and the payment system; the ARDFM regulates and supervises financial organizations and protects consumers’ rights.
- **AI is already in banks.** According to the ARDFM chair in November 2025, 75% of Kazakhstan’s banks use AI: in credit scoring, fraud prevention, marketing, and chatbots.
- **Rules by risk.** The ARDFM has said it is preparing a framework for AI in the financial market: light oversight where the risk is low and strict controls for high-risk uses. This is the [[risk-based-approach|risk-based approach]].
- **Regulatory sandbox.** In the National Bank’s sandbox, banks, payment organizations, and other organizations test digital asset projects under a special regulatory regime.
- **Data quality.** The regulator names the lack of common standards, data quality, and a shortage of specialists as the main obstacles.

## Good to know {#details}

- The ARDFM was created by Decree of the President of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. 203 of 11 November 2019, has operated since 1 January 2020, and took over regulation and supervision functions from the National Bank.
- In November 2025, the ARDFM chair said there was no dedicated document on AI for the financial market yet, but there would be one. We have not confirmed whether one has been adopted since.
- The National Bank’s sandbox is meant for digital asset projects, not specifically for AI.
- The requirements of these bodies are binding on financial organizations in Kazakhstan; for us they are a benchmark.
