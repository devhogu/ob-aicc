This guide is for everyone who works with an AI assistant. After it, you will be able to look at your document and say what in it you can give the assistant and what you need to remove.

Everything you paste into an assistant or attach as a file goes to the service that runs it. If that service is not approved for such data, it is [[data-leakage|data leakage]], even if the assistant “won't tell anyone”.

An **approved service** is an assistant that your organization has allowed you to use for a particular kind of data, usually through a work account. A personal account or any AI website you happen to find is not suitable for work data: using them this way is called [[shadow-ai|shadow AI]].

<div class="rai-cards rai-cards--3 kb-traffic">
<article class="is-ok"><h3>Allowed</h3><p>Public information, your own drafts without confidential data, anonymized examples, “how do I do this” questions.</p></article>
<article class="is-care"><h3>Only in an approved service</h3><p>Internal documents, procedures, work correspondence, anonymized exports, and only in the service approved for that kind of data.</p></article>
<article class="is-no"><h3>Not allowed</h3><p>Personal data of customers and employees, banking secrecy, passwords and access keys, information you are not allowed to disclose.</p></article>
</div>

To **anonymize** means to remove or replace everything that could identify a person or a customer: full names, account and card numbers, phone numbers, addresses, PINs. Write “Customer A” or “[account number]” instead.

## Before you paste {#check}

1. **Are there people in it?** Names, account numbers, phone numbers, PINs: remove them or replace them with “Customer A”.
2. **Could you show this to an outsider?** If not, only in an approved service.
3. **Does the task need it?** Often the structure or an example is enough, not the whole export. For the assistant to help with an Excel formula, the column headers and a couple of made-up rows will do.

## Example: a customer complaint {#example}

You want the assistant to help you reply politely to a complaint.

| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| “Customer Asel N., account No. 1234…, tel. …, writes that a transfer of 15,000 som from 3 October has not arrived and threatens to close her account.” | “A customer writes that a transfer has not reached the recipient and threatens to close the account. Draft a polite reply: an apology, what we are doing, and when to expect an answer. No promises of deadlines or amounts.” |

To draft the reply, the assistant needs neither the name, nor the account number, nor the amount: you will fill those in yourself in the work system.

## Try it now {#try}

Open one of your work documents: an email, minutes or an export. Go through the three questions above and mark everything you need to remove or replace before sending it to the assistant. If nothing meaningful is left of the document after that, it is better to do the task without AI or only in an approved service.

## If in doubt {#doubt}

Don't paste it. Ask your manager or [write to us](page:services/how-to-engage). If data has already gone to the wrong place, report it at once: a mistake noticed quickly is easy to fix; a hidden one is not.

Why this matters and what other risks there are is covered in the section [Responsible AI](page:responsible-ai#risks).
