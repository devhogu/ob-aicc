Every project has three roles. These are roles in the project, not job titles: several people can share one role, and one person can hold two. The card shows who is in which role right now. You don't need a role to bring an idea.

<ul class="o-grid card-list cards-compact role-cards">
<li class="o-card card--compact"><p class="card-kicker">Why</p><h3>[[business-owner|Business owner]]</h3><p class="card-desc">Represents the department that needs the result. Decides whether the work is worth starting and continuing, and accepts the result.</p></li>
<li class="o-card card--compact"><p class="card-kicker">What and in what order</p><h3>[[product-manager|Product manager]]</h3><p class="card-desc">Defines the value, runs the funnel and the backlog, and accepts each Feature.</p></li>
<li class="o-card card--compact"><p class="card-kicker">How and when</p><h3>[[project-manager|Project manager]]</h3><p class="card-desc">Runs the project day to day and keeps the card up to date.</p></li>
</ul>

## Two forums {#forums}

A forum is a regular meeting of the people who have cards. A forum doesn't approve or sign off on anything: it looks at the flow of work and settles what couldn't be settled on the spot.

| | [[product-management-forum|Product management forum]] | [[program-decision-forum|Program decision forum]] |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it looks at | the funnel and the portfolio | the program board |
| What it decides | what to start, what is stuck, what it's time to stop, which initiatives have reached a checkpoint | order of work, dependencies, missed deadlines, out-of-turn work, readiness for the next increment |
| Who takes part | business owners and product managers | project managers and product managers |
| How often | every two weeks, up to 45 minutes | once per iteration |

**How the meeting runs.** Cards on the screen, not slides. Exceptions come first: what is marked red, overdue, or not moving. Every decision is written straight into the [[decision-log|decision log]] on the card. If a question can be settled by a rule, we agree on the rule and don't come back to it.

## Flow measures {#measures}

The measures are calculated from the cards and help us see where work gets stuck. The Hub doesn't set target values: each project compares itself with its own past.

| Measure | What it shows | How to read it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[lead-time|Lead time]] | from the entry in the funnel to completion | rising: look for where work sits idle |
| [[cycle-time|Cycle time]] | how long the work is in active development | rising: the tasks are too big or something is in the way |
| [[throughput|Throughput]] | how much gets done in a period | compare with ourselves |
| Work in progress | how many work items are under way at once | no higher than the [[wip-limit|WIP limit]] |
| Work item age | how long the work has been in its current state | look at the ones that have stayed longer than usual |
