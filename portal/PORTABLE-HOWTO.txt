AICC published portal — how to regenerate

Run from the AICC source repository, not from the exported folder.

1. Rebuild the current content:
   python3 portal/tools/build.py

2. Generate separate English and Russian editions without JavaScript:
   python3 portal/tools/export_portable.py --static

3. Copy portal/published/en/ or portal/published/ru/ separately, or copy all of portal/published/. Keep each edition's assets and subfolders together. Open its index.html. The parent aicc.html links to both editions.

4. Optional direct-file browser check:
   portal/tools/with-browser-env.sh portal/.venv/bin/python portal/tools/check_static_export_browser.py

Light theme only. Native disclosures and links replace popups and tab switching. Topics and browser Find replace live search. Select text to copy; open the full diagram and use browser zoom. Email feedback and external citations need their normal applications/access.

For the earlier interactive edition, omit --static. To keep both editions, use a separate --output folder. Regeneration is on demand and does not upload files or update the hosted website.
