# National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic

The central bank and the supervisor of the Bank. Its regulations govern how a bank in the Kyrgyz Republic runs its systems, manages its risks, uses third parties, operates payments, and treats its customers, and they apply to every system that carries a banking process, whether or not it uses AI.

## 1. Identity

| Item | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | Central bank and banking supervisor |
| Issued or overseen by | National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic |
| Jurisdiction | Kyrgyz Republic |
| Status | Standing body; the regulations of the National Bank on banking, information security, outsourcing, operational risk, payment systems, and the protection of consumers of financial services |

## 2. What it sets

2.1. Licensing and prudential requirements; the regulation of information security and of the use of third parties and outsourcing in banks; operational risk management and the reporting of incidents; the regulation of payment systems and services; the protection of consumers of financial services; and the requirements of anti-money-laundering supervision in the banking sector.

## 3. Whom it reaches

3.1. The banks and the other financial institutions of the Kyrgyz Republic.

## 4. Relevance to the Bank

4.1. The supervisor of the Bank. Any AI use that touches a banking process, customer data, or a third party answers to its regulations, and its expectations on AI will form from the work of the global standard-setters and the practice of the region.

## 5. How the charter relates to it

5.1. The Risk Tiers, the provider rules, and the AI Incident handling of the AI Policy, the AI Risk Appetite Statement of the Charter, and the reporting to the Board Committee are designed so that the Bank can show its supervisor how AI is governed; the Acts and compliance page states what the regulations require of a use of AI.
