# Kazakhstan: the Law on Personal Data and Their Protection

The personal data law of Kazakhstan: lawful grounds, consent, the rights of subjects, security, and conditions for the transfer of personal data abroad, with requirements on the storage of certain data within the country.

## 1. Identity

| Item | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan |
| Issued or overseen by | The authorized body for personal data protection of Kazakhstan |
| Jurisdiction | Kazakhstan |
| Status | In force since 2013, amended, with localization and cross-border transfer rules |

## 2. What it sets

2.1. Grounds for collection and processing, consent, purpose and retention, the rights of the subject, the duties of owners and operators, security measures, and the conditions for cross-border transfer.

## 3. Whom it reaches

3.1. Owners and operators that process personal data in Kazakhstan, and of its residents.

## 4. Relevance to the Bank

4.1. Reaches a partner or a provider of the Bank that processes the data of residents of Kazakhstan, and any service of the Bank that reaches them.

## 5. How the charter relates to it

5.1. The data classification rules of the AI Policy and the provider check address where data is processed and kept.
