# Bank of Russia on artificial intelligence in the financial market

The view of the central bank and financial supervisor of the Russian Federation on AI in finance: its benefits, its risks, and the approach to its regulation, developed in consultation with the market and leaning on soft law and codes before binding rules.

## 1. Identity

| Item | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | Reports and guidance of a central bank and financial supervisor |
| Issued or overseen by | Bank of Russia |
| Jurisdiction | Russian Federation |
| Status | Consultation and reports on the application of AI in the financial market from 2023; guidance and codes developed with the market |

## 2. What it sets

2.1. The themes: the responsible use of AI by financial organizations, model risk, data, consumer protection and explainability, the risks of concentration and of large providers, and the balance between innovation and stability.

## 3. Whom it reaches

3.1. The financial sector of the Russian Federation.

## 4. Relevance to the Bank

4.1. The supervisory practice of the largest neighbouring market, and a likely influence on the supervisors of the region.

## 5. How the charter relates to it

5.1. The AI Risk Appetite Statement, the Risk Tiers, and the reporting to the Board Committee address the themes the Bank of Russia names.
