# Russian Federation: the National Strategy for the Development of AI and the experimental legal regimes

The direction of the Russian Federation on AI: a national strategy with goals to 2030, and a mechanism of experimental legal regimes under which AI and other digital innovations are trialled with temporary exemptions from ordinary rules, in defined places and sectors.

## 1. Identity

| Item | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | Presidential decree and federal laws |
| Issued or overseen by | The President and the Government of the Russian Federation; the ministries concerned |
| Jurisdiction | Russian Federation |
| Status | National strategy adopted 2019 and updated 2024, to 2030; experimental legal regimes for digital innovation, including AI, since 2020 |

## 2. What it sets

2.1. Goals for research, talent, infrastructure, and the adoption of AI in the economy and the public sector; principles of trustworthy AI; and the regimes that allow regulated experimentation, with reporting and liability rules for the participants.

## 3. Whom it reaches

3.1. The Russian Federation; the participants of the regimes.

## 4. Relevance to the Bank

4.1. A large neighbouring jurisdiction whose approach shapes the region and the providers in it. The regime of regulated experimentation is also a reference for how a sandbox and a Lab can be governed.

## 5. How the charter relates to it

5.1. The Experiment workflow and the Lab of AICC, with their time-box, their read-only data, and their decision at the end, follow the same idea of bounded trial under rules.
