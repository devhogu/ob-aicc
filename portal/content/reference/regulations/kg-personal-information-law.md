# Kyrgyz Republic: the Law on Personal Information and the authorized body for personal data

The personal data law of the Kyrgyz Republic and the body that oversees it. The law sets the grounds, the purposes, the consent, the protection, and the transfer rules for the processing of personal data, and the authorized body registers holders, handles complaints, and inspects.

## 1. Identity

| Item | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | Law of the Kyrgyz Republic and its authorized body |
| Issued or overseen by | The authorized state body for personal data protection of the Kyrgyz Republic |
| Jurisdiction | Kyrgyz Republic |
| Status | Law in force since 2008, amended; the authorized body established under the Cabinet of Ministers |

## 2. What it sets

2.1. Lawful grounds and purpose for processing, the consent of the subject where required, the rights of the subject, the duties and the registration of holders of personal data, security measures, and the conditions for transfer to third parties and abroad.

## 3. Whom it reaches

3.1. Every holder and processor of personal data in the Kyrgyz Republic, including the Bank and its providers.

## 4. Relevance to the Bank

4.1. Applies directly to every use of AI that touches customer or employee data: which data may be used, for what purpose, with what consent, where it may be processed, and with whom it may be shared.

## 5. How the charter relates to it

5.1. The data classification rules of the AI Policy, the minimization rule, the provider check on where data is processed and kept, and the Data Sharing Arrangement of the Charter answer the law; the Control Function Contact for data protection confirms the application.
