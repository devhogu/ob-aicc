# Russian Federation: the Code of Ethics in the Field of AI

A voluntary code of ethics for the development and use of AI, drafted by the industry alliance and signed by a wide range of organizations in the Russian Federation. It states the ethical commitments that signatories undertake and creates a commission that interprets them.

## 1. Identity

| Item | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | Voluntary industry code |
| Issued or overseen by | The AI Alliance Russia and the signatory organizations |
| Jurisdiction | Russian Federation |
| Status | Adopted 2021; signed by companies, universities, and public bodies |

## 2. What it sets

2.1. Commitments on human-centred and humanistic AI, respect for human autonomy and freedom of will, non-discrimination, safety and security, transparency and the identification of AI, responsibility, and the precautionary principle for high-risk uses.

## 3. Whom it reaches

3.1. The signatories. It binds no one else.

## 4. Relevance to the Bank

4.1. A reference for ethical practice in the region, in the language the region's industry uses, consistent with the international principles.

## 5. How the charter relates to it

5.1. The values and the Principles of Application of the Statement of Intent are consistent with the code.
