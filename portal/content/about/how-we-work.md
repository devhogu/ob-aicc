# How we work

A function brings a need; AICC takes it in, studies it, commits to it, proves it, builds it, and supports it, with a record and a decision at each step. The Portfolio decides what AICC works on; Delivery builds it in small steps on a common cadence, with quality and control inside the flow.

## 1. The engagement model

1.1. Six steps: contact, study, Service Agreement, delivery, Outcome Report, support. AICC commits in a Service Agreement on a best-effort basis within its capability; the function commits to nothing. The page How to engage states the steps, the commitment, and the service levels.

## 2. The method in brief

2.1. Two lanes. Run-rate work, a document, an automation, a view, a training, an assessment, is taken in at the Weekly Review and done in days to one Iteration. A program, a charter pack, a knowledge base, an engine, a trial, is an Initiative with a business case and an MVP, decided through the four portfolio loops within the limit on the Active Initiatives.

2.2. Delivery runs in Program Increments of one quarter and Iterations of one month, on fixed dates. Work is visible and limited in progress; Features are accepted at the Iteration Review and Demo; a Solution is checked or validated by a person other than its builder, accepted by the Team, and accepted by the Domain Owner before it reaches its users. An Experiment runs in the Lab, on read-only extracts, and ends in a decision.

2.3. A Service that AICC runs lives through seven states after release, is operated under named practices, and is read on four signals: service levels, incidents, adoption, cost. The unit is controlled through five control loops and a catalogue of controls, reports each quarter to the Board Committee, and is assured by internal audit.
