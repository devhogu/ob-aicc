# What we do

AICC is the internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank for artificial intelligence. It researches and advises, proves and builds, runs what is worth running, teaches, and oversees, across strategy, programs, solutions, and ways of working. It is a small delivery unit that proves the case and relies on the platform teams and the IT functions of the Bank to scale.

## 1. The three families and the nine lines

| Family | Line | In one line | Mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Advise and found | Strategy and governance office | Strategy, charter, operating and governance model, process set, portal and repository for a unit, developed with AI, as AICC did for itself | Program |
| Advise and found | Normative documents and processes | Policies, procedures, regulations, runbooks, and process descriptions drafted, aligned, and maintained with AI for any function | Run-rate and program |
| Advise and found | Watch, research, and partnering | Regulatory and technology watch with digests, trials, relations with organizations and providers | Run-rate and program |
| Build and run | Knowledge services | The corpus of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, as a governed knowledge base it can ask | Program |
| Build and run | Workplace automation | Routing, forms, reports, consolidation, and documents from templates, done by assistants under human validation | Run-rate |
| Build and run | Information and decision support | ETL and consolidation, dashboards, analytical and research tooling, the factual base for decisions | Run-rate and program |
| Build and run | Content and document engines | Public, investor, and management material generated from governed data and templates, pre-filled for people to finish | Program |
| Enable and assure | Enablement at the workplace | Training by role, coaching at the desk, Domain Experts, skill libraries, communities of practice | Run-rate |
| Enable and assure | Assurance and governance support | Risk Tier, AI Registry entry, provider assessment, evaluation before use, Acceptance Checklist, oversight of Adopted Solutions | Run-rate |

The lines are directions along which AICC provides services, with examples on each page; what AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement. Every Engagement leaves a reusable package, and the catalog of packages is how a small unit serves the whole Bank. The Services section describes the lines, the service model, how a function engages AICC, and the form of the catalog.

## 2. What AICC is not

2.1. AICC is not a platform team, not a Control Function, and not the owner of the business results of a Domain. It does not validate its own work and does not deliver at scale. The AICC Charter 3.2 states these limits.
