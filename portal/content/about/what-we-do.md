# What we do

AICC is the internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank for artificial intelligence. It researches and advises, proves and builds, runs what is worth running, teaches, and oversees, across strategy, programs, solutions, and ways of working. It is a small delivery unit that proves the case and relies on the platform teams and the IT functions of the Bank to scale.

## 1. The service lines

| Service line | In one line |
| --- | --- |
| Advisory | Strategy and roadmap for a Domain, use-case discovery, the business case, readiness and provider assessment, Risk Tier advice |
| Proof and prototyping | Experiments, proofs of concept, MVPs, and trials, each ending in evidence and a decision |
| Solution delivery | Products for one function: knowledge bases, assistants, and automation of routine work, handed over and supported |
| AI services run by AICC | Portals, engines, and knowledge services that AICC operates as Services, with Service Management support |
| Enablement | Training by role, coaching of Domain Experts, communities of practice, playbooks and reusable assets |
| Oversight of Adopted Solutions | The Solutions that others deliver, recorded, reviewed, and reported under the same rules |
| Research and partnering | The watch on models, platforms, methods, and regulation; trials; relations with other organizations and providers |

The Services section describes each line, how a function engages AICC, and the service levels.

## 2. What AICC is not

2.1. AICC is not a platform team, not a Control Function, and not the owner of the business results of a Domain. It does not validate its own work and does not deliver at scale. The AICC Charter 3.2 states these limits.
