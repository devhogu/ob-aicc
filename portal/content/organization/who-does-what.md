# Who does what

Along the life of an Initiative and a Solution, each Role is responsible, accountable, consulted, or informed for each activity, and the pattern is the same from the first contact to the retirement. This part states the pattern in brief, by family of activity; the Organization guide holds the full responsibility matrix, activity by activity.

## 1. The pattern

| Activity | Accountable | Responsible | Consulted | Informed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Taking a need in and scoping it | AICC Lead | AICC Lead with the Domain Owner | Domain Expert | The function; the Steering |
| The business case and its clearance | Domain Owner, or Executive Sponsor above a guardrail or across Domains | AICC Lead writes it with the Domain Owner | Control Function Contacts clear it for Risk Tier 2 and 3 | AI Steering Committee |
| The Service Agreement | AICC Lead | AICC Lead | Domain Owner | The function |
| Ranking and pulling into work | AICC Lead | AICC Lead | Domain Owners state the value | Executive Sponsor, at the monthly Steering |
| Design, build, and the MVP | Solution Engineer | Solution Engineer with the Domain Expert | Platform Owner; Control Function Contacts for Risk Tier 2 and 3 at definition | Product owner |
| Test, check, or validation | AICC Lead for the arrangement | A person other than the builder; the Checker for Risk Tier 1; the Control Function Contacts for Risk Tier 2 and 3 | Platform Owner for logs and traces | Domain Owner |
| Acceptance of Features and Capabilities | Product owner | Product owner at the Iteration Review and Demo | Domain Expert | Domain Owner |
| Final acceptance of the Team | AICC Lead | AICC Lead | Solution Engineer | Domain Owner |
| Business acceptance and release | Domain Owner; Executive Sponsor for Risk Tier 3, across Domains, enabling work, or where the AICC Lead built it | Domain Owner | Control Function Contacts and the IT function on the Acceptance Checklist | The Steering |
| Operation, support, and the live review | Domain Owner for the results; Solution Engineer for a Service AICC runs | Solution Engineer; the IT function that operates | Platform Owner | AICC Lead |
| Change and retirement | Domain Owner | Solution Engineer | AICC Lead on the need for a new check | Control Function Contacts |
| Adoption in the Domain and training | Domain Owner | Domain Expert; AICC Lead for the training | Solution Engineer | Executive Sponsor |
| Oversight, the Risk Tier, and the AI Registry | AICC Lead | AICC Lead | Control Function Contacts | Executive Sponsor; Board Committee |
| The Quarterly Report | Executive Sponsor | AICC Lead | Domain Owners; Control Function Contacts | Board Committee |

## 2. How to read it

2.1. Accountable is the one Role that answers for the activity; Responsible does the work; Consulted is asked before; Informed is told after. The two rules that recur: the person who states the value is not the person who ranks, and the person who builds is not the person who checks, accepts for the business, or releases. Where the Team is small and one person holds several Roles, the matrix is read by Role, and the limits accepted are those stated on The Roles.
