# Learning paths

A learning path is a reading order for a role: what to read, in which sequence, and what the reader can do at the end. The paths use the courses of this site, each of which can be read in an hour, and point to the rule where the role needs it. Everyone starts with the first path.

## 1. Everyone: the orientation

| Step | Read | So that you can |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [About AICC](page:about/index), with [What we do](page:about/what-we-do) and [How we work](page:about/how-we-work) | Say what AICC is, its mission, its values, and how an Engagement runs |
| 2 | [Responsible AI](page:responsible-ai/index), the course | Explain what AI is today, where its value and its risks are, and what the Bank's rules of use are |
| 3 | [Services](page:services/index), the areas | Name what a function can ask AICC for |
| 4 | [Questions people ask](page:knowledge-base/questions-people-ask) | Answer the common questions yourself |
| 5 | The [Vocabulary](page:reference/vocabulary), kept at hand | Use the terms of the charter as they are defined |

## 2. A head of function or a Domain Owner

| Step | Read | So that you can |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [How to engage](page:services/how-to-engage), with the Engagement workflow and guide | Bring a need, know the six steps, and know what you commit to and what AICC commits to |
| 2 | [The Portfolio](page:portfolio/index), the course, parts 2 to 5 | Know how your business case is approved, ranked, probed, and continued |
| 3 | [The Roles](page:organization/the-roles) and [Who does what](page:organization/who-does-what) | Know what you decide as Domain Owner, and what the Domain Expert does |
| 4 | [How the Bank applies it](page:responsible-ai/how-the-bank-applies-it) | Know the Risk Tier your Solution will carry and what it requires of you |
| 5 | The [Initiative Brief](page:knowledge-base/initiative-brief) and the [Service Agreement](page:knowledge-base/service-agreement) | Fill the two forms of the start |
