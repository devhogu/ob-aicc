# Enablement at the workplace

Each person able to use AI for their own routine, within the rules: training by role, coaching at the desk, Domain Experts who carry adoption in their function, prompt and skill libraries per line of work, and communities of practice.

## 1. What it is

1.1. The line covers training by role before people use AI; coaching at the workplace on the tools the Bank approves; the appointment and coaching of Domain Experts; prompt and skill libraries built for a function's line of work; help in designing and setting up a person's own tooling; and communities of practice across the Bank.

1.2. Enablement is how the Bank advances through the Maturity Levels: the share of employees trained and with access to an approved assistant is among the Measures of Maturity Level 2.

## 2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the line, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

- Training by role before people use AI
- Coaching at the desk and clinics in the function
- A Domain Expert coached and equipped
- A prompt and skill library for a line of work
- A community of practice across functions
- Playbooks and lessons from the Engagements

## 3. What the function receives

2.1. A training path for each role, delivered with the function; a Domain Expert coached and equipped; a skill library the function owns and extends; clinics at the workplace; and the playbooks and lessons of the Engagements.

## 4. How it runs

3.1. Run-rate: training is scheduled with the function; coaching and clinics run in the Iterations; a skill library is built in one Iteration with the Domain Expert and grows afterwards.

## 5. What it leads to

4.1. Functions that use AI in their own processes with governed knowledge, within the shared principles and standards, and that put forward their own priorities to the Portfolio.

## 6. The reusable package

5.1. The training paths by role, the skill libraries, the playbooks, and the clinic format.

## 7. Run-rate or program

6.1. Run-rate.

## 8. Who decides

7.1. The AICC Lead sets the training; the Domain Owners appoint their Domain Experts and decide the adoption within their Domains.
