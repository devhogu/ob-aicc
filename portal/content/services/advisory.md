# Advisory

AICC advises a function of the Bank on where and how to use AI: which needs are worth an Initiative, what a Solution would take, what it would cost to run, which Risk Tier it would carry, and which provider or platform fits. Advisory is the study phase of an Engagement, and it is how most work with AICC starts.

## 1. What it is

1.1. Advisory covers the AI strategy and roadmap of a Domain, the discovery and shaping of use cases, the business case of an Initiative, the assessment of the readiness of a Domain in people, data, and process, the assessment of a provider of models or services, and advice on the Risk Tier that a Solution would carry.

1.2. Advisory is work on paper and in workshops. It builds nothing, and it ends in a decision that the function and the Executive Sponsor can take on evidence.

## 2. What the client receives

2.1. A scoped need with its intended outcome and its success Measures; an Initiative Brief that states the business case, the leading indicators, the run cost, and the expected Risk Tier; the clearance of the Control Function Contacts where Risk Tier 2 or 3 is expected; and a recommendation: continue to a proof, adopt what exists, or stop.

2.2. For a Domain that asks for a roadmap: the use cases of the Domain ranked by value and effort, mapped to the Strategic Priorities and to the Maturity Levels, as a Proposal.

## 3. How it runs

3.1. A head of function brings the need to the AICC Lead in any form. The AICC Lead takes it in when it fits a Strategic Priority, has a Domain Owner, and the limit on the Active Initiatives permits it, and issues a Service Agreement that covers the study.

3.2. The study runs in the Iterations of the Program Increment, with the Domain Owner and a Domain Expert of the function. A short study takes one Iteration; a roadmap for a Domain takes two to three.

## 4. What it leads to

4.1. The business case is approved by the Domain Owner, or by the Executive Sponsor above a guardrail, across Domains, or for enabling work. An approved Initiative goes on to a proof, the MVP. A deferred or rejected need keeps its lessons in the Portfolio Backlog.

## 5. Records and templates

5.1. The Initiative Brief; the Service Agreement; a Decision Record for the approval; a Proposal where the advice is to adopt at scale.

## 6. Who decides

6.1. The AICC Lead takes the need in. The Domain Owner approves the business case; the Executive Sponsor approves it above a guardrail, across Domains, or for enabling work. The Control Function Contacts clear it when Risk Tier 2 or 3 is expected.
