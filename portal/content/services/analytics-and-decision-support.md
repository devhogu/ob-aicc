# Analytics and decision support

Facts from many sources prepared, consolidated, and presented so that a function decides on data: the pipelines that collect and transform, the dashboards that show, the analyses that explain, and the research tooling that supports the work.

## 1. What it is

1.1. The category covers extraction, transformation, and consolidation from the systems and documents of the Bank; dashboards and recurring analyses with lineage to governed sources; analytical and research tooling for analysts; and the preparation of the factual base for a decision, a committee, or a report.

1.2. The figures of the Bank stay in the systems of the Bank; the pipelines read them, and the outputs point back to them. Publication requires human approval.

## 2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the category, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

- A pipeline that collects and consolidates figures from governed sources with lineage
- A dashboard with owners and refresh rules
- A recurring analysis pack: variance flash, risk synthesis, steering pre-read
- Research tooling for analysts
- The factual pack for a committee or a decision

## 3. What the function receives

3.1. Pipelines with documented sources and transformations, dashboards with owners and refresh rules, analytical notebooks and tools the function can run, and a factual pack for a recurring decision, each traceable to its sources.

## 4. How it runs

4.1. A study of one Iteration maps the sources, their owners, and their data classes. The MVP delivers the first pipeline and view; the rest follows in Iterations. The function owns the definitions of its measures; AICC builds the plumbing and the views.

## 5. What it leads to

5.1. Better-founded and faster decisions, consistent figures across the function, and reporting cycles that shrink.

## 6. The reusable package

6.1. The ETL and dashboard patterns and the source connectors, re-used across functions.

## 7. Run-rate or program

7.1. Run-rate for a view or a consolidation; a program for a pipeline that feeds a recurring report of the Bank.

## 8. Who decides

8.1. The Domain Owner owns the measures and approves the use for its data class; the Control Function Contacts clear it where the data requires.
