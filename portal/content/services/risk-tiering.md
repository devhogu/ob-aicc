# Risk tiering

Every use of AI in the Bank carries a Risk Tier, and the Risk Tier sets the review, the validation, the human oversight, and the speed of release that apply to it. AICC assigns the Risk Tier of each Solution, records it in the AI Registry, tells the function what the tier requires, and reassesses it when the use changes.

## 1. What it is

1.1. The category covers the assignment of the Risk Tier of a Solution, built or bought, from the class of its data, the influence of the AI on a decision, whether its output reaches or affects a customer, and its degree of autonomy, the Risk Tier being the highest that any of them indicates (AI Policy 3.1); the entry in the AI Registry with the owner, the scope, the data access, and the tier; the approval of the use for a data class by the Domain Owner; the explanation to the function of what the tier requires before use and in operation; and the reassessment when the use changes. A Control Function Contact may raise the tier within its remit, and only the Contact of model risk may lower it (AI Policy 3.2).

1.2. The Risk Tier is assigned by the AICC Lead, and by the Executive Sponsor for a Solution that the AICC Lead built. It is assessed against the AI Risk Appetite Statement of the Bank.

## 2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the category, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

- A Risk Tier assigned to a new use of AI, with its reasons
- An AI Registry entry made for a Solution, built or bought
- The requirements of a tier explained to a function before it starts
- A reassessment when a change raises the tier, and the return to Discovery for the checks the change touches
- An inventory of the AI uses of a function with their tiers, where none existed
- A tiering rule for a family of uses, which the AICC Lead applies to each use of the family

## 3. What the function receives

3.1. A Risk Tier with its reasons, an AI Registry entry, the list of what the tier requires, and a reassessment when the use changes.

## 4. How it runs

4.1. Run-rate work: a tiering takes days, in the study of an Engagement or on request for an existing use. The entry is made before the first use. A change that raises the tier returns the Solution to Discovery for the checks that the change touches.

## 5. What it leads to

5.1. One AI Registry of every AI use of the Bank with its tier, controls proportionate to the risk, and no use of AI that nobody has tiered.

## 6. The Package

6.1. The tiering questionnaire, the AI Registry entry form, and the tiering rules by family of uses.

## 7. Run-rate work or an Initiative

7.1. Run-rate work.

## 8. Who decides

8.1. The AICC Lead assigns and reassesses the Risk Tier, and the Executive Sponsor assigns it for a Solution the AICC Lead built; a Control Function Contact may raise it, and only the Contact of model risk may lower it; the Domain Owner approves the use for its data class; a Risk Tier higher than the one cleared in the business case returns to the Control Function Contacts.

## 9. Rule source

AI Policy 3; Operating Model 4.4; AICC Charter 5; Statement of Intent 7.8.
