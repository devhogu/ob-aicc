# Risk tiering

Every use of AI in the Bank carries a Risk Tier, and the Risk Tier sets the review, the validation, the human oversight, and the speed of release that apply to it. AICC assigns the Risk Tier of each Solution, records it in the AI Registry, tells the function what the tier requires, and reassesses it when the use changes.

## 1. What it is

1.1. The category covers the assignment of the Risk Tier of a Solution, built or bought, from its data classes, its users, its degree of autonomy, and the process it touches; the entry in the AI Registry with the owner, the scope, the data access, and the tier; the approval of the use for a data class by the Domain Owner; the explanation to the function of what the tier requires before use and in operation; and the reassessment when a change raises or lowers the tier.

1.2. The Risk Tier is assigned by the AICC Lead, and by the Executive Sponsor for a Solution that the AICC Lead built. It is assessed against the AI Risk Appetite Statement of the Bank.

## 2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the category, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

- A Risk Tier assigned to a new use of AI, with its reasons
- An AI Registry entry made for a Solution, built or bought
- The requirements of a tier explained to a function before it starts
- A reassessment when a change raises the tier, and the return to Discovery for the checks the change touches
- An inventory of the AI uses of a function with their tiers, where none existed
- A tiering rule applied to a family of uses, so that the function tiers its own small uses within the rule

## 3. What the function receives

3.1. A Risk Tier with its reasons, an AI Registry entry, the list of what the tier requires, and a reassessment when the use changes.

## 4. How it runs

4.1. Run-rate: a tiering takes days, in the study of an Engagement or on request for an existing use. The entry is made before the first use. A change that raises the tier returns the Solution to Discovery for the checks that the change touches.

## 5. What it leads to

5.1. One register of every AI use of the Bank with its tier, controls proportionate to the risk, and no use of AI that nobody has tiered.

## 6. The reusable package

6.1. The tiering questionnaire, the AI Registry entry form, and the tiering rules by family of uses.

## 7. Run-rate or program

7.1. Run-rate.

## 8. Who decides

8.1. The AICC Lead assigns and reassesses the Risk Tier, and the Executive Sponsor for a Solution the AICC Lead built; the Domain Owner approves the use for its data class; a Risk Tier higher than the one cleared in the business case returns to the Control Function Contacts.
