# Strategy and governance office

What AICC built for itself it builds for any unit of the Bank: a strategy, a charter, an operating and governance model, a process set with its templates, and a portal and repository that hold them, developed with AI in weeks and maintained with it afterwards. AICC acts here as the strategy office and the governance office of the function, and the function keeps the ownership.

## 1. What it is

1.1. The office covers the strategy paper of a function or a program, its charter and mandate, its operating model with roles, decisions, and control loops, its governance package with a control catalogue and a registry of records, its process set with templates, and a portal and repository that publish them, as this site publishes the charter of AICC.

1.2. The method is the one AICC used on itself: the documents are written in one voice from a single source, every rule is stated once, the processes are drawn as flows and loops, the controls are testable, and the portal is generated from the documents so that it cannot drift from them.

## 2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the line, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

- A strategy paper for a function or a program, with its priorities and its measures
- A charter and mandate for a unit, in the form of the AICC Charter
- An operating and governance model: Roles, decisions, control loops, records, controls
- A process set with its templates, drawn as flows and loops
- A portal and repository generated from the pack, in English and Russian
- A readiness and source audit: what documents, systems, and owners the function has
- Service-management set-up for the AI services a function runs itself: catalog entry, service levels, request and incident route

## 3. What the function receives

2.1. A charter pack in the style of the Bank: the defining documents, the workflows, the guides, and the templates; a registry structure for the live records; a control catalogue mapped to the loops of the unit; a portal generated from the pack, in English and in Russian where the function needs it; and the method and the tooling to keep it current.

2.2. For a program rather than a unit: the strategy, the governance, and the reporting chain, ready for its first Steering.

## 4. How it runs

3.1. The engagement starts with a study of two to three Iterations: the mandate, the line of work, the existing documents, and the bodies the function answers to. The pack is drafted with AI from that material and reviewed with the head of function in each Iteration; the portal is generated as the pack settles. The function reviews, decides, and owns; AICC drafts, structures, and builds.

## 5. What it leads to

4.1. A function with a baselined charter and a working portal, able to show its auditors, its regulator, and its staff how it is mandated, governed, and operated; and a pack it maintains with the same method.

## 6. The reusable package

5.1. The charter method and its templates, the governance catalogue, and the portal generator, proven on AICC itself and re-deployed for each function. The second function takes days where the first took weeks.

## 7. Run-rate or program

6.1. A program: an Initiative with a business case and an MVP, which is the first part of the pack and the portal.

## 8. Who decides

7.1. The head of function owns the pack and decides its content; the Executive Sponsor approves the Initiative where it spans Domains. AICC drafts and builds and decides nothing of the function's substance.

## 9. Rule source

Business Model 2.4 and 4; Portfolio Management Model 6 and 7. A clause for this service line is proposed for Business Model 4.
