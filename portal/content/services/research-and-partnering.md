# Research and partnering

AICC watches the field so the Bank does not have to: the models, the platforms, the methods, and the rules that change every quarter. It tests what matters in trials, keeps the Bank's reference of the industry body of knowledge and of the regulators and acts, and works with other organizations and with providers where the Bank gains from it.

This service line is stated on this site and not yet in the Business Model. It is offered within the capability that AICC has, and it becomes a commitment when the Business Model states it.

## 1. What it is

1.1. Research covers the watch on models, platforms, methods, and regulation; trials of what matters, as Experiments; and the maintenance of the Knowledge base and the Reference of this site. Partnering covers the relations with other organizations that adopt AI, with the providers of models and services, and with the professional bodies of the field.

## 2. What the client receives

2.1. For a function: an answer on what exists, what works, and what the rules require, before it invests. For the Bank: the Industry body of knowledge and the Regulators and acts pages kept current, publications on what AICC has learned, and trials that turn into Proposals.

## 3. How it runs

3.1. Research runs as enabling work of AICC under an Initiative of enabling work, with the Executive Sponsor as its client. Partnering follows the rules of the Bank for relations with third parties, and a provider is checked by the Control Function Contacts before use as the AI Policy states.

## 4. What it leads to

4.1. Proposals to the Bank, publications in the Knowledge base, and providers and partners that the Bank can use with confidence.

## 5. Records and templates

5.1. The Proposal; the Publications page; the AI Registry for a provider in use.

## 6. Who decides

6.1. The Executive Sponsor, as the client of enabling work. The Control Function Contacts check a provider before use.

## 7. Rule source

Business Model 2.3 and 2.4; Statement of Intent 10.5; AI Policy 4. A clause for this service line is proposed for Business Model 4.
