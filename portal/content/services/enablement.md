# Enablement

AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches the Domain Experts who carry adoption in their Domains, runs the communities of practice, and keeps the playbooks and the reusable assets of its Engagements so that the next function starts further ahead.

## 1. What it is

1.1. Enablement covers training by role, the coaching of Domain Experts, communities of practice, support on AI topics to the functions, and the maintenance of templates, methods, playbooks, and other reusable assets.

1.2. Enablement is how the Bank advances through the Maturity Levels: the share of employees trained and with access to an approved assistant, and the share of priority processes with an owned knowledge source, are among the Measures of Maturity Level 2.

## 2. What the client receives

2.1. For a function: training for its employees by role before they use AI, a Domain Expert coached to enable adoption in the Domain, and the playbooks and templates of AICC. For the Bank: communities of practice, and the lessons of the Engagements kept in the Portfolio.

## 3. How it runs

3.1. Training is set by the AICC Lead and delivered with the Domains. A Domain appoints its Domain Experts; AICC coaches them in the Engagements and in the communities of practice. The reusable assets are kept in the Portfolio and in the Knowledge base of this site.

## 4. What it leads to

4.1. Domains that use AI in their own processes with governed knowledge, within the shared principles and standards, and that put forward their own priorities to the Portfolio.

## 5. Records and templates

5.1. The training records of the Bank; the Appointments Record for the Domain Experts; the Portfolio for the playbooks and the lessons.

## 6. Who decides

6.1. The AICC Lead sets the training. The Domain Owners appoint their Domain Experts and decide the adoption within their Domains.
