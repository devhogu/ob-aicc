# Enablement

The people and the adoption: each person trained and coached to use AI for their own work, each Domain carrying its adoption through its Domain Experts, and each Solution managed through its life after delivery.

## 1. The categories of the area

| Category | Coverage | Run-rate or program |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Training and knowledge sharing](../training-and-knowledge-sharing.md) | Training by role, coaching at the desk, skill libraries, communities of practice, playbooks and publications | Run-rate |
| [Adoption and lifecycle management](../adoption-and-lifecycle-management.md) | Domain Experts and adoption plans per Domain; hand-over, support, revision, and retirement of Solutions; the life and operation of a Service | Run-rate for the hand-over, the support, and the reviews; program for the adoption of a Domain |

1.1. A category is a direction along which AICC provides services, with examples on its page; what AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement. Run-rate work is asked for through the front door and done in days to one Iteration; a program starts with a study and a business case.

## 2. How a function engages the area

2.1. A function brings its need to the AICC Lead in any form. The need is screened at the front door on the problem it answers, its size, its likely Risk Tier, and whether a package exists that answers it already, and it enters the run-rate lane or the program lane. The page How to engage states the steps, the commitment, and the service levels; the page The service categories in the Portfolio states the lanes.

## 3. What the area leaves behind

3.1. Every Engagement in the area leaves a reusable package, a method, a kit, an engine, or a catalog entry, that the next function takes in days where the first took weeks. The form of a package and of a catalog entry is stated on the page Service catalog: the form; the packages in preparation and available are kept in the Portfolio.
