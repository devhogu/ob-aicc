# Advise and formulate

The strategy, governance, and process office of the Bank for AI: the direction and the mandate of a unit, its normative documents and processes, the watch on the field, and the business cases and scenarios that the Portfolio decides on.

## 1. The categories of the area

| Category | Coverage | Run-rate or program |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Strategy and governance](../strategy-and-governance.md) | Strategy, charter, operating and governance model, portal and repository for a unit or a program, developed with AI, as AICC did for itself | Program |
| [Normatives and processes](../normatives-and-processes.md) | Policies, procedures, regulations, runbooks, and process descriptions drafted, aligned, and maintained with AI for any function | Run-rate for single documents and runbooks; program for a corpus |
| [Research and exploration](../research-and-exploration.md) | Regulatory and technology watch with digests, trials in the Lab, partnering with organizations and providers | Run-rate for the watch and the digests; program for a trial |
| [Business cases and scenarios](../business-cases-and-scenarios.md) | Use-case discovery, scenarios with problem, solution, and measures, readiness and source audit, the business case the Portfolio decides on | Run-rate for a single scenario or an audit; program for a Domain roadmap. The study itself is the first phase of every program |

1.1. A category is a direction along which AICC provides services, with examples on its page; what AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement. Run-rate work is asked for through the front door and done in days to one Iteration; a program starts with a study and a business case.

## 2. How a function engages the area

2.1. A function brings its need to the AICC Lead in any form. The need is screened at the front door on the problem it answers, its size, its likely Risk Tier, and whether a package exists that answers it already, and it enters the run-rate lane or the program lane. The page How to engage states the steps, the commitment, and the service levels; the page The service categories in the Portfolio states the lanes.

## 3. What the area leaves behind

3.1. Every Engagement in the area leaves a reusable package, a method, a kit, an engine, or a catalog entry, that the next function takes in days where the first took weeks. The form of a package and of a catalog entry is stated on the page Service catalog: the form; the packages in preparation and available are kept in the Portfolio.
