# Build and run

The solutions and the platforms: knowledge a function can ask, routine work done with AI and reviewed by a person, the factual base for decisions, the content a function publishes, and the shared engines and environments behind them.

## 1. The categories of the area

| Category | Coverage | Mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Knowledge services](../knowledge-services.md) | The corpus of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, as a governed knowledge base it can ask | An Initiative for a new knowledge base; run-rate work for adding a source set to an existing one |
| [Workplace automation](../workplace-automation.md) | Routing, forms, reports, consolidation, documents from templates, and case assistance, done with AI and reviewed by a person | Run-rate work for single automations; an Initiative for a process |
| [Analytics and decision support](../analytics-and-decision-support.md) | Pipelines, dashboards, analyses, and research tooling that prepare the factual base for decisions, with lineage to governed sources | Run-rate work for a view or a consolidation; an Initiative for a pipeline that feeds a recurring report of the Bank |
| [Content management](../content-management.md) | Public, investor, and management material generated from governed data and templates, and the templates, editions, versions, and languages behind it | An Initiative for an engine; run-rate work for a template or an edition change |
| [Platforms](../platforms.md) | The shared engines and environments that AICC builds, the requirements of AICC on the AI Platform, and the Handover of an engine to an IT function of the Bank | Initiative |

1.1. A category is a direction along which AICC provides services, with examples on its page; what AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement. Run-rate work is a small, repeatable request that is done within one Iteration (Business Model 4.7); an Initiative starts with a study and a business case.

## 2. How a function engages the area

2.1. A function brings its need to the AICC Lead in any form. The need is screened at the front door on the problem it answers, its size, its likely Risk Tier, and whether a Package or a Solution of the catalog answers it already; its service category proposes the mode, run-rate work or an Initiative, and the AICC Lead confirms it (Business Model 7.2). The page How to engage states the steps, the commitment, and the support levels; the page The modes and the front door in the Portfolio states the modes.

## 3. What the area leaves behind

3.1. An Engagement in the area leaves its lessons and, where one results, a Package, such as a method, a kit, an engine, or a catalog entry, that the next function takes in days where the first took weeks (Business Model 4.4). The form of a Package Definition and of a catalog entry is stated on the page Service catalog: the form; the Packages themselves are kept in the Portfolio.
