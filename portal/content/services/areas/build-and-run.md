# Build and run

The solutions and the platforms: knowledge a function can ask, routine work done by assistants under human validation, the factual base for decisions, the content a function publishes, and the shared engines and environments behind them.

## 1. The categories of the area

| Category | Coverage | Run-rate or program |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Knowledge services](../knowledge-services.md) | The corpus of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, as a governed knowledge base it can ask | Program |
| [Workplace automation](../workplace-automation.md) | Routing, forms, reports, consolidation, documents from templates, and case assistance, done by assistants under human validation | Run-rate for single automations; program for a process |
| [Analytics and decision support](../analytics-and-decision-support.md) | Pipelines, dashboards, analyses, and research tooling that prepare the factual base for decisions, with lineage to governed sources | Run-rate for a view or a consolidation; program for a pipeline that feeds a recurring report of the Bank |
| [Content management](../content-management.md) | Public, investor, and management material generated from governed data and templates, and the templates, editions, versions, and languages behind it | Program |
| [Platforms](../platforms.md) | The shared engines and environments of AICC, the requirements on the AI Platform of the Bank, and the hand-over of a platform to the teams that run at scale | Program |

1.1. A category is a direction along which AICC provides services, with examples on its page; what AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement. Run-rate work is asked for through the front door and done in days to one Iteration; a program starts with a study and a business case.

## 2. How a function engages the area

2.1. A function brings its need to the AICC Lead in any form. The need is screened at the front door on the problem it answers, its size, its likely Risk Tier, and whether a package exists that answers it already, and it enters the run-rate lane or the program lane. The page How to engage states the steps, the commitment, and the service levels; the page The service categories in the Portfolio states the lanes.

## 3. What the area leaves behind

3.1. Every Engagement in the area leaves a reusable package, a method, a kit, an engine, or a catalog entry, that the next function takes in days where the first took weeks. The form of a package and of a catalog entry is stated on the page Service catalog: the form; the packages in preparation and available are kept in the Portfolio.
