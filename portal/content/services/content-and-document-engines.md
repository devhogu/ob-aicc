# Content and document engines

Public, investor, and management material generated from governed data and templates, pre-filled for people to finish: periodic reports, disclosures, investor material, standard letters and documents, in the languages the Bank uses.

## 1. What it is

1.1. The line covers rendering engines that take data from governed sources and templates approved by the function and produce the draft of a document, a report edition, or a set of pages; the collection and pre-fill of the information a document needs; and the publishing step, which is a human act after the approval the Bank requires.

1.2. The engine drafts; a person reviews, approves, and publishes. Material that reaches investors, regulators, or customers follows the approval route of the Bank.

## 2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the line, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

- Monthly and quarterly report editions from governed data
- Board and investor material pre-filled for review
- Disclosures and regulatory narratives drafted for the approval route
- Standard letters and documents from templates
- Publishing to a portal as a human act after approval

## 3. What the function receives

2.1. An engine with its templates, its sources, its approval step, and its output channel; the data trail of each edition; and the operating notes for the function that runs it.

## 4. How it runs

3.1. A program: the study maps the editions, the sources, and the approvals; the MVP produces the first edition in parallel with the current process and measures the difference; the function then runs it with AICC's support, or AICC runs it as a Service.

## 5. What it leads to

4.1. Faster and more consistent editions, fewer manual steps, and a traceable base for every figure and statement that leaves the function.

## 6. The reusable package

5.1. The template and rendering engine and the collection and pre-fill pattern, re-used for each new document family.

## 7. Run-rate or program

6.1. A program.

## 8. Who decides

7.1. The Domain Owner owns the templates and the editions; the Executive Sponsor approves published output where the Operating Model so states; the Control Function Contacts clear the data classes.
