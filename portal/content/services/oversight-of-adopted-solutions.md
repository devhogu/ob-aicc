# Oversight of Adopted Solutions

Not every AI Solution of the Bank is built by AICC. When a Proposal is adopted and a Domain or a platform team delivers it, or when a function brings in a Solution of its own, AICC oversees it: it keeps it in the AI Registry with its Risk Tier, follows its checks and its incidents, and reports on it with the rest of the Portfolio.

## 1. What it is

1.1. Oversight covers the Adopted Solutions: Solutions that others deliver and run, which AICC records, reviews, and reports on. It is not ownership: the Domain that runs the Solution owns its results, and the Control Functions keep their own remit.

## 2. What the client receives

2.1. For the Domain: an AI Registry entry with the Risk Tier assigned, the gates before use made explicit, and a place in the Quarterly Report. For the Bank: one view of every AI use, built or bought, under one set of rules.

## 3. How it runs

3.1. An Adopted Solution enters the Portfolio when a Receiver accepts the Handover of an Experiment, or when a function declares a Solution. The AICC Lead assigns the Risk Tier and records it. The Solution passes the gates of the AI Policy before use, and AICC follows its live review, its AI Incidents, and its changes through the Registry.

## 4. What it leads to

4.1. Adopted Solutions that meet the same standard as the Solutions AICC builds, and a Portfolio that shows the whole use of AI in the Bank.

## 5. Records and templates

5.1. The AI Registry; the Portfolio entry of the Adopted Solution; the Control Sign-Off; the AI Incident Review; the Quarterly Report.

## 6. Who decides

6.1. The Domain Owner owns the Adopted Solution and its results. The AICC Lead assigns the Risk Tier and oversees. The Control Function Contacts validate and may stop.
