# Watch, research, and partnering

The function knows what exists, what works, and what the rules require, before it invests. AICC watches the models, the platforms, the methods, and the regulation; digests the changes for the functions concerned; tests what matters in trials; and works with other organizations and with providers where the Bank gains from it.

This service line is stated on this site and not yet in the Business Model. It is offered within the capability that AICC has, and it becomes a commitment when the Business Model states it.

## 1. What it is

1.1. The line covers a regulatory watch that digests the circulars and acts of the bodies the Bank answers to, for compliance, legal, and the functions concerned; a technology watch on models, platforms, and methods; trials of what matters, as Experiments; the maintenance of the Industry body of knowledge and the Regulators and acts pages of this site; and relations with other organizations that adopt AI, with providers, and with the professional bodies of the field.

## 2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the line, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

- A digest of the circulars and acts of the National Bank and other bodies for the functions concerned
- A technology watch on models, platforms, and methods
- A trial of a platform or a concept, as an Experiment, with its write-up
- A provider or partner relation prepared for the Bank
- The Industry body of knowledge and the Regulators and acts pages kept current

## 3. What the function receives

2.1. Change digests for the functions concerned, an answer on what exists and what works before a function invests, trial write-ups that turn into Proposals, and the Reference and the Knowledge base of this site kept current.

## 4. How it runs

3.1. Research runs as enabling work of AICC under an Initiative of enabling work, with the Executive Sponsor as its client, in a fixed share of the capacity of each Iteration. Partnering follows the rules of the Bank for relations with third parties; a provider is checked by the Control Function Contacts before use.

## 5. What it leads to

4.1. Proposals to the Bank, publications in the Knowledge base, and providers and partners the Bank can use with confidence.

## 6. The reusable package

5.1. The watch format and its digests, the trial write-up format, and the Reference pages.

## 7. Run-rate or program

6.1. Run-rate for the watch and the digests; a program for a trial.

## 8. Who decides

7.1. The Executive Sponsor, as the client of enabling work. The Control Function Contacts check a provider before use.

## 9. Rule source

Business Model 2.3 and 2.4; Statement of Intent 10.5; AI Policy 4. A clause for this service line is proposed for Business Model 4.
