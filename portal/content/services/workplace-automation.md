# Workplace automation

The routine work of a function done by assistants under human validation: routing requests, reading and filling forms, processing reports, consolidating inputs, and producing documents from templates. The person validates and promotes; the assistant prepares.

## 1. What it is

1.1. The category covers automations in the tools the function already uses: the triage and routing of requests and messages, the extraction and the filling of forms, the processing and the summary of reports, the consolidation of inputs from several sources, the drafting of standard documents from templates with the information collected, and case assistance under limited authority.

1.2. Every automation has a person who validates its output before it takes effect, in proportion to the Risk Tier. An automation that would act on systems or funds without review is not in this category.

## 2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the category, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

- Triage and routing of requests, complaints, and messages
- Extraction from forms and documents, and the filling of forms
- Processing and summary of recurring reports; reconciliation break triage
- Consolidation of inputs from several sources into one working file
- Documents from templates with the information collected, for a person to validate
- Case assistance under limited authority: related contacts detected, the permitted next step proposed, a grounded explanation drafted

## 3. What the function receives

3.1. Working automations, each with its Solution Definition, its Risk Tier, its validation step, and its owner in the function; a short guide for the people who use it; and an entry in the catalog of automations so that another function can take it.

## 4. How it runs

4.1. Run-rate: a need comes through the front door, is scoped in days, built in one Iteration with the Domain Expert, checked, and handed over. A set of automations for a whole process is a program with a study first.

## 5. What it leads to

5.1. Time released for higher-value work, more consistent output, and a growing catalog of automations that functions re-use.

## 6. The reusable package

6.1. The catalog of automations: each a package with its connectors, its prompts and rules, its validation step, and its deployment notes.

## 7. Run-rate or program

7.1. Run-rate for single automations; a program for a process.

## 8. Who decides

8.1. The Domain Owner approves the use for its data class; the owner in the function validates the output; the AICC Lead assigns the Risk Tier.
