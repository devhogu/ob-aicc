# Proof and prototyping

Before the Bank invests in a Solution, AICC proves it. A proof is a probe: an Experiment, a proof of concept, a minimum viable product, or a trial of a platform or a concept, run for a stated number of Iterations against leading indicators, and ending in evidence and a decision.

## 1. What it is

1.1. Proof and prototyping covers the MVP of an approved Initiative, time-boxed Experiments with no consumer yet, proofs of concept for a function, and trials of platforms, models, and concepts that the Bank considers adopting.

1.2. A proof uses real sources and, where the Risk Tier allows, real users in a bounded group. It is checked or validated before it reaches them, as any Solution is.

## 2. What the client receives

2.1. A working prototype with its measured results against the leading indicators of the business case; an Outcome Report that states what was delivered, what was learned, and who accepted it; and, where the evidence supports adoption, a Proposal that states what the Bank should adopt at scale and what it would take.

## 3. How it runs

3.1. The proof is the first phase after the approval of the business case. The Solution Engineer builds it with the Domain Expert in the Iterations of the Program Increment; the Domain Owner reviews it at each Iteration Review and Demo. An Experiment is time-boxed to a stated number of Iterations, and at the end of its time-box it goes to review.

## 4. What it leads to

4.1. The decision after the MVP, taken by the approver of the business case: continue to delivery, pivot to a new Initiative, defer, or reject. An Experiment ends accepted with its lessons, as a Proposal, or rejected; when a Receiver accepts its Handover, AICC oversees it as an Adopted Solution.

## 5. Records and templates

5.1. The Solution Definition, created in the MVP; the Outcome Report; the Proposal; a Decision Record for the decision after the MVP; the Control Sign-Off where a validation applies.

## 6. Who decides

6.1. The approver of the business case takes the decision after the MVP. The Domain Owner accepts the proof as the requester; the Executive Sponsor accepts an Experiment that has no Domain. The Checker or the Control Function Contacts check or validate before the first real users.
