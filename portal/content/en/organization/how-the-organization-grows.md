# How the organization grows

The method is written for a Team of a few people and does not change when the Team grows. What changes is who holds the Roles, which events are combined, and whether a Domain runs a Team of its own. This part states light mode, the steps out of it, and the shape of the organization as it scales.

## 1. Light mode

1.1. While the Competence Center Team has up to three people, the Competence Center runs in light mode: the Competence Center Lead is the product owner of the Team; the Weekly Planning and the Weekly Review are one session; the Iteration Retrospective and the monthly Steering are held in the Iteration Review and Demo, and Inspect and Adapt in the PI Review and Demo; the Daily Stand-up, Backlog Refinement, and Innovation are optional; Work Items are not tracked; a smaller set of states is used; and the check by a person other than the builder is done by an engineer of the IT function or the Domain whom the Competence Center Lead names. Everything else stays as stated.

## 2. The steps out of light mode

| Step | Trigger | What changes | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A second Solution Engineer | Appointed from the people the functions assign | The check by a person other than the builder is done inside the Competence Center; the accepted limit on the Competence Center Lead's acceptances is reviewed | Appointments Record; Risks and Issues |
| A product owner other than the Competence Center Lead | The Competence Center Lead names another person | The acceptance of Features and Capabilities moves to that person; the Competence Center Lead keeps the final acceptance of the Team | Appointments Record |
| The Team beyond three people | The fourth person | Light mode ends: the events are held separately, the full set of states is used, Work Items are tracked | The Calendar Record; the tracker |
| A Domain Team | A Domain assigns people who work the same method for its own Solutions | A second Team on the same cadence, the same boards, and the same rules, with its own product owner; the Competence Center Lead ranks the Program Backlog across Teams and keeps the Program Board | Appointments Record; the Program Board |
| Domain Experts in each Domain | Each Domain appoints its Domain Experts | Adoption carried in the Domain; a community of practice across them | Appointments Record |

## 3. The shape as it scales

3.1. At every size the shape is the same: the Executive Sponsor with the mandate, a lead accountable for the method and the records, Teams of Solution Engineers with Domain Experts, Domain Owners who own the results, the Control Functions beside, the Platform Owner outside, and the Steering as the one forum. Growth adds Holders and Teams; it does not add layers, meetings, or records. What the Competence Center does not do at any size is scale the operation of a Solution itself: a Solution the Bank adopts at scale is handed to its Receiver, and a Service that the Competence Center runs is handed over to an IT function of the Bank when the Bank should run it at scale (Business Model 2.5).
