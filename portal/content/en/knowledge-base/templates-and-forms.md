# Templates and forms

A template gives the form of a record that the Competence Center produces; its rules are in the documents. There are fourteen, used along the life of an Engagement and on the cycle of the unit. This page says which form is used when, who fills it and who signs it, and where the record is kept; each form has its own page with a copy button.

## 1. Which form when

### Before the work: the start of an Engagement

| Form | Used when | Filled by | Signed or approved by | Kept in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Initiative Brief](page:knowledge-base/initiative-brief) | A need becomes an Initiative: the one-page business case | The Competence Center Lead with the Domain Owner | The Domain Owner, or the Executive Sponsor above a guardrail, across Domains, or for enabling work; cleared by the Control Function Contacts for Risk Tier 2 and 3 | The Registry, with the Initiative |
| [Service Agreement](page:knowledge-base/service-agreement) | The study of an Engagement starts; amended when the business case is approved | The Competence Center Lead | Issued by the Competence Center Lead; the function is notified | The Registry, with the Initiative |

## 2. How a form is used

2.1. A form is copied from its page with the copy button, or from the charter folder, and its metadata block is omitted. The record it becomes is kept where the table says, under the rules of the Registry: closed and dated when it is evidence, corrected only by a new dated entry. A figure of the Bank stays in its source system, and the record points to it. The Document Catalog states the life cycle of a template: a template is activated, and a change to it is activated, by an entry in the Decision Log (Document Catalog 4.1).
