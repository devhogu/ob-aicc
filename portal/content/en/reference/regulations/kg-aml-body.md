# Kyrgyz Republic: the financial intelligence body and the legislation against money laundering and the financing of terrorism

The law and the body that oversee the duties of banks and other reporting entities to know their customers, monitor transactions, and report suspicious activity, in line with the international standards against money laundering and the financing of terrorism.

## 1. Identity

| Item | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | Law and its authorized body |
| Issued or overseen by | The financial intelligence body of the Kyrgyz Republic; the National Bank in banking supervision |
| Jurisdiction | Kyrgyz Republic |
| Status | Law in force, aligned with the international standards of the Financial Action Task Force; amended as those standards evolve |

## 2. What it sets

2.1. Customer due diligence and identification, risk-based monitoring, record keeping, the reporting of suspicious transactions, internal controls and training, and the supervision and sanctions that enforce them.

## 3. Whom it reaches

3.1. Banks and the other reporting entities of the Kyrgyz Republic.

## 4. Relevance to the Bank

4.1. AI that assists know-your-customer, monitoring, screening, or reporting touches a regulated process: it keeps the controls, the audit trail, and the human decision that the law requires, and it is validated accordingly.

## 5. How the charter relates to it

5.1. The Risk Tiers of the AI Policy place such uses at the higher tiers, with validation by the Control Function Contacts and human decision in each case; the customer intelligence priority of the Statement of Intent is pursued within these rules.
