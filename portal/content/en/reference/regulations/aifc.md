# Astana International Financial Centre

A financial center in Kazakhstan with its own jurisdiction, courts, and regulator, built on common-law principles, whose rules on financial services, data protection, and technology, and whose fintech sandbox, shape practice in the region.

## 1. Identity

| Item | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | Financial center with its own regulatory framework |
| Issued or overseen by | The AIFC and its Financial Services Authority |
| Jurisdiction | Kazakhstan |
| Status | Established 2018; its own rules on financial services, data, and technology; a regulatory sandbox for fintech |

## 2. What it sets

2.1. Licensing and conduct rules for the participants of the center; data protection regulations; a regulatory sandbox for fintech and for the testing of new services; and guidance on technology and digital assets.

## 3. Whom it reaches

3.1. The participants of the center and the persons dealing with them.

## 4. Relevance to the Bank

4.1. A regional center whose rules and sandbox are a reference for fintech practice and for how regulated experimentation can be organized; a possible venue for partners of the Bank.

## 5. How the charter relates to it

5.1. The Experiment workflow and the Lab of AICC follow the idea of bounded trial that a regulatory sandbox embodies.
