# G7 Hiroshima AI Process

The Group of Seven initiative on advanced AI produced guiding principles and a voluntary code of conduct for organizations developing the most capable models. These cover safety testing, transparency, incident reporting, and the responsible release of those models.

## 1. Identity

| Item | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | Voluntary international commitments |
| Issued or overseen by | The Group of Seven, with the OECD as secretariat |
| Jurisdiction | Global |
| Status | Guiding principles and a code of conduct for organizations developing advanced AI systems, 2023; a reporting framework followed |

## 2. What it sets

2.1. Commitments for developers of advanced AI systems: identify and mitigate risks across the life cycle, publish transparency reports, share information on incidents, invest in security, label AI-generated content, and prioritize research on societal risks.

## 3. Whom it reaches

3.1. Developers that adhere to the process, including the major providers of foundation models. It binds no user of those models.

## 4. Relevance to the Bank

4.1. The Bank buys its models from providers. Their commitments under this process shape the transparency and safety information the Bank can expect from them and inform the provider assessment.

## 5. How the charter relates to it

5.1. The provider check of the AI Policy and the provider assessment of the assurance services draw on the information that providers publish under these commitments.
