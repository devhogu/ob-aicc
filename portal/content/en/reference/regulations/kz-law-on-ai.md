# Kazakhstan: the Law on Artificial Intelligence and the national concept for AI

The first dedicated law on AI in the region, preceded by a national concept for the development of AI. It establishes the framework for the development and use of AI systems in Kazakhstan, with a classification of systems by their risk and duties for the persons who own and operate them.

## 1. Identity

| Item | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan and government concept |
| Issued or overseen by | The Government of Kazakhstan; the ministry responsible for digital development, innovations, and aerospace industry |
| Jurisdiction | Kazakhstan |
| Status | Concept for the development of AI adopted for 2024 to 2029; the Law on Artificial Intelligence adopted in 2025, the first dedicated AI law of the region |

## 2. What it sets

2.1. Principles for the use of AI, a classification of AI systems by risk with particular duties for the higher classes, the rights of persons affected, the duties of owners and operators, the roles of state bodies, and the support of the national platform and infrastructure for AI; together with the goals of the concept for talent, data, infrastructure, and adoption.

## 3. Whom it reaches

3.1. Kazakhstan: the state bodies, the owners and operators of AI systems in the country, and the persons affected.

## 4. Relevance to the Bank

4.1. A neighboring jurisdiction with a dedicated AI law and a risk-based classification, and a model for what the Kyrgyz Republic may adopt. A partner or a service of the Bank that operates in Kazakhstan falls under it.

## 5. How the charter relates to it

5.1. The Risk Tiers of the AI Policy and the accountability principle of the Statement of Intent follow the same risk-based logic; the Bank watches the law as a sign of the region's direction.
