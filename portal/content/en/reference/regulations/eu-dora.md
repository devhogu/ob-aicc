# EU Digital Operational Resilience Act and the guidance of the European Banking Authority

The regime for the operational resilience of the financial sector of the Union: how financial entities manage the risk of their information and communication technology, report incidents, test their resilience, and manage the risk of the third parties that provide them with technology, including cloud and AI services.

## 1. Identity

| Item | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | Regulation of the European Union and supervisory guidance |
| Issued or overseen by | European Supervisory Authorities; the European Banking Authority; national financial supervisors |
| Jurisdiction | European Union |
| Status | DORA applies since January 2025; EBA guidelines on ICT and security risk and on outsourcing precede it |

## 2. What it sets

2.1. A framework for ICT risk management with the management body accountable; classification and reporting of major ICT incidents; testing of resilience; management of ICT third-party risk with contractual requirements, registers of arrangements, and concentration risk; and oversight of the critical third-party providers of the sector.

## 3. Whom it reaches

3.1. Banks, payment and e-money institutions, investment firms, insurers, and the other financial entities of the Union, and their ICT providers. It does not bind a bank in the Kyrgyz Republic; it reaches the Bank through providers that serve the Union and as the model that supervisors of the region draw on.

## 4. Relevance to the Bank

4.1. It is the clearest statement of what a financial supervisor expects on incident reporting, resilience testing, and third-party risk, which are the operational side of using AI services from providers. The Bank's provider assessments, fallback and exit arrangements, and incident handling are judged against this pattern.

## 5. How the charter relates to it

5.1. The provider rules of the AI Policy, the fallback and exit arrangements of the Statement of Intent, the AI Incident review, and the incident management of the Bank address the same expectations.
