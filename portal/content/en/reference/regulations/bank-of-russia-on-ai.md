# Bank of Russia on artificial intelligence in the financial market

The view of the central bank and financial supervisor of the Russian Federation on AI in finance: its benefits, risks, and regulation. Developed in consultation with the market, the approach favors soft law and codes before binding rules.

## 1. Identity

| Item | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | Reports and guidance of a central bank and financial supervisor |
| Issued or overseen by | Bank of Russia |
| Jurisdiction | Russian Federation |
| Status | Consultation and reports on the application of AI in the financial market from 2023; guidance and codes developed with the market |

## 2. What it sets

2.1. The themes are responsible use of AI by financial organizations, model risk, data, consumer protection and explainability, risks from concentration and large providers, and the balance between innovation and stability.

## 3. Whom it reaches

3.1. The financial sector of the Russian Federation.

## 4. Relevance to the Bank

4.1. Supervisory practice in the largest neighboring market, and a likely influence on the region’s supervisors.

## 5. How the charter relates to it

5.1. The AI Risk Appetite Statement, the Risk Tiers, and reporting to the Board Committee address the themes the Bank of Russia names.
