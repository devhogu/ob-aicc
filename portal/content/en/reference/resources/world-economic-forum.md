# World Economic Forum on AI governance and financial services

The forum where industry, regulators, and civil society state shared positions on AI and on the future of financial services, in reports written to be read by boards. Its AI Governance Alliance publishes frameworks on responsible generative AI; its financial services work publishes on the transformation of banking.

## 1. Identity

| Item | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | International organization for public-private cooperation; reports and toolkits |
| Who | The World Economic Forum, through its AI Governance Alliance, its Centre for the Fourth Industrial Revolution, and its financial services platform |
| Access | Free; reports on its site |

## 2. What it publishes

2.1. The reports of the AI Governance Alliance on the safe development, deployment, and governance of generative AI, with briefing papers for executives; toolkits for boards and for the responsible use of AI; the reports of the financial services platform on AI, data, and the future of the industry; and the Global Risks Report, with AI among the risks it ranks.

## 3. Why it matters to the Bank

3.1. The Forum's material is the common language of boards and executives on AI; a position stated there is one the Board Committee and the investors of the Bank may have read. Its generative AI governance framework is consistent with the Bank's principles and is useful as an external check of them.

## 4. How to take it, and for what

4.1. For Board-level framing, use the executive briefings and the board toolkits. For cross-industry positions on generative AI governance, read the AI Governance Alliance reports and compare them with the Statement of Intent. For the state of the industry, read the financial services reports as a view shaped with the industry's own participants.

## 5. Cautions

5.1. The Forum's reports are produced with member companies, among them the large technology and consulting firms; read the industry positions as positions. The material is not regulation and binds no one.
