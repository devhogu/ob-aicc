# Training and knowledge sharing

AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches them at the workplace, builds the prompt and skill libraries for each line of work, runs the communities of practice, and publishes what it learns so that the next function starts further ahead.

## 1. What it is

1.1. The category covers training by role before people use AI; coaching at the workplace and clinics in the function; prompt and skill libraries built for a line of work; help in designing and setting up the tooling at a person's workplace; communities of practice across the Bank; and the publications, playbooks, and lessons of the Engagements.

1.2. Training is how the Bank advances through the Maturity Levels: the share of employees trained and with access to an approved assistant is among the Measures of Maturity Level 2.

## 2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the category, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

- Training by role before people use AI
- Coaching at the workplace and clinics in the function
- A prompt and skill library for a line of work
- Help in setting up the tooling at a workplace, within the tools the Bank approves
- A community of practice across functions
- Playbooks, lessons, and publications from the Engagements

## 3. What the function receives

3.1. A training path for each role, delivered with the function; clinics at the workplace; a skill library the function owns and extends; a community of practice; and the playbooks and the publications of AICC.

## 4. How it runs

4.1. Run-rate work: training is scheduled with the function; coaching and clinics run in the Iterations; a skill library is built in one Iteration with the Domain Expert and grows afterwards.

## 5. What it leads to

5.1. People who use AI for their own work within the rules, and a Bank whose knowledge of how to use AI is shared rather than held by a few.

## 6. The Package

6.1. The training paths by role, the skill libraries, the clinic format, and the playbooks.

## 7. Run-rate work or an Initiative

7.1. Run-rate work.

## 8. Who decides

8.1. The AICC Lead sets the training; the heads of function schedule it for their people.
