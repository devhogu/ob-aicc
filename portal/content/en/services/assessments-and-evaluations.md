# Assessments and evaluations

Before a model, a provider, or a Solution is used, it is assessed and evaluated: does it do what it claims, on the cases of the function, within the rules, at an acceptable cost? The Competence Center runs these assessments with the function and the Control Function Contacts, with an evaluation harness that tests a Solution against the cases of the Bank, so that the decision to use rests on evidence.

## 1. What it is

1.1. The category covers the evaluation of a model or a Solution against an evaluation set built from the cases of the function, with its accuracy, its failure modes, and its cost; the preparation of the check of a provider of models or services, which the Control Function Contacts make (AI Policy 4.1): where data is processed and kept, whether the provider may train on it, the contractual terms, the fallback and the exit; the readiness assessment of a Domain in people, data, and process; benchmarking against the current way of working and its cost; and the check or the validation of a Solution before its first real users, prepared for the Checker or the Control Function Contacts.

1.2. The person who evaluates is not the person who built; The Competence Center does not validate its own work.

## 2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the category, not a list of commitments. What the Competence Center commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

- A model or a Solution evaluated against the cases of a function before use
- A provider check prepared for the Control Function Contacts of information security, data protection, and legal, or of information security alone for a Risk Tier 1 Solution or a provider already checked
- A readiness assessment of a Domain in people, data, and process
- A benchmark of a Solution against the current way of working and its cost
- A check or a validation prepared for a Solution before its first real users, with the evidence the Checker or the Control Function Contacts need
- A post-implementation review of a Solution against its business case

## 3. What the function receives

3.1. An evaluation report with the results, the failure modes, and the cost; a provider check that the Control Function Contacts have made; a readiness report; a benchmark; and the evidence pack for a check or a validation.

## 4. How it runs

4.1. Run-rate work: an evaluation takes days to one Iteration with the Domain Expert; a provider assessment follows the AI Policy and the Control Function Contacts; a readiness assessment is one Iteration. The evaluation set stays with the Solution and grows with its use.

## 5. What it leads to

5.1. Models and providers the Bank can use with confidence, Solutions that pass their gates on evidence, and a growing library of evaluation sets by Domain.

## 6. The Package

6.1. The evaluation harness, the provider assessment checklist, the readiness assessment, and the evaluation sets by Domain.

## 7. Run-rate work or an Initiative

7.1. Run-rate work.

## 8. Who decides

8.1. The Competence Center Lead decides whether a check or a validation is needed; the Checker checks for Risk Tier 1; the Control Function Contacts validate for Risk Tier 2 and 3, check providers, and may stop; the Domain Owner decides on the use.
