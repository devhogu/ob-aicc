# Advise and formulate

The strategy, governance, and process work that AICC drafts with AI for a function, which owns the substance: the direction and the mandate of a unit and its normative documents and processes; with them the watch on the field, and the business cases and scenarios that the Portfolio decides on.

## 1. The categories of the area

| Category | Coverage | Mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Strategy and governance](../strategy-and-governance.md) | Strategy, charter, operating and governance model, portal and repository of a function or an Initiative, drafted with AI, as AICC did for itself | Initiative |
| [Normatives and processes](../normatives-and-processes.md) | Policies, procedures, regulations, runbooks, and process descriptions drafted, aligned, and maintained with AI for a function | Run-rate work for single documents and runbooks; an Initiative for a corpus |
| [Research and exploration](../research-and-exploration.md) | Regulatory and technology watch with digests, trials in the Lab, partnering with organizations and providers | Run-rate work for the watch and the digests; an Initiative for a trial |
| [Business cases and scenarios](../business-cases-and-scenarios.md) | The discovery of needs, scenarios with their problem, Solution, and leading indicators, the audit of readiness and of sources, and the business case the Portfolio decides on | Run-rate work for a single scenario or an audit; an Initiative for a Domain roadmap. The study itself is the first phase of every Initiative |

1.1. A category is a direction along which AICC provides services, with examples on its page; what AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement. Run-rate work is a small, repeatable request that is done within one Iteration (Business Model 4.7); an Initiative starts with a study and a business case.

## 2. How a function engages the area

2.1. A function brings its need to the AICC Lead in any form. The need is screened at the front door on the problem it answers, its size, its likely Risk Tier, and whether a Package or a Solution of the catalog answers it already; its service category proposes the mode, run-rate work or an Initiative, and the AICC Lead confirms it (Business Model 7.2). The page How to engage states the steps, the commitment, and the support levels; the page The modes and the front door in the Portfolio states the modes.

## 3. What the area leaves behind

3.1. An Engagement in the area leaves its lessons and, where one results, a Package, such as a method, a kit, an engine, or a catalog entry, that the next function takes in days where the first took weeks (Business Model 4.4). The form of a Package Definition and of a catalog entry is stated on the page Service catalog: the form; the Packages themselves are kept in the Portfolio.
