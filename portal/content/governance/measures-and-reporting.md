# Measures and reporting

Governance reads a small set of measures at each loop and reports the whole each quarter. This part states what the unit measures about its own control, what each loop reads, what the Quarterly Report contains, and how the Measures of the Maturity Levels sit above it. The definitions of the measures of the Portfolio and of delivery are on their own pages; this page states the measures of governance itself.

## 1. The measures of governance

| Measure | Definition | Source | Read at | Target rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Control status | The share of controls Operating, and the count Open, Deficiency, No occurrence yet, Not yet due | Control Matrix | Quarterly Steering | No Deficiency open past its due date |
| Deficiencies and findings | Open items in the Risks and Issues from controls that did not operate and from audit or supervisory findings, with age against due date | Risks and Issues | Monthly Steering | Reviewed monthly until closed; an overdue item decided at once |
| Exceptions | Open Exceptions by Control Function, with their end dates; expired Exceptions | Risks and Issues | Monthly Steering | An expired Exception decided at once |
| Decision sample | The Decisions of the AICC Lead sampled at the monthly Steering and the share found in order | Decision Log | Monthly Steering | Set by the Steering; a finding is a deficiency |
| Risk Tier reassessments due | Reassessments due by the rule of the tier and not yet done | AI Registry | Quarterly Steering | None overdue |
| AI Incidents and control breaches | Count by severity; reconciled each quarter with the incident management of the Bank | AI Incident Reviews; incident management | Quarterly Steering; a major incident at once | Reported each quarter; a major incident to the Board Committee without waiting |
| Risks beyond appetite | Risks accepted beyond the AI Risk Appetite Statement, each with its report to the Board Committee | Decision Records | Quarterly Steering | Each reported |
| Access review | The review of access to the Registry and the tools, done or due | Registry | Quarterly Steering | Done each quarter |
| Appointments | Roles with a Holder named, deputies named, and appointments past due | Appointments Record | Yearly Steering; on a change of Holder | Every Role held; the limits recorded in Risks and Issues |
| Document review | Documents reviewed in the year, and changes activated | Document Catalog; Decision Log | Yearly Steering | Each document reviewed at least annually |

## 2. What each loop reads

2.1. The Weekly Review reads the flow and raises what it cannot settle; it carries no control of its own. The monthly Steering reads the sample of Decisions, the deficiencies, the Exceptions, the gate decisions, and the acceptances and live reviews of the month. The quarterly Steering reads the Control Matrix, the quarterly risk check, the reassessments due, the incidents reconciled, the access review, the measures of the Portfolio and of delivery for the quarter, and the Maturity Level. The yearly Steering reads the findings of the year, the documents, the appetite, and the appointments.

## 3. The Quarterly Report

3.1. The Quarterly Report is the one report of the unit, written by the AICC Lead, approved and issued to the Board Committee by the Executive Sponsor, and it is the report to the Board. It states, with each figure traced to a Record or a governed source with its date: each Active Initiative and its decision; the benefit confirmed against the Envelope of each Strategic Priority; the value scored for the PI Objectives and the predictability trend; the Domains and employees using approved Solutions; the AI Incidents and control breaches; the control status and the open deficiencies; the risks accepted beyond appetite; the reliance on providers; and the Maturity Level of each priority. Its form is the Quarterly Report template.

## 4. The Measures of the Maturity Levels

4.1. Above the measures of governance stand the Measures of the Maturity Levels of the Statement of Intent, by which a Strategic Priority advances: at level one the AI Policy activated, the Control Function Contacts appointed, each known AI use recorded and tiered, and the baselines established; and from level two on, the shares of employees trained and with access, the processes with AI embedded and their change against baseline, the benefits against the Envelope, the predictability of delivery, the audit and regulatory findings, and, for agents, the actions stopped or reversed and the evaluation coverage. The quarterly Steering confirms the Maturity Level of each priority on them; the yearly Steering sets the targets.
