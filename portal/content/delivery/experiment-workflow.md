# The Experiment workflow: the Lab

Before the Bank invests, AICC proves. An Experiment is a time-boxed trial of a Solution, a platform, or a concept, run on governed data in a bounded environment, measured against leading indicators, and ended with a decision: promote to delivery, hand over to a Receiver, or shelve with its lessons. This page states the workflow of an Experiment and the rules of the environment in which it runs, the Lab of AICC. The workflow is stated on this site and is proposed for the Solution Lifecycle Model 7.

## 1. The stages

| Stage | What happens | Concerns addressed | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Define | The hypothesis is written: the need, the leading indicators, the acceptance criteria, the time-box in Iterations, the data needed and its classes, the likely Risk Tier | Governance: the sponsor and the Domain Owner named; risk: the Risk Tier and the data classes; the provider check where a new platform is used | The Initiative Brief or the Experiment entry; the Solution Definition opened |
| 2. Establish | The environment is prepared: an isolated workspace, access by role, the provider cleared by the Control Function Contacts, logging on | Operations: isolation, access, logging; risk: no production write-back | The environment record; the provider assessment |
| 3. Prepare data | Read-only extracts under the data classification, with an owner for each source, personal data assessed and minimized, quality checked | Data: ownership, classification, quality; risk: personal data | The data extract record; the Data Sharing Arrangement where data leaves the Bank |
| 4. Build | The workflow or the agent is built with the Domain Expert, with guardrails for grounding, drift, and bias, and an evaluation set | Operations; observability: the traces and the measures | The evaluation set; the build notes |
| 5. Validate | The results against the leading indicators and the acceptance criteria, the benchmark against the current way and its cost, the correctness of the data, the demonstration to the Domain Owner | Governance: acceptance; risk: the check or validation before any real user | The Outcome Report; the Control Sign-Off where a validation applies |
| 6. Decide | Promote to delivery, hand over to a Receiver, or shelve with the lessons; the write-up kept | Governance: the decision after the MVP by the approver of the business case; a Proposal where the Bank should adopt | The Decision Record; the Proposal; the Portfolio entry |

## 2. The rules of the Lab

2.1. The Lab is an environment, not an authority. Decision rights stay where the charter puts them: the approver of the business case decides after the MVP, the Domain Owner accepts, the Control Function Contacts validate for Risk Tier 2 and 3 and may stop, and the release beyond the first users is a separate decision.

2.2. Data enters the Lab as read-only extracts under the classification of the Bank, with a named owner for each source and no write-back to a system of the Bank. Personal data is minimized and is assessed with the Control Function Contact for data protection. A cloud or external environment is a provider and passes the check of the AI Policy before use.

2.3. Every Experiment has a time-box in Iterations and ends at its close: accepted with its lessons, as a Proposal, or rejected. An Experiment that proves its case becomes a Product or a Service through the decision after the MVP, not by drift.

2.4. The guardrails of the Lab, among them isolation, access, personal data protection, logging, grounding and bias checks, a failure protocol, and the promotion pathway, are kept as a scorecard with the coverage of each, reviewed at the quarterly Steering as evidence of the Maturity Level.
