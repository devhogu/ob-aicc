## What an initiative presents

An initiative brings together a clear business need, an accountable owner, a bounded scope and an expected outcome. Its record distinguishes established facts from assumptions and identifies the evidence still needed for a decision.

Selection, commitment to delivery and confirmation of benefit are separate decisions. The record shows the decision actually made and the date of its latest update.
