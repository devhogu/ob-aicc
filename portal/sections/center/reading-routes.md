# Reading routes

The routes are formal reading orders for a reader who wants a path through the site. They are listed on the Overview and on the home page, and each states its purpose. A page is named by its identifier in the sitemap.

| Route | Reader | Purpose | Sequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Orientation | A new member of staff, or any reader | To learn what AICC is and how its pages relate | `about/statement-of-intent`, `about/aicc-charter`, `about/values-and-principles`, `services/business-model`, `about/charter-outline`, `reference/vocabulary` |
| Executive | The Executive Sponsor and the Board Committee | To read the intent, the Strategic Priorities, the roadmap, the decisions, and the oversight | `about/statement-of-intent`, `about/statement-of-intent/strategic-priorities`, `about/statement-of-intent/capability-and-maturity-roadmap`, `about/aicc-charter`, `organization/operating-model/decisions`, `governance/control-loops`, `responsible-ai/ai-policy` |
| Function | A head of function or a Domain Owner | To learn how a need becomes an Initiative and a Solution, and what the function decides | `services/business-model`, `services/engagement-workflow`, `portfolio/portfolio-management-model/the-business-case-and-the-mvp`, `knowledge-base/initiative-brief`, `responsible-ai/ai-policy` |
| Delivery | A Solution Engineer or a member of a Team | To learn the method of delivery and the forms that it uses | `delivery/solution-lifecycle-model/the-flow-of-value`, `delivery/service-delivery-workflow`, `delivery/service-delivery-guide`, `delivery/solution-lifecycle-model/verification-release-and-acceptance`, `delivery/cadence-workflow`, `knowledge-base/index` |
| Review | Internal audit and the control functions | To read the rules, the controls, and the evidence, and to find where each record is kept | `about/aicc-charter`, `organization/operating-model/roles`, `governance/controls`, `responsible-ai/ai-policy`, `governance/unit-governance-workflow`, `reference/records-and-systems`, `reference/change-history` |
