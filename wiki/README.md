# AICC Portal Wiki

Working notes for the static HTML portal of the AI Competence Center (AICC) at O!Bank Kyrgyzstan.

The wiki holds lineage, research, open items, and an archive. It is explanatory only and is not a document of the charter folder. Portal product authority lives in `.grace/`; the formal documents that define AICC live in [`charter/`](../charter/documents/document-catalog.md).

## Conventions

- One topic per page, plain Markdown, American spelling.
- Link to `.grace/` artifacts instead of copying them.
- A lineage page records the source of a charter document. The charter documents carry no references.
- Remove an item from an open items page when it is decided and stated in a charter document.
- Do not store secrets or bank-internal data here until audience and classification are confirmed.
