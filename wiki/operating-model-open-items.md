# AICC: open items

Undecided questions behind the charter documents, which hold no open questions. Remove an item when it is decided and stated in a charter document. Decisions that AICC has taken are in the Decision Log.

| # | Question | Why it matters | Decided by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Who owns the shared AI platform (model gateway, knowledge layer, tool gateway, agent registry, guardrails, evaluation)? AICC owns nothing, so an owner outside AICC is needed. | The statement of intent and the stage policies depend on it. Funding model and decision rights need a named owning role. | Executive Sponsor |
| 2 | How much of a domain expert's time is dedicated to AI adoption, and how a domain releases that capacity. | The model relies on this capacity. | Domain Owners with the Executive Sponsor |
| 3 | Is functional direction by AICC enough authority over engineers who are line-managed by IT or a domain? Who agrees to it? | The model relies on AICC steering their AI work without line management. | Executive Sponsor with IT and the Domain Owners |
| 4 | Which engineer source applies to each domain (AICC, IT, or the domain's own IT specialist), and how many engineers are assigned to AICC as headcount. | Determines the size of AICC and the funding model. | Executive Sponsor |
| 5 | Confirm the cadence intervals (monthly Iterations, a quarterly Program Increment, and the Steering monthly and quarterly) and the initial limits on work in progress. | These are the starting defaults. They set the rhythm the domains commit to. | AICC Lead with the AI Steering Committee |
| 6 | Guardrail thresholds: the amount above which an initiative needs a brief and Executive Sponsor approval, and the expected investment mix across new solutions, enabling work, and running solutions. Until set, initiative briefs are not required. | The funding model cannot be written without them. | Executive Sponsor |
| 7 | Which group entities take part first, and in what order after the bank? | Sets the scope of the first charter and of the control function contacts. | Executive Sponsor |
| 8 | For an entity with a different regulator, is there a group-level control function contact, or only entity-level contacts? | Determines how many appointments the register needs and who resolves a conflict between regimes. | Executive Sponsor with the control function heads |
| 9 | May the shared platform hold data of several entities, and under which group arrangement may data be shared between them? | Drives the data classification rules and the platform requirements. | Executive Sponsor with legal and data protection |
| 10 | Which laws bind each group entity: the Kyrgyz Digital Code AI provisions, existing National Bank rules on risk, outsourcing, and IT, the Kazakhstan AI law, and the EU AI Act where an entity has a link? | The charter says "applicable law". The policies and risk tiers need the actual list. | Legal and compliance heads |
| 11 | List of prohibited AI uses for the AI Policy. No bank reviewed publishes one. | The policy needs a starting list. | AI Steering Committee with the control function heads |
| 12 | Should sustainability be a named principle? Five of eleven banks name it, mostly European or Australian. | Alignment with peers versus focus. | AI Steering Committee |
| 13 | Which Board committee receives the periodic AICC report: technology or risk? Does each regulated entity report to its own board? | Sets the addressee of the report in the AICC Charter. | Board secretary |
| 14 | Group and bank values: none were found. The statement proposes integrity, prudence, and respect for people. Replace or confirm once the group or the bank publishes its own. Ask the group's strategy or investor relations team. | AICC scales down from the group's values. | Executive Sponsor |
| 15 | The mapping of AI adoption to the group's six strategic pillars is our reading. Confirm with the group's strategy owner. | The statement claims alignment with the group strategy. | Executive Sponsor |
| 16 | AI Incident timings as drafted: report of a High Severity incident at once, review within ten working days. Confirm with compliance and information security. | Regulatory notification duties may set shorter periods. | Control Function heads |
| 17 | When to introduce a work tracker, and whether the Records of the Portfolio remain in this repository. | The Records may move to a tracker. | AICC Lead |
| 18 | Is three Risk Tiers enough for each Control Function, and are the requirements in the AI Policy right? | Every Stage condition depends on them. | Control Function heads |
| 19 | Is the data classification of the Bank consistent with the rules of use in the AI Policy? | The AI Policy relies on the classification. | Data protection and information security |
