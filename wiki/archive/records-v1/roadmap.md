The Portfolio Roadmap shows the current quarter as committed, the next two quarters as planned, and the period beyond as
indicative. Quarters are counted from the quarter in which the Decision Records of the activation of the Statement of Intent on the Adoption of Artificial Intelligence and the AICC Charter are dated, which is quarter 0.
Dates are entered when the quarters are fixed.
