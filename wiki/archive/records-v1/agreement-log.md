# Agreement Log

The Record of every Engagement: the function that is the client, the Initiative, the phases and the support level of its Service
Agreement, its state, and its outcome. The Service Agreements and the Outcome Reports are in the folder of each Initiative. The log
holds no figures of the Bank: capacity is in days, and benefit is a reference to where the figure lives.
