# Vocabulary and Style

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1. This document fixes the terms, names, and style used in every document of the AICC charter folder, so that each
term carries one meaning in every document.

1.2. It applies to the Statement of Intent, the Operating Model, the Roles and Responsibilities, the Decision Rights, the
Register of Appointments, the policies, and every later document added to the charter folder.

1.3. The AICC Lead owns this document, maintains it, and approves changes to it. A term defined in this document is used with its defined meaning. A document does not redefine it. A concept has one
term, and the alternative expressions listed in section 4 are not used.

## 2. Document set and precedence

2.1. The documents of the charter folder are listed in the Document Catalog.

2.2. Where documents differ, the document of the higher category prevails. The order of the categories, from the highest, is
MND, ORG, GOV, OPS, POL, PLN, and REF, as the Document Catalog labels them. Within a category the document with the lower
number prevails. A Template yields to the document that prescribes it. This document prevails on the meaning of terms and on
style.

## 3. Style

3.1. English is used, in American spelling.

3.2. Text is written in the third person. The first and second person are not used.

3.3. Description uses the present tense. An obligation is stated with "shall". A permission is stated with "may". A
prohibition is stated with "shall not".

3.4. Defined terms begin with a capital letter. Abbreviations are defined at first use.

3.5. Clauses are numbered. A clause states one requirement or one fact. Lists use parallel wording and end with a
semicolon, and the last item ends with a full stop.

3.6. Contractions, colloquial expressions, idioms, questions, and exclamations are not used.

3.7. A defining document contains no references to sources, no open questions, and no examples of a competitor's or a
provider's practice. Sources are recorded in the reference documents, and open questions in the open items documents.

3.8. A document does not state a name, a date, or a figure that changes. It refers to the Role. The Register of
Appointments records names and dates.

3.9. A Role is named for its responsibility, not for a Position.

3.10. Each document begins with a metadata block and ends with a change log, as the Document Catalog states.

3.11. A document refers to another document by its title. A document does not refer to a file path.

3.12. Headings use sentence case. Document titles use title case.

3.13. Diagrams are written in Mermaid notation and are numbered as figures. Figures are numbered within each document,
starting from 1. A clause that states what the figure shows follows each figure.

3.14. Identifiers of Records follow the scheme in the Artifact Standards.

## 5. Naming of documents and roles

5.1. Role names begin with a capital letter and are written in full. The codes used in the Decision Rights are defined in
the Decision Rights.

5.2. The term "Portfolio" refers to the managed set of Initiatives and Use Cases. The term "Delivery Program" refers to the
Delivery Teams. The two are not interchanged.

5.3. The term "Stage" refers only to a step of the Portfolio Flow. The term "Maturity Level" refers only to a level of the
Maturity Roadmap.

5.4. The term "guardrails" is always qualified as Investment Guardrails or Platform Guardrails.
