# AI Incident Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1. This policy states what constitutes an AI Incident, how it is reported, contained, assessed, and reviewed, and when
regulators, providers, and customers are notified.

1.2. It applies to every use of AI in the Bank and in each Participating Entity.

## 2. AI Incident

2.1. An AI Incident is an event in which the use of AI causes, or could cause, harm, a breach of law or policy, or a loss of
control. It includes:

(a) harm to a customer or an employee, or an unfair or wrong decision;

(b) a leak or misuse of data;

(c) an attack on or through an AI system;

(d) an action of an AI agent beyond its limits or permissions;

(e) a breach of law, regulation, contract, or policy;

(f) a material failure of a Solution or a material degradation of its performance; and

(g) a near miss that would have been one of these events.

## 3. Severity

3.1. Each AI Incident is assigned a Severity of High, Medium, or Low.

3.2. The Severities are defined as follows.

| Severity | Description |
| --- | --- |
| High | Serious harm to a customer or an employee, a material breach or loss of data, a breach that a regulator must be told of, or an agent acting beyond its limits with effect |
| Medium | Harm that is limited or reversible, a breach of policy, or a failure that affects a Domain |
| Low | A near miss, or an event without harm that needs correction |

## 4. Responsibilities

4.1. Every employee who becomes aware of an AI Incident reports it to the AI Solution Engineer of the Solution or, if that is
not known, to the AICC Lead.

4.2. The AI Solution Engineer contains the AI Incident and leads its handling. The Domain Owner is accountable for it. The
Platform Owner assists with containment on the AI Platform.

4.3. The Control Function Contacts assess the AI Incident within their remits. The Control Function Contact of compliance
decides whether a regulator must be notified, and the Control Function Contact of data protection decides whether a person
whose data is affected must be notified, in each case in accordance with the law of the Entity.

4.4. The AICC Lead coordinates and enters the AI Incident in the AI Incident Register.

## 5. Process

5.1. The handling of an AI Incident follows these steps.

(a) **Report.** The AI Incident is reported as soon as it is detected. An AI Incident of High Severity is reported to the AICC Lead
and to the Control Function Contacts within one hour of detection.

(b) **Contain.** The AI Solution Engineer contains the effect. Any Control Function Contact or the AICC Lead may suspend a
Solution pending review.

(c) **Assess.** The Severity, the cause, the data and persons affected, and the obligations to notify are assessed.

(d) **Notify.** Regulators, providers, and customers are notified as the law and the contracts require.

(e) **Remedy.** Corrective actions are assigned with owners and dates.

(f) **Review.** A review of the AI Incident takes place within ten working days after it is contained, and its findings
are entered in the AI Incident Register. An AI Incident of High Severity is reported to the AI Steering Committee at its next
meeting or by Written procedure.

## 6. Records

6.1. An AI Incident is documented in the AI Incident Report and entered in the AI Incident Register.
