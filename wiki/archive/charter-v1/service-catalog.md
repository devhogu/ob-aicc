# Service Catalog

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1. This document defines the Service Portfolio of AICC, its Service Catalog, and the AICC Services that AICC provides to
Domains.

1.2. It applies to the AICC Services and to the Solutions that are available to more than one Domain.

## 2. Service Portfolio

2.1. The Service Portfolio has three parts, which follow the life of a service.

| Part | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Pipeline | AICC Services and Solutions that are proposed or in development and are not yet available |
| Service Catalog | AICC Services and Solutions that are currently available to Domains |
| Retired | AICC Services and Solutions that are being withdrawn or that have been withdrawn, with their history |

2.2. The Portfolio Manager keeps the Service Portfolio. It is reviewed each quarter and when a service is added, changed, or
retired.

2.3. The Service Catalog is the part that Domains see. Each entry states what the service is, who may use it, how it is
requested, and what it provides.

## 3. AICC Services

3.1. AICC provides the following AICC Services to Domains.

| Service | Description | Provided by | Request |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Adoption engagement | Discussion of adoption with the Domain Owner, identification of routine work suitable for AI, and preparation of the first Use Case | AICC Lead | On request of a Domain Owner, or at the initiative of AICC |
| Intake and discovery support | Description of the Use Case, assignment of the Risk Tier with the Control Function Contacts, assessment of benefits, cost, and risks, and preparation of the Initiative Brief where required | Portfolio Manager | Submission of a need at Intake |
| Solution design and build | Definition of the technical Solution, design approval, and, where AICC supplies the AI Solution Engineer, the building of the Solution | Lead Architect and AI Solution Engineer | Approved Use Case at Discovery |
| Pilot support | Training of the Domain Expert, support during the Pilot, and preparation of the Pilot report | Enablement Coach and AI Solution Engineer | Use Case at Pilot |
| Training and coaching | Training of employees, Domain Experts, and AI Solution Engineers by role | Enablement Coach | Request of a Domain Owner |
| Communities of Practice | Regular exchange of practice across Domains | Enablement Coach | Open to Domain Experts and AI Solution Engineers |
| Architecture guidance | The standards and platform requirements in the Standards Record, patterns and templates for Solutions, and review of designs | Lead Architect | Request, or at design approval |
| Portfolio and benefits reporting | Reports on the Portfolio, the flow of work, and the benefits realized | Portfolio Manager and Delivery Lead | Scheduled, and on request of a Domain Owner |
| Functional Direction | Direction of the AI work of AI Solution Engineers who are line-managed elsewhere | Delivery Lead | Arrangement in the Operating Model |

3.2. Each AICC Service has an identifier, an owner, the Roles that may request it, the inputs it needs, the outputs it
produces, and the time within which AICC responds. The response times are stated in the Service Catalog record.

## 4. Solutions in the Service Catalog

4.1. A Solution enters the Service Catalog when it has passed Scale and is available for reuse by other Domains. It leaves the
Service Catalog when it is retired.

4.2. Each entry states the identifier, name, description, Domain of origin, Domains that may use it, Risk Tier, owner,
AI Solution Engineer, data classes used, status, link to its entry in the AI Registry, and support arrangement.

4.3. The status of an entry is one of proposed, in development, available, being retired, or retired. These correspond to the
Stages of the Portfolio Flow.

## 5. Records

5.1. The Service Portfolio is kept as a Record in accordance with the Artifact Standards. The Record lists the AICC Services and
the Solutions in each part.
