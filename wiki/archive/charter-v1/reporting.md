# Reporting

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1. This document defines the reports that AICC produces, their audience, content, timing, and owner.

1.2. It applies to the reports on the Portfolio, the Delivery Program, and the Maturity Roadmap. The Metrics state the
Measures that the reports contain.

## 2. Reports

2.1. AICC produces the following reports.

| Report | Audience | Owner | Timing | Template |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quarterly Report | AI Steering Committee | AICC Lead | Each quarter, before the AI Steering Committee | Quarterly Report |
| Report to the Board Committee | Board Committee | Executive Sponsor, prepared by the AICC Lead | At the intervals that the Board Committee sets | Quarterly Report, in summary |
| Benefits Report | Domain Owner | Portfolio Manager | Each quarter, for each Use Case in Operate | Benefits Report |
| Delivery Review Notes | Domain Owners and Domain Experts | Delivery Lead | After each Delivery Review | Delivery Review Notes |
| Control Review report | AI Steering Committee | Control Function Contact designated by the Control Functions | Each quarter, from the Control Review | Meeting Minutes |

## 3. Content of the Quarterly Report

3.1. The Quarterly Report contains the following sections.

(a) Summary of progress against the Strategic Priorities and the Maturity Roadmap, with the Maturity Level of each
Strategic Priority.

(b) The Portfolio: Initiatives and Use Cases by Stage, with movements in the quarter and the Limits on Work in Progress.

(c) The Measures of the Maturity Levels, against baseline and target.

(d) Delivery: predictability of the Objectives, and flow measures of lead time, throughput, work in progress, and aging
work.

(e) Benefits realized against the Investment Envelope of each Strategic Priority.

(f) Risks: the Risk Tier profile of the Portfolio, AI Incidents, Exceptions, and control findings.

(g) Decisions taken in the quarter and Decisions required.

(h) Changes proposed to the Strategic Priorities, the Investment Envelopes, or the Investment Guardrails.

(i) The Readiness Level of each document of the charter folder, the score of each criterion of the Corpus Assessment, and the open Findings of the
latest Assessment.

## 4. Rules

4.1. Each figure in a report is traceable to a Record or to a governed source, and a report states the date of its data.

4.2. A report to the Board Committee or to investors is approved by the Executive Sponsor before it is issued.

4.3. Reports are stored in the Records of the Portfolio.
