# Register of Appointments

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1. This Register maps Positions and named Holders to the Roles defined in the Roles and Responsibilities, for AICC and for
each Domain and Control Function of the Bank and of each Participating Entity.

1.2. This Register is the only document of the charter folder that names persons. The other documents refer to Roles, so
that they remain stable when persons change.

## 2. Conventions

2.1. A Role is defined in the Roles and Responsibilities. A Position is a job in the organization chart. A Holder is the
person in that Position.

2.2. One person may hold several Roles, and one Role may have several Holders. The rules of separation in the Roles and
Responsibilities apply to every entry.

2.3. Each entry takes effect on its date and remains in effect until it is closed. Closed entries are retained.

2.4. Each change is made by the appointer named for the Role and is recorded here with its date.

2.5. The column Roles held states every Role that the Holder holds, so that the rules of separation in the Roles and
Responsibilities can be tested.

2.6. A delegation under the Decision Management is entered in the Delegations block in section 4.6.

## 3. Appointers

3.1. Each Role is filled by the appointer shown in the following table.

| Role | Appointed by |
| --- | --- |
| Executive Sponsor | Executive management |
| AI Steering Committee | Executive Sponsor |
| AICC Lead | Executive Sponsor |
| Lead Architect, Portfolio Manager, Delivery Lead, Enablement Coach | AICC Lead |
| Domain Owner | The Position of the head of the Domain, by virtue of the Role, confirmed by the Executive Sponsor. For an Entity, by the management of that Entity |
| Domain Expert | Domain Owner |
| AI Solution Engineer | Domain Owner, the technology function, or the AICC Lead, according to the arrangement in the Operating Model |
| Control Function Contact | The head of the Control Function, for its remit, the Entity, and the regulatory regime served |
