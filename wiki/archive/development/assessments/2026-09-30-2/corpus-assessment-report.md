## 2. Summary

The corpus is **not ready for activation and not ready to operate**. Of 28 items assessed (25 documents, the Templates as one group, and two items that the documents require and that do not exist), no item is at R3 or above: 2 are absent (R0), 8 are stubs (R1), and 18 are drafted (R2). The Records of the Portfolio are at R2. The wiki and the published portal copy reach R3 by the scores only; they have no activation, and two Major findings (F-004, F-020) are open against them. Four Blocker findings and 42 Major findings are open (72 findings in all).

What has improved since the first Assessment. The light lifecycle removed the Document Control, the classes, and the approvers; the metadata block and change log are on every document and agree with the Catalog; titles are consistent (F-001 closed); no link is broken; every row of the Decision Rights has exactly one accountable Role; every Stage exit Decider matches the Decision Rights; every Decision Category has a Decider, a Forum or means, and a Record; and the Template list and status rules now have one home. Of the 37 first findings, 2 are closed, 8 are changed, and 27 remain open as they were.

What the first Assessment missed or scored too generously (section 12). (1) D-8 (operability) was scored 3 for documents whose Roles have no Holder and whose Templates have never been used; read literally, D-8.1 to D-8.3 fail for every document that names a Role, and the result is that no document can reach R3 (F-049). (2) The Vocabulary defines 102 terms against a limit of 100 (F-046), 17 Roles against 15 (F-047), and about 6 percent of the clauses are restated (F-005), against a recorded 2.2 percent. (3) "Shall" for obligations is used in two documents only (F-045). (4) The Risk Tier Policy contradicts the Roles and the Statement of Intent on the validation of a Use Case by its owner (F-038, Blocker). (5) The Document Catalog gives activation of the documents that bind Domains and Control Functions to the AICC Lead (F-042). (6) The Design Review has no standards to review against (F-056).

The main defects are of five kinds.

1. **Contradictions that block activation.** The release of a Risk Tier 4 Use Case has no Decision Category, row, or agenda item (F-009); the owner of the Templates differs in three documents (F-011); Tier 1 validation by the owner contradicts the separation rule (F-038).
2. **Missing carriers.** Eight stubs, the AI Risk Appetite Statement, the architecture standards, the yearly cycle, the Investment Guardrails thresholds, and the Statement of Intent commitments without a carrier (F-010, F-007, F-056, F-065, F-057).
3. **Operability gate and staffing.** No Role has a Holder (F-017); no procedure has been run (F-033); the standard makes activation depend on operation (F-049); the Hub is one person who may hold four Roles, which the separation rules forbid in part (F-051).
4. **Proportionality.** Budget breaches (102 terms, 17 Roles, 6 percent restated); every Forum, Register, Template, and service active on paper (F-012); a standard of 81 items for a unit with no staff (F-028).
5. **Currency and drift.** The published Statement is the superseded v0.1 (F-004); legacy words in the Roadmap, the Standards, and the assessment Templates (F-043, F-044); stale wiki counts (F-067).

Independence. This Assessment is made by an assessor who did not write the documents. The first Assessment was made by the author and said so. Section 12 shows where this Assessment differs.
