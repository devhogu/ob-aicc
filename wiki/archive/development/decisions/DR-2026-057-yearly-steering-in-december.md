## 1. Facts

- DR-2026-052 (item 4) set the yearly direction loop and the yearly strategic loop at the quarterly Steering that ends PIQ4, for the next year, and the pack carried that wording on 2026-10-02 (Operating Model 6.1, 6.5; Portfolio Management Model 4.1, 4.2).
- That Steering is held in the IP week of PIQ4, the week before the year-end week. In 2026 the Friday of that week and the day before it are gray (24 and 25 December), so the Steering moves to 23 December 2026, and a frame for the next year set then is in force only days before the year starts.
- The owner of the pack asked that the Steering move to the first weeks of January or to the first weeks of December, whichever makes more sense. Early January is blocked and gray (2027-01-04 to 2027-01-08 in the Calendar), and the frame of the next year must be in force before the year starts. The first weeks of December are ordinary working weeks.
- The monthly Steering of December exists in any case, and the Calendar places its week in I12W1 or I12W2.

## 2. Options

1. Keep the yearly loops at the quarterly Steering that ends PIQ4.
2. Hold the yearly loops in the first weeks of January, at the first Steering of the year.
3. Hold the yearly loops at the monthly Steering of December, held in the first two weeks of December, as the yearly Steering.

## 3. Decision

The AICC Lead takes the third option, and decides the following.

1. D1: The yearly direction loop (Operating Model 6.5) and the yearly strategic loop (Portfolio Management Model 4.2) run at the yearly Steering, for the next year. They set the documents, the AI Risk Appetite Statement, the appointments, and the policy, and the Strategic Priorities, the Investment Envelopes, the Investment Guardrails, and the yearly Proposal of the AI adoption strategy.
2. D2: The yearly Steering is the monthly Steering of December, held in the first two weeks of December (I12W1 or I12W2). It is the ordinary monthly Steering of that month, with the monthly control loop and the portfolio sync, and in addition it carries the direction loop and the strategic loop. It is not an extra meeting. The Steering Summary has the type yearly.
3. D3: The yearly Steering takes the Quarterly Report of PIQ3 and the findings of the year to that date as its input. A change that the result of PIQ4 calls for in the frame is taken at the first quarterly Steering of the next year.
4. D4: The rule D2 of DR-2026-055 has one exception. In December the monthly Steering is held in the first two weeks as the yearly Steering, and the quarterly Steering that ends the IP week carries only the assurance loop and the portfolio review. It still confirms the PI Objectives and the Roadmap, takes the quarterly risk check, the Maturity Level, the access review and the reconciliation of the AI Incidents, and reports to the Board Committee. It no longer sets the Strategic Priorities, the Envelopes, the Guardrails, the documents, or the appetite.
5. D5: The controls stay at 32, C-01 to C-32, and none is added. C-01 to C-04 and C-20 operate at the yearly Steering for their yearly part, and C-06, C-07, and C-27 operate at the quarterly Steering of the IP week. The Calendar enters the yearly Steering in I12W2 of 2026-PIQ4 (the week of 2026-12-07) and of 2027-PIQ4 (the week of 2027-12-06), and the day is set with the calendar of the Executive Sponsor.

## 4. Conflicts and advice

None declared. D1 to D4 give duties to the Executive Sponsor, who runs the loops, and no record exists of the agreement of the Executive Sponsor. The AICC Lead tells the Executive Sponsor of the change (Document Catalog 4.2).
