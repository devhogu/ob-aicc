## 1. Facts

- The execution layer had to be consistent with the portfolio and the governance models (Decision Log, DR-2026-049).
- The loops of the Program Increment, the Iteration, and the week had no section of their own, and the dependencies were kept in a Dependency Map introduced by DR-2026-020 and used in DR-2026-021.

## 2. Options

1. Keep the model as it stood, with the flow of work in clauses by topic.
2. Restructure it as the method of the Teams, with a section and a diagram for each loop, and the Program Board in place of the Dependency Map.

## 3. Decision

1. The Solution Lifecycle Model is restructured from top to bottom as the method of the Teams, in operating instructions: the principles and values; the flow of value from the intake; the levels, the backlogs, and the boards; the states and the Stages; the cadence with the loops of the Program Increment, the Iteration, and the week; verification, release, and acceptance; the life cycle; and the records and controls.
2. Each loop of the cadence is its own section with its own Plan, Do, Check, Act diagram and its interfaces to the loop above and below, as in the Operating Model and the Portfolio Management Model.
3. The Program Board is the board of the dependencies of a Program Increment. It replaces the Dependency Map.
4. The intake is stated: the Capabilities of an Initiative after the decision to continue, a change or a new feature of a released Solution, and enabling work.
5. The citations of the clauses of the model are remapped in the pack.
