# DR-2026-045 The portfolio flow clarified

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Decision

1. After the MVP the approver decides to continue, to pivot, or to reject. To reject means the value is not seen, and it is a decision on the merits. A pivot closes the Initiative as Pivoted and starts a new Initiative at the funnel with a full Initiative Brief and the whole cycle, linked to the first so that what was learned survives.
2. Rejected is a decision on the merits: before approval, after the MVP, or when the outcome under review is not wanted. Cancelled is a withdrawal without a decision on the merits, such as an error, a mistake, or a duplicate. A stop by a Control Function cancels the Solution.
3. Discovery is research: it learns the need and the requirements, builds nothing, and shapes the Initiative Brief. The MVP is a probe: the smallest version of the first Solution that is tried, to see whether it works and satisfies the need.
4. The Control Function Contacts are gates on the business case in the form proposed. For an Initiative that expects Risk Tier 2 or 3, the Contacts concerned clear it before it is approved.
5. The rule that the Executive Sponsor approves any commitment until the Guardrails are set is removed from the operating models, the guide, the template, and the briefs.

## Revisit

At the first business case that is approved.
