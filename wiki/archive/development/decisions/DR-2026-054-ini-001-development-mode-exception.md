# DR-2026-054 INI-001 runs in development mode without the approval of its business case

| Decided by | AICC Lead (Timur Alimbayev) |
| Status | Decided |

## 1. Facts

- INI-001 Charter corpus readiness is Active, in the Stage Implementation, and is enabling work of AICC with the Executive Sponsor as client (Initiative Brief, `initiatives/INI-001-charter-corpus-readiness/brief.md`).
- Section 6 of its Initiative Brief, Decision and acceptance, is Open: the business case is not approved. The Portfolio Management Model 6.3 gives the approval of the business case of enabling work to the Executive Sponsor, and the control C-09 evidences it.
- The charter is in development mode, and the work runs under the AICC Lead. The Executive Sponsor has confirmed the goals of the first 100 days (DR-2026-012 and DR-2026-013), and no record exists that the Executive Sponsor has approved the business case of INI-001.
- An Exception to a requirement that AICC sets alone is decided by the AICC Lead in a Decision Record (control C-17).

## 2. Options

1. Stop INI-001 until the Executive Sponsor approves its business case.
2. Record that the Executive Sponsor approved it. This is rejected, because no approval was given.
3. Let INI-001 continue in development mode as an Exception to the approval step, decided by the AICC Lead, for ratification by the Executive Sponsor when the charter is baselined.

## 3. Decision

The AICC Lead decides, as an Exception to the approval of the business case (Portfolio Management Model 6.3, Solution Lifecycle Model 5.1), that INI-001 stays Active in development mode. The Exception covers INI-001 only, and no other Initiative: the others are in Scoping and are approved in the usual way. The Executive Sponsor has not approved the business case. When the charter is baselined, the Executive Sponsor, as the client of enabling work, approves the business case and ratifies this Exception, with a Decision Record, or decides otherwise. Until then the Exception stands as the decision of the AICC Lead, and it is entered in the Risks and Issues as RI-031, with an item in the Initiative Brief, the Portfolio Backlog, and the Control Matrix (C-09, C-17).

## 4. Conflicts and advice

The AICC Lead leads INI-001, acts as its Domain Owner (Operating Model 4.6), and decides this Exception on the work of the AICC Lead. The Operating Model 5.7 asks the next level to decide where a conflict exists, and the next level here is the Executive Sponsor, who ratifies afterwards. The conflict is accepted for this Exception only, and the Steering reviews the open Exception each month. No advice was taken.

## 5. Evidence of the Decision

This Record; the Decision Log entry DR-2026-054; RI-031; section 6 of the Initiative Brief of INI-001. The Executive Sponsor has not approved or ratified it, and the Record carries no message of the Executive Sponsor.
