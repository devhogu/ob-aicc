# DR-2026-036 The charter is of the Bank

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Facts

The documents named the Group, the Entities, and the Participating Entities, and gave rules for data and decisions between Entities. AICC serves the functions of the Bank, and no other company takes part.

## Options

1. Keep the Group and Entity language for a later extension.
2. Remove it, and keep one control for sharing outside the Bank.

## Decision

1. The charter names the Bank and its functions and Domains. The Group, Entity, and Participating Entity are removed from the documents, the workflows, the guides, the Templates, and the live records.
2. The Group Arrangement becomes the Data Sharing Arrangement: it permits the sharing of data or decisions outside the Bank, is decided by the Executive Sponsor after consulting the Control Function Contacts of data protection, legal, compliance, and information security, and is recorded in the Decision Log. Control C-19 stays.
3. Each Control Function names one Control Function Contact for the Bank.
4. The Statement of Intent is the Statement of the Bank. Its alignment with strategy is with the Strategic Pillars of the Bank, which the Bank confirms: the e-commerce and synergy pillars of the earlier text are removed because they rest on the structure of the Group.
5. Earlier Decision Records, assessments, and the archive keep the earlier language as history.

## Revisit

When another company takes part, or when the Bank confirms its Strategic Pillars.
