# DR-2026-047 The portfolio loops

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Facts

The Portfolio Management Model drew the flows and the decisions of the portfolio, but not the loops that run continuously with a cadence, a forum, and a review.

## Decision

1. The portfolio runs on four loops, each a Plan, Do, Check, Act cycle with its own cadence, forum, decider, interfaces, and records: the strategic loop (yearly), the portfolio review loop (quarterly), the portfolio sync loop (monthly), and the backlog care loop (weekly). Each is bound to its layer and shows only its interface to the loop above and below it.
2. The loops use the events of the control loop of the Operating Model 6 and add no meeting.
3. Defer is an option beside reject at the gates and after the MVP: the Initiative is put on hold with a reason and a date to look again.
4. The figures of the Roles, the gate, the pull, and the funding are folded into the text of the loops, because the loops show them.

## Revisit

At the first quarterly Steering.
