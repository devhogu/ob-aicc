# DR-2026-037 The Service Agreement settles the commercial details

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Facts

The Business Model listed the content of the Service Agreement, and rules on its assumptions, its backlog, and its end. Those are details of an Engagement that the two parties settle for themselves.

## Decision

1. The Business Model states the principles: AICC commits in a Service Agreement, which is a working agreement without a signature chain; the AICC Lead issues it when the study starts and amends it at the approval of the business case; AICC commits to the capacity and the outcome, and the function commits to nothing; an Engagement ends with an Outcome Report.
2. The support level, the response targets, and the content of the commitment and of the working agreement are settled in the Service Agreement, on the form of its Template. The Template now carries the support through Service Management, the response targets as targets and not guarantees, and the scope as an intent and a backlog.

3. The Business Model states that the Service Agreement is a form of agile working agreement, which follows the principles and the ways of working of agile delivery.

4. The Service Agreement is a working agreement on a best-effort basis, within the capacity and the capability that AICC has available. AICC commits to the capacity, and works toward the outcome.
