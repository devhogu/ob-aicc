# DR-2026-059 The Templates aligned with the documents

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Identifier | DR-2026-059 |
| Title | The Templates aligned with the documents |
| Date | 2026-10-02 |
| Type | check of the documents |
| Level | AICC Lead |
| Decided by | AICC Lead (Timur Alimbayev) |
| Status | Decided |

## 1. Facts

- A review of the Templates against the documents of the pack, after DR-2026-050 to DR-2026-058, found the Templates aligned in their citations and their identifiers.
- The Templates did not yet carry the fields that the decisions of the same days require: the Business acceptor, the Service Agreement for enabling work, the funding sentence, the pivot link, the Risk Tier 3 review and the Control Matrix in the Quarterly Report, the expiry date of an Exception and its entry in the Risks and Issues Record, the identifiers SGN and AIR, the notice of a major AI Incident, the yearly and the December content of the Steering Summary, the approval of the data class in the Solution Definition, the acceptance of a significant change, the emergency change, the acceptance of a Handover, and the Receiver.
- The Acceptance Checklist did not state its prerequisites or the rule of the signature of the Executive Sponsor, the Templates README did not show the form of a Feature, and the Decision Record had no block for a Data Sharing Arrangement.
- Some Templates held audit-style sections that the work does not need to run: the Acceptance Checklist, section 6 of the Initiative Brief, section 1 of the Steering Summary, the log of the Appointments Record, the Registry Snapshot, the Control Sign-Off, and the AI Incident Review.

## 2. Options

1. Leave the Templates as they are and correct each record when it is made.
2. Align the Templates with the documents, add the missing fields, trim the audit-style sections, and add no new Template.
3. Align the Templates and add a new Template for each record that the new fields touch.

## 3. Decision

The AICC Lead takes the second option, and decides the following.

1. D1, the fields added: the Business acceptor; the Service Agreement for enabling work; the funding sentence; the pivot link; the Risk Tier 3 review and the Control Matrix in the Quarterly Report; the expiry date of an Exception and its entry in the Risks and Issues Record (AI Policy 6.1); the identifiers SGN and AIR; the notice of a major AI Incident; the yearly content and the December content of the Steering Summary; the approval of the data class in the Solution Definition; the acceptance of a significant change; the emergency change; the acceptance of a Handover; and the Receiver.
2. D2, the Acceptance Checklist: its prerequisites are stated, and so is the rule of the signature of the Executive Sponsor.
3. D3, the Templates README shows the form of a Feature, and the Decision Record has a block for a Data Sharing Arrangement.
4. D4, the audit-style sections are trimmed: the Acceptance Checklist, section 6 of the Initiative Brief, section 1 of the Steering Summary, the log of the Appointments Record, the Registry Snapshot, the Control Sign-Off, and the AI Incident Review.
5. D5, no new Template is added. The Outcome Report is revised only for the wording of the Business acceptor.
6. D6, the Records of the Registry are kept in step: the AI Registry has a column for the completion of the training before first use (AI Policy 2.1) and a column for the Checker and the date of the check of a Risk Tier 1 Solution (AI Policy 3.3), and the Risks and Issues Record has a column for the expiry date of an Exception. The earlier Records, such as the Initiative Briefs, stay as they were made, because they are records and not forms.
7. D7, open points: none new.

## 4. Conflicts and advice

None declared. D1 and D2 give duties to the Executive Sponsor: the signature on the Acceptance Checklist and the monthly review of an Exception that has an expiry date. No record exists of the agreement of the Executive Sponsor. The AICC Lead tells the Executive Sponsor of the change (Document Catalog 4.2).
