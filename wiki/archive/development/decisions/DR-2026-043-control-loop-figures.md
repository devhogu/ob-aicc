# DR-2026-043 The control loops as figures in the Operating Model

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Facts

The governance of AICC repeats one pattern: a trigger, a decision at the right level, a record, and a review. It shows in the loop by horizon, in the movement of a Decision, in the handling of an event, and in the way evidence reaches internal audit. The figures were in the Unit governance workflow, apart from the clauses that they illustrate.

## Decision

1. The Operating Model carries six figures, each beside the clause that it illustrates: the movement of a Decision (5), the control loop by horizon (6), the handling of an event (6), the life of an item of the Risks and Issues Record (8), the cycle of a control (8), and the status of a control in the Control Matrix (8).
2. A figure states no rule of its own, and the clause prevails (clause 1.4).
3. The Unit governance workflow no longer draws these loops. It points to the figures of the Operating Model and shows the sequences between the Roles: a month and a quarter, and an AI Incident. It keeps the reporting chain and the life of a document, which the Operating Model does not draw.

## Revisit

At the first independent audit of the unit.
