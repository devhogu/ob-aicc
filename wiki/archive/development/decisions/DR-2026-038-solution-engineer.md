# DR-2026-038 The Solution Engineer

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Facts

The Role of the AICC Engineer assumed that AICC owns engineers. AICC owns none: the people who build are assigned by the functions. The title "AI engineer" is ambiguous, and the Role covers more than building.

## Options

1. Developer.
2. Solution Architect.
3. Solution Engineer.
4. Forward Deployed Engineer.

## Decision

1. The Role is the Solution Engineer: the person who owns a Solution end to end, designs it, decides its architecture, builds it, deploys it, and runs it with the Domain. The Role of the AICC Engineer is removed.
2. The AICC Lead, or a person whom a function assigns to AICC, holds the Role. The AICC Lead appoints the Solution Engineers from the people whom the functions assign, with the consent of their line managers, and enters them in the Appointments Record.
3. The requirements on the design of human oversight, testing, and logging, and the duty to contain an AI Incident, bind the Solution Engineer of the Solution, wherever they are assigned from.
4. The market titles, such as AI automation engineer or AI engineer, are job titles that HR may map onto the Role.

## Revisit

When AICC has a second Solution Engineer.
