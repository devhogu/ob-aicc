## 2. Context

Roles 3.2, Decision Rights, and Decision Management give the Templates to different Roles. Catalog 1.2 makes the AICC Lead owner of every document. Finding: F-011, ISS-003.

## 3. Options and criteria

1. Keep three owners.
2. One owner: the AICC Lead decides, the Enablement Coach maintains, the Lead Architect decides technical standards only.

Criteria: one accountable Role, consistency with higher documents, proportionality to a unit run by one person.

## 4. Decision

The AICC Lead decides the Templates. The Enablement Coach maintains them. The Lead Architect decides technical standards only. Roles, Decision Rights, and Decision Management are corrected to this wording in Iteration 3.

## 5. Rationale

It follows Catalog 1.2 and leaves one accountable Role.

## 6. Risks accepted

The AICC Lead decides as drafter and owner before an Executive Sponsor is appointed. The Executive Sponsor may revisit the Decision at activation.
