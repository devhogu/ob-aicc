# DR-2026-040 The Operating Model and the Solution Lifecycle Model

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Facts

The Operating Model held two different subjects in one document of about 6,800 words: the governance and control of AICC as a unit of the Bank, which an auditor tests, and the flow of work from a business need to retirement, which the teams use. The standards separate them. TOGAF separates architecture governance from the Architecture Development Method. COBIT separates governance from management. ITIL 4 separates the governance of the service value system from the service value chain.

## Options

1. Keep one document.
2. Split into the Operating Model for the unit and the Solution Lifecycle Model for the work. Alternatives for the name of the second were SDLC, which is limited to software, and ALM, which is the name of a class of tools.

## Decision

1. The Operating Model (AICC-ORG-01) is the governance and control model of AICC as a unit: purpose, what AICC is, principles of work, Roles, Decisions, the control loop, records and evidence, and controls.
2. The Solution Lifecycle Model (AICC-ORG-02) states how a Solution moves from a business need to its retirement: the portfolio, the states and Stages, the cadence of the delivery loops, verification, release, and acceptance, and the life cycle by offering type, including the Adopted Solutions.
3. The control loop is a new section of the Operating Model. It states the horizons (yearly, quarterly, monthly, weekly, and on an event), the Steering, the AI Steering Committee, and the reporting chain, which were in the cadence and in the workflow of the unit governance.
4. The Operating Model prevails. The Solution Lifecycle Model works within it. The control rows of the Operating Model cite the Solution Lifecycle Model for the rules of delivery.
5. The section numbers of the cited rules changed. The records and evidence are Operating Model 7, the controls are 8, and the rules of the work are in the Solution Lifecycle Model.

## Revisit

When the Solution Lifecycle Model is shaped for audit, after the Operating Model.
