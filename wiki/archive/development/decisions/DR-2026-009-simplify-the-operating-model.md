# DR-2026-009 Simplify the operating model

*Written before the Decision Record Template (DR-2026-029), in its own form.*

Date: 2026-09-30. Decided by the AICC Lead, on the instruction that AICC is a few engineers who work in an agile way and that decisions are distributed, light, and based on facts.

## Context

The corpus had 25 documents, 13 Roles, 8 Forums with secretaries and quorums, 47 decision rows, 7 decision categories, 7 Registers, and 23 Templates. A team of a few engineers cannot run that, and the independent assessment found 72 findings, most of them about the process itself.

## Decision

1. Six documents: Statement of Intent, AICC Charter, Operating Model, AI Policy, Vocabulary and Style, Document Catalog.
2. Seven Roles: Executive Sponsor, AICC Lead, AICC Engineer, Domain Owner, Domain Expert, Control Function Contact, Platform Owner. The other duties are hats that the team takes, and need no appointment.
3. Three meetings: Sync and Demo, Quarterly Review, Steering. No secretaries, quorums, or written procedures.
4. Decisions are taken by the person doing the work, on the facts. Three levels: Team, AICC Lead, Executive Sponsor. Control Functions decide within their remit, and nobody overrides them.
5. One Decision Log with one line for each Decision. Full notes only for hard-to-reverse Decisions of the Executive Sponsor.
6. Three Risk Tiers instead of four. A Tier 3 release is decided by the Executive Sponsor after validation.
7. Records are files kept by whoever does the work. Five Templates.
8. Ten questions replace the Corpus Assessment and its Readiness Levels.
