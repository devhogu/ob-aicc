# DR-2026-039 AI Incidents are handled in the incident management of the Bank

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Facts

The AI Policy gave AICC its own severity scale, its own reporting path, and its own time limits for AI Incidents. The Bank already has an incident management process in Service Management, which meets the compliance requirements for incidents, and the operation of a Solution is the responsibility of the IT function that operates it. The relevant rules, such as those on ICT-related incidents, on the serious incidents of AI systems, and on personal data breaches, set their own classification and reporting times. The research note `wiki/research/operating-model/incident-regulation.md` records what the sources say.

## Options

1. Keep AICC's own incident process beside the incident management of the Bank.
2. Refer to the incident management of the Bank, and define the part of AICC in it.

## Decision

1. An AI Incident is handled in the incident management of the Bank. AICC sets no severity scale and no time limit of its own.
2. The IT function that operates a Solution handles its incidents. For a trial in AICC and for a Service that AICC runs, the Solution Engineer acts as that function.
3. The AICC Lead is a stakeholder in every AI Incident and is notified of it. The AICC Lead advises on the aspects that concern AI, and may bring the Solution Engineers of the Solution to join the incident.
4. The Control Function Contacts of compliance and data protection decide the notifications to the regulator and to persons, as the law requires.
5. The compliance function confirms which requirements apply to the Bank. The AI Policy holds an open comment for it.

## Revisit

When the compliance function has confirmed the applicable requirements.
