# DR-2026-032 The guides at the level of the charter

*In its own short form, not the Decision Record Template.*

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Decision

1. The guides explain how each workflow is used, by whom, when, and what it leaves on record: Engagement, Service delivery, Cadence, Unit governance, and the Organization. They are static and governing, state no rule of their own, use Roles only, and end each chapter with the clauses they explain.
2. The Organization guide holds the Role profiles, the RACI by Role with one accountable Role for each activity, the governing bodies, the people events (appointment, acting, change, assigned people, competence, access), and the audit and HR reference, which is general practice to be validated with the internal audit and HR of the Bank.
3. The live guidance on development and portfolio management, which changes with the work, is kept in Confluence and in the portfolio management set, not in the charter.

## Revisit

When the internal audit and HR of the Bank have validated the audit and HR reference.
