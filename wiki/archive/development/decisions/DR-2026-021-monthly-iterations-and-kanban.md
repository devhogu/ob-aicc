# DR-2026-021 Monthly Iterations, Kanban lanes, and a year calendar

*Written before the Decision Record Template (DR-2026-029), in its own form.*

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead. The work of AICC is exploratory, and about 60 percent or more of it depends on people and events outside AICC, so tight two-week sprints cannot be kept. The Iteration follows the calendar month, so that anyone can map it without knowing an artificial number.

## Decision

1. An Iteration is one calendar month of four or five whole weeks, named IT01 to IT12, with weeks W1 to W5. A week runs Monday to Sunday and belongs to the month that contains its Thursday. A week is named for example 2026-PIQ4 IT10W1.
2. A Program Increment is a quarter of three Iterations. Its last week of the third Iteration is the Innovation and Planning week, or the week before when it is blocked or gray.
3. The Calendar plans the whole year, with blocked days (official and expected), gray days (common practice), and short days. An event that falls on one moves to the working day before it, never after.
4. The Cadence is a separate Record: the events, when they happen, and how they move. The monthly Steering is held in the Iteration Review week, on a day fixed when the calendars of the people outside AICC are known. There are no approvals of the Calendar or the Cadence.
5. The Program Kanban has lanes: Incident, High priority, and Normal. Work waiting on someone outside AICC is marked with its Dependency. A Weekly Review keeps control of the flow.
6. The items of a Program Increment state intent and direction. The Program Backlog changes continuously. The Dependency Map breaks each item into the scope of each month.
