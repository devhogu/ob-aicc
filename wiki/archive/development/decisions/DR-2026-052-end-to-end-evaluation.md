## 1. Facts

- An auditor reading the pack end to end found the back half stated but not testable (Decision Log, DR-2026-052).
- The reader traced one Solution from intake to retirement and the findings that held were applied.

## 2. Options

1. Leave the back half as stated.
2. Apply the findings that held, lightly: test and release evidence, first users, emergency change, release apart from acceptance, the yearly loops, and the widened controls.

## 3. Decision

1. An independent reader traced one Solution from intake to retirement as an auditor would, and the findings that held were applied, lightly.
2. Every Feature and the MVP are tested by a person other than the builder before deployment. First users are defined. The Acceptance Checklist is the record of every release beyond the first users. A first deployment leaves its change ticket and test reference in the Solution Definition.
3. An emergency change follows the emergency procedure of the change management of the Bank, and the Executive Sponsor reviews it. Release is separate from the acceptance of a Feature. Where the AICC Lead is the Domain Owner, the Executive Sponsor accepts and releases a Solution that the AICC Lead built.
4. The yearly direction and strategic loops run at the quarterly Steering that ends PIQ4, for the next year, and the PI Objectives and the Roadmap that the PI Planning proposes are confirmed at the Steering that ends the IP week.
5. C-09, C-10, C-26, and C-30 are widened, and the Solution Lifecycle Model shows the controls by step. The measures are aligned: one change failure rate, a Release measure, and benefit against the Envelope.

## 4. Conflicts and advice

None recorded.

## 5. Evidence of the Decision

This Record; the Decision Log entry; the change-log rows of the Operating Model 10.9, the Solution Lifecycle Model 2.7, and the Portfolio Management Model 1.7.
