# DR-2026-034 Fixes from the final audit and the walk-through of the journeys

*In its own short form, not the Decision Record Template.*

Date: 2026-10-01. Decided by the AICC Lead.

## Facts

An independent audit of the charter and the registry, and a walk-through of four journeys (a function head, a new joiner, an internal auditor, HR), found the following that needed a decision.

## Decision

1. The Registry record that maps the Roles to people is the Appointments Record, in `registry/appointments.md`, in the form of Template TPL-09 with Parts A to E. The name Assignment Map is retired.
2. The activation of the Business Model and of the twelve Templates, with the Appointments Record, is entered here. The Templates are activated like the documents (Document Catalog 4.1). Decimal revisions are allowed until the first publication of the charter.
3. The state table (Operating Model 6.4) adds Active to Closed for a retired Solution. Rejected means decided against on its merits before approval, and Cancelled means no longer needed in any state from discovery to review.
4. The Checker of a Risk Tier 1 Solution is a designation that the AICC Lead names, not a Role. The check is noted in the AI Registry. The Control Sign-Off covers the validation, a stop, a provider check, and an Exception of a Control Function. An Exception of the AICC Lead is a Decision Record. The Risk Tier is reassessed by the AICC Lead.
5. Until the Guardrails are set, the Executive Sponsor approves any business case (AICC Charter 4.2). The Organization guide and the Initiative Briefs say so.
6. The rules on the content of the tools moved from the collaboration tooling workflow to the Operating Model 9.6. A workflow states no rule.
7. The Registry gains places for Control Sign-Offs, AI Incident Reviews, and Registry Snapshots, and nil statements for AI Incidents, Exceptions, and AI uses listed. The Program Backlog gains an acceptance column, and Features closed before it show an open comment, not an invented acceptance.
8. An Initiative Brief has the six sections of the template from the day it is created, with a Completeness table, and an Initiative is approved only when every section is complete. The seven briefs are written anew on that basis. They carry their current position only, and an open place holds a comment in brackets. After approval a change is entered in the amendments.
9. The templates README gives the order of use, the Engagement guide gains the start of an Engagement, and the Organization guide gains the first week of a Holder.

## Revisit

At the independent re-check after these fixes.
