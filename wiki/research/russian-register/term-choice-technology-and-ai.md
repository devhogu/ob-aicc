# Term choice: technology and AI terms, organizations and products

Scope: sections 5 (Технологические термины и термины AI) and 6 (Организации, продукты и защищённые наименования) of `charter/ru/shared-technology-terminology.md` (rev. 1.5; English edition `charter/en/shared-technology-terminology.md`), plus a sweep of `charter/ru/` for technology, AI, data, security and ITSM vocabulary that the shared terminology does not list. Brief: `BRIEF-term-choice.md`. Owner decisions recorded, not researched: AI → «AI» (Latin, also in compounds «AI-агент», «AI-решение», «платформа AI»); IT → «IT»; business case → «бизнес-кейс»; company and product names keep their form.

Source keys: T-numbers are sources I read for this note (section 3). AP-S and SL-S numbers are sources already read and cited in `ai-policy-and-vocabulary.md` (section 2) and `solution-lifecycle.md` (section 2); I reuse them without re-fetching. "Snippet" means a search-result summary only, not a fetched page; "not fetched" marks well-known usage I did not open. Occurrence counts are for `charter/ru/` excluding the terminology file itself, unless stated.

## 4. Pattern observed

1. Russian practice keeps Latin only for acronyms and product names that have no Cyrillic form: LLM, RAG, DAG, ACL, CI/CD, AWS, Jira; these stay undeclined and attach to Russian through a head noun («архитектура RAG») or a hyphen prefix («LLM-модель», «DAG-файл»).
2. For an English acronym that has a Cyrillic equivalent in official use (ICT → ИКТ, AI → ИИ), regulators and banks use the Cyrillic form; the owner's Latin «AI» and «IT» are a deliberate exception, which Yandex Cloud's «AI‑агент», «агентный AI» shows is also current practice.
3. Compounds with a declinable Russian noun («AI-агент», «ИИ-инциденты», «ML-пайплайн») are how practitioners avoid English plurals; the corpus's undeclined «AI agents» is the one place in my scope that breaks Russian grammar.
4. Security, ITSM, data and governance concepts are Russian everywhere it counts (ГОСТ Р 57580, ГОСТ Р ИСО/МЭК 20000-1, NBKR, Bank of Russia): управление доступом, регистрация событий, инцидент, запрос на изменение, откат, резервное копирование, модельный риск, предвзятость, наблюдаемость.
5. Newer AI engineering words split by register: practitioners write Latin or hybrids (Fallback, Guardrails, LLM-gateway, Data Lineage, промпт-инъекция, пайплайн), while regulators and localized vendor docs use descriptive Russian (резервный вариант, меры защиты, происхождение данных, конвейер); the hybrid wins only where it declines cleanly (промпт-инъекция, пайплайн).
