# Term choice: business, management, delivery and workflow terms

Scope: sections 3 and 4 of `charter/ru/shared-technology-terminology.md` (English edition `charter/en/shared-technology-terminology.md`), plus a sweep of `charter/ru/` for business, portfolio, agile and delivery vocabulary that the shared terminology does not list. Brief: `BRIEF-term-choice.md`. Date: 4 October 2026.

Source keys: R* are sources read for this note (section 3). BP-S* and SL-S* are sources already read for `business-and-portfolio.md` and `solution-lifecycle.md`; they are reused, not re-fetched. "Not fetched" marks well-known usage or a search-result title that I did not open. Counts are occurrences in `charter/ru/` outside the terminology file, from grep.

Owner decisions recorded without research: AI and IT in Latin; business case → «бизнес-кейс»; company and product names (AICC, O!Bank, Jira, Confluence, Atlassian, AWS, CloudWatch) keep their form.

## 4. Pattern

1. Russian practice keeps Latin for acronyms that have no settled Cyrillic form and treats them as indeclinable: KPI, SLA, MVP, OKR, ESG, WSJF, WIP, PI, KYC, RACI (always with a Russian head noun: «матрица RACI», «метод WSJF»).
2. Where a compound is needed, practice builds a hyphenated hybrid with a Russian head noun, never an English plural: «WIP-лимиты», «PI-планирование», «BI-система», «ESG-факторы», «Scrum-команда».
3. Spoken agile vocabulary has settled as Cyrillic borrowings that decline normally: бэклог, спринт, релиз, эпик, дашборд, канбан, код-ревью (and «фича», which is too informal for a regulation).
4. Process concepts with an established standard or interface term are written in Russian: поток создания ценности (ГОСТ Р 57524), рабочий процесс (Jira RU), развёртывание, промышленная среда, пропускная способность, время цикла, анализ первопричин, запрос на изменение, служба поддержки.
5. The corpus breaks this pattern in four places, all multi-word English phrases inside Russian syntax: «business case» (owner has decided «бизнес-кейс»), «WIP limits», «value stream», and «workflow(s)». It also keeps Latin «Epic», although the Russian Jira interface itself writes «Эпик».
