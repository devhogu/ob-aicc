# Service ideas: the work-in-progress collection

Recorded 2026-10-02. The running collection of service ideas for AICC, converged on 2026-10-03 into the fifteen service categories in four areas of the Services section (advise and formulate; build and run; enablement; assurance) and the catalog of packages. The column Line keeps the name at the time of recording; the mapping of the lines to the categories is in the site structure, section 11.6. Three sources: the direction given by the AICC Lead, the additions proposed in the shaping of the lines, and the four concept portals read in [portal-findings.md](portal-findings.md). Each idea names the line it would fall under, its mode, and its status. Status: adopted (on the Services pages), proposed (shaped, not yet on the pages), candidate (from a portal, not yet shaped). The areas and categories and their framing are recorded in the site structure, sections 11 and 11.6.
