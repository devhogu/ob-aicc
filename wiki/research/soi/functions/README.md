## Cross-cutting gaps

- No National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic (NBKR) rule on bank AI use found. Confirm on nbkr.kg.
- Kyrgyz data-localization rules for AI hosting unclear.
- No Kyrgyz or Central Asian bank case studies found for most functions.
- Kyrgyz and Russian model quality unmeasured.
- Kyrgyz advertising, consent, and Digital Code obligations unverified. Needs local counsel.
- O!Bank listing status and ownership unverified. Board-level IR framing assumes the user's statement that the bank is listed and reports to ALGA Group investors.
- Several pages rate maturity from author judgement where no named-bank case exists. Each page says where.
