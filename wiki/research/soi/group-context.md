## Six strategic pillars

From the group's [strategy overview](https://algagroup.com/strategy-overview): "six core pillars to drive financial and
digital inclusion."

| Pillar | Stated content |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Innovative leadership | 5G leadership. AI and big data to "develop a deep understanding of our customers" |
| 2 Financial inclusion | One-stop financial platform integrated with telecom. Loyalty for multi-service use. Digital customer journey to reach the unbanked in remote areas |
| 3 E-commerce | Market leadership by gross merchandise value. Better customer experience. Business partner for merchants |
| 4 Monetize business synergies | Shared technology infrastructure and common digital platforms. Shared big data and resources across business units |
| 5 Shareholder value and dividends | Better profitability through cost and revenue optimization. A dividend policy that "maximizes long-term shareholder value" |
| 6 ESG goals | Community support through connectivity, social initiatives, and responsible lending. "Best governance practices of International Companies" |

## Use in the statement of intent

The statement maps AI adoption to the six pillars and does not restate them as its own. The group's pillars, mission,
and vision are the higher-level goals. AI values in the statement are proposed and should be replaced or confirmed if
the group or the bank publishes its own.
