### CARD 1 [Automation|S] Rating-agency relationship briefings
urn: urn:financial-services:scenario:strategic-portfolio/rating-agency-relationship-briefings
intent: Synthesized posture briefings on demand for rating-agency interactions. Continuous-form posture summary gives agency leads a coherent current-state view without days of manual reconstruction.
Problem to solve: Each rating-agency interaction requires a fresh, hand-assembled briefing; agency-relationship leads spend days reconciling current posture with prior commentary.
Solution: Agent maintains a rating-agency-facing posture summary in continuous form; briefings produced on demand with traceable links to source data. Agency leads work from the continuous-form posture summary.
OKR objective: Rating-agency-facing posture briefings are available on demand from a continuously maintained posture summary — with traceable source links — giving agency-relationship leads a coherent, current-state view without days of manual reconstruction.
OKR KR [Adoption]: Agent maintains a continuous-form rating-agency posture summary updated within 48 hours of material data changes; briefings produced on demand for ≥100% of rating-agency interactions.
OKR KR [Acceptance]: ≥85% of on-demand briefings accepted by relationship leads without material amendment; source-link accuracy confirmed in ≥95% of briefings.
OKR KR [Cycle]: Per-briefing preparation time reduced from days of manual assembly to ≤30 minutes of relationship lead review.

OKR KR [Cycle]: Quarterly board-pack assembly time reduced by ≥50% because the rolling narrative serves as a current baseline rather than requiring a multi-week rebuild.
