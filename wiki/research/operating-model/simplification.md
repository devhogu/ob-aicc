# Simplification of the corpus

On 2026-09-30 the first corpus was cut to six documents (DR-2026-009).

## Why

The first corpus had 25 documents, 13 Roles, 8 Forums, 47 decision rows, 7 decision categories, 7 Registers, and 23 Templates. The AICC is a few engineers who work in an agile way. The independent assessment of the first corpus found 72 findings, most of them about the weight of the process: secretaries, quorums, readiness levels, and duplicated rules. An agile team decides where the facts are and keeps only the records that someone uses.

## What was removed

| Removed | Replaced by |
| --- | --- |
| Roles and Responsibilities, Decision Rights, Governance Forums, Decision Management, Portfolio Management, Registers, Reporting, Service Catalog, Artifact Standards | The Operating Model |
| Funding Model, Metrics, Evolution Plan, Enablement Plan | Sections of the AICC Charter |
| AI Use, Risk Tier, Third-Party AI, AI Incident policies; Data Classification Policy | The AI Policy, and the data classification rules of the Bank |
| Corpus Assessment with Readiness Levels and 81 checks | Ten questions in the Document Catalog |
| Register of Appointments | The Appointments Record |
| 23 Templates | Five Templates |

## Basis

Decide at the lowest competent level, keep decisions reversible and fast, limit work in progress, and record only what others must find later. The bank-specific part that stays is the independence of the Control Functions, the Risk Tiers, the AI Registry, and the reporting to the Board Committee. The first versions are in the archive.
