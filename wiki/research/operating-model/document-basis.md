# Document basis: Vocabulary and Style, Corpus Assessment, Register of Appointments, Document Catalog, Reporting, Data Classification Policy, Decision Rights

> This page describes the lineage of the first corpus, now archived. See [Simplification](simplification.md) for what replaced it.

Where seven documents of the [charter folder](../../../charter/README.md) come from. The charter documents carry no
references. This page keeps the lineage of the documents that the other basis pages
([operating model basis](basis.md), [scaffolding basis](scaffolding-basis.md), [agile mapping](agile-mapping.md)) do not
cover. It restates what those pages and the documents already say. Where a document has no external basis, the page says so.

| Document | Basis |
| --- | --- |
| Vocabulary and Style | External basis: none by design. The defined terms replace scaled agile and PMO names with business names; the mapping is in the [agile mapping](agile-mapping.md), and the excluded names are listed in the "Not used" column. The style (numbered clauses, "shall", third person, American spelling, no references in defining documents) is our own drafting rule, made to keep the defining documents stable and checkable. |
| Corpus Assessment | External basis: none by design. The Readiness Levels, the ten document criteria, the corpus criteria, the scoring, and the complexity budget are our own design. Its proportionality test follows the decision that a unit run by one person keeps only the constructs that one person can run (see the [scaffolding basis](scaffolding-basis.md)). The independent Assessment by a person other than the author follows the independence expected of validation in the three-lines model (see the [operating model basis](basis.md)). |
| Register of Appointments | Our own drafting rule, to keep names out of foundational documents: the Operating Model, the Roles and Responsibilities, and the Decision Rights name Roles, and this Register alone names persons. The split between Roles, Positions, and named people is kept from the first version of the operating model (see the [agile mapping](agile-mapping.md)). No external source. |
| Document Catalog | Versioning and the review interval follow policy and charter template practice ([Pertama Partners](https://www.pertamapartners.com/insights/ai-governance-charter-template-documenting-your-approach)), which finds that Board approval of policies gives the strongest mandate; this is reflected in the activation of the documents that bind persons outside AICC by the Executive Sponsor. Keeping the documents in a repository like software documentation, and the light lifecycle in which activation replaces approval, are our own decisions. The identifier scheme is our own design. |
| Reporting | The Quarterly Report follows the cadence of the Quarterly Planning and Review Event and the scoring of value achieved, taken from the program events of scaled agile (see the [scaffolding basis](scaffolding-basis.md)). The content of the report to the Board Committee follows the contents that AI center-of-excellence sources list (metrics and executive sponsorship; see the [operating model basis](basis.md)) and the performance and reporting section of the public-sector form of a statement of intent (see the [Statement of Intent form](../soi/soi-form.md)). The intervals and the owner are our own proposals. |
| Data Classification Policy | External basis: none at this revision. The document is a stub that records its intent. It is to be written from the existing classification of the Bank, which is not held in this repository, and it has to respect the separation of data between Entities and the sharing of data only under a Group Arrangement (see the [agile mapping](agile-mapping.md)). |
| Decision Rights (allocation) | The notation of responsible, accountable, consulted, and informed is the common RACI notation. The rule of exactly one accountable Role in each row is kept from the first version of the operating model, which had more rows; the rows were reduced to one decision for each Stage gate (see the [agile mapping](agile-mapping.md)). The columns are Roles, not Positions, as the Register of Appointments requires. That a charter states its decision rights follows the AI center-of-excellence sources (see the [operating model basis](basis.md)). The independence of each Control Function remit follows the three-lines model. The allocation of each row is our own drafting; the open points are in the [decision rights open items](../../archive/decision-rights-open-items.md). |
