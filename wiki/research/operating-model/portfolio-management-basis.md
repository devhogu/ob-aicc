# Portfolio management: the basis of the Portfolio Management Model

Research note of 2026-10-01. It records what the sources say and how the model uses them. The pages of the Scaled Agile Framework that describe the portfolio Kanban, the epic, and the strategic themes are behind a login, so the states and the contents of the lean business case below come from secondary sources and must be checked against the framework before they are cited as its words.

## Lean Portfolio Management (Scaled Agile Framework)

- It applies lean and systems thinking to three areas: strategy and investment funding, agile portfolio operations, and governance. It funds long-lived priorities and decides in small steps.
- Strategic themes are portfolio-level business objectives that connect the enterprise strategy to the portfolio and keep decisions tied to it. They are best written as objectives with key results.
- An epic is a significant solution development initiative. It needs a defined minimum viable product, a lean business case, and a cost estimate, and it must be approved by portfolio leadership.
- The portfolio Kanban has six states. Funnel: anyone proposes an epic hypothesis. Reviewing: leadership checks fit with the strategic themes. Analyzing: the epic owner writes the lean business case, defines the MVP, and estimates the cost, and leadership decides go or no-go. Portfolio Backlog: approved epics wait for capacity, ranked by weighted shortest job first. Implementing: the MVP is built to test the hypothesis, then the epic persevere, pivots, or stops. Done: portfolio oversight is no longer needed.
- The lean business case covers the epic hypothesis statement, the business outcomes, the leading indicators, the MVP, the cost estimate within the guardrails, and the weighted shortest job first score.
- Business Owners and enterprise architecture review value, technical fit, and compliance. The core of the portfolio function sets the policies, the limits on work in progress, and the prioritization criteria, and decides go or no-go.

## Other disciplines

- ISO 21504: portfolio management is a continuous decision process that aligns the components with the strategic objectives, with defined and verifiable criteria for selecting and prioritizing, explicit authority and accountability, balancing of resources and risk, and regular reporting.
- Standard for Portfolio Management (PMI): components are categorized, evaluated, selected, prioritized, and balanced; a governance board of executives sets the criteria and reviews continuance, funding changes, suspension, or termination.
- Five case model for business cases (HM Treasury Green Book): the strategic, economic, commercial, financial, and management cases answer why change is needed, which option is best, whether it can be bought, whether it can be afforded, and whether it can be delivered.

## How the model uses them

The funnel, the review, the analysis, the portfolio backlog, the MVP, and the decision to persevere, pivot, or stop follow the portfolio Kanban. The business case is one page, as in the lean business case, and its criteria follow the five questions of the five case model. The ranking is the weighted shortest job first method, which matches the scores that AICC already keeps. Authority and accountability follow ISO 21504 through the Roles of the Operating Model. A stop is Cancelled and a pivot is Pivoted, because Rejected applies only before approval in the states of the Solution Lifecycle Model.
