# Consulting practices borrowed

What AICC takes, lightly, from the way consulting firms engage with clients. This page is lineage. The Business Model and the Engagement workflow hold the rules.

| Consulting practice | Taken as | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hypothesis first | The hypothesis statement of the Initiative Brief (SAFe lean business case) | Initiative Brief |
| Statement of work, with assumptions and out of scope | The Service Agreement | Business Model 5 |
| Engagement letter and working terms | The working agreement, the second part of the Service Agreement | Business Model 5.2 |
| Closing deliverable with an executive summary | The Outcome Report | Business Model 5.5 |
| Lessons learned and reusable assets | The Portfolio keeps them | Business Model 4.4 |
| The client relationship and the account view | The Agreement Log, by function | Business Model 5.5 |
| Managed service after delivery | The support level | Business Model 4.2 |

Left out: the staffing pyramid, billing and utilization targets, partner reviews, and heavy approvals. They would add weight that a very small unit can't carry.
