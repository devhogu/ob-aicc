# Charter scaffolding: basis

> This page describes the lineage of the first corpus, now archived. See [Simplification](simplification.md) for what replaced it.

Where the forums, decision constructs, records, and templates of the charter folder come from. The charter documents carry
no references. Gathered 2026-09-30.

## What comes from where

| Element | Basis |
| --- | --- |
| Portfolio events: strategic review, portfolio sync, budgeting | SAFe Lean Portfolio Management has a cadence of Strategic Portfolio Review, Portfolio Sync, and Participatory Budgeting (renamed Strategic Investment Planning). AICC folds these into the AI Steering Committee and the yearly cycle, to stay small ([Ivar Jacobson](https://www.ivarjacobson.com/publications/blog/lean-agile/safe/portfolio-events-structuring-events), [Portfolio Sync](https://agility-at-scale.com/safe/lpm/portfolio-sync/), [LPM guide](https://www.ppm.express/blog/safe-lean-portfolio-management)) |
| Portfolio artifacts: portfolio backlog, lean business case, guardrails, portfolio Kanban | Portfolio Backlog, Initiative Brief, Investment Guardrails, and the Portfolio Flow ([Portfolio Kanban](https://agility-at-scale.com/safe/lpm/portfolio-kanban/), [Lean budget guardrails](https://agility-at-scale.com/safe/lpm/portfolio-guardrails/)) |
| Program events: PI planning, ART sync, PO sync, system demo, inspect and adapt | Quarterly Planning and Review Event, Replenishment, Delivery Review, and the improvement items of the review. AICC drops the separate syncs at its size ([Agile release train](https://agility-at-scale.com/safe/team-technical-agility/agile-release-train/), [ART guide](https://axify.io/blog/agile-release-train)) |
| Program artifacts: program backlog, team backlog, PI objectives, program board | Delivery Backlog, Work Items on the team board, Objectives with a confidence rating, Roster. The program board of dependencies is deferred to the Evolution Plan |
| Decision Record | Architecture decision records and DACI: short records, single decider, states Proposed, Decided, Superseded, and immutability once decided ([ADR templates](https://hidekazu-konishi.com/entry/architecture_decision_records_templates_and_operations.html), [DACI](https://umbrex.com/resources/frameworks/organization-frameworks/daci-decision-making-framework/), [decision log practice](https://kiolo.com/en/blog/decision-log-template/)) |
| Committee rules: quorum, decision rule, signatory | DACI guidance to write quorum, voting rule, and signatory into the terms of reference |
| Service Portfolio, Service Catalog | ITIL: pipeline, catalog, and retired services ([BMC](https://www.bmc.com/blogs/service-catalog-vs-service-portfolio-whats-the-difference/), [InvGate](https://blog.invgate.com/service-portfolio)) |
| AI Registry fields | Public-sector AI register templates and use case templates ([Australian Government AI register template](https://www.industry.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-10/ai_register_template.pdf), [CIOB template](https://www.ciob.org/media/2636/download)) |
| Risk Tiers | Tiering by materiality across frameworks ([responsible AI alignment](responsible-ai/README.md)). The four tiers and their requirements are our own design |
| Chair, secretary, quorum, cadence, response times, tier requirements, incident timings | Our own proposals |

## Confidence and gaps

- SAFe sources are secondary summaries. The portfolio roadmap and the program board were not covered in detail.
- No committee terms-of-reference or minutes template was found. The forum rules are our own composition.
- No bank-specific AI register template was found.
