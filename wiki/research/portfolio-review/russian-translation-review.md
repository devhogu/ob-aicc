# Russian edition of the "Портфель инициатив" section: translation review

Scope: rendered pages `ru/initiatives/` (dashboard with the Доска, Очередь на рассмотрение, Постоянные инициативы and Дорожная карта tabs; `register/`; `selection/`; `ini-002` to `ini-012`). Checked against `charter/ru/translation-en-ru-map.md` v0.18 (sections 3–4), `charter/ru/documents/vocabulary.md`, `charter/ru/shared-technology-terminology.md`, `portfolio-management-model.md` (PMM) and `solution-lifecycle-model.md` (SLM).

Where to fix: **UI** means the `UI['ru']` dict in `portal/tools/portfolio.py` (lines 250–280). **CODE** means rendering logic in `portfolio.py`, which affects both editions. **REG** means a Russian Registry record under `registry/ru/…`, with the file named. The `selection/` page takes its gate table straight from the corpus (`_gates`), so its gate rows already match PMM 5.2. Most of the defects below come from UI strings and from the RU `Состояние и стадия` brief field.
