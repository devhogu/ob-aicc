# Portfolio section: logic review against the AICC corpus

Scope: the English Portfolio section (dashboard, Review and decisions, Initiative register, INI-002 to INI-012 pages) as rendered on 2026-10-06 from commit e2247ed2, the renderer `portal/tools/portfolio.py`, the Registry records it reads, and the corpus (PMM = Portfolio Management Model 2.2, SLM = Solution Lifecycle Model 2.1, BM = Business Model 2.1, OM = Operating Model, Vocabulary, Cadence workflow, Collaboration tooling). Read-only; nothing was changed. The Russian edition was not reviewed.

## Verdict

The board skeleton is faithful: the seven columns, the state/stage mapping, the gate names and deciders of PMM 5.2, Waiting keeping its column, Standing Initiatives outside the ordinary limit, and MVP and Implementation sharing one Active limit all match the corpus. The renderer also keeps names, Bank figures and milestone owners off the pages.

The problems are in what the board implies. The Roadmap and PI Objectives promise six outcomes in PIQ4, but the Briefs gate all of that work behind approval and a pull, and the limit is one Active Initiative. The Briefs also put the discovery work inside the MVP. Each page shows the Approval-gate gaps while the next gate is Scoped, and the real blocker (no Domain Owner) goes unflagged. The ranking departs from WSJF with no substantive recorded reason. INI-002's owner is not the corpus owner for enabling work. The Standing Initiatives are shown as Approved, but the approval was not recorded as the Executive Sponsor's. The measures panel contradicts figures shown elsewhere on the same page. Most fixes belong in the Registry; the rest are label and logic changes in `portfolio.py`.

## Answers to the seven questions

1. **Columns, states, gates.** The mapping matches PMM 5.1–5.2, Figure 6, SLM 4.2 (Portfolio Kanban row) and SLM 5.1: Funnel=Proposed, Reviewing=Discovery: Scoping, Analyzing=Discovery: Business case, Portfolio Backlog=Approved, MVP/Implementation=Active stages, Done=Review/Accepted/Closed, and Deferred/Rejected/Pivoted/Cancelled off-flow. Waiting is placed in the column of its underlying state, as SLM 4.2 requires. Placing Completed under Implementation is a defensible analogy with the Program Kanban (SLM 4.2: "Active is Active and Completed"), but the corpus is silent on it for Initiatives, and the renderer then does not count it against the limit (finding M2). The gate names and deciders come verbatim from the PMM 5.2 table, so they are right. The dashboard column captions "MVP decision" and "Delivery" are not corpus gates (M3). The Scoped decider, "AICC Lead with the Domain Owner", cannot act for four Initiatives that have no Domain Owner (W3). INI-002's "Domain Owner" should be the Executive Sponsor (W5).
2. **WIP and capacity.** "Ordinary Active 0 / 1" matches the PMM 9.2 Work-in-progress measure and PMM 5.4. Standing Initiatives are excluded (PMM 5.4, 9.2; BM 4.9), and MVP and Implementation share the limit (both are Active). The Feature and Capability limits match DR-2026-063 and board.md. Gaps: Completed Initiatives are not counted (M2). Waiting-from-Approved Features are not counted in Ready (latent). "Capacity" mislabels WIP (Vocabulary, Limit on Work in Progress), and "Ready 0/2" reads as headroom where SLM 10.3 "Ready ahead" reads 0 as a shortfall (M5). The limits were set by the "baseline owner", not by the AICC Lead within the Executive Sponsor's mix (PMM 5.4; see W6).
3. **Ranking.** No. PMM 6.5 ranks the Portfolio Backlog by WSJF. The Domain Owner (or the Executive Sponsor for enabling work) states the value, and the AICC Lead scores the rest, ranks, and may depart "for a stated reason, and records it" (also PMM 3.1, 4.4). DR-2026-061 confirms the 100-day goals but sets no order, so "order follows the goals of the first 100 days" is not a substantive reason (W4). The AICC Lead owns the ranking; the page does not say so.
4. **Roadmap and cadence.** The labels are correct. "2026-PIQ4 I10W1" follows Calendar rule 3 and the Cadence workflow; the PI dates and IP week match calendar.md; the three horizons match SLM 4.4 and the Vocabulary; the quarterly Steering of 2026-12-23 matches ip-week.md. The content is not consistent with the gates and the limit (W1). In light mode the monthly Steering is held inside the Iteration Review and Demo (SLM 6.6), and the cadence list does not say so. No event dates are shown (G6).
5. **Audience and exposure.** No Bank figures, data or personal names appear in the section; the renderer strips milestone owners, Brief holders and Priority owners. OM 7.8 is respected. However, the corpus places "the progress and the Measures of the Dashboard" on the Operating portal (Collaboration tooling 2; OM 7.6). The AICC portal is meant to explain the charter and to link to Registry evidence on the corporate share (OM 7.4, 7.8; Collaboration tooling 3), but the record references are unlinked repository paths (M9). For the Board, the views that matter are missing: the mix by priority, the Envelope, decisions due and their dates. For Domain Owners, what is needed from them is not shown. For Bank managers, there is no way to propose a need (G-items). A site-wide note: the footer uses a personal mailbox rather than the mailbox of AICC (OM 7.8), outside this section.
6. **Gaps.** These are listed under Missing (G1–G9) and "Shown but not defined" (N).
7. **Projects consistency.** The Projects path matches the portfolio columns, and release sits inside Implementation (SLM 7.1, 8.1). However, Projects calls its pilot a "Proposed initiative … to be entered in the Portfolio Backlog", while the Portfolio shows the Funnel empty and no link to INI-006 (P1). The renderer would also refuse to build with the pilot's owner role.

## Where the corpus is silent or contradictory (corpus change justified only here)

- **C1.** The column of a Completed Initiative, and whether it counts against the Active limit (PMM 5.2 table and Figure 6 versus SLM 4.2 for the Program Kanban). Suggest one sentence in PMM 5.2.
- **C2.** Whether the Active limit gates the start of a study and its Service Agreement. BM 5.1 says the Service Agreement is "issued when the study starts". BM 7.1 says "no Service Agreement for an Initiative that the limit does not allow". The limit counts Active Initiatives only (PMM 5.4, 9.2), yet the PMM 5.2 Reviewing exit requires "permitted by the limit". This is what makes the six "Not issued (RI-004)" entries ambiguous.
- **C3.** What the MVP is for enabling work that builds no Solution (PMM 5.3, 7.1 versus 8.2).
- **C4.** Deferred: "stays in the funnel" (PMM 5.2, in practice) versus off the flow (PMM Figure 5; SLM 4.2) versus "returned to its backlog" (Vocabulary).
- **C5.** Waiting for Initiatives: "of an Active Initiative" (note under PMM Figure 6) versus Discovery and Approved → Waiting (SLM 5.1).
- **C6.** The off-flow list in the SLM 4.2 board table omits Cancelled, which PMM 5.1 and 5.2 allow.
- **C7.** The PMM 5.2 Implementation row has "Gate: None" but "Decided by: … accepts".

The placement of progress views on the AICC portal rather than the Operating portal (M9) is not a corpus gap. The corpus is explicit, so that is an owner decision about the site.

## What is right and should be kept

- The seven columns, the off-flow set, and the state/stage mapping (PMM 5.1–5.2; SLM 4.2, 5.1).
- Waiting keeps its column and its capacity place, and must name a Dependency (SLM 4.2).
- MVP and Implementation share one Active limit, with Standing Initiatives excluded from the limit and from the flow counts (PMM 5.4, 9.2; BM 4.9).
- The Standing Initiatives' admission rule and decider (BM 4.8; SLM 3.1, 5.2) and their quarterly review on 2026-12-23 (BM 4.9; ip-week.md).
- Gate names, criteria and deciders read directly from the PMM 5.2 table, so they cannot drift.
- An expected Risk Tier is not shown as assigned; a goal confirmation (DR-2026-061) is not shown as approval of the business case.
- No Bank figures, no data and no person names; milestone and Priority owners are stripped (OM 7.8; PMM 6.1).
- WSJF is computed correctly (PMM 6.5).
