## Budget

The Document Catalog caps the documents at nine and about 80 clauses each. Current clause counts: Statement of Intent 50, Operating Model 44, Solution Lifecycle Model 41, Portfolio Management Model 31, Business Model 26, AI Policy 24, AICC Charter 17, Document Catalog 17, Vocabulary 12. The SLM takes the most new clauses (about 40 proposed); D3–D5 should be written as tables under few clauses, or the measures move to one measures table per model, to stay within the cap.

## Method

Each document change takes a change-log row and a Decision Log entry; a change of meaning takes the next whole revision (2.0). Templates are activated by a Decision Log entry. Records change in place. The site is regenerated from the corpus after each document change, and its authored pages are revised to cite the new clauses.
