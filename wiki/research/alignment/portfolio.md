### A.2 Contradictions

- C1 Who accepts. strategy-and-investment 3.1 and table 4: Executive Sponsor "above a guardrail". PMM 5.2 Done, 9.3, Vocabulary, SLM 7.3(c): the Domain Owner, or the Executive Sponsor for items that span Domains, enabling work, or an Experiment with no Domain. No guardrail test for acceptance in the corpus.
- C2 Exceeding the limit. definitions 5 "Limit adherence": "an excess is a decision of the Steering, recorded". PMM 4.4 and BM 7.1 allow no excess.
- C3 Baseline timing. measures-and-tracking 1.2: baseline before the MVP or the MVP's first job. PMM 6.2 and 5.2 Analyzing: baseline needed before approval.
- C4 Mapping to the OM loops. the-loops-and-governance 4.1: the portfolio loops are the unit loops. OM 6.1: the loops share events; OM 6.5 keeps the direction loop separate.
- C5 Decider in the portfolio review: Executive Sponsor (site) vs the approver of 6.3 (PMM 4.3). Tension already inside the PMM (4.1 vs 4.3).
- C6 Lead time. Site extends it to proposal→approval; SLM 10.3 Approved→Accepted; PMM 9.2 separate; the site's own definitions page calls it "Time to decision".
- C7 Standing Initiatives vs PMM 6.1, Charter 4.2 (every Initiative has a brief), PMM 5.1 (no permanent state), PMM 1.2 (a new feature of an existing service is not an Initiative).
