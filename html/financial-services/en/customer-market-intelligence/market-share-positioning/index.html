Market share & competitive positioning

Market share and competitive positioning tracks the bank's relative standing — by product, segment, and geography — against the set of institutions and non-bank providers competing for the same customer relationships. It covers both the quantitative dimension (deposit market share, loan portfolio rank, card-spend volume position) and the qualitative dimension (where the bank is differentiated and where it is undifferentiated in the eyes of target segments). In KZ, KG, and RU markets, competitive positioning is shaped by the dominant state-bank incumbency, the rising digital-challenger presence, and the cross-border payment competitive dynamic. GenAI enables continuous competitive-signal monitoring — synthesising public filings, regulatory data, and news signals into positioning intelligence that historically required weeks of analyst time.

Market share by product

The bank's share of each product market — retail deposits, consumer credit, mortgages, SME lending, corporate facilities, card-spend volume — measured against total market size from regulator and industry-association data. Product-level market share is the primary output of competitive positioning analysis: it reveals where the bank is gaining, stable, or ceding ground to peers and non-bank alternatives. In Kazakhstan, NBK publishes monthly banking-sector statistics with a six-to-eight-week lag; in Russia, CBR quarterly reports are the primary source.

Peer product benchmarking

Systematic comparison of the bank's product features, pricing tiers, and service terms against the peer set — the input that informs product-refresh decisions and reveals where the bank has feature gaps or pricing vulnerabilities. Peer benchmarking must cover the full competitive set: state-bank incumbents, private domestic peers, foreign-bank subsidiaries, and digital challengers. In KZ and KG markets, the digital-challenger set now includes several mobile-only neobanks with pricing structures that undercut traditional banks on fee transparency.

Market share by segment

Market share disaggregated by customer segment — retail mass, emerging affluent, SME micro, SME core, corporate — revealing where the bank holds a defensible position and where it is underweight relative to its strategic priorities. Segment market share is the harder measurement: regulatory data is product-level, not segment-level, so segment share requires combining regulatory data with customer-research estimates and internal portfolio composition. Strategy teams rely on annual commissioned research for segment-share estimates in the absence of an automated assembly process.

Geographic market share

Market share at the regional and city level — essential for distribution-investment decisions, branch-network prioritisation, and geographic-expansion sequencing. Geographic market share is particularly relevant in KZ and KG, where banking penetration is concentrated in Almaty and Bishkek and regional markets are significantly underserved, creating a material growth opportunity for the first bank to invest in regional digital infrastructure. NBKR and NBK publish regional banking statistics as part of their standard sector reporting.
