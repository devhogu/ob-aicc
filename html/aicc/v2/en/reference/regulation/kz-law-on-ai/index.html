Law “On Artificial Intelligence”

← All regulators and actsKazakhstanLaw and national conceptreference point

Briefly

The Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan “On Artificial Intelligence” (Закон «Об искусственном интеллекте»), No. 230-VIII, was signed by the President on 17 November 2025 and came into force on 18 January 2026. It is the first dedicated AI law in Central Asia: it sorts AI systems by level of risk, bans certain dangerous uses, and requires AI-generated content to be labeled. The law is binding in Kazakhstan; it does not apply to us in Kyrgyzstan directly, but it serves as a benchmark. Alongside the law there is a government program, the Concept for the Development of Artificial Intelligence for 2024–2029.

What it means in practice

For an organization in Kyrgyzstan, this is first of all a neighboring country’s example. Our own rules, the Digital Code and the requirements for high-hazard AI systems, are also built on sorting systems by risk. Kazakhstan’s law helps us check whether we set our risk tiers correctly and whether we are missing something our neighbors already treat as mandatory.

It becomes a direct requirement when we work with Kazakh partners and suppliers. If a partner offers an AI solution that serves customers in Kazakhstan, it must comply with this law: for example, it must label AI-generated images and texts and tell people when an automated system makes a decision. It is worth asking the partner about this in advance.

For an employee who uses AI at work, the essentials match our own rules: don’t give AI personal data in breach of the law, mark what AI created if it goes outside, and remember that a person is responsible for the result.

Key ideas

  • Three levels of risk. AI systems are divided into minimal-, medium-, and high-risk systems. The higher the risk, the stricter the requirements. This is the risk-based approach, as in the EU AI Act and in Kyrgyzstan’s rules.
  • Banned uses. You may not create or use AI systems that manipulate people through subliminal techniques, exploit their vulnerabilities (age, disability), give people social scores based on their behavior, detect emotions without consent, or discriminate, nor systems that process personal data in breach of the law.
  • Labeling AI-generated content. Synthetic images, video, audio, and texts may be distributed only if they are labeled in a machine-readable form and the person sees a clear warning.
  • The right to know. People must be informed about automated processing; they can ask for information about a decision made by AI and demand that a mistake be corrected.
  • The owner’s responsibility. Owners of AI systems manage risk continuously and keep technical documentation in proportion to the risk; requirements are stricter for high-risk systems.
  • Authorship stays with people. A work created with AI is protected by copyright only if it contains a person’s creative contribution.

Good to know

  • Signed on 17 November 2025, in force since 18 January 2026.
  • The authorized body is the Ministry of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Development of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
  • It applies in Kazakhstan: to government bodies, companies, and individuals that create and use AI systems.
  • The Concept for the Development of Artificial Intelligence for 2024–2029 was adopted by the government in July 2024; it is a policy program, not a law.
  • We don’t cover the secondary legislation under the law here; for the exact requirements and the current version, see the Adilet portal.
