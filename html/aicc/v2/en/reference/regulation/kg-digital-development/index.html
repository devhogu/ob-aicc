Digital development

← All regulators and actsKyrgyz RepublicMinistry, acts, and programsapplies to us

Briefly

State policy in the digital sphere is run by the Ministry of Digital Development and Innovative Technologies of the Kyrgyz Republic. The course is set by the Concept of Digital Transformation for 2024–2028, approved by presidential decree, and by the Digital Code. AI development is coordinated by the National Council for the Development of Artificial Intelligence. Programs and concepts set the direction; binding rules appear in the laws and resolutions that grow out of them.

What it means in practice

For the organization. This is where you can see where AI regulation in the country is heading. The ministry prepares new rules, for example the draft regulation on AI ethics, which was put out for public consultation in September 2026. Drafts are published before adoption, and during that time organizations can respond to them. Once a draft is adopted, its requirements become binding, and AI solutions have to be checked against them.

Working with the state. The Digital Code sets the rules for digital identification and data exchange with government systems. If an AI solution takes part in know your customer (KYC) checks or connects to government systems, it must follow these rules.

For employees. News about a draft is not yet a rule: until a draft is adopted, it does not apply. Work by the rules in force and only with approved tools.

Key ideas

  • Digital transformation. Moving the state and the economy to digital services and data exchange; the Concept for 2024–2028 sets its goals and framework.
  • National Council on AI. An advisory body that brings together government, business, science, and society on AI development. It is chaired by the Chairman of the Cabinet of Ministers, and the authorized body for digital development runs its secretariat.
  • AI ethics (draft). According to reports on the draft, it proposes labeling AI-generated content; making clear that developers, owners, and users, not the system itself, answer for AI's errors and harm; giving people the right to know when they are dealing with AI; and assessing the ethics of government and hazardous systems before launch.
  • Digital identification. Confirming a person's identity in the digital environment; its general rules are set by the Digital Code.
  • Interaction between systems. How owners of digital systems exchange data with each other and with government bodies is set by a separate chapter of the Digital Code.

Good to know

  • The Concept of Digital Transformation of the Kyrgyz Republic for 2024–2028 was approved by Presidential Decree No. 90 of 5 April 2024.
  • Presidential Decree No. 91 of 17 March 2025 gave the ministry its current name, the Ministry of Digital Development and Innovative Technologies.
  • The National Council for the Development of Artificial Intelligence was set up by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 29 of 22 January 2025.
  • The regulation on AI ethics is still a draft: as of 28 September 2026 it had been put out for public consultation. According to reports, it will not apply to defense, national security, or law enforcement. We could not confirm the final text or an adoption date.
  • The requirements for increased-hazard AI systems are already in force: they are set by the Digital Code and Resolution No. 770.
