National AI development strategy and experimental legal regimes

← All regulators and actsRussian FederationPresidential decree and federal lawsreference point

Briefly

The National Strategy for the Development of Artificial Intelligence for the Period until 2030 (Национальная стратегия развития искусственного интеллекта на период до 2030 года) was approved by Russian Presidential Decree No. 490 of 10 October 2019 and updated on 15 February 2024. It is a strategic planning document: goals, principles, and targets that federal government bodies must follow; for business it is a reference point. Alongside it is a 2020 law on experimental legal regimes, which allows new technologies, including AI, to be tested under temporary exemptions from the general rules. Since 1 September 2026 the first federal AI law has also been in force: Federal Law No. 243-FZ on supporting the development of artificial intelligence technologies («О поддержке развития технологий искусственного интеллекта в Российской Федерации»).

What it means in practice

For us this is a reference point, not binding rules. It is useful in three cases.

The first is choosing suppliers. The 2026 law introduces sovereign and national statuses for Russian models: their developers are Russian companies, and the data is stored in Russia. The Government may define areas where only such models are allowed. When a Russian partner offers an AI solution, ask which model it runs on, where the data is processed, and which requirements apply to it.

The second is how to set up an experiment. An experimental legal regime is a good example of a regulated pilot: an approved program, a limited term, a known set of participants, a record of who uses the solution, and insurance and review of cases of harm. Our own pilots are worth building the same way: a proof of concept with a clear term, participants, and criteria for stopping.

The third is comparing goals. The strategy sets the goal that by 2030 at least 80% of workers in Russia should know how to use AI. This is a useful reference point for our AI literacy programs.

Key ideas

  • The strategy’s principles. Protection of human rights and freedoms, safety, including the confidentiality of personal data, transparency and explainability of how AI works, technological sovereignty, and others.
  • Targets for 2030. Among them: the share of workers with skills in using AI at no less than 80% (5% in 2022), and citizens’ level of trust in AI at no less than 80% (55% in 2022).
  • Experimental legal regime. A temporary right not to apply certain rules in order to test a new technology under an approved program. The regime is established by the Government or the Bank of Russia, for no more than five years.
  • Responsibility in an experiment. A participant in a regime keeps a record of those who use AI solutions and of their insurance; cases of harm from AI solutions are reviewed by a special commission. In essence, this is work with AI incidents.
  • Sovereign and national models. The 2026 law covers large foundation models. It introduces two statuses for such models; only Russian companies may develop them, and the data must be stored in Russia. The law rests on a risk-based approach.
  • Labeling. The law provides for labeling material created with the help of AI.

Good to know

  • The strategy: Presidential Decree No. 490 of 10 October 2019, with a new version in Decree No. 124 of 15 February 2024. Its horizon is 2030.
  • Experimental regimes: Federal Law No. 258-FZ of 31 July 2020. A regime lasts no more than five years.
  • The AI law: Federal Law No. 243-FZ of 26 July 2026. It came into force on 1 September 2026; the obligations of developers of sovereign and national models, labeling, and the rules on using other people’s works come into force on 1 March 2027.
  • Many details of the AI law will be set by Government resolutions that are still being prepared; as of August 2026, no specific penalties for violations had been set.
  • These are Russian documents: they are binding in Russia, and for us they serve as a reference point.
