Learning and research resources

Open resources worth knowing: where to learn, where to follow the industry, what financial regulators read, and where to find information on attacks and incidents. External services are for reading only: we never send work data to them.

Showing: 9

Learn

Learn

DeepLearning.AI

Free short courses, an hour or two each, on prompting, working with sources, evaluation, and agents, plus The Batch, a weekly newsletter.

Why. A quick way to learn the methods we use to build solutions.

Learn

Hugging Face

Open models and datasets with cards and licenses, documentation, and a free course.

Why. This is where open models are compared; the model card is the first thing to read before choosing one.

Follow the industry

Follow the industry

arXiv and Hugging Face Papers

Preprints of AI research and a daily selection of papers with links to code; it replaced Papers with Code, which closed in 2025.

Why. For checking a vendor’s claim: what the paper actually says and whether the result holds up.

Follow the industry

Stanford HAI — AI Index

An annual report with data on AI research, models, costs, adoption, and regulation.

Why. A reliable source of figures on AI for reports and proposals.

Follow the industry

McKinsey and QuantumBlack

An annual survey of the state of AI and estimates of the value of generative AI by function.

Why. Leaders often cite these surveys; the estimates are a first approximation, not a promise.

Financial sector

Financial sector

BIS and its Innovation Hub

Research that central banks read, and projects the Innovation Hub carries out together with them.

Why. What the BIS writes about today, regulators eventually ask about.

Financial sector

IMF and World Bank

Analysis of digital finance, financial inclusion, and AI for governments and central banks.

Why. The region’s digital finance policy takes shape with their involvement.

Financial sector

World Economic Forum

Reports on AI governance and the future of financial services, written for boards of directors.

Why. The shared language leaders use when they talk about AI.

Security and incidents

Security and incidents

MITRE ATLAS and the AI Incident Database

A matrix of attacks on AI systems and an open database of real AI incidents.

Why. Terms for threat modeling, and examples of what has already happened to others.
