Terms of use

This is the website of the AI Competence Hub. It explains how we work, what we can offer, how an idea becomes a result, and how projects are going, and it brings together materials that help the organization's employees use AI.

Who can use the site

The site is for employees of the organization who have been given access through the organization's gateway. The organization's rules on information security, information classification, and acceptable use of its systems apply to the site.

The organization's information

The content of the site is for internal use. Do not disclose, copy, or share it outside the organization without the permission required by the organization's information-handling rules. The content of the site, including diagrams, text, and design, belongs to the organization. Third-party fonts and software components are used under their licenses, which are included with the site.

How to read the site's pages

  • The site shows how we work. The pages on process, services, and values explain our practice. They are there to help you find your way and do not commit anyone to anything.
  • Projects are shown as the project teams themselves describe them. The information on a card is accurate as long as the card is kept up to date.
  • The pages on standards and regulators summarize them in general terms, for information only. This is not legal advice, and a mention in a list is not an endorsement. The requirements that apply to the organization are determined by the organization's specialists responsible for this.
  • The site does not create obligations. Anything that needs to be agreed is agreed separately by the people working together.

How to use the site

  • Use the site for the organization's work. Do not try to bypass the access gateway, change the site, or extract its content for purposes unrelated to the organization.
  • Do not enter the organization's data, its customers' or employees' data, or internal documents into external websites, forms, or models found through this site.
  • When you use AI, follow the organization's rules: use approved solutions, and check the result before you use it in your work.
  • Found a mistake? Write to us through “Page feedback” at the bottom of the page, and include the page ID.

Changes

The organization may change the site and these terms. The published version applies. How information about users is handled is explained on the Privacy page.
