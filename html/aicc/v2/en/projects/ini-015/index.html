Problem and outcome

ProblemCompliance staff spend a lot of time preparing regular reports and filling in standard forms by hand.
Intended outcomeDrafts of regular reports and forms are prepared by the Claude assistant on each employee's computer, and the employee checks the result and uses it.
How we measure itTime to prepare a standard report or form.
Where we are nowNot measured.
Where we want to beNot set.

Conditions

AppetiteOne iteration per type of report or form.
Review pointAfter three finished templates.
Exit criterionThe employee gets a draft that can be used after checking.
Stop thresholdChecking the result takes as long as preparing it by hand.

Practice checklist

  • ○ Workstation review: what the assistant sees and changes on the employee's computer — Reviewed together with the people responsible for information security; the decision rests with the Compliance function. not started
  • ○ Where the results are stored and who has access to them not started
  • ○ How the employee checks the result before using it not started
  • ○ What we do if the assistant makes a mistake not started

Now

Next step. Describe the first types of reports and forms we want to automate.

Main risks

  • The workplace assistant may get access to confidential data: a workstation review is needed before starting.

Dependencies

  • An information security decision on where and how the assistant runs.
