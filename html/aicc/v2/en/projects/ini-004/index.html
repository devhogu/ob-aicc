Problem and outcome

ProblemFP&A already prepares reports with AI, but the reporting differs from period to period, depends on manual operations, and has no common channel to the Bank's management.
Intended outcomeThe management portal is live with its first monthly issue; the pipeline built on AI agents has prepared a full issue once; metric definitions are agreed within FP&A, and the format of the quarterly issue is ready.
How we measure itNumber of issues delivered on time; share of values with a reference to a controlled source; number of manual operations removed; hours spent preparing an issue; number of metrics defined.
Where we are nowNot measured.
Where we want to beNot set.

Conditions

AppetiteNo separate budget: the working time of whoever runs the project.
Review pointBy the end of 2026: we review the results and decide whether to continue.
Exit criterionA monthly issue has been prepared by the pipeline and delivered through the portal once.
Stop thresholdThe values in an issue cannot be backed by a reference to a controlled source.

Practice checklist

  • ○ Describe the reports FP&A already prepares with AI: metrics, sources, schedule, manual operations not started
  • ○ Agree with FP&A on a standard set of metrics and the contents of the monthly and quarterly issues not started
  • ○ Build the management portal together with FP&A and publish the monthly report through it not started
  • ○ Set up the pipeline built on AI agents: it collects, calculates, drafts, and publishes the metrics not started
  • ○ Back every value with a dated reference to a controlled source not started
  • ○ Prepare a complete monthly issue and design the format of the quarterly one not started

Now

Next step. Describe which reports FP&A prepares now and which manual operations remain in them.

Main risks

  • The reporting is read by the Bank's management and may reach investors: every value must have a controlled source, and a person checks each issue before it is sent.
  • Investor communication beyond financial performance metrics and changes to FP&A methods and accounting systems are out of scope.

Dependencies

  • The time of FP&A staff, the data sources, and the management portal.
  • A review of the issue before it is first delivered.
  • Review by representatives of the control functions.
