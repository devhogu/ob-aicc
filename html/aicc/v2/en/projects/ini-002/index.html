Problem and outcome

ProblemKnowledge about the Bank's services is spread across departments. There is no single view that links the commercial portfolio with processes, applications, and infrastructure, so AI adoption starts wherever the first request came from.
Intended outcomeA service landscape portal where every part of the map has been checked by its owner; a list of cases where AI is already used; a ranked list of candidates for AI adoption.
How we measure itNumber of departments and services on the map and the share checked by their owners; number of AI use cases found; number of ranked candidates.
Where we are nowNot measured.
Where we want to beNot set.

Conditions

AppetiteNo separate budget: the working time of whoever runs the project.
Review pointBy the end of 2026: we review the results and decide whether to continue.
Exit criterionThe first departments and services are shown on the map and checked by their owners; the portal opens and shows them.
Stop thresholdService owners cannot find the time to check the map, and it stays incomplete.

Practice checklist

  • ○ Agree on a common way to describe a service: commercial portfolio, services, processes, organizational structure, applications and data, infrastructure not started
  • ○ Collect the documents, diagrams, and lists that departments already have, without creating new ones not started
  • ○ Bring what was collected into the enterprise architecture repository and open the landscape portal not started
  • ○ Check each part of the map together with its owner not started
  • ○ Mark on the map where AI is already used and where the work is routine, manual, or document-heavy not started
  • ○ Turn the marked places into a ranked list of candidates for the backlog not started
  • ○ Decide who gets access to the portal and how: it shows the Bank's internal information not started

Now

Next step. Agree with the enterprise architecture department on a common way to describe a service.

Main risks

  • The portal shows the Bank's internal information: access rules need to be worked out with information security.
  • The map describes the current state only; changes to services, processes, systems, and architecture are out of scope.

Dependencies

  • The time and knowledge of department heads and service owners.
  • Access to the enterprise architecture repository and the portal, and the data class of their content.
