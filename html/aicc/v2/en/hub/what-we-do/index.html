What we do

The Hub's job is to bring together in one place everything that helps you use AI at work: building and running solutions, enablement, and reliability checks. Here you can find the service you need, see how work on projects is going, and pick up ready-made assets. An idea is first tested in practice, and whatever proves itself and is needed by many is handed over to those who will support it at scale.

Areas of work

Building and running solutions

Category What's included
Knowledge services Department and regulatory documents gathered into a knowledge base that answers your questions
Input processing Letters, applications, scans, statements, spreadsheets, and forms that come into the department are read, broken down into fields, checked for completeness and consistency, and passed on in the right format. An employee handles the non-standard cases
Workspace automation Routing, forms, reports, documents from templates, help with handling requests. AI does the work, and an employee checks the result
Analytics and decision support Data pipelines, dashboards, and analytical materials that show where the data came from: a basis for decisions
Management reporting and investor materials Materials for management and investors: management reporting, plan-vs-actual analysis and forecasts (financial planning and analysis, FP&A), presentations, disclosures, and answers to investors (investor relations, IR). Prepared from verified data and templates, in the languages needed
Platforms Shared components and environments that solutions are built on, and the handover of finished components to those who will support them
Governance The whole governance framework, from idea to working solution: strategy and working principles, internal documents and processes, rules for using AI and acceptance criteria, risk level, assessment of solutions and suppliers, monitoring of running solutions, and incident analysis.

Enablement

Category What's included
Training and knowledge sharing Role-based training, help at the workplace, skill libraries, communities of practice, and publications
Adoption and solution lifecycle Experts in the department and an adoption plan; handover to maintenance; support, review, and planned retirement of solutions
