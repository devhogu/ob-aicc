Roles and forums

Every project has three roles. These are roles in the project, not job titles: several people can share one role, and one person can hold two. The card shows who is in which role right now. You don't need a role to bring an idea.

  • Why

    Business owner

    Represents the department that needs the result. Decides whether the work is worth starting and continuing, and accepts the result.

  • What and in what order

    Product manager

    Defines the value, runs the funnel and the backlog, and accepts each Feature.

  • How and when

    Project manager

    Runs the project day to day and keeps the card up to date.

Two forums

A forum is a regular meeting of the people who have cards. A forum doesn't approve or sign off on anything: it looks at the flow of work and settles what couldn't be settled on the spot.

Product management forum Program decision forum
What it looks at the funnel and the portfolio the program board
What it decides what to start, what is stuck, what it's time to stop, which initiatives have reached a checkpoint order of work, dependencies, missed deadlines, out-of-turn work, readiness for the next increment
Who takes part business owners and product managers project managers and product managers
How often every two weeks, up to 45 minutes once per iteration

How the meeting runs. Cards on the screen, not slides. Exceptions come first: what is marked red, overdue, or not moving. Every decision is written straight into the decision log on the card. If a question can be settled by a rule, we agree on the rule and don't come back to it.

Flow measures

The measures are calculated from the cards and help us see where work gets stuck. The Hub doesn't set target values: each project compares itself with its own past.

Measure What it shows How to read it
Lead time from the entry in the funnel to completion rising: look for where work sits idle
Cycle time how long the work is in active development rising: the tasks are too big or something is in the way
Throughput how much gets done in a period compare with our own past
Work in progress how many work items are under way at once no higher than the WIP limit
Work item age how long the work has been in its current state look at items that have sat there longer than usual
