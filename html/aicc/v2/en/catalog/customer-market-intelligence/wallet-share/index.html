Discovery Catalog / Customer & Market Intelligence / Wallet share

Wallet share

Wallet share measures the proportion of a customer's total financial-services spend — across credit, savings, insurance, investment, and payment categories — that flows through the Bank. For most retail and SME customers, a bank commonly holds less than 40% of the total financial wallet; the remainder sits with competitors, non-bank providers, or in informal savings. Growing wallet share within the existing customer base is more capital-efficient than acquiring new customers. GenAI enables continuous estimation of competitor-held share and systematic identification of cross-sell opportunity windows — turning wallet-share intelligence from an annual planning input into a weekly commercial signal.

Primary bank relationship share

The proportion of customers for whom the Bank is the primary financial-services provider — defined by salary crediting, main transaction account, or majority-wallet relationship. Primary relationship depth is the most important single indicator of wallet share: primary-bank customers have three to five times the revenue potential of secondary-bank customers. The primary relationship is typically defined by the salary-crediting account, which concentrates competitive vulnerability at payroll-cycle moments.

Primary Relationship Migration Monitor

Automated monthly report of movements between primary and secondary relationship tiers for commercial leadership, showing the net change in primary-bank share and the segments where migration is most active.

S
Problem to solve

Primary-bank share is measured annually in commissioned customer research. In-year movements — customers migrating from primary to secondary following a competitor salary-account campaign, or secondary customers upgrading after a new product offer — are not tracked. Commercial leadership cannot see whether primary-bank share is improving or declining during the year, reducing the responsiveness of the commercial program.

Solution

The AI agent reads the monthly primary-bank classification output, computes net tier migration flows, and produces a month-on-month primary-relationship monitor. Commercial leadership receives the migration report monthly; the annual commissioned research becomes a validation of the in-year signal rather than the sole measurement of primary-bank share.

OKR

Commercial leadership receives a monthly primary-relationship monitor showing net migration between primary and secondary tiers and the segments where migration is most active, giving an in-year view of primary-bank share.

Adoption

The AI agent delivers the migration monitor for ≥95% of scheduled monthly cycles for ≥12 consecutive months post go-live.

Acceptance

≥80% of monitors accepted by commercial leadership without supplementary analysis; in-year trend confirmed as directionally consistent with the annual commissioned research.

Cycle

Primary-bank share measurement cycle reduced from annual commissioned research to a monthly monitor within 5 business days of month close.

Salary-Switch Risk Alert

The AI agent identifies customers showing pre-switch salary-crediting signals — salary credits declining over consecutive cycles, new inbound payments from a competing institution — and surfaces them as salary-switch risk for priority retention outreach ahead of the payroll-cycle moment.

S
Problem to solve

Salary crediting is commonly the most important primary-bank signal in retail banking. A customer who switches salary crediting to a competitor represents an immediate and material wallet-share loss. The switch decision typically happens at an HR payroll-change moment and is irreversible within the quarter. The Bank currently identifies salary-switch exits after the first missed salary credit — at the point of loss, not before.

Solution

The AI agent monitors salary-credit patterns for all primary-bank customers weekly, flags customers showing pre-switch signals — credit amount declining over two consecutive cycles, a new inbound payment from a competing institution appearing alongside the salary credit — and produces a priority alert list. Relationship managers receive the salary-switch risk alert at least one payroll cycle before the likely switch date.

OKR

Relationship managers receive a weekly priority alert list of primary-bank customers showing pre-switch salary-crediting signals, at least one payroll cycle before the likely switch date.

Adoption

The AI agent monitors salary-credit patterns for 100% of primary-bank customers and delivers the alert list for ≥95% of weekly cycles for ≥48 consecutive weeks post go-live.

Acceptance

≥65% of alerted customers confirmed as at risk of switching by the relationship manager on contact; ≥80% of alerted customers contacted before their next payroll date.

Cycle

Salary-switch identification advanced from after the first missed salary credit to ≥1 payroll cycle before the likely switch date.

Primary-Bank Identification Model

The AI agent classifies the active customer base into primary-bank and secondary-bank relationships monthly, using salary-credit patterns, transaction volume share, and product depth as classification signals, and surfaces the secondary-bank cohort as the primary wallet-share growth opportunity.

M
Problem to solve

Primary-bank relationship status is not classified systematically. Commercial teams know that salary crediting is the strongest primary-bank signal, but customers without salary crediting whose main transaction account is with the Bank are not identified, and customers with salary crediting who have migrated their primary spend to a competitor card are not flagged. The commercial opportunity in each relationship tier is invisible without explicit classification.

Solution

The AI agent reads salary-credit events, transaction volume, inbound payment sources, and product-depth data monthly, classifies each active customer as primary-bank, probable-primary, secondary-bank, or transactional-only, and produces a classification report with the wallet-share opportunity sized per tier. Commercial teams prioritize secondary-bank customers with high CLV as the first wallet-share growth cohort.

OKR

Every active customer is classified each month as primary-bank, probable-primary, secondary-bank, or transactional-only, with the wallet-share opportunity sized per tier for commercial teams.

Adoption

The AI agent classifies 100% of the active customer base and delivers the classification report for ≥95% of scheduled monthly cycles for ≥12 consecutive months post go-live.

Acceptance

≥80% of classifications confirmed as correct by commercial teams on a sampled monthly review.

Cycle

Primary-bank classification moved from not performed systematically to a monthly classification within 1 week of month close.

Cross-sell opportunity identification

Systematic identification of customers with a high probability of accepting a specific product offer — based on their current product profile, financial behavior, and CLV tier. Cross-sell opportunity identification is the commercial application of segmentation, CLV, and wallet-share estimation: the output is a ranked, actionable list of customer-product opportunities for commercial teams. The highest cross-sell opportunity concentrations are commonly in salary-account holders without a retail credit product and SME transactional-account holders without a trade-finance product.

Cross-Sell Campaign Performance Report

Automated monthly report of cross-sell campaign conversion rates, product-take-up by segment, and wallet-share impact, delivered to commercial leadership without manual extraction from campaign management and CRM systems.

S
Problem to solve

Cross-sell campaign performance is assembled from campaign management tool exports, CRM contact records, and core banking product-opening data — three systems with different identifiers that require manual matching. The monthly performance report takes two to three analyst days to produce and arrives in the commercial review cycle late, limiting its influence on the following month's campaign design.

Solution

The AI agent reads campaign contact records, offer details, and product-opening events from source systems, matches by customer identifier, computes conversion rates by segment and product, and produces the standard monthly campaign performance report. Commercial leadership reviews the AI-generated output; campaign design for the following month references the performance data within one week of month close.

OKR

Commercial leadership receives the monthly cross-sell campaign performance report — conversion rates, product take-up by segment, and wallet-share impact — within one week of month close, in time to shape the following month's campaign design.

Adoption

The AI agent delivers the campaign performance report for ≥95% of scheduled monthly cycles for ≥12 consecutive months post go-live, matching records across campaign management, CRM, and core banking.

Acceptance

≥85% of reports accepted by commercial leadership without correction; customer-identifier match rate across the three source systems ≥98%.

Cycle

Report production reduced from two to three analyst days of manual matching to delivery within one week of month close with ≤2 hours of analyst review.

Next-Best-Offer Prioritization

The AI agent scores the customer base weekly for product-specific cross-sell propensity, producing a ranked next-best-offer list for campaign execution and relationship-manager queues.

M
Problem to solve

Cross-sell targeting is produced through periodic campaign planning cycles. Customers who become cross-sell eligible between campaigns — a salary-account holder who completed three consecutive months of payroll crediting — are missed until the next campaign cycle, and eligibility signals decay before they are acted on.

Solution

The AI agent reads product-ownership, transaction-behavior, and eligibility-signal data weekly, scores each customer for cross-sell propensity by product, and produces a ranked next-best-offer list. The campaign lead reviews the top-ranked offers each week before campaign teams execute against the list; relationship managers receive their individual customer queue, and offer eligibility is refreshed every week.

OKR

A weekly ranked next-best-offer list — scored for product-specific cross-sell propensity across the active customer base — is available for campaign execution and relationship-manager queues.

Adoption

The AI agent produces the next-best-offer ranked list for ≥95% of weekly scoring cycles for ≥48 consecutive weeks post go-live.

Acceptance

≥75% of top-ranked offers confirmed as appropriate for the targeted segment by the campaign lead on weekly review; cross-sell conversion rate tracked as primary outcome metric.

Cycle

Next-best-offer scoring cycle reduced from monthly batch campaign production to weekly automated ranked output.

Propensity Model Cross-Sell Insights

The AI agent analyzes the product-acceptance and product-decline patterns from the prior six months to identify the propensity signals most predictive of cross-sell conversion, and produces a model-insight brief for the data science and commercial teams.

M
Problem to solve

Cross-sell propensity models are built and validated at initial deployment. The signals that were predictive at build time may decay as customer behavior evolves — a signal that predicted credit card acceptance at salary crediting three years ago may be less predictive now that the digital-channel onboarding journey has changed. Model-insight reviews are conducted only at the annual model rebuild and do not reflect in-year signal drift.

Solution

The AI agent reads product-acceptance and decline outcomes from the prior six months, analyzes which propensity signals at the point of offer are most correlated with acceptance in the current customer base, and produces a model-insight brief showing signal importance rankings and the direction of any drift from the model's original calibration. The data science team uses the brief to prioritize the next model-refresh cycle.

OKR

The data science and commercial teams receive a model-insight brief ranking the propensity signals most predictive of cross-sell conversion in the prior six months and showing the direction of drift from the model's original calibration.

Adoption

The AI agent produces the model-insight brief for ≥90% of scheduled review cycles from go-live, covering every cross-sell propensity model in production.

Acceptance

≥80% of reported signal drifts confirmed by the data science team on validation; the brief used to prioritize 100% of model-refresh cycles.

Cycle

Propensity signal review cycle reduced from the annual model rebuild to a brief every six months.

Product penetration depth

The number of active product categories held per customer, and the distribution of that depth across the customer base — the structural measure of wallet share within the Bank's own product set. Deep penetration (three or more product categories per customer) is the strongest predictor of retention and CLV. Under-penetrated customers — holding only a salary account or a single credit product — represent the highest wallet-share upside for cross-sell programs. Product penetration analysis by segment reveals which combinations have the highest retention and revenue effects.

Penetration Gap Analysis

The AI agent maps the product-penetration distribution across segments weekly, identifying the highest-upside cross-sell gaps by product pair and the retention correlation by penetration depth tier.

S
Problem to solve

Product penetration depth is reported quarterly as an aggregate statistic — average products per customer by segment. The distribution underneath — how many customers hold only one product, which product combinations correlate with retention — is not surfaced, and cross-sell program design defaults to the same standard offer set regardless of the penetration pattern.

Solution

The AI agent reads product-ownership data from CRM and core banking weekly, produces a penetration-gap analysis showing the proportion of customers at each depth level per segment, the top cross-sell gaps by product pair, and the retention correlation by depth tier. Segment heads confirm the priority gaps on monthly review; commercial and product teams use the output to prioritize cross-sell campaigns.

OKR

A weekly product-penetration distribution map — identifying the highest-upside cross-sell gaps by product pair and retention correlation by penetration depth tier — is available to segment heads and the commercial leadership.

Adoption

The AI agent produces the penetration gap analysis for ≥95% of scheduled weekly cycles for ≥48 consecutive weeks post go-live.

Acceptance

≥75% of identified cross-sell gaps confirmed as priority targets by segment heads on monthly review; cross-sell penetration rate per product pair tracked as primary outcome metric.

Cycle

Product Penetration Milestone Alert

The AI agent monitors product-penetration depth across the customer base weekly and alerts relationship managers and campaign teams when a customer crosses a depth milestone — reaching a second or third active product — for timely follow-on offer sequencing.

S
Problem to solve

Product-penetration milestones — the moment a customer moves from one to two or from two to three active products — are strong signals for follow-on offer sequencing: retention rates and CLV increase materially at each additional product. These milestones are not monitored in real time; campaign targeting is based on the penetration-depth snapshot from the last campaign cycle, missing the window immediately after the milestone crossing.

Solution

The AI agent reads product-holding events weekly, identifies customers crossing penetration-depth milestones in the prior seven days, and alerts relationship managers and campaign teams with the customer's current product set and the highest-propensity next-product recommendation. Offers are sequenced within the optimal post-milestone window rather than at the next campaign cycle.

OKR

Relationship managers and campaign teams are alerted each week to customers who crossed a penetration-depth milestone in the prior seven days, with the current product set and the highest-propensity next-product recommendation.

Adoption

The AI agent identifies milestone crossings and issues alerts for ≥95% of weekly cycles for ≥48 consecutive weeks post go-live.

Acceptance

≥70% of alerts acted on with a follow-on offer within 14 days; ≥75% of next-product recommendations confirmed as appropriate by campaign teams on sampled review.

Cycle

Milestone detection reduced from the penetration snapshot of the last campaign cycle to ≤7 days from the crossing.

Product Combination Retention Analysis

The AI agent analyzes historical retention rates by product combination, identifying which two- and three-product combinations produce the strongest retention and CLV, and produces a combination-ranking brief for product and commercial strategy.

M
Problem to solve

Cross-sell strategy targets individual product additions without a systematic view of which product combinations produce the strongest retention outcomes. Whether a salary-account plus credit-card combination retains customers materially better than salary-account plus personal loan — and by how much — is not available as a standard analytical input. Product prioritization decisions do not differentiate by retention impact.

Solution

The AI agent reads product-holding history alongside 12-month retention outcomes, computes retention rates and CLV averages by product combination, ranks the top two- and three-product combinations by retention differential above the single-product baseline, and produces the combination-ranking brief. Product and commercial strategy uses the ranking to sequence cross-sell program priorities toward the highest-retention combinations.

OKR

Product and commercial strategy receive a combination-ranking brief identifying which two- and three-product combinations produce the strongest retention and CLV above the single-product baseline, for sequencing cross-sell program priorities.

Adoption

The AI agent produces the combination-ranking brief for ≥90% of scheduled refresh cycles from go-live, covering all two- and three-product combinations with enough holders for comparison.

Acceptance

≥80% of ranked combinations accepted by product and commercial strategy as valid; the ranking used to sequence cross-sell program priorities in ≥75% of planning cycles.

Cycle

Combination-level retention analysis moved from not available as a standard input to a brief refreshed at least twice a year.

Competitor-held share estimation

Estimation of the proportion of a customer's financial wallet held by competing institutions. Competitor-held share is not directly observable but can be estimated from outbound payment flows, salary-credit channel patterns, credit-bureau product-ownership data, and external market-share benchmarks. Banks with significant retail deposit books commonly monitor primary-bank attrition as part of their funding-risk assessment.

Competitor Share Inference Model

The AI agent infers each customer's estimated competitor-held wallet share from outbound payment flows, salary-credit channel patterns, and credit-bureau product-ownership signals, producing a ranked opportunity list for commercial targeting.

M
Problem to solve

The proportion of a customer's financial wallet held by competitors is not directly observable. Commercial teams operate on the assumption that secondary-bank customers have competitor-held share but cannot quantify it per customer. Without a per-customer estimate, cross-sell prioritization cannot distinguish a customer with 60% competitor-held share from one with 15%, even though the commercial opportunity is four times larger.

Solution

The AI agent reads outbound payment destinations, salary-credit source patterns, bureau product-ownership flags where available, and account-balance trend data to construct a per-customer competitor-held share estimate. Customers are ranked by estimated competitor-held share within their CLV tier, producing a cross-sell priority list ordered by wallet-share opportunity. Commercial teams focus contact capacity on the highest-opportunity cohort.

OKR

Each customer carries an estimated competitor-held wallet share — inferred from outbound payment flows, salary-credit patterns, bureau signals, and balance trends — and commercial teams receive a cross-sell priority list ranked by that estimate within CLV tier.

Adoption

The AI agent produces per-customer competitor-held share estimates for ≥90% of the active customer base on each monthly cycle for ≥12 consecutive months post go-live.

Acceptance

≥70% of top-ranked customers confirmed as holding material competitor products by commercial teams on contact or sampled validation.

Cycle

Per-customer competitor-held share moved from not quantified to a monthly estimate and ranked list within 1 week of month close.
