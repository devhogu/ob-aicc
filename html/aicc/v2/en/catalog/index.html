Discovery Catalog

The catalog is a map of a bank: for each part of it, it lists scenarios in which AI could help. It covers the core business processes, shared banking functions, governance and control, and shows where AI helps people see and analyze better, automate operations, support staff in their work, and create new products. The catalog holds 1,110 scenarios in nine areas. Use it to find and compare opportunities: no scenario has been selected or approved yet.
