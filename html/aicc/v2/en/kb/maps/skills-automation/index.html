Learning map: Skills and workspace automation

Turn recurring work into skills and automated tasks, test them, and share them safely with colleagues.

RoadmapStop 4 of 4Start with step 1 →
← Previous mapClaude in everyday workNext on the roadmap →Choose a specialization
Who it's for: for everyone who wants to automate their workSteps: 10 · about 2 h
0 of 10
Level 1

Beginner

  1. 1
    Skills: teach Claude your procedureguide · 6 min

    Tell a skill apart from a project or a connector.

    Try this: Find an instruction you have pasted into conversations three times and decide whether it belongs in a project, a skill, or a connector.

  2. 2
    How to automate a routine taskguide · 7 min

    Follow the five steps from a manual task to automation.

    Try this: Describe one of your routine tasks in three lines: input, output, and who reads it.

Checkpoint. You understand what a skill is, and you have found the first task in your work to automate.

Level 2

Practitioner

  1. 3
    How a skill is builtpart of a guide · 10 min

    Write a SKILL.md with a good description.

    Try this: Write the description of your skill following the “good” example.

  2. 4
    Skills in the Claude apppart of a guide · 10 min

    Turn on, upload, and create a skill in a conversation.

    Try this: Make an “internal memo” skill following the example in the guide.

Checkpoint. You have written and turned on your first skill.

Level 3

Advanced

  1. 5
    How to write a good skillpart of a guide · 10 min

    Improve a skill with the nine rules, starting with the description and the example of the result.

  2. 6
    Testing and improving a skillpart of a guide · 10 min

    Test a skill on three tasks, with and without it.

    Try this: Test the “internal memo” skill on three past memos, with the skill and without it.

  3. 7
    Scheduled tasks in Coworkpart of a guide · 10 min

    Set up a weekly task that prepares a draft.

Checkpoint. Your skills trigger reliably, and recurring work runs on a schedule.

Level 4

Expert

  1. 8
    Skill safety and sharingpart of a guide · 10 min

    Check skills before turning them on, and share them through your organization's review.

  2. 9
    People and agents on one teamguide · 12 min

    Understand how an agent becomes a team member and how trust in it grows step by step.

    Try this: Write down three questions your team asks every week.

  3. 10
    Course: Introduction to Agent Skills ↗course, EN · 60 min

    Complete the Anthropic course on skills.

Checkpoint. You share tested skills with colleagues, and you set up teams where people and agents work together.
