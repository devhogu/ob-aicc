Learning map: Basics of working with AI

Understand what AI can and cannot do, work with it thoughtfully and safely, and take responsibility for the result.

Who it's for: for everyoneSteps: 11 · about 7 h
0 of 11
Level 1

Beginner

  1. 1
    Getting started with an AI assistantguide · 5 min

    Start working with the assistant on a simple task that is easy to check.

    Try this: Take a long email with no personal data, cut it down to five points, and compare the result with the original.

  2. 2
    What you can and cannot share with AIguide · 5 min

    Tell data you can share from data you can't.

    Try this: Take one of your work documents and mark what has to be removed before you send it to AI.

  3. 3
    How to check AI outputguide · 5 min

    Check an AI answer against a list: facts, figures, completeness, and tone.

    Try this: Find a statement in one of Claude's answers that needs to be checked against a source.

Checkpoint. You have had several useful conversations, you know what you can share with AI, and you check its answers.

Level 2

Practitioner

  1. 4
    What AI can and cannot doguide · 6 min

    Explain why a model can be confidently wrong and where it needs your documents.

    Try this: Ask about one of your internal procedures, first without the document and then with it, and compare the answers.

  2. 5
    Four skills for working with AI (4D)guide · 6 min

    Break a task down by the four skills: Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence.

    Try this: Run one work task through the 4Ds and write down what you handed to AI and what you kept for yourself.

Checkpoint. You understand how a language model works, and you use the 4D framework in your work.

Level 3

Advanced

  1. 6
    AI Fluency: the complete guideguide · 30 min

    Choose between automation, augmentation, and agency.

    Try this: Write down three tasks for the week and choose a way of working with AI for each; then do the “Bad prompt” exercise.

  2. 7
    Free Anthropic courses and where to startguide · 4 min

    Choose the Anthropic courses that fit your work and learn on real work tasks.

  3. 8
    Course: AI Capabilities and Limitations ↗course, EN · 60 min

    Consolidate your picture of what models can and cannot do, and earn a certificate.

Checkpoint. You choose how to work with AI deliberately, and you run the description–discernment loop.

Level 4

Expert

  1. 9
    Personal rules for working with AIpart of a guide · 10 min

    Write down your own policy: which tasks, what limits, how you check, and how you disclose AI's part.

    Try this: Write your rules on one page and discuss them with your manager.

  2. 10
    Responsible AI: a course in six partsHub section · 20 min

    Understand the risks of AI in finance and the principles of responsible use.

  3. 11
    Course: “AI Fluency: Framework and Foundations” ↗course, EN · 240 min

    Complete the full Anthropic course and earn a certificate.

Checkpoint. You have your own rules for working with AI, and you help colleagues work responsibly.
