Learning map: Adoption and leadership

Make decisions about adopting Claude in your organization and organize the work of people and agents in teams, with regulation in mind.

0 of 8
Level 1

Beginner

  1. 1
    Four skills for working with AI (4D)guide · 6 min

    Use the shared 4D language with your team.

  2. 2
    Responsible AI: a course in six partsHub section · 20 min

    Understand the risks and the principles of responsible use.

Checkpoint. You understand AI’s capabilities and risks well enough to make decisions.

Level 2

Practitioner

  1. 3
    People and agents on one teamguide · 12 min

    Assess your team's readiness and plan the first two weeks.

    Try this: Go through the five readiness questions with your team.

  2. 4
    Claude Tag: Claude in your team's Slackguide · 10 min

    If you use Slack, understand how an agent works in shared channels and what to decide before a pilot.

Checkpoint. You know how a team of people and agents works, and you are ready for a pilot.

Level 3

Advanced

  1. 5
    Rolling out Claude in an organization: five decisions — the complete guideguide · 35 min

    Work through the five adoption decisions and settle the position for a regulated department.

    Try this: Write down which features and connectors your pilot needs, what limit it needs, and who owns each decision.

Checkpoint. You can work through the five adoption decisions for your organization.

Level 4

Expert

  1. 6
    Adoption signalspart of a guide · 10 min

    Read adoption signals and find their causes.

    Try this: Choose two signals and write down a pace goal in one sentence.

  2. 7
    Course: Deploying Claude Enterprise with Confidence ↗course, EN · 150 min

    Complete the Anthropic course and come away with a working adoption plan.

  3. 8
    Course: Building Effective Human-Agent Teams ↗course, EN · 45 min

    Complete the course on people and agents working together.

Checkpoint. You lead adoption and measure it by signals, not by quotas.
