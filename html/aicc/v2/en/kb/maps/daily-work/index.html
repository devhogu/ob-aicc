Learning map: Claude in everyday work

Work confidently in Claude every day — projects, documents, Office, connectors, and Cowork — while keeping your organization’s data safe.

Who it's for: for everyoneSteps: 14 · about 6 h
0 of 14
Level 1

Beginner

  1. 1
    Claude: what it can do and where to startguide · 6 min

    Choose between a conversation, a project, an artifact, and a skill.

    Try this: Write down three tasks from your week and choose a conversation, a project, a file, or a skill for each.

  2. 2
    Interface, settings, and memorypart of a guide · 10 min

    Find the main buttons and set standing instructions for all your conversations.

  3. 3
    Projects in Claude: standing context for recurring workguide · 6 min

    Set up a project with documents and instructions.

    Try this: Create a project for one of your recurring tasks.

Checkpoint. You know what Claude can do, and you have your first project up and running.

Level 2

Practitioner

  1. 4
    Projects from creation to upkeeppart of a guide · 10 min

    Write good project instructions and share the project with colleagues.

    Try this: Rewrite your project's instructions using the four parts and compare the answers.

  2. 5
    Files and documents in a conversationpart of a guide · 10 min

    Tell which files Claude can read, how large they can be, and how to refer to them.

  3. 6
    Creating Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and PDF filespart of a guide · 10 min

    Get finished files and artifacts.

    Try this: Ask Claude for an Excel spreadsheet with formulas, built from your data export.

  4. 7
    Claude in Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and Outlookpart of a guide · 10 min

    Work with an open file right inside Office.

    Try this: Ask Claude in Excel to explain the bottom line of your report, with cell references.

Checkpoint. You work with files, get finished Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files, and use Claude right inside Office.

Level 3

Advanced

  1. 8
    Connectors and researchpart of a guide · 10 min

    Work with email, files, and your calendar through connectors.

    Try this: Ask Claude which of this week's meetings need materials prepared, and check against your calendar.

  2. 9
    Claude Cowork: working with your files and appsguide · 6 min

    Understand when you need Cowork, and start safely.

  3. 10
    Cowork: hand work to Claudepart of a guide · 10 min

    Run the task loop and set up standing context and scheduled tasks.

    Try this: Ask Cowork to put together a briefing note from a folder of document copies.

Checkpoint. You hand Claude multi-step work in Cowork and use connectors.

Level 4

Expert

  1. 11
    Safety and datapart of a guide · 10 min

    Recognize where work with Claude leaves no audit trail, and when Cowork and the add-ins are not suitable.

  2. 12
    Scenarios by rolepart of a guide · 10 min

    Put one scenario to work in your department.

    Try this: Run a scenario for your role yourself three times and show it to colleagues.

  3. 13
    Course: Claude 101 ↗course, EN · 90 min

    Complete Anthropic's introductory course, with a certificate.

  4. 14
    Course: Introduction to Claude Cowork ↗course, EN · 150 min

    Complete the Cowork course, with a certificate.

Checkpoint. You use Claude in scenarios for your role, and you know the data safety rules.
