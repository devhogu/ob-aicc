Claude: from zero to expert

One learning route for working with AI and Claude. Start with the basics, go through the stops in order, then choose a branch: for developers or for leaders. Each stop is a separate map from beginner to expert.

4 stops for everyone, then a specialization by roleStart: stop 1 — Basics of working with AI →

Mark the steps you have done on the maps: your progress is saved in your browser and appears here.

Stops in order

  1. 1Stop 1 · for everyone

    Basics of working with AI

    Understand what AI can and cannot do, work with it thoughtfully and safely, and take responsibility for the result.

    4 levels · 11 steps · about 7 h
    0 of 11
  2. 2Stop 2 · for everyone

    Prompting Claude

    Ask Claude for what you need and get it right the first time, from a simple request to a complex multi-step scenario.

    4 levels · 12 steps · about 6 h
    0 of 12
  3. 3Stop 3 · for everyone

    Claude in everyday work

    Work confidently in Claude every day — projects, documents, Office, connectors, and Cowork — while keeping your organization’s data safe.

    4 levels · 14 steps · about 6 h
    0 of 14

    Skills and workspace automation

    Turn recurring work into skills and automated tasks, test them, and share them safely with colleagues.

    4 levels · 10 steps · about 2 h
    0 of 10
