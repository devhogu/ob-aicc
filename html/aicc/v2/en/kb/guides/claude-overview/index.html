Claude is an AI assistant made by Anthropic. In six minutes you will learn what Claude is made of, and for any task of yours you will be able to choose the right way to work: a plain conversation, a project, an artifact, or a skill.

You talk to Claude in plain language, give it documents, and hand it work: read, compare, write, calculate. The rules of the “Basics of working with AI” map apply here too: share only what may be shared, and check the answer before you pass it on.

What Claude has

Conversation

Describe the task in your own words and refine it as you go. Every new conversation starts from a blank page: Claude doesn't remember past conversations unless memory is turned on.

Files

Attach a document, a spreadsheet, or an image, and Claude will read it and answer from it. Only data that may be shared.

Projects

A separate folder for recurring work: it holds standing documents and rules, and Claude takes them into account in every conversation in the project. Learn more.

Artifacts

A finished result (a document, a table, a diagram, a small app) in its own window next to the conversation, where it is easy to edit and download.

Skills

A written-down procedure: exactly how to do a particular piece of work. Claude applies it on its own when it fits the task. Learn more.

Connectors and research

Connectors give Claude access to your work systems: email, files, calendar. Research is a report drawn from many sources, with links. They work only if your organization has turned them on.

Your first conversation in Claude

  1. Open Claude in an approved work account. Write in your own words: Claude replies in the language of your question.
  2. Start a new conversation and begin with a task you can easily check yourself: a summary of an email, a draft internal memo, a look at a small spreadsheet.
  3. Say who you are and who the result is for: “I'm a specialist in the operations department, preparing a summary for my manager.”
  4. Refine: “shorter”, “as a table”, “no introductory phrases”. The second answer is usually better than the first.

When to choose what

Your situation What to choose Example
A one-off task a plain conversation shorten a long email to five points
The same work every week with the same documents a project replies to typical customer requests according to the procedure
You need a finished document or spreadsheet to download an artifact or a file a one-page internal memo, an Excel table from a data export
The same procedure across different tasks a skill checking any document against your checklist

If in doubt, start with a plain conversation. When you notice you are attaching the same procedure and explaining the same thing for the third time, it's time to create a project.

Try it now. Write down three tasks from your working week and pick a table row for each. Take the task that landed in the “project” row and work through it with the guide Projects in Claude.

Which features are available to you depends on your plan and your organization's settings. Work only in an approved work account; if a feature you need is missing, write to us.

For a detailed, step-by-step walkthrough, see the guide Working in Claude: projects, documents, Office, and Cowork.
