What you can and cannot share with AI

This guide is for everyone who works with an AI assistant. By the end, you will be able to look at a document of yours and tell what you can give the assistant and what you need to remove.

Everything you paste into an assistant or attach as a file goes to the service that runs it. If that service is not approved for that kind of data, sending it is data leakage, even if the assistant “won't tell anyone”.

An approved service is an assistant that your organization has allowed you to use for a particular kind of data, usually through a work account. A personal account or any AI website you happen to find is not suitable for work data: using them this way is called shadow AI.

Allowed

Public information, your own drafts without confidential data, anonymized examples, “how do I do this” questions.

Only in an approved service

Internal documents, procedures, work correspondence, anonymized exports, and only in the service approved for that kind of data.

Not allowed

Personal data of customers and employees, banking secrecy, passwords and access keys, information you are not allowed to disclose.

To anonymize means to remove or replace everything that could identify a person or a customer: full names, account and card numbers, phone numbers, addresses, PINs. Write “Customer A” or “[account number]” instead.

Before you paste

  1. Are there people in it? Names, account numbers, phone numbers, PINs: remove them or replace them with “Customer A”.
  2. Could you show this to an outsider? If not, it goes only into an approved service.
  3. Does the task need it? Often the structure or an example is enough, not the whole export. To get help with an Excel formula, the column headers and a couple of made-up rows are enough.

Example: a customer complaint

You want the assistant to help you reply politely to a complaint.

Before After
“Customer Asel N., account No. 1234…, tel. …, writes that a transfer of 15,000 som from 3 October has not arrived and threatens to close her account.” “A customer writes that a transfer has not reached the recipient and threatens to close the account. Draft a polite reply: an apology, what we are doing, and when to expect an answer. No promises of deadlines or amounts.”

To draft the reply, the assistant doesn't need the name, the account number, or the amount: you will fill those in yourself in the work system.

Try it now

Open one of your work documents: an email, meeting minutes, or an export. Go through the three questions above and mark everything you need to remove or replace before sending it to the assistant. If nothing meaningful is left of the document after that, it is better to do the task without AI or only in an approved service.

If in doubt

Don't paste it. Ask your manager or write to us. If data has already gone to the wrong place, report it at once: a mistake noticed quickly is easy to fix; a hidden one is not.

For why this matters and what other risks there are, see the section Responsible AI.
