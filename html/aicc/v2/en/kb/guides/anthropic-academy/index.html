This guide is for those who want to take Anthropic's original courses, not just our summaries. Here you will find which courses to start with depending on your work, and how to learn so that it pays off as early as this week.

Anthropic, the company behind Claude, has a free learning platform: Claude Academy (formerly Anthropic Academy). The courses are short and in English, and each ends with a test and a certificate. You don't need a Claude work account for the courses.

If you would rather start with something shorter, begin with our summaries: the map Basics of working with AI takes you through them step by step and points you to the original course where it fits.

For everyone

Claude 101 ↗: everyday work with Claude, including conversations, projects, artifacts and skills.
AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations ↗: how to work with AI deliberately, using the four 4D skills.
AI Capabilities and Limitations ↗: what AI can do and where it goes wrong.

For working with documents

Introduction to Claude Cowork ↗: Claude works with your files, folders and apps, while you direct and check.

For developers

Claude Code 101 ↗ and Claude Code in Action ↗: working in a repository.
Introduction to Agent Skills ↗, Introduction to Subagents ↗: skills and subagents.
Introduction to Model Context Protocol ↗: connecting tools.

Leaders who are deciding how to roll out Claude in their organization should take Deploying Claude Enterprise with Confidence ↗.

How to learn so that it pays off

  • Take a course alongside a real task: after each lesson, try the technique on your own work, for example on a meeting summary or a draft email.
  • If a course has exercises in Claude, do them on training or anonymized examples. Work data goes only into a work account approved in your organization: see What you can and cannot share with AI.
  • Start with Claude 101 and AI Fluency: the other courses build on them.
  • Short summaries are in the Knowledge Base, in the categories “Getting started”, “Prompting Claude”, “Claude” and “Claude Code”.
  • The full list of courses and the official documentation in Russian are in the Reference section: Anthropic learning.
