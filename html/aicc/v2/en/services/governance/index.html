Services / Governance

Governance

The whole governance framework, from an idea to a working solution, is brought together in one service. Instead of separate documents and checks, the department gets a complete system: how the work is organized, which rules apply, how risk is assessed, and how to make sure a solution works as intended.

What's included

Part What it is What you keep
Strategy and ways of working direction, way of working, and a shared workspace for a department or an initiative a one-page strategy, a way of working, a workspace
Internal documents and processes procedures, instructions, and process descriptions prepared with AI a set of documents and a procedure for updating them
Rules for using AI and acceptance criteria what is allowed and what isn't, a map of checks, acceptance criteria, and guardrails, all before the work starts rules and a map of checks on the card
Risk tier how serious the consequences of an error are and how much checking is justified a tier with its rationale and a review date
Evaluation of solutions and vendors testing on an evaluation set built from the department's scenarios before use begins an evaluation report
Monitoring of working solutions how a solution behaves in operation, analysis of AI incidents a regular review of the state of all solutions

How the work goes

We build the framework together with the department, step by step: first what you can't start without (rules, criteria, and the risk tier); then documents and processes; and finally monitoring of working solutions. A single part is done as run-rate work, the whole framework as an initiative. The department's business owner makes the decisions about the framework, and checks that need risk, security, or legal specialists are carried out together with them.
