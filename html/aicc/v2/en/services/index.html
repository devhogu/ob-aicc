Services

The Hub helps departments see where AI is really useful, test an idea in practice, and turn it into a working solution. You don't need to study the services in advance: just tell us about your task, and we'll decide together where to start. For details, see the page How to engage.

Building and running solutions

This is where solutions are built, along with everything they need to work: knowledge bases, processing of incoming documents, automation of routine work, analytics, reporting, shared components, and the system for governing them.

Knowledge servicesDepartment and regulatory documents, gathered into a knowledge base that answers your questions.

What's included

  • a document corpus with owners and review dates
  • an assistant that answers with links to the sources
  • search across the corpus and keeping the sources up to date

What to bring

  • a knowledge base of procedures for lawyers, compliance, and accounting
  • hints for a contact center operator during a call
  • a base of answers to regulator requests

Form of work

An initiative for a new knowledge base; run-rate work for a new set of sources. The business owner decides what goes into the corpus and who uses it.

Input processingEmails, applications, scans, statements, and spreadsheets are read, broken down into fields, checked, and passed on; an employee handles the unusual cases.

What's included

  • intake and recognition of documents in any format
  • breaking down into fields, classification, checks against reference lists
  • handover to a system with a link to the source

What to bring

  • applications that are typed into a system by hand
  • partner registers in different formats
  • requests to a shared mailbox that need to be routed

Form of work

Run-rate work for one type of document; an initiative for the whole incoming flow.

More about this service ›

Workspace automationAI does the everyday work and an employee checks the result: routing, forms, reports, documents from templates.

What's included

  • classifying and routing requests
  • filling in forms and putting together summary reports
  • standard documents from templates

What to bring

  • preparing standard reports and forms
  • pulling data together into one working file
  • help with answering requests, with links to the sources

Form of work

Run-rate work for a single automation; an initiative for a whole process. Every automation has an employee who checks the result.

Analytics and decision supportData pipelines, dashboards, and analytical materials with traceable data lineage.

What's included

  • collecting and combining data from systems and documents
  • dashboards and regular analytics
  • tools for analysts

What to bring

  • a metrics pipeline from verified sources
  • variance analysis and a risk summary
  • materials for management decisions

Form of work

Run-rate work for a single data view; an initiative for a pipeline behind a regular report.

Management reporting and investor materialsMaterials for management and investors, based on verified data and templates, in the languages you need.

What's included

  • management reporting, plan-versus-actual analysis, and forecasts (FP&A)
  • presentations, disclosures, and answers to investors (IR)
  • templates, releases, versions, and languages

What to bring

  • monthly and quarterly releases
  • pre-filled materials for management
  • drafts of disclosures

Form of work

An initiative for a component that assembles the releases; run-rate work for a new template or a change to a release. An employee publishes the materials after checking them.

PlatformsShared components and environments that solutions run on, and their handover to those who will run them at scale.

What's included

  • shared components: knowledge base tools, an automation environment, evaluation tools
  • a sandbox for experiments
  • requirements for a shared AI platform

What to bring

  • an automation environment shared by several departments
  • evaluation tools for your scenarios
  • handing a component over to the team that will run it

Form of work

An initiative.

GovernanceThe whole governance framework, from an idea to a working solution.

What's included

  • strategy and ways of working, internal documents and processes
  • rules for using AI, acceptance criteria, risk tier
  • evaluation of solutions and vendors, monitoring of working solutions

What to bring

  • a strategy and a way of working for a department
  • rules and a map of checks before the work starts
  • monitoring of solutions that are already working

Form of work

An initiative for the whole framework; run-rate work for a single part.

Enablement

This is where we help people: employees learn to apply AI in their own work, and every solution is supported from launch to retirement.

Training and knowledge sharingRole-based training, help at the workplace, skill libraries, and communities of practice.

What's included

  • role-based training before you start working with AI
  • help and advice at the workplace
  • prompt and skill libraries, communities of practice

What to bring

  • “train our people, each for their own role”
  • “show us on the spot how to do this in our work”
  • “put together a skill library for our kind of work”

Form of work

Run-rate work.

Adoption and solution lifecycleSpecialists in the department and an adoption plan; handover to maintenance; support, review, and retirement.

What's included

  • an adoption plan: solutions, order, training, metrics
  • support for the specialists who lead adoption in their own department
  • a solution's life after launch: handover, support, review, retirement

What to bring

  • “help our employee lead the adoption”
  • “hand the product over to us together with the know-how”
  • “it's time to review the solution or retire it”

Form of work

Run-rate work for handover and support; an initiative for adoption across a whole department.

Two forms of work

Work on any service takes one of two forms. A small recurring request is run-rate work: a line on a standing card, one iteration, no start decision. Anything bigger is an initiative, which goes through the whole path, with a hypothesis test and decisions at checkpoints.
