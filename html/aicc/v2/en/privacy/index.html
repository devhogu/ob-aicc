Privacy

This page explains how the AI Competence Hub website handles information about its users. This statement covers every page of the site.

What the site collects

The site is a set of ready-made pages. It has no user accounts, no forms, and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not contact any service outside the infrastructure in which the organization hosts it.

  • Search. Search runs in your browser, over an index that is part of the site. Your query is not sent anywhere and leaves no trace.
  • Cookies. The site does not set any. If you choose a theme with the button in the site header, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It is not sent anywhere, and you can delete it by clearing the site's data in your browser.
  • Logs. The server that hosts the site keeps technical access logs for security and operations, under the organization's rules. The site itself does not store any information about users.

Feedback

Feedback links open your email program with a ready-made message to the organization's work address. The subject line contains the page ID, and the body contains the page's title and address. The message is sent only when you send it yourself; until then, you can see all of it and change it.

Once sent, the message becomes ordinary work correspondence of the organization. We use it to reply to you and to improve the site.

Links

The site has no links to third-party resources, other than the publicly available sources mentioned in the text so that you can find them. Visiting them is governed by the organization's rules.

People and data

The pages name roles and positions, not people. The site contains no customer or employee data, no confidential figures, and none of the organization's program code.

Questions

Send any questions about this statement to the contact address at the bottom of the page. This statement is updated together with the site, and the site version is shown at the bottom of every page.
