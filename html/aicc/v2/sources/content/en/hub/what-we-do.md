The Hub's job is to bring together in one place everything that helps you use AI at work: building and running solutions, enablement, and reliability checks. Here you can find the service you need, see how work on projects is going, and pick up ready-made assets. An idea is first tested in practice, and whatever proves itself and is needed by many is handed over to those who will support it at scale.

## Areas of work {#services}

### Building and running solutions

| Category | What's included |
| --- | --- |
| [Knowledge services](page:services#knowledge-services) | Department and regulatory documents gathered into a knowledge base that answers your questions |
| [Input processing](page:services/input-processing) | Letters, applications, scans, statements, spreadsheets, and forms that come into the department are read, broken down into fields, checked for completeness and consistency, and passed on in the right format. An employee handles the non-standard cases |
| [Workspace automation](page:services#workplace-automation) | Routing, forms, reports, documents from templates, help with handling requests. AI does the work, and an employee checks the result |
| [Analytics and decision support](page:services#analytics) | Data pipelines, dashboards, and analytical materials that show where the data came from: a basis for decisions |
| [Management reporting and investor materials](page:services#reporting) | Materials for management and investors: management reporting, plan-vs-actual analysis and forecasts (financial planning and analysis, FP&A), presentations, disclosures, and answers to investors (investor relations, IR). Prepared from verified data and templates, in the languages needed |
| [Platforms](page:services#platforms) | Shared components and environments that solutions are built on, and the handover of finished components to those who will support them |
| [Governance](page:services/governance) | The whole governance framework, from idea to working solution: strategy and working principles, internal documents and processes, rules for using AI and acceptance criteria, risk level, assessment of solutions and suppliers, monitoring of running solutions, and incident analysis. |

### Enablement

| Category | What's included |
| --- | --- |
| [Training and knowledge sharing](page:services#training) | Role-based training, help at the workplace, skill libraries, communities of practice, and publications |
