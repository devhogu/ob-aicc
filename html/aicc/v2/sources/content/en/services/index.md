The Hub helps departments see where AI is really useful, test an idea in practice, and turn it into a working solution. You don't need to study the services in advance: just tell us about your task, and we'll decide together where to start. For details, see the page [How to engage](page:services/how-to-engage).

## Building and running solutions {#build}

This is where solutions are built, along with everything they need to work: knowledge bases, processing of incoming documents, automation of routine work, analytics, reporting, shared components, and the system for governing them.

## Two forms of work {#forms}

Work on any service takes one of two forms. A small recurring request is [[run-rate|run-rate work]]: a line on a standing card, one [[iteration|iteration]], no start decision. Anything bigger is an [[initiative|initiative]], which goes through the whole [path](page:hub/how-we-work#path), with a hypothesis test and decisions at checkpoints.
