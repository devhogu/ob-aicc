Kyrgyz Republic: the ministry responsible for digital development and the acts on digitalization

The body that sets the direction of digitalization in the Kyrgyz Republic and the rules for information systems and electronic services, and through which the national policy on AI is formed as acts on the subject are adopted.

1. Identity

Item Entry
Kind Ministry and the acts and programs of digital development
Issued or overseen by The ministry responsible for digital development of the Kyrgyz Republic; the Cabinet of Ministers
Jurisdiction Kyrgyz Republic
Status Standing body; the programs of digital development, the rules for state information systems and electronic government, and the acts on digital matters as adopted

2. What it sets

¶2.1. The programs and the concepts of digital development; the rules for state information systems, electronic government, and electronic identification; and the policy on data and on AI as it is adopted.

3. Whom it reaches

¶3.1. State bodies; the economy as the acts provide.

4. Relevance to the Bank

¶4.1. Sets the frame in which AI is adopted in the country and the interfaces the Bank uses with the state, among them identification and electronic services; to be followed as acts on AI are adopted.

5. How the charter relates to it

¶5.1. The Statement of Intent commits the Bank to satisfy the legal and regulatory requirements that apply to the use of AI as they are adopted; the Research and exploration category of AICC keeps the watch on them.

    National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic

    The central bank and the supervisor of the Bank. Its regulations govern how a bank in the Kyrgyz Republic runs its systems, manages its risks, uses third parties, operates payments, and treats its customers, and they apply to every system that carries a banking process, whether or not it uses AI.

  • Reference

    Kyrgyz Republic: the Law on Personal Information and the authorized body for personal data

    The personal data law of the Kyrgyz Republic and the body that oversees it. The law sets the grounds, the purposes, the consent, the protection, and the transfer rules for the processing of personal data, and the authorized body registers holders, handles complaints, and inspects.

  • Reference

    Kyrgyz Republic: the financial intelligence body and the legislation against money laundering and the financing of terrorism

    The law and the body that oversee the duties of banks and other reporting entities to know their customers, monitor transactions, and report suspicious activity, in line with the international standards against money laundering and the financing of terrorism.
