Russian Federation: the Federal Law on Personal Data (152-FZ)

The personal data law of the Russian Federation, notable for its requirement that the personal data of Russian citizens be recorded and stored in databases located in the Russian Federation and for its conditions on transfers abroad.

1. Identity

Item Entry
Kind Federal law
Issued or overseen by Roskomnadzor, the federal supervisor for communications, information technology, and mass media
Jurisdiction Russian Federation
Status In force since 2006, amended many times; localization and cross-border transfer rules strengthened over the years

2. What it sets

¶2.1. Lawful grounds and consent for processing, the rights of data subjects, security measures, the localization of the data of citizens, notification of the supervisor, and conditions and notifications for cross-border transfers.

3. Whom it reaches

¶3.1. Operators that process the personal data of citizens of the Russian Federation, wherever they are. It reaches a provider or a partner of the Bank that processes such data.

4. Relevance to the Bank

¶4.1. Relevant to the choice of providers and partners that handle Russian personal data, and to any service of the Bank that reaches residents of the Russian Federation.

5. How the charter relates to it

¶5.1. The data classification rules of the AI Policy and the provider check address where data is processed and kept; the Data Sharing Arrangement of the Charter governs data that leaves the Bank.
