Regulators and acts

The Bank operates in the Kyrgyz Republic, within a region and a field where the rules for AI and data are set by many bodies. This page lists the regulators and the acts that the Bank is aware of, by jurisdiction: who they are, what they set, whom they reach, and why they matter to the Bank. The Knowledge base page Acts and compliance states what the applicable ones require and how AICC complies.

This is the first edition, a curated list for global awareness, to be verified with the Control Function Contacts of compliance and legal. An entry here does not mean that the instrument binds the Bank; the column "Reach" says whom it binds, and the Acts and compliance page says what applies.

1. Kyrgyz Republic

Body Instrument Sets Reach Why it matters to the Bank
National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic Banking legislation and the regulations of the National Bank on information security, outsourcing, operational risk, and consumer protection The rules for banks on systems, providers, incidents, and the treatment of customers Banks and financial institutions of the Kyrgyz Republic The supervisor of the Bank; its regulations apply to every system that carries a banking process
The authorized state body for personal data Law on Personal Information, and its by-laws The lawful basis, the purpose, the consent, the protection, and the transfer of personal data Every processor of personal data in the Kyrgyz Republic Applies to every use of AI that touches customer or employee data
Ministry responsible for digital development The programs and acts of digital development, and the rules for information systems and electronic government The direction of digitalization and the rules for state information systems State bodies; the economy as the acts provide Sets the frame in which AI is adopted in the country; to be followed as acts on AI are adopted
The financial intelligence body Legislation on combating money laundering and the financing of terrorism Know-your-customer, monitoring, and reporting duties Banks and other reporting entities The AI that assists these processes keeps their controls and audit trail

2. Kazakhstan

Body Instrument Sets Reach Why it matters to the Bank
Ministry responsible for digital development, innovations, and aerospace industry Law on Artificial Intelligence; the national concept for the development of AI; Law on Personal Data and Their Protection The first dedicated AI law of the region, with the classification of systems and the duties of their owners; the rules for personal data Kazakhstan; its data law reaches processors of the data of its residents A neighbouring jurisdiction with a dedicated AI law, a model for what the region may adopt
National Bank of Kazakhstan; Agency for Regulation and Development of the Financial Market Regulation of the financial market, including the use of technology and data by financial organizations The rules for banks and financial organizations The financial sector of Kazakhstan Supervisory practice of a neighbouring market
Astana International Financial Centre Its own regulatory framework for financial services, data, and technology Rules for the participants of the centre The participants of the centre A regional centre whose rules shape practice in the region

3. Russian Federation

Body Instrument Sets Reach Why it matters to the Bank
The Government and the President of the Russian Federation National Strategy for the Development of Artificial Intelligence; experimental legal regimes for AI The direction of AI development and the regimes under which AI is trialled The Russian Federation A large neighbouring jurisdiction whose approach shapes the region and the providers in it
Roskomnadzor Federal Law on Personal Data (152-FZ) and its requirements on localization and cross-border transfer The processing, the localization, and the transfer of personal data Processors of the data of residents of the Russian Federation Reaches a provider or a partner that processes such data; relevant to the choice of providers
Bank of Russia Its reports and guidance on the application of AI in the financial market The supervisory view on AI in finance The financial sector of the Russian Federation Supervisory practice of a neighbouring market
AI Alliance Russia Code of Ethics in the Field of AI Voluntary ethical commitments of its signatories Signatory organizations A reference for ethical practice in the region

4. European Union

Body Instrument Sets Reach Why it matters to the Bank
European Parliament and Council; the European AI Office of the European Commission Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, the Artificial Intelligence Act A risk-based regime: prohibited practices, high-risk systems with conformity, oversight, and transparency duties, obligations for general-purpose models, applied in phases from 2025 Providers and deployers of AI placed on the EU market or whose output is used in the EU The reference regime of the field; it reaches a provider or a partner that serves the EU, and it shapes the models and the services the Bank buys
European Data Protection Board and the national authorities Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the General Data Protection Regulation The processing and the transfer of personal data, including automated decisions Processors of the data of persons in the EU Reaches a provider or a partner that processes such data
European Supervisory Authorities and the European Commission Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, the Digital Operational Resilience Act, and the guidance of the European Banking Authority ICT risk, incident reporting, testing, and third-party risk for financial entities Financial entities of the EU and their ICT providers The reference for operational resilience and provider risk in finance

5. United States

Body Instrument Sets Reach Why it matters to the Bank
National Institute of Standards and Technology AI Risk Management Framework and its profiles A voluntary framework for the governance of AI risk Voluntary; used worldwide The framework the charter draws on for the Risk Tiers and the gates
The federal executive and the Office of Management and Budget Executive orders and memoranda on the development and the federal use of AI, which changed in 2025 The federal policy on AI Federal agencies; the market through procurement The direction of the largest market for AI models and services; follow the current instruments
Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, Consumer Financial Protection Bureau Supervisory guidance on model risk management (SR 11-7) and guidance on adverse action notices and the use of AI in credit The validation of models in banks; the explanation of credit decisions to consumers Banks and lenders of the United States The reference practice for model validation and for explainability in lending
State legislatures State laws on AI, among them the Colorado Artificial Intelligence Act, and state privacy laws Duties for developers and deployers of high-risk AI; consumer rights The states concerned A sign of where regulation of consumer-facing AI is going

6. Global bodies

Body Instrument Sets Reach Why it matters to the Bank
Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development OECD Principles on Artificial Intelligence; the OECD AI Policy Observatory Intergovernmental principles for trustworthy AI, and the reference definition of an AI system Adhering countries; used worldwide The common ground of the Principles of Application
UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence A global normative instrument on the ethics of AI The member states, including the Kyrgyz Republic The ethical reference the Bank's values align with
Council of Europe Framework Convention on Artificial Intelligence and Human Rights, Democracy and the Rule of Law The first international treaty on AI, open to non-member states The states that sign and ratify it The treaty-level reference for the rights of persons affected by AI
G7 Hiroshima AI Process, the code of conduct for organizations developing advanced AI systems Voluntary commitments for the developers of advanced models The developers that adhere Shapes the conduct of the providers whose models the Bank uses
Financial Stability Board; Basel Committee on Banking Supervision; Bank for International Settlements Reports on AI in the financial sector and on the digitalization of finance The view of the standard-setters on the risks and the supervisory expectations of AI in finance Standard-setting; implemented by national supervisors The source of what the supervisor of the Bank is likely to expect
International Organization for Standardization and International Electrotechnical Commission ISO/IEC 42001, 23894, and 22989 The management system, the risk management, and the vocabulary of AI Voluntary; certifiable The standards the control framework of AICC is built to answer
