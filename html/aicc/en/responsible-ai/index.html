Responsible AI

The Bank uses AI under rules that are proportionate to risk. Each Solution has a Risk Tier, is checked or validated before use, and is overseen by people who can stop it. The AI Policy states the rules, the Risk Tiers, the review of providers, the handling of AI Incidents, and the Exceptions, within the AI Risk Appetite Statement of the Bank.
