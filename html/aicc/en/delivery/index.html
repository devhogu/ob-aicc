Delivery

AICC delivers in small steps on a common cadence: the Capabilities and Features of an Initiative are built in Iterations of one month within Program Increments of one quarter, the work is visible and limited in progress, and a Solution is checked or validated, accepted by the Team, and accepted by its Domain Owner before it reaches its users and for the rest of its life.
