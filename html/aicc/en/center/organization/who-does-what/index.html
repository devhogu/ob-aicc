Who does what

OverviewPlace in the BankRolesWho does whatAppointmentsGrowth

Along the life of an Initiative and a Solution, each Role is responsible, accountable, consulted, or informed for each activity, and the pattern is the same from the first contact to the retirement. This part states the pattern in brief, by family of activity; the Organization guide holds the full responsibility matrix, activity by activity.

2. How to read it

¶2.1. Accountable is the one Role that answers for the activity; Responsible does the work; Consulted is asked before; Informed is told after. The two rules that recur: the Role that states the value is not the Role that ranks, and the person who builds is not the person who checks, validates, accepts for the business, or releases. Where the Team is small and one person holds several Roles, the matrix is read by Role, and the limits accepted are those stated on The Roles.
