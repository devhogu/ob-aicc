Solution Engineer

Owns a Solution end to end: designs it, decides its architecture, builds it, deploys it, and runs it with the Domains; keeps the work visible; coaches Domain Experts; checks the work of others

Profile

Purpose
Owns a Solution end to end with the Domains
Main responsibilities
Designs the architecture, builds, deploys, and runs a Solution, checks the work of others, coaches Domain Experts, and keeps work visible
Authority
How a Solution is designed and built; the approval of Features at Iteration Planning; the order in which the team pulls work within the agreed ranking
Reports in
The Competence Center Lead for the Competence Center; otherwise the own line
