Guides

OverviewLearning pathsTemplates and formsGuidesPlaybooks and lessonsActs and complianceQuestions

A guide explains a workflow or an area to the people who use it: who does what, when, and what remains on record. It sets no rules of its own. The five guides appear as tabs beside their subjects, under the workflow or document they explain. This page lists them by the question each answers.

1. The guides by question

If you ask Read Beside
How does a function start with the Competence Center, what does the Competence Center commit to, and what happens after delivery Guide: Engagement How to engage, in Services
How does an item move from a need to a retired Solution, and who decides at each step Guide: Service delivery Service delivery, in Delivery
When is each event held, what does each beat leave on record, and what happens when the calendar is not clean Guide: Cadence Cadence, in Delivery
How is the unit directed, reported, and controlled, and how is each control tested Guide: Unit governance Unit governance, in Governance
Who is responsible for what, what are the Roles and their profiles, and how are people appointed and changed Guide: Organization Operating Model, in Organization

2. The guides and the courses

¶2.1. The courses on this site provide the overview: Responsible AI, Services, Portfolio, Delivery, Governance, Organization. The guides provide step-by-step explanations beneath them, with tables showing who does what, when, and what goes on record. The documents set the rules. A reader moves from a course to a guide when the question becomes "how exactly" and from a guide to a document when it becomes "by what rule".
