O!Bank · AI Competence Center

Welcome to the AI Competence Center

We are the Bank's own Artificial Intelligence Lab: a Center of Competence and Solution Hub that proves what AI can do for the people who serve our customers, sets the practice by which the Bank adopts it within the standards that the Bank sets, and keeps every use of it accountable to people.

Your AI journey begins here!
