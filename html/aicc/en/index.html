Charter and governance of the unit

AI Competence Center

The mission of AICC is to enable the Bank to adopt AI as a governed capability that delivers measured value. This site is the charter of AICC: its intent, its method, its organization, and its controls.

AICC at a glance

  • What AICC does

    Explores, trials, and proves the use of AI with the functions, and proposes what the Bank should adopt at scale.

  • →

  • How AICC works

    Takes in Initiatives, decides on them, delivers Solutions, and measures the flow.

  • →

  • How AICC is safeguarded

    Sets the rules of use, assigns Risk Tiers, controls and reports.

Strategic Priorities

  • PRI-1

    Customer intelligence

    To develop an end-to-end understanding of each customer across every service and product of the Bank.

  • PRI-2

    Business intelligence

    To provide AI-supported analysis to those who direct the business.

  • PRI-3

    Adoption within Domains

    To enable each Domain to use AI in support of its own processes.

  • PRI-4

    Expertise at the point of work

    To make the Bank's current and compliant expertise available to each employee and, where appropriate, to each customer, at the point where it is needed.

  • PRI-5

    AI in banking operations and systems

    To embed AI in the flow of banking operations and in the banking systems that carry them, with decisions remaining with qualified persons.

  • PRI-6

    Information technology operations and service lifecycle

    To apply AI to the daily operation of technology, separately from the development of software.

  • PRI-7

    Software engineering

    To apply AI throughout the software engineering lifecycle.

Maturity Roadmap

  1. Level 1FoundationAI use is governed before it is extended
  2. Level 2Controlled adoptionEmployees have secure access to AI, and knowledge capture begins
  3. Level 3EmbeddedAI operates within selected processes and systems, under human decision
  4. Level 4ScaledOne governed AI Platform is reused across Domains
  5. Level 5AgenticBounded tasks are delegated to agents under continuous evaluation

Sections

  • About AICC

    AICC is the internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank for the adoption of artificial intelligence.

  • Responsible AI

    The Bank uses AI under rules that are proportionate to risk, and it asks its people to understand the technology they use.

  • Services

    AICC serves the functions and units of the Bank as their internal consulting and innovation lab.

  • Portfolio

    The Portfolio decides what AICC works on.

  • Delivery

    AICC delivers in small steps on a common cadence: the Capabilities and Features of an Initiative are built in Iterations of one month within Program Increments of one quarter, the work is visible and limited in progress, and a Solution is checked or validated, accepted by the Team, and accepted by its Domain Owner before it reaches its users.

  • Governance and oversight

    AICC is controlled, reported, and assured through five control loops, a catalogue of thirty-two controls, and the records that evidence them.

  • Organization

    AICC is a joint team, led by the AICC Lead and formed of the people whom the functions of the Bank assign to it.

  • Knowledge base

    The working knowledge of AICC: the thirteen templates that give the form of every record, the guides that explain the workflows, the acts and policies that the use of AI answers to and how AICC complies, and the publications of AICC for the Bank.

  • Reference

    The Vocabulary defines the terms of the charter, the Document Catalog lists its documents, the change history records their revisions, Records and systems states where each record is kept, the Industry body of knowledge lists the frameworks the charter draws on, and Regulators and acts positions the bodies and the instruments of AI and data regulation, global, European, and regional, with a page for each.
