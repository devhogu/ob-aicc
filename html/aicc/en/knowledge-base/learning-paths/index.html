Learning paths

OverviewLearning pathsTemplates and formsGuidesPlaybooks and lessonsActs and complianceQuestions

A learning path is a reading order for a role: what to read, in what sequence, and what the reader can do at the end. The paths use this site's courses, each of which can be read in an hour, and point to the rules each role needs. Everyone starts with the first path.

1. Everyone: the orientation

Step Read So that you can
1 About AICC, with What we do and How we work Describe AICC, its mission and values, and how an Engagement runs
2 Responsible AI, the course Explain what AI is today, its value and risks, and the Bank's rules for its use
3 Services, the areas Identify what a function can ask AICC for
4 Questions people ask Answer the common questions yourself
5 The Vocabulary, kept at hand Use the charter's terms as defined

2. A head of function or a Domain Owner

Step Read So that you can
1 How to engage, with the Engagement workflow and guide Bring a need, understand the six steps, and know your commitments and those of AICC
2 The Portfolio, the course, parts 2 to 5 Know how your business case is approved, ranked, probed, and continued
3 The Roles and Who does what Understand what you decide as Domain Owner and what the Domain Expert does
4 How the Bank applies it Understand the Risk Tier your Solution will carry and what it requires of you
5 The Initiative Brief and the Service Agreement Complete the two forms used at the start

3. A Solution Engineer or a member of a Team

Step Read So that you can
1 Delivery, the course, all parts Work with the cadence, boards, gates, and loops
2 The Solution Lifecycle Model, the rule Understand the states, the Stages, and the conditions for moving on
3 Service delivery and Cadence, the workflows with their guides Understand what each step and event leaves on record
4 The Experiment workflow, The life of a Service, Service operations Run a probe in the Lab and a Service after release
5 The Solution Definition, the Acceptance Checklist, and Delivery measures Maintain a Solution's record and read the measures

4. A Domain Expert

Step Read So that you can
1 The orientation, path 1
2 How to engage and Adoption and lifecycle management Understand your role in an Engagement and in the adoption of your Domain
3 Quality, verification, and release Understand the acceptances and your role in the evaluation set and with the first users
4 Training and knowledge sharing Know what training and coaching AICC provides and the skill library for your line of work

5. The Executive Sponsor and the AI Steering Committee

Step Read So that you can
1 The Statement of Intent and the Strategy Maintain the framework: the priorities, the Envelopes, the Guardrails, the roadmap
2 The Charter Understand the mandate, limits, funding, risk appetite, and what is reported
3 Strategy and investment and The loops and the governance Run the yearly and quarterly Steering of the Portfolio
4 Governance, the course Run the control loops, read the Quarterly Report, and know what is escalated to you
5 The Quarterly Report and the Decision Record Approve the report and decide whether to bring it to the Board Committee or the Board; record a decision

6. A Control Function Contact

Step Read So that you can
1 How the Bank applies it and the AI Policy Understand the Risk Tiers, gates, providers, incidents, and Exceptions
2 The AI risk and control workflow Understand where your clearance, validation, and stop fit in the flow
3 Decisions and escalation and Controls and the control catalog Understand your remit, its finality, and the controls you sign
4 Acts and compliance and the Regulators and acts Confirm what applies and keep the pages current
5 The Control Sign-Off and the Acceptance Checklist Record a clearance, a validation, or a stop

7. Internal audit

Step Read So that you can
1 Governance, the course, with Records, evidence, and assurance Understand the loops, controls, and where to find the evidence
2 The control catalog and the Unit governance set, with the guide on how each control is tested Test a control by its reference
3 Records and systems and the Change history Find each record and each document's revision
4 The Roles and People and appointments Check the separations and appointments

8. Human resources

Step Read So that you can
1 Organization, the course Understand the Roles, profiles, who appoints whom, and how people join and leave
2 The Guide: Organization Use the profiles, the responsibility matrix, and the people records
3 The Appointments Record Maintain the record of the Holders
