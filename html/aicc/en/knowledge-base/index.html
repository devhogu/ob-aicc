Acts, compliance, and publications

  • Page

    Package Definition (draft template)

    A draft template, proposed for the templates of the charter. A package is the reusable asset that an Engagement leaves: a method, a kit, an engine, a catalog of automations, or a template set, which the next Engagement takes in days where the first took weeks. The Package Definition is the record of one package. Until it is adopted into the charter by a decision record, this page is the draft form.

  • Page

    Guides

    The five guides in one place: who does what, when, and what is left on record, each beside its workflow

  • Page

    Acts and compliance

    The use of AI at the Bank answers to law, to regulation, and to the policies of the Bank. This page states, for each act or policy that applies, what it requires of a Solution and where the AI Policy and the controls of AICC answer it. It is a working reference kept with the Control Function Contacts; it states no rule of its own, and the acts and the policies prevail.

  • Page

    Publications

    What AICC learns it writes down and makes available to the Bank: the documents of the charter, the Proposals that the Bank has decided on, the method notes and playbooks from the Engagements, and the lessons of what did not work. This page is the list.
