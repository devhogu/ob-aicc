Measures and reporting

OverviewDecisionsControl loopsSteerings and bodiesControlsRecords and evidenceMeasures

Governance reads a small set of measures at each loop and reports the whole each quarter. This part states what the unit measures about its own control, what each loop reads, what the Quarterly Report contains, and how the Measures of the Maturity Levels sit above it. The definitions of the measures of the Portfolio and of delivery are on their own pages; this page states the measures of governance itself.

1. The measures of governance

The measures are those of the Operating Model 6.11. The AICC Lead owns each, reads it at the Steering that the table names, and records the reading in the Steering Summary, and the first reading is its baseline.

2. What each loop reads

¶2.1. The Weekly Review reads the flow and raises what it cannot settle; it carries the control of the intake and the limit on work in progress (C-23). The monthly Steering reads the sample of Decisions, the deficiencies, the Exceptions, the gate decisions, and the acceptances and live reviews of the month. The quarterly Steering reads the Control Matrix and the governance measures, the quarterly risk check, the reassessments due, the incidents reconciled, the access review, the measures of the Portfolio and of delivery for the quarter, and the Maturity Level. The yearly Steering reads the findings of the year, the documents, the appetite, and the appointments.

3. The Quarterly Report

¶3.1. The Quarterly Report is the only periodic report of the unit, the report of AICC to the Executive Sponsor: the AICC Lead prepares it and takes it to the quarterly Steering, and the Executive Sponsor approves it. The Executive Sponsor may bring it to the Board Committee or the Board when the Executive Sponsor judges it useful or the Board asks; no quarterly submission is required. It states, with each figure traced to a Record or a governed source with its date: each Active Initiative and its decision; the benefit confirmed against the Envelope of each Strategic Priority; the value scored for the PI Objectives and the predictability trend; the Domains and employees using approved Solutions; the AI Incidents and control breaches; the control status and the open deficiencies; the risks accepted beyond appetite; the reliance on providers; and the Maturity Level of each priority. Its form is the Quarterly Report template.

4. The Measures of the Maturity Levels

¶4.1. Above the measures of governance stand the Measures of the Maturity Levels of the Statement of Intent, by which a Strategic Priority advances: at level one the AI Policy activated, the Control Function Contacts appointed, each known AI use recorded and tiered, and the baselines established; and from level two on, the shares of employees trained and with access, the processes with AI embedded and their change against baseline, the benefits against the Envelope, the predictability of delivery, the audit and regulatory findings, and, for AI agents, the actions stopped or reversed and the evaluation coverage. The quarterly Steering confirms the Maturity Level of each priority on them; the yearly Steering sets the targets.

5. Rule source

Operating Model 6, 6.11, 7.6, and 8; AICC Charter 7; Statement of Intent 11.3 and 12; AI Policy 3.3 and 5; the Quarterly Report template.
