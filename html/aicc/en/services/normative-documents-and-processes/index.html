Normative documents and processes

Every function writes and keeps normative documents: policies, procedures, regulations, instructions, runbooks, and the process descriptions behind them. AICC helps HR, legal, accounting, compliance, operations, technology, and any other function draft, align, and maintain them with AI, in the style of the Bank and in one voice, under the ownership of the function.

1. What it is

¶1.1. The line covers the drafting of a new document from the facts the function gives and the acts it answers to; the rewrite of an existing set for consistency, style, and coverage; the mapping and the drawing of a process from the documents and the artifacts of the function; the runbooks and the checklists that operations and technology keep; and the translation of a set across Kyrgyz, Russian, and English.

¶1.2. AICC does not set the substance. The function states what the rule is; AICC states it well, once, in its place, and shows where it conflicts with another document.

2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the line, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

  • A policy, a procedure, a regulation, an instruction, or a runbook drafted from the facts the function gives
  • A corpus rewritten for one voice, with a consistency and conflict report
  • A process mapped and drawn from the documents and artifacts of the function, with the records each step leaves
  • A bilingual set across Kyrgyz, Russian, and English
  • A glossary of the function maintained with its documents

3. What the function receives

¶2.1. Documents ready for the approval route of the Bank, each with its purpose, scope, definitions, numbered clauses, roles, records, and change log; a process set drawn as flows and loops with the records each step leaves; a consistency report over the function's set, with the conflicts and the gaps; and a maintained glossary.

4. How it runs

¶3.1. Run-rate work goes through the front door and is done in one Iteration: a procedure, a runbook, a review of a set. A rewrite of a whole corpus is a program with a study first. The function names an owner for each document and reviews each draft; the Control Function concerned reviews a document that touches its remit.

5. What it leads to

¶4.1. A function whose documents say one thing in one voice, map to its processes, and can be found and maintained; and a process set that its people and its auditors can follow.

6. The reusable package

¶5.1. The style and the templates of the Bank's normative documents, the drafting and consistency assistants, the process-drawing kit, and the glossary; re-used for every function.

7. Run-rate or program

¶6.1. Run-rate for single documents and runbooks; a program for a corpus.

8. Who decides

¶7.1. The owner of the document in the function decides its content and takes it through the approval route of the Bank. AICC drafts and checks.

9. Rule source

Business Model 4; Statement of Intent 9.4 and 9.5. A clause for this service line is proposed for Business Model 4.

  • Service line

    Strategy and governance office

    What AICC built for itself it builds for any unit of the Bank: a strategy, a charter, an operating and governance model, a process set with its templates, and a portal and repository that hold them, developed with AI in weeks and maintained with it afterwards. AICC acts here as the strategy office and the governance office of the function, and the function keeps the ownership.

  • Service line

    Knowledge services

    The documents of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, turned into a governed knowledge base that it can ask. Legal, compliance, accounting, operations, and any function with a corpus get an assistant that answers from approved, current, cited sources, for its own people and, where the function decides, for its clients.

  • Service line

    Workplace automation

    The routine work of a function done by assistants under human validation: routing requests, reading and filling forms, processing reports, consolidating inputs, and producing documents from templates. The person validates and promotes; the assistant prepares.

  • Service line

    Information and decision support

    Facts from many sources prepared, consolidated, and presented so that a function decides on data: the pipelines that collect and transform, the dashboards that show, and the analytical and research tooling that supports the work.

  • Service line

    Content and document engines

    Public, investor, and management material generated from governed data and templates, pre-filled for people to finish: periodic reports, disclosures, investor material, standard letters and documents, in the languages the Bank uses.

  • Service line

    Enablement at the workplace

    Each person able to use AI for their own routine, within the rules: training by role, coaching at the desk, Domain Experts who carry adoption in their function, prompt and skill libraries per line of work, and communities of practice.
