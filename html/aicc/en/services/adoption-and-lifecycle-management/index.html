Adoption and lifecycle management

Training and knowledge sharingAdoption and lifecycle management

A Solution is not adopted when it is released; it is adopted when the function uses it, keeps it, and knows when to change or retire it. AICC supports the adoption of AI in each Domain through its Domain Experts, and manages the life of each Solution after delivery: hand-over, support, revision, and retirement, with the health of the Solution read at each review.

1. What it is

¶1.1. The category covers the appointment and the coaching of the Domain Experts who carry adoption in their Domain; the adoption plan of a Domain, which Solutions its people take up, in which order, with what training and what measures; the life of a Solution after delivery by its type, the hand-over of a Product, the support at the agreed level, the revision through the Portfolio Backlog, and the retirement; and the life of a Service through its seven states, with its service operations.

¶1.2. Adoption and lifecycle management are where the measures of the Statement of Intent are read: the Domains and employees using approved Solutions, the processes with AI embedded, the change in time and errors against the baseline.

2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the category, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

  • A Domain Expert appointed and coached for a Domain
  • An adoption plan for a Domain: the Solutions, the order, the training, the measures
  • The hand-over of a Product to its consumer, with the knowledge to use and keep it
  • Support at the agreed level, through Service Management
  • A revision of a Solution through the Portfolio Backlog, or its retirement for a consumer
  • The operation of a Service through its states, with its health read at each review

3. What the function receives

¶3.1. A Domain that adopts AI in its own processes with a named Domain Expert and a plan; Solutions that are used, supported, and retired in order; and the health signals that tell the Domain Owner and the Steering what each Solution is worth.

4. How it runs

¶4.1. Run-rate: the Domain Expert is coached in the Engagements and the communities of practice; the hand-over and the support follow the Service Agreement; the live review of each Solution takes place at the Iteration Review and Demo; a revision enters the Program Backlog; a retirement is decided by the Domain Owner. An adoption program for a Domain is a program with a study first.

5. What it leads to

¶5.1. Domains that use AI in their own processes with governed knowledge, within the shared principles and standards, and Solutions whose life is managed rather than left to drift.

6. The reusable package

¶6.1. The adoption plan format, the hand-over checklist, the run-book template of a Service, and the health signals.

7. Run-rate or program

¶7.1. Run-rate for the hand-over, the support, and the reviews; a program for the adoption of a Domain.

8. Who decides

¶8.1. The Domain Owners appoint their Domain Experts and decide the adoption within their Domains, the acceptance of a Solution, and its retirement; the Executive Sponsor decides for a Service across Domains; the AICC Lead decides whether a change needs a new check or validation.

9. Rule source

Statement of Intent 10.1 and 11.3; Business Model 4.2; Solution Lifecycle Model 8; Operating Model 4.
