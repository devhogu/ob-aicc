Advise and formulate

OverviewAdvise and formulateBuild and runEnablementAssurance

The strategy, governance, and process work that AICC drafts with AI for a function, which owns the substance: the direction and the mandate of a unit and its normative documents and processes; with them the watch on the field, and the business cases and scenarios that the Portfolio decides on.

1. The categories of the area

Category Coverage Mode
Strategy and governance Strategy, charter, operating and governance model, portal and repository of a function or an Initiative, drafted with AI, as AICC did for itself Initiative
Normatives and processes Policies, procedures, regulations, runbooks, and process descriptions drafted, aligned, and maintained with AI for a function Run-rate work for single documents and runbooks; an Initiative for a corpus
Research and exploration Regulatory and technology watch with digests, trials in the Lab, partnering with organizations and providers Run-rate work for the watch and the digests; an Initiative for a trial
Business cases and scenarios The discovery of needs, scenarios with their problem, Solution, and leading indicators, the audit of readiness and of sources, and the business case the Portfolio decides on Run-rate work for a single scenario or an audit; an Initiative for a Domain roadmap. The study itself is the first phase of every Initiative

¶1.1. A category is a direction along which AICC provides services, with examples on its page; what AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement. Run-rate work is a small, repeatable request that is done within one Iteration (Business Model 4.7); an Initiative starts with a study and a business case.

2. How a function engages the area

¶2.1. A function brings its need to the AICC Lead in any form. The need is screened at the front door on the problem it answers, its size, its likely Risk Tier, and whether a Package or a Solution of the catalog answers it already; its service category proposes the mode, run-rate work or an Initiative, and the AICC Lead confirms it (Business Model 7.2). The page How to engage states the steps, the commitment, and the support levels; the page The modes and the front door in the Portfolio states the modes.

3. What the area leaves behind

¶3.1. An Engagement in the area leaves its lessons and, where one results, a Package, such as a method, a kit, an engine, or a catalog entry, that the next function takes in days where the first took weeks (Business Model 4.4). The form of a Package Definition and of a catalog entry is stated on the page Service catalog: the form; the Packages themselves are kept in the Portfolio.

  • Service category · 4 parts

    Strategy and governance

    AICC builds for any unit of the Bank what it built for itself: a strategy, a charter and mandate, an operating and governance model with its roles, decisions, control loops, and controls, and the portal and repository that publish them. AICC develops these with AI in weeks and maintains them with it afterward. AICC drafts them for the function, and the function owns their substance; AICC decides none of it (Business Model 4.10).

  • Service category · 5 parts

    Knowledge services

    The documents of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, turned into a governed knowledge base that it can ask. Legal, compliance, accounting, operations, and any function with a corpus get an assistant that answers from approved, current, cited sources, for its own people and, where the function decides, for its clients.

  • Service category · 2 parts

    Training and knowledge sharing

    AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches them in the workplace, builds prompt and skill libraries for each line of work, runs communities of practice, and publishes what it learns so the next function starts further ahead.

  • Service category · 4 parts

    Policies, controls, criteria

    The use of AI in a function answers to the AI Policy of the Bank, to the controls of AICC, and to criteria that say when a Solution is good enough and safe enough to use. AICC helps a function state its own rules of use within the policy, map the controls that apply to its Solutions, and define the acceptance and evaluation criteria before any build, so that compliance is designed in and not inspected in.

  • Page · 3 parts

    How to engage

    A function does not need a form, funding of its own, or a plan to start with AICC. It brings a need. From there the Engagement follows six steps, each with a record and a decision that the function can see.

  • Page · 2 parts

    Service catalog

    The catalog of AICC lists what AICC runs, has delivered, is proving, and can re-deploy. This page states the form of the catalog: the two kinds of entry, the fields of each, and the state or the status of each. The live catalog is an instance of this form, kept in the Portfolio of AICC (Business Model 4.4); this site states the form only (Document Catalog 5.4). One entry of each kind is shown as an illustration.
