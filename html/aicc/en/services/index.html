Services

AICC serves the functions and units of the Bank as their internal consulting and innovation lab. Its services run along fifteen categories in four areas, read here by tab; How to engage states the steps and the commitment; the Service catalog states the form of an entry and the service model; the Business Model is the document that states the offer and prevails.

OverviewAdvise and formulateBuild and runEnablementAssurance

An area is the posture of AICC toward the work; a category is one kind of service a function asks for, with examples on its page. The areas are read by tab, and each area page lists its categories with their coverage and their mode. What AICC commits to in any of them is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

1. Advise and formulate

The strategy, governance, and process office of the Bank for AI: the direction and the mandate of a unit, its normative documents and processes, the watch on the field, and the business cases and scenarios that the Portfolio decides on.

  • Strategy and governance: Strategy, charter, operating and governance model, portal and repository for a unit or a program, developed with AI, as AICC did for itself
  • Normatives and processes: Policies, procedures, regulations, runbooks, and process descriptions drafted, aligned, and maintained with AI for any function
  • Research and exploration: Regulatory and technology watch with digests, trials in the Lab, partnering with organizations and providers
  • Business cases and scenarios: Use-case discovery, scenarios with problem, solution, and measures, readiness and source audit, the business case the Portfolio decides on

2. Build and run

The solutions and the platforms: knowledge a function can ask, routine work done by assistants under human validation, the factual base for decisions, the content a function publishes, and the shared engines and environments behind them.

  • Knowledge services: The corpus of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, as a governed knowledge base it can ask
  • Workplace automation: Routing, forms, reports, consolidation, documents from templates, and case assistance, done by assistants under human validation
  • Analytics and decision support: Pipelines, dashboards, analyses, and research tooling that prepare the factual base for decisions, with lineage to governed sources
  • Content management: Public, investor, and management material generated from governed data and templates, and the templates, editions, versions, and languages behind it
  • Platforms: The shared engines and environments of AICC, the requirements on the AI Platform of the Bank, and the hand-over of a platform to the teams that run at scale

3. Enablement

The people and the adoption: each person trained and coached to use AI for their own work, each Domain carrying its adoption through its Domain Experts, and each Solution managed through its life after delivery.

  • Training and knowledge sharing: Training by role, coaching at the desk, skill libraries, communities of practice, playbooks and publications
  • Adoption and lifecycle management: Domain Experts and adoption plans per Domain; hand-over, support, revision, and retirement of Solutions; the life and operation of a Service

4. Assurance

The compliance of every use of AI: the rules, controls, and criteria stated before the build, the assessments and evaluations before use, the Risk Tier of each Solution, and the oversight of the whole, reported to the Steering and the Board Committee.

  • Policies, controls, criteria: Rules of use within the AI Policy, control maps, acceptance and evaluation criteria, guardrails, stated before the build
  • Assessments and evaluations: Tools, providers, and Solutions evaluated against the cases of the function and the rules before use; readiness and benchmarks
  • Risk tiering: The Risk Tier assigned, recorded in the AI Registry, explained, and reassessed when the use changes
  • Oversight: Adopted Solutions, live reviews, AI Incidents, and changes followed through the Registry and reported to the Steering and the Board Committee

5. From the areas to an Engagement

¶5.1. A function does not choose an area; it brings a need, and the need finds its category at the front door. The page How to engage states the six steps, the commitment, and the service levels; the Service catalog states the form of a Solution entry and of a package; the Business Model is the document of the charter that states what AICC offers and how it commits, and it prevails over these pages.

  • Service category · 4 parts

    Strategy and governance

    What AICC built for itself it builds for any unit of the Bank: a strategy, a charter and mandate, an operating and governance model with its roles, decisions, control loops, and controls, and the portal and repository that publish them, developed with AI in weeks and maintained with it afterwards. AICC acts here as the strategy office and the governance office of the function, and the function keeps the ownership of what is decided.

  • Service category · 5 parts

    Knowledge services

    The documents of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, turned into a governed knowledge base that it can ask. Legal, compliance, accounting, operations, and any function with a corpus get an assistant that answers from approved, current, cited sources, for its own people and, where the function decides, for its clients.

  • Service category · 2 parts

    Training and knowledge sharing

    AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches them at the desk, builds the prompt and skill libraries for each line of work, runs the communities of practice, and publishes what it learns so that the next function starts further ahead.

  • Service category · 4 parts

    Policies, controls, criteria

    The use of AI in a function answers to the AI Policy of the Bank, to the controls of AICC, and to criteria that say when a Solution is good enough and safe enough to use. AICC helps a function state its own rules of use within the policy, map the controls that apply to its Solutions, and define the acceptance and evaluation criteria before any build, so that compliance is designed in and not inspected in.

  • Page · 3 parts

    How to engage

    A function does not need a form, a budget line, or a project to start with AICC. It brings a need. From there the Engagement follows six steps, each with a record and a decision that the function can see.

  • Page · 2 parts

    Service catalog

    The catalog of AICC lists what AICC runs, has delivered, is proving, and can re-deploy. This page states the form of the catalog: the two kinds of entry, the fields of each, and the states an entry passes through. The live catalog is an instance of this form, kept in the Portfolio of AICC and presented in the live portal; this site states the form only. One entry of each kind is shown as an illustration.
