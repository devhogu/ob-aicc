Services

AICC serves the functions and units of the Bank as their internal consulting and innovation lab. Its services run along fifteen categories in four areas: advise and formulate, build and run, enablement, and assurance. Each category is a direction with examples; each Engagement follows one service model and leaves a reusable package. This section states the areas and categories, how a function engages AICC, the service model, and the form of the catalog.

Areas and categories

Fifteen categories in four areas. A category is a direction along which AICC provides services, with examples on its page; what AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement. Run-rate work is asked for through the front door and done in days to one Iteration; a program starts with a study and a business case.

Advise and formulate

The strategy, governance, and process office of the Bank for AI: the direction and the mandate of a unit, its normative documents and processes, the watch on the field, and the business cases and scenarios that the Portfolio decides on.

  • Service category

    Strategy and governance

    What AICC built for itself it builds for any unit of the Bank: a strategy, a charter and mandate, an operating and governance model with its roles, decisions, control loops, and controls, and the portal and repository that publish them, developed with AI in weeks and maintained with it afterwards. AICC acts here as the strategy office and the governance office of the function, and the function keeps the ownership of what is decided.

  • Service category

    Normatives and processes

    Every function writes and keeps normative documents: policies, procedures, regulations, instructions, runbooks, and the process descriptions behind them. AICC helps HR, legal, accounting, compliance, operations, technology, and any other function draft, align, and maintain them with AI, in the style of the Bank and in one voice, under the ownership of the function.

  • Service category

    Research and exploration

    AICC watches the field so that the Bank does not have to: the models, the platforms, the methods, and the rules that change every quarter. It digests the changes for the functions concerned, explores what matters in trials, keeps the reference of the industry body of knowledge and of the regulators and acts, and works with other organizations, with providers, and with the professional bodies where the Bank gains from it.

  • Service category

    Business cases and scenarios

    Before the Bank invests in a use of AI, it needs to know what the use is, what it would take, what it would return, and what it would risk. AICC does this work with the function: it finds and shapes the use cases, writes each as a scenario with its problem, its solution, and its measures, assesses the readiness of the function and its sources, and writes the business case that the Portfolio decides on.

Build and run

The solutions and the platforms: knowledge a function can ask, routine work done by assistants under human validation, the factual base for decisions, the content a function publishes, and the shared engines and environments behind them.

  • Service category

    Knowledge services

    The documents of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, turned into a governed knowledge base that it can ask. Legal, compliance, accounting, operations, and any function with a corpus get an assistant that answers from approved, current, cited sources, for its own people and, where the function decides, for its clients.

  • Service category

    Workplace automation

    The routine work of a function done by assistants under human validation: routing requests, reading and filling forms, processing reports, consolidating inputs, and producing documents from templates. The person validates and promotes; the assistant prepares.

  • Service category

    Analytics and decision support

    Facts from many sources prepared, consolidated, and presented so that a function decides on data: the pipelines that collect and transform, the dashboards that show, the analyses that explain, and the research tooling that supports the work.

  • Service category

    Content management

    Public, investor, and management material generated from governed data and templates, pre-filled for people to finish, and the content behind it kept in order: the templates, the editions, the versions, the languages, and the approval that precedes publication.

  • Service category

    Platforms

    Some of what AICC builds serves more than one function and more than one Solution: the portal generator, the knowledge-base kit, the workflow runtime of the automations, the evaluation harness, and the Lab in which Experiments run. AICC builds and runs these as its platforms, states the requirements that the use of AI places on the AI Platform of the Bank, and hands a platform to a platform team when the Bank should run it at scale.

Enablement

The people and the adoption: each person trained and coached to use AI for their own work, each Domain carrying its adoption through its Domain Experts, and each Solution managed through its life after delivery.

  • Service category

    Training and knowledge sharing

    AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches them at the desk, builds the prompt and skill libraries for each line of work, runs the communities of practice, and publishes what it learns so that the next function starts further ahead.

  • Service category

    Adoption and lifecycle management

    A Solution is not adopted when it is released; it is adopted when the function uses it, keeps it, and knows when to change or retire it. AICC supports the adoption of AI in each Domain through its Domain Experts, and manages the life of each Solution after delivery: hand-over, support, revision, and retirement, with the health of the Solution read at each review.

Assurance

The compliance of every use of AI: the rules, controls, and criteria stated before the build, the assessments and evaluations before use, the Risk Tier of each Solution, and the oversight of the whole, reported to the Steering and the Board Committee.

  • Service category

    Policies, controls, criteria

    The use of AI in a function answers to the AI Policy of the Bank, to the controls of AICC, and to criteria that say when a Solution is good enough and safe enough to use. AICC helps a function state its own rules of use within the policy, map the controls that apply to its Solutions, and define the acceptance and evaluation criteria before any build, so that compliance is designed in and not inspected in.

  • Service category

    Assessments and evaluations

    Before a tool, a provider, or a Solution is used, it is assessed and evaluated: does it do what it claims, on the cases of the function, within the rules, at an acceptable cost? AICC runs these assessments with the function and the Control Function Contacts, with an evaluation harness that tests a tool against the cases of the Bank, so that the decision to use rests on evidence.

  • Service category

    Risk tiering

    Every use of AI in the Bank carries a Risk Tier, and the Risk Tier sets the review, the validation, the human oversight, and the speed of release that apply to it. AICC assigns the Risk Tier of each Solution, records it in the AI Registry, tells the function what the tier requires, and reassesses it when the use changes.

  • Service category

    Oversight

    AICC keeps one view of every AI use of the Bank, built or bought, and reports on it. It oversees the Adopted Solutions that others deliver and run, follows the live review, the incidents, and the changes of every Solution through the Registry, and brings the whole to the Steering and to the Board Committee in the Quarterly Report.

How to engage

A function brings a need. The Engagement follows six steps, each with a record and a decision the function can see; AICC commits in a Service Agreement on a best-effort basis, and the function commits to nothing.

  1. 1ContactA head of function raises a need, in any form
  2. 2StudyThe need is scoped and the business case written and cleared
  3. 3Service AgreementAICC states what it commits to and the support level
  4. 4DeliveryMVP first, then build, verify, and deploy to the first users
  5. 5Outcome ReportThe outcome with its evidence, accepted by the Domain Owner
  6. 6SupportAt the agreed level, with a check-in at each Iteration

What AICC does not do

AICC does not own or operate the AI Platform, own the business results of a Domain, set the rules of a Control Function, or validate its own work. It does not deliver at scale: it proves and builds, and it relies on the platform teams and the IT functions of the Bank to run what the Bank adopts (AICC Charter 3.2).

The service model and the catalog

Every service of AICC follows one model: its composition, its life, its operation, and its records. The catalog has a defined form for a Solution and for a package. This site states the model and the form; the live catalog is an instance kept in the Portfolio and presented in the live portal of AICC.

  • Page

    The service model

    A service of AICC is not a promise in the air. It is composed of defined parts, it has a life with states and gates, it is operated under named practices, and it leaves records. This page states the model that every service of AICC follows, whatever its area and category. It is the template; the services in operation are its instances and are kept in the Portfolio.

  • Page

    Service catalog: the form

    The catalog of AICC lists what AICC runs, has delivered, is proving, and can re-deploy. This page states the form of the catalog: the two kinds of entry, the fields of each, and the states an entry passes through. The live catalog is an instance of this form, kept in the Portfolio of AICC and presented in the live portal; this site states the form only. One entry of each kind is shown as an illustration.
