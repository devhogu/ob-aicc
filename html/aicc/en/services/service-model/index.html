The service model

The form of the catalogThe service model

A service of AICC is not a promise in the air. It is composed of defined parts, it has a life with states and gates, it is operated under named practices, and it leaves records. This page states the model that every service of AICC follows, whatever its area and category. It is the template; the services in operation are its instances and are kept in the Portfolio.

1. The composition of a service

¶1.1. Every service of AICC, whatever its category and whether run-rate or program, is described by the same fields.

Field Meaning Where it is set
Area and category One of the fifteen categories in the four areas (advise and formulate; build and run; enablement; assurance) The Services section
Mode Run-rate, done in days to one Iteration through the front door; or program, an Initiative with a business case and an MVP The category, confirmed at intake
Client The function, represented by its Domain Owner; the Executive Sponsor for enabling work Service Agreement
Solution type Experiment, Product, or Service, which sets the life after delivery Solution Definition
Support level None, on demand, agreed response targets, or run by AICC Service Agreement
Risk Tier 1, 2, or 3, which sets the review, the validation, the human oversight, and the speed of release Assigned by the AICC Lead; AI Registry
Owner The Domain Owner for the results; the Solution Engineer for the build and the run; the product owner for acceptance during development Appointments Record; Solution Definition
Package The reusable asset the service leaves, by its Package Definition The catalog of packages
Records The Initiative Brief, the Service Agreement, the Solution Definition, the Control Sign-Off, the Acceptance Checklist, the Outcome Report, and the Service Management records, as the mode and the type require The Registry

2. The life of a service

¶2.1. A service is born as a need at the front door and lives through the phases of an Engagement: study, proof, delivery, support. A Service that AICC runs then lives through the states of operation that the page The life of a Service describes: admitted, catalogued, active, improving, transition planned, migrating, and closed, each with its question, its signals, its action, and its gate.

¶2.2. A Product lives in the hands of its consumer, supported on demand and revised through the Portfolio Backlog. An Experiment lives for its time-box and ends in a Proposal, a Handover, or its lessons.

3. The operation of a service

¶3.1. A Service that AICC runs is operated under the practices that the page Service operations describes: request and incident handling by class of service, problem management, change enablement, knowledge, service level, service financial management, and supplier management, each sized to a small unit and to the Risk Tier of the Service.

¶3.2. The health of a Service is read at each Iteration Review and Demo in four signals: service levels, incidents, adoption, and cost. The signals feed the sunset rule of the Service and the Quarterly Report.

4. The records of a service

¶4.1. Every state change of a service leaves a record in the Registry, in the form of the templates of the Knowledge base. The site states the forms; the Registry holds the instances; the Quarterly Report reads them.

5. The catalog

¶5.1. The catalog of AICC has two parts: the Solutions, each by its Solution Definition, and the packages, each by its Package Definition. The page Service catalog states the form of each entry. The live catalog is kept in the Portfolio and will be presented in the live portal of AICC.

6. Rule source

Business Model 4 and 5; Solution Lifecycle Model 7 and 8; AI Policy 3; Operating Model 7. The states of operation and the practices of 2.1 and 3.1 are stated on this site and are proposed for the Solution Lifecycle Model 8.

  • Page · 5 parts

    Advise and formulate

    The strategy, governance, and process office of the Bank for AI: the direction and the mandate of a unit, its normative documents and processes, the watch on the field, and the business cases and scenarios that the Portfolio decides on.

  • Service category · 4 parts

    Strategy and governance

    What AICC built for itself it builds for any unit of the Bank: a strategy, a charter and mandate, an operating and governance model with its roles, decisions, control loops, and controls, and the portal and repository that publish them, developed with AI in weeks and maintained with it afterwards. AICC acts here as the strategy office and the governance office of the function, and the function keeps the ownership of what is decided.

  • Service category · 5 parts

    Knowledge services

    The documents of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, turned into a governed knowledge base that it can ask. Legal, compliance, accounting, operations, and any function with a corpus get an assistant that answers from approved, current, cited sources, for its own people and, where the function decides, for its clients.

  • Service category · 2 parts

    Training and knowledge sharing

    AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches them at the desk, builds the prompt and skill libraries for each line of work, runs the communities of practice, and publishes what it learns so that the next function starts further ahead.

  • Service category · 4 parts

    Policies, controls, criteria

    The use of AI in a function answers to the AI Policy of the Bank, to the controls of AICC, and to criteria that say when a Solution is good enough and safe enough to use. AICC helps a function state its own rules of use within the policy, map the controls that apply to its Solutions, and define the acceptance and evaluation criteria before any build, so that compliance is designed in and not inspected in.

  • Page · 3 parts

    How to engage

    A function does not need a form, a budget line, or a project to start with AICC. It brings a need. From there the Engagement follows six steps, each with a record and a decision that the function can see.
