Platforms

Knowledge servicesWorkplace automationAnalytics and decision supportContent managementPlatforms

Some of what AICC builds serves more than one function and more than one Solution: the portal generator, the knowledge-base kit, the workflow runtime of the automations, the evaluation harness, and the Lab in which Experiments run. AICC builds and runs these as its platforms, states the requirements that the use of AI places on the AI Platform of the Bank, and hands a platform to a platform team when the Bank should run it at scale.

1. What it is

¶1.1. The category covers the shared engines and environments of AICC: the engines behind its Services and packages, the Lab environment for Experiments, and the tooling that the Teams use; the statement of the requirements of AICC on the AI Platform of the Bank, which is provided and operated outside AICC; and the hand-over of a platform to a platform team or an IT function through the states of the life of a Service.

¶1.2. AICC does not own or operate the AI Platform of the Bank, and it does not run platforms at the scale of the Bank. It builds what proves the case and what its own Services need, and it relies on the platform teams and IT to scale.

2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the category, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

  • A portal generated and run for a function from its charter pack
  • A knowledge-base kit deployed for a function on its own corpus
  • The workflow runtime that carries the automations of several functions
  • The evaluation harness that tests a tool against the cases of a function
  • The Lab environment for an Experiment: isolated, logged, on read-only extracts
  • A requirements statement to the AI Platform of the Bank: model gateway, knowledge layer, tool gateway, registry, guardrails, observability
  • A platform packaged and handed over to a platform team or IT to run at scale

3. What the function receives

¶3.1. A platform of AICC in operation for the function, under the service model of AICC: catalog entry, service levels, request and incident route, health signals; or a requirements statement that the Platform Owner can act on; or a hand-over pack for the receiving team.

4. How it runs

¶4.1. A platform of AICC is born as the package of an Engagement and becomes a Service when it has a second consumer, a business case with its run cost and its sunset rule, and a named owner. It is operated as any Service of AICC. When the Bank should run it at scale, it goes through Transition planned and Migrating to a platform team or an IT function, with a Proposal and the acceptance of the receiving team.

5. What it leads to

¶5.1. Reuse across functions, one way of doing the same thing in the Bank, and an AI Platform of the Bank that grows from what is proven rather than from what is assumed.

6. The reusable package

¶6.1. The platforms themselves are packages: each has a Package Definition, a Solution Definition when it is a Service, and the notes to re-deploy or to hand over.

7. Run-rate or program

¶7.1. A program.

8. Who decides

¶8.1. The AICC Lead decides which packages become platforms of AICC; the Executive Sponsor approves a Service across Domains; the Platform Owner decides on the AI Platform of the Bank; the receiving team accepts a hand-over.

9. Rule source

AICC Charter 3.2; Statement of Intent 10.3 and 11.2; Business Model 4.2; Solution Lifecycle Model 8. The hand-over states are stated on this site and proposed for the Solution Lifecycle Model 8.

    Advise and formulate

    The strategy, governance, and process office of the Bank for AI: the direction and the mandate of a unit, its normative documents and processes, the watch on the field, and the business cases and scenarios that the Portfolio decides on.

  • Service category · 4 parts

    Strategy and governance

    What AICC built for itself it builds for any unit of the Bank: a strategy, a charter and mandate, an operating and governance model with its roles, decisions, control loops, and controls, and the portal and repository that publish them, developed with AI in weeks and maintained with it afterwards. AICC acts here as the strategy office and the governance office of the function, and the function keeps the ownership of what is decided.

  • Service category · 2 parts

    Training and knowledge sharing

    AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches them at the desk, builds the prompt and skill libraries for each line of work, runs the communities of practice, and publishes what it learns so that the next function starts further ahead.

  • Service category · 4 parts

    Policies, controls, criteria

    The use of AI in a function answers to the AI Policy of the Bank, to the controls of AICC, and to criteria that say when a Solution is good enough and safe enough to use. AICC helps a function state its own rules of use within the policy, map the controls that apply to its Solutions, and define the acceptance and evaluation criteria before any build, so that compliance is designed in and not inspected in.

  • Page · 3 parts

    How to engage

    A function does not need a form, a budget line, or a project to start with AICC. It brings a need. From there the Engagement follows six steps, each with a record and a decision that the function can see.

  • Page · 2 parts

    Service catalog

    The catalog of AICC lists what AICC runs, has delivered, is proving, and can re-deploy. This page states the form of the catalog: the two kinds of entry, the fields of each, and the states an entry passes through. The live catalog is an instance of this form, kept in the Portfolio of AICC and presented in the live portal; this site states the form only. One entry of each kind is shown as an illustration.
