Privacy

1. Scope

¶1.1. This statement describes what this site, the charter portal of the AI Competence Center ("AICC") of the Bank, does with information about the persons who use it. It applies to every page of the site, in both languages.

¶1.2. The policies of the Bank on personal data, information security, the classification of information, and the acceptable use of its systems apply to the use of this site and prevail over this statement. The site is one of the tools and portals of AICC listed in the collaboration tooling workflow of the charter, and the rules of the Operating Model on the content of the tools apply to it.

2. What the site collects

¶2.1. The site is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, no forms, no analytics, and no tracking, and it sends no request to any service outside the hosting of the site by the Bank. The search on the site runs in the browser over an index that is part of the site; a search query leaves no trace and is sent nowhere.

¶2.2. The site sets no cookies. When a user chooses a theme with the button in the header, the site stores that one preference in the local storage of the browser. The preference is not sent anywhere, and the user removes it by clearing the site data in the browser. The copy buttons of the site write to the clipboard of the user's device and nowhere else.

¶2.3. The access gateway of the Bank through which the site is reached keeps its own sign-in session and may set a cookie for that purpose. The gateway is operated under the policies of the Bank, and the site does not read it.

¶2.4. The server that hosts the site keeps technical access logs for its security and operation, as the rules of the Bank for its systems provide. The site is kept in a version-controlled repository; the history of its publication is kept, and no information about readers is part of it.

3. Feedback and contact

¶3.1. The feedback and contact links open the mail program of the user with a message addressed to the mailbox of AICC, with the identifier of the page in the subject and the title and the address of the page in the body. Nothing is sent until the user sends the message, and the user sees and may edit the whole of it.

¶3.2. A message that a user sends is internal correspondence of the Bank. It is used to answer the user and to improve the site and the documents, and it is kept as the rules of the Bank for correspondence provide.

4. Links from the site

¶4.1. The site links to the charter and to the Registry on the corporate share of the Bank. Those links open only on the network of the Bank, under the permissions of the share, and opening one tells the site nothing. The site contains no link to any site outside the Bank; the external sources it names in its Reference are named so that a reader can find them, and the rules of the Bank apply to visiting them.

5. Persons named on the site

¶5.1. The documents and the pages on this site name Roles, not persons. The persons who hold the Roles are recorded in the Appointments Record of the Registry, which is not part of this site. The contact address of the site is a work mailbox of the Bank.

¶5.2. The site holds no figure of the Bank, no data of the Bank, no customer or employee data, and no code of the Bank, as the Operating Model requires of the tools and portals of AICC.

6. Questions and concerns

¶6.1. A question about this statement goes to the contact address in the footer. A concern about personal data is raised under the procedure of the Bank for personal data, with the Control Function Contact for data protection.

7. Revision

¶7.1. This statement is revised with the site, and at least with the yearly review of the documents of the charter. The revision of the site is stated in the footer of every page.
