Explore AICC

The charter is the body of knowledge of the AI Competence Center. It states how the unit is mandated, governed, and operated, and it is the source of truth for what AICC is, what it produces, and how it keeps its records. This page shows how the charter is organized on this site and where to start.

The charter in four parts

The charter has four kinds of content. Each answers one question, and each has its place on this site. The Registry and the Portfolio hold the records of the work; they are outside the charter and are not on this site.

PartAnswersOn this site
DocumentsWhat are the rules?About AICC, What AICC does, How AICC works, Organization, Responsible AI, and Governance and oversight, each document in the section of its subject; long documents are split into parts
WorkflowsHow does the work flow, and on which events?Beside the documents that they illustrate, in What AICC does, How AICC works, Responsible AI, and Governance and oversight
GuidesWho does what, when, and what is left on record?Beside their workflows, as companion guides
TemplatesWhat is the form of each record?Library

The sections of this site

About AICC

AICC is the internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank for the adoption of artificial intelligence.

  • Statement of Intent · Document — The intent, values, principles, and strategy of the Bank for AI
  • Charter · Document — Mission, authority, funding, risk appetite, offer, and measures of AICC
  • Values and principles · Page — The values and the principles of adoption, application, work, and delivery, and what each applies to
  • Explore AICC · Page — How the charter is organized on this site, where each part is, and where to start

What AICC does

AICC serves the functions of the Bank.

  • Business Model · Document — What AICC is, whom it serves, what it offers, how it commits, and how it tracks value and flow
  • Engagement workflow · Workflow — The top-level workflow between AICC and the rest of the Bank: how AICC works as an internal consulting unit, from the first contact to the follow-on, with its Service Agreement, Outcome Report, and support levels
  • Guide: Engagement · Guide — How AICC engages a function, commits to it, delivers, and reports

How AICC works

AICC works by one method: Initiatives are taken in and decided in the Portfolio, Solutions are delivered, released, and operated through the Solution Lifecycle, and the work runs on a common cadence and is measured.

  • Portfolio Management Model · Document — How AICC decides which business initiatives to take in, fund, continue, defer, or reject: the strategic inputs, the four portfolio loops, the portfolio Kanban, the business case, and the MVP
  • Solution Lifecycle Model · Document — How AICC delivers: the principles, the flow of value, the backlogs and the Program Board, the states, the cadence and its loops, verification and release, the life cycle, and the measures
  • Portfolio and service delivery workflow · Workflow — The value stream from a business need to a retired solution: the portfolio Kanban (funnel, review, analysis, backlog, MVP, and the decision after it), the Capabilities and Features, execution, deployment, operation, and the life cycle with its change, retirement, and measures
  • Guide: Service delivery · Guide — How an item moves from a need to a retired Solution, and who decides on the way
  • Cadence · Workflow — The events of the loops by week, Iteration, and PI, without dates
  • Guide: Cadence · Guide — The rhythm of the events, the calendar, and what each beat leaves on record
  • Collaboration tooling · Workflow — The tools and the portals that AICC uses, for what, and in which workflows

Organization

AICC is a joint team, led by the AICC Lead and formed of the people whom the functions of the Bank assign to it.

  • Operating Model · Document — AICC as a unit of the Bank: Roles, Decisions, the five control loops, Records, and controls
  • Guide: Organization · Guide — The Roles, the profiles, the RACI, and the people records
  • The Roles · Page — The seven Roles and their pages

Responsible AI

The Bank uses AI under rules that are proportionate to risk.

  • AI Policy · Document — Rules of use, Risk Tiers, providers, AI Incidents, and Exceptions
  • AI risk and control workflow · Workflow — How a Solution, a provider, and a use of AI pass the controls of the AI Policy, from the Risk Tier to the gates before use, the live review, and the Exception, suspension, and stop

Governance and oversight

AICC is controlled, reported, and assured through five control loops, a catalogue of thirty-two controls, and the records that evidence them.

  • Operating Model · Document — The control loops; Records and evidence; Controls
  • Solution Lifecycle Model: Records, controls, and measures · Document — Records and controls; Measures
  • Unit governance workflow · Workflow — The control of AICC as a unit: the loops on the Steerings and what each Steering carries, how a decision escalates, the events, a month, a quarter, and a year in sequence, an AI Incident, the reporting chain, and the life of a document
  • Guide: Unit governance · Guide — How the unit is directed, reported, and controlled

Library

The thirteen templates are the forms of the records that AICC produces, from the Initiative Brief to the Quarterly Report.

  • Initiative Brief, Service Agreement, Solution Definition, Acceptance Checklist, Control Sign-Off, Decision Record, Steering Summary, Outcome Report, AI Incident Review, Registry Snapshot, Quarterly Report, Appointments Record, Proposal

Reference

The Vocabulary defines the terms of the charter, the Document Catalog lists its documents, the change history records their revisions, and Records and systems states where each record is kept.

  • Vocabulary · Reference — The defined terms of the charter and its style
  • Document Catalog · Document — This Catalog
  • Change history · Reference — The revision history of the documents
  • Records and systems · Reference — Where each record is kept, and which control it evidences

How the charter is kept

Every page of this site is generated from the documents of the charter at the baseline revision stated in the footer; where a page and a document differ, the document prevails. The Document Catalog states the status, the revision, and the life cycle of each document and template, and the AICC Lead activates them. Each document carries its change history, shown at the end of its page. The controls that can be tested are listed in the Operating Model, section 8, and the evidence of each is held in the Registry.
