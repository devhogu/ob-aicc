Explore AICC

How the charter is organized, and how its documents, workflows, guides, and templates relate

1. Purpose

The charter is the body of knowledge of the AI Competence Center (AICC) of the Bank. It states how the unit is mandated, governed, and operated, and it is the source of truth for what AICC is, what it produces, and how it keeps its records. This README is the index of the charter and the guide to reading it.

2. Structure

The charter has four parts. The table also shows the Registry and the Portfolio, which hold the records of the work and are outside the charter.

Part Folder Answers
Documents documents/ What are the rules?
Workflows workflows/ How does the work flow, and on which events?
Guides guides/ Who does what, when, and what is left on record?
Templates templates/ What is the form of each record?
Registry registry/ What is the state of the work, and what is the evidence?
Portfolio portfolio/ Which Solutions exist, and in what state?

4. How to read the charter

A reader who is new to the charter reads the items 1, 2.1 to 2.6, and 2.7 as a reference, in the order of the contents. The Statement of Intent (2.8) gives the reasons behind the rules, and the Document Catalog (2.9) gives the status and the life cycle of each document. The workflows (3), the guides (4), and the Templates (5) follow, as the reader needs them.

A reader with a specific purpose follows the route in the following table.

Reader Route To find
A head of function who brings a need Business Model; Portfolio Management Model; Engagement guide; Engagement workflow; the Initiative Brief and Service Agreement Templates How AICC engages a function, what it commits to, and what the function receives
A new member of AICC Operating Model; Portfolio Management Model; Solution Lifecycle Model; AI Policy; AI risk and control workflow; Organization guide; Cadence guide The Roles, the rules, the first week of a Holder, and the rhythm of the events
The Executive Sponsor or the Board Committee AICC Charter; Unit governance guide, sections 3 and 5; the Quarterly Report Template The mandate, the funding, the risk appetite, the reporting chain, and what is reported
A Control Function Contact AI Policy; Solution Lifecycle Model, sections 7 and 8; Operating Model, section 8; AI risk and control workflow; the Control Sign-Off Template The Risk Tiers, the checks, the validation, and the right to stop
Internal audit Operating Model, sections 6 to 8; the Control Matrix; Unit governance guide; Organization guide, section 8; the Registry Each control with its rule, owner, timing, and evidence record, and the records themselves
Human resources Organization guide; Operating Model, sections 4 and 5; the Appointments Record Template The Roles, the profiles, the RACI, and the people records

5. Control of the charter

The Document Catalog states the status, the revision, and the life cycle of each document and Template, and the AICC Lead activates them. The documents carry a change log, and a Template carries a revision only. The workflows and the guides are changed with the history of the repository. The Operating Model 8 lists the controls that can be tested, and the Registry holds the evidence of each.
