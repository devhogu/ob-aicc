Explore AICC

The charter is the body of knowledge of the AI Competence Center. It states how the unit is mandated, governed, and operated, and it is the source of truth for what AICC is, what it produces, and how it keeps its records. This page shows how the charter is organized on this site and where to start.

The charter in four parts

The charter has four kinds of content. Each answers one question, and each has its place on this site. The Registry and the Portfolio hold the records of the work; they are outside the charter and are not on this site.

PartAnswersOn this site
DocumentsWhat are the rules?About AICC, Responsible AI, Services, Portfolio, Delivery, Governance and oversight, and Organization, each document in the section of its subject; long documents are split into parts
WorkflowsHow does the work flow, and on which events?Beside the documents that they illustrate, in Responsible AI, Services, Delivery, and Governance and oversight
GuidesWho does what, when, and what is left on record?Beside their workflows, as companion guides
TemplatesWhat is the form of each record?Knowledge base, with the guides, the acts and compliance, and the publications

The sections of this site

About AICC

AICC is the internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank for the adoption of artificial intelligence.

  • Statement of Intent · Document — The intent, values, principles, and strategy of the Bank for AI
  • Strategy · Page — The strategy of the Bank, the Strategic Priorities, the strategic choices across every aspect of AICC, and the road of Maturity Levels
  • Charter · Document — Mission, authority, funding, risk appetite, offer, and measures of AICC
  • What we do · Page — AICC is the internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank for artificial intelligence. It advises and formulates, builds and runs, enables, and assures, across strategy, programs, solutions, and ways of working. It is a small delivery unit that proves the case and relies on the platform teams and the IT functions of the Bank to scale.
  • How we work · Page — AICC works as a consulting unit works. A function brings a need; AICC takes it in, studies it, commits to it, proves it, builds it, and supports it, and each step leaves a record and a decision that the function can see. The Portfolio decides what AICC works on. Delivery builds it in small steps on a common cadence, with quality and control inside the flow rather than after it.
  • Values and principles · Page — The values and the principles of adoption, application, work, and delivery, and what each applies to
  • Explore AICC · Page — How the charter is organized on this site, where each part is, and where to start

Responsible AI

The Bank uses AI under rules that are proportionate to risk, and it asks its people to understand the technology they use.

  • A short course on AI · Page — Every wave of technology promises more than it delivers at first and more than anyone expected in the end. Generative AI is in its first years, and the honest view is that its value is real, uneven, and earned by the organizations that do the unglamorous work of governance, data, and adoption. This page states where the value is appearing in banking, what the evidence says, and where the Bank has chosen to look first.
  • AI Policy · Document — Rules of use, Risk Tiers, providers, AI Incidents, and Exceptions
  • AI risk and control workflow · Workflow — How a Solution, a provider, and a use of AI pass the controls of the AI Policy, from the Risk Tier to the gates before use, the live review, and the Exception, suspension, and stop

Services

AICC serves the functions and units of the Bank as their internal consulting and innovation lab.

  • Service areas · Page — The strategy, governance, and process office of the Bank for AI: the direction and the mandate of a unit, its normative documents and processes, the watch on the field, and the business cases and scenarios that the Portfolio decides on.
  • Advise and formulate · Service category — What AICC built for itself it builds for any unit of the Bank: a strategy, a charter and mandate, an operating and governance model with its roles, decisions, control loops, and controls, and the portal and repository that publish them, developed with AI in weeks and maintained with it afterwards. AICC acts here as the strategy office and the governance office of the function, and the function keeps the ownership of what is decided.
  • Build and run · Service category — The documents of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, turned into a governed knowledge base that it can ask. Legal, compliance, accounting, operations, and any function with a corpus get an assistant that answers from approved, current, cited sources, for its own people and, where the function decides, for its clients.
  • Enablement · Service category — AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches them at the desk, builds the prompt and skill libraries for each line of work, runs the communities of practice, and publishes what it learns so that the next function starts further ahead.
  • Assurance · Service category — The use of AI in a function answers to the AI Policy of the Bank, to the controls of AICC, and to criteria that say when a Solution is good enough and safe enough to use. AICC helps a function state its own rules of use within the policy, map the controls that apply to its Solutions, and define the acceptance and evaluation criteria before any build, so that compliance is designed in and not inspected in.
  • How to engage · Page — A function does not need a form, a budget line, or a project to start with AICC. It brings a need. From there the Engagement follows six steps, each with a record and a decision that the function can see.
  • Service catalog · Page — The catalog of AICC lists what AICC runs, has delivered, is proving, and can re-deploy. This page states the form of the catalog: the two kinds of entry, the fields of each, and the states an entry passes through. The live catalog is an instance of this form, kept in the Portfolio of AICC and presented in the live portal; this site states the form only. One entry of each kind is shown as an illustration.
  • Business Model · Document — What AICC is, whom it serves, what it offers, how it commits, and how it tracks value and flow

Portfolio

The Portfolio is where the Bank decides what AICC works on: a commercial decision body and a control loop in one.

  • The Portfolio · Page — The Portfolio starts from a frame that is set once a year: the Strategic Priorities the Bank pursues, the Investment Envelope of each, and the Investment Guardrails that say what may be decided where. Within that frame, decisions are taken close to the work; outside it, they rise to the Executive Sponsor. This part explains the frame and the decision rights that follow from it.
  • Portfolio Management Model · Document — How AICC decides which business initiatives to take in, fund, continue, defer, or reject: the strategic inputs, the four portfolio loops, the portfolio Kanban, the business case, and the MVP
  • Portfolio measures · Page — The course states what the Portfolio measures and why. This page states the measures themselves, one by one, as lean and portfolio practice defines them: the definition, the formula where there is one, the source, where it is read, and the rule for its target. It is the reference for the Dashboard, the Steering Summary, and the Quarterly Report, and for the live portal that will show them. The measures that the charter defines are marked as such; the others are the standard measures of lean flow and portfolio economics, stated here and proposed for the Solution Lifecycle Model 10 and the AI Competence Center Charter 7.

Delivery

Delivery is how an approved idea becomes a working Solution in the hands of a function, and how that Solution is kept, changed, and retired: in small steps, on a fixed cadence, with the work visible and limited and quality built into the flow.

  • Delivery · Page — Value flows from a strategic theme to a task on a board through a small number of levels, and each level is worded in the language of the business so that the client, the Team, and the product owner read the same words. This part states the levels, how work enters, the Team that delivers it, and the stream of Stages a Feature travels.
  • Solution Lifecycle Model · Document — How AICC delivers: the principles, the flow of value, the backlogs and the Program Board, the states, the cadence and its loops, verification and release, the life cycle, and the measures
  • Service delivery · Workflow — The value stream from a business need to a retired solution: the portfolio Kanban (funnel, review, analysis, backlog, MVP, and the decision after it), the Capabilities and Features, execution, deployment, operation, and the life cycle with its change, retirement, and measures
  • Cadence · Workflow — The events of the loops by week, Iteration, and PI, without dates
  • Collaboration tooling · Workflow — The tools and the portals that AICC uses, for what, and in which workflows
  • The Experiment workflow: the Lab · Page — Before the Bank invests, AICC proves. An Experiment is a time-boxed trial of a Solution, a platform, or a concept, run on governed data in a bounded environment, measured against leading indicators, and ended with a decision: promote to delivery, hand over to a Receiver, or shelve with its lessons. This page states the workflow of an Experiment and the rules of the environment in which it runs, the Lab of AICC. The workflow is stated on this site and is proposed for the Solution Lifecycle Model 7.
  • The life of a Service · Page — A Service that AICC runs lives after its release. The Solution Lifecycle Model gives it three Stages, Operate, Evolve, and Retire; this page refines them into seven states, each with the question that is asked at it, the signals that answer it, the action that follows, and the gate to the next state. The refinement is stated on this site and is proposed for the Solution Lifecycle Model 8.
  • Service operations · Page — The Business Model states that the support of a Service is managed in Service Management and that requests and incidents come to the queue of AICC. This page states the practices behind that sentence, sized to a small unit: how a request, an incident, a problem, a change, and a question are handled for a Service that AICC runs, what the service levels are, and what each practice leaves on record. It is the run-book template of a Service of AICC; each Service fills it in its Service Agreement and its Solution Definition.
  • Delivery measures · Page — The course states what delivery measures and why. This page states the measures themselves: the definition, the formula where there is one, the source, where it is read, and the rule for its target. It is the reference for the boards, the Dashboard, and the records of the events, and for the live portal that will show them. The measures that the Solution Lifecycle Model defines are marked as such; the others are the standard measures of lean flow, software delivery, and service operation, stated here and proposed for the Solution Lifecycle Model 10.

Governance

Governance is how AICC is directed, controlled, reported, and assured as a unit of the Bank.

  • Governance · Page — A Decision is taken by the person who does the work, on the facts, at the moment it is needed. It rises to a higher level only when one of four conditions applies, and it is recorded so that others can find it. This part states the levels, the conditions, the stop right of the Control Functions, and how a decision is kept.
  • Operating Model · Document — The control loops; Records and evidence; Controls
  • Solution Lifecycle Model: Records, controls, and measures · Document — Records and controls; Measures
  • Unit governance · Workflow — The control of AICC as a unit: the loops on the Steerings and what each Steering carries, how a decision escalates, the events, a month, a quarter, and a year in sequence, an AI Incident, the reporting chain, and the life of a document

Organization

AICC is a joint team, led by the AICC Lead and formed of the people whom the functions of the Bank assign to it, organized by Roles and not by persons.

  • Organization · Page — AICC is an internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank, mandated by the Executive Sponsor whom the Board names, reporting through the Executive Sponsor to the Board Committee, serving the functions of the Bank as its clients, and executing through the Domains. This part states where it sits, what it is, and what it is not.
  • Operating Model · Document — AICC as a unit of the Bank: Roles, Decisions, the five control loops, Records, and controls
  • The Roles · Page — The seven Roles and their pages

Knowledge base

The working knowledge of AICC: the thirteen templates that give the form of every record, the guides that explain the workflows, the acts and policies that the use of AI answers to and how AICC complies, and the publications of AICC for the Bank.

  • Initiative Brief, Service Agreement, Solution Definition, Acceptance Checklist, Control Sign-Off, Decision Record, Steering Summary, Outcome Report, AI Incident Review, Registry Snapshot, Quarterly Report, Appointments Record, Proposal
  • Package Definition (draft template) — A draft template, proposed for the templates of the charter. A package is the reusable asset that an Engagement leaves: a method, a kit, an engine, a catalog of automations, or a template set, which the next Engagement takes in days where the first took weeks. The Package Definition is the record of one package. Until it is adopted into the charter by a decision record, this page is the draft form.
  • Guides — The five guides in one place: who does what, when, and what is left on record, each beside its workflow
  • Acts and compliance — The use of AI at the Bank answers to law, to regulation, and to the policies of the Bank. This page states, for each act or policy that applies, what it requires of a Solution and where the AI Policy and the controls of AICC answer it. It is a working reference kept with the Control Function Contacts; it states no rule of its own, and the acts and the policies prevail.
  • Publications — What AICC learns it writes down and makes available to the Bank: the documents of the charter, the Proposals that the Bank has decided on, the method notes and playbooks from the Engagements, and the lessons of what did not work. This page is the list.

Reference

The Vocabulary defines the terms of the charter, the Document Catalog lists its documents, the change history records their revisions, Records and systems states where each record is kept, the Industry body of knowledge lists the frameworks the charter draws on, and Regulators and acts positions the bodies and the instruments of AI and data regulation, global, European, and regional, with a page for each.

  • Vocabulary · Reference — The defined terms of the charter and its style
  • Document Catalog · Document — This Catalog
  • Change history · Reference — The revision history of the documents
  • Records and systems · Reference — Where each record is kept, and which control it evidences
  • Industry body of knowledge · Page — The charter of AICC is its own, and it draws on the common body of knowledge of the field: the frameworks for managing the risk of AI, the standards for managing it as a system, the principles that governments have agreed, and the practice of model risk, service management, and agile and lean delivery. This page lists the sources that matter to the Bank, in one line each, with where the charter uses them.
  • Regulators and acts · Page — The Bank operates in the Kyrgyz Republic, within a region and a field where the rules for AI and data are set by many bodies. This page is the index of the regulators, the acts, the standards, and the frameworks that the Bank is aware of, by jurisdiction, with a page for each: who they are, what they set, whom they reach, why they matter to the Bank, and how the charter relates to them. The pages are for orientation; they quote no provision, and an entry here does not mean that the instrument binds the Bank. The Knowledge base page Acts and compliance states what the applicable ones require of a use of AI at the Bank.

How the charter is kept

Every page of this site is generated from the documents of the charter at the baseline revision stated in the footer; where a page and a document differ, the document prevails. The Document Catalog states the status, the revision, and the life cycle of each document and template, and the AICC Lead activates them. Each document carries its change history, shown at the end of its page. The controls that can be tested are listed in the Operating Model, section 8, and the evidence of each is held in the Registry.
