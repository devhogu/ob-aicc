How we work

A function brings a need; AICC takes it in, studies it, commits to it, proves it, builds it, and supports it, with a record and a decision at each step. The Portfolio decides what AICC works on; Delivery builds it in small steps on a common cadence, with quality and control inside the flow.

1. The engagement model

¶1.1. Six steps: contact, study, Service Agreement, delivery, Outcome Report, support. AICC commits in a Service Agreement on a best-effort basis within its capability; the function commits to nothing. The page How to engage states the steps, the commitment, and the service levels.

2. The method in brief

¶2.1. The Portfolio runs on four loops, from the strategic loop of the year to the backlog care of the week, through a portfolio Kanban with a limit on the Active Initiatives. Every Initiative has a business case and is probed as an MVP before the decision to continue.

¶2.2. Delivery runs in Program Increments of one quarter and Iterations of one month, on fixed dates. Work is visible and limited in progress; Features are accepted at the Iteration Review and Demo; a Solution is checked or validated by a person other than its builder, accepted by the Team, and accepted by the Domain Owner before it reaches its users.

¶2.3. The unit is controlled through five control loops and a catalogue of controls, reports each quarter to the Board Committee, and is assured by internal audit.

3. Where to read on

¶3.1. The Portfolio section states how Initiatives are decided; the Delivery section states how Solutions are built and looked after; Governance and oversight states how the unit is controlled.
