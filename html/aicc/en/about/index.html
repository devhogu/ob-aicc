About AICC

AICC is the internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank for the adoption of artificial intelligence. It explores and proves the use of AI with the functions of the Bank, delivers measured outcomes, and sets the way in which the Bank adopts AI at scale, with accountability that stays with people.

What AICC is

AICC is an internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank. It explores, trials, and proves the use of AI with the functions, delivers the outcome, and proposes what the Bank should adopt at scale. It works as a consulting unit does: it engages with a function on a defined scope, delivers an outcome with its evidence, and keeps its commitments and its records in order.

Purpose

The mission of AICC is to enable the Bank to adopt AI as a governed capability that delivers measured value. The adoption improves the quality, speed, and consistency of the operations and services of the Bank, and serves its mission to improve people's lives with digital products and services. The direction is clear: from governed foundations, through assistants and embedded AI, to bounded agents under human oversight, with accountability for every Solution with a named individual.

Statement of Intent AICC Charter

Strategy

The Bank pursues seven Strategic Priorities, from customer intelligence to software engineering, each resting on a governed foundation of people, data, and a shared AI Platform. Each Strategic Priority advances at its own pace through five Maturity Levels, and a level is reached when its Measures are met.

  1. Customer intelligence
  2. Business intelligence
  3. Adoption within Domains
  4. Expertise at the point of work
  5. AI in banking operations and systems
  6. Information technology operations and service lifecycle
  7. Software engineering

Maturity Levels Foundation → Controlled adoption → Embedded → Scaled → Agentic

Approach

A function brings a need to AICC. AICC takes it in as an Initiative, probes it first as a minimum viable product, measures it against defined success Measures, and extends it only on that evidence. The work is visible, limited in progress, and paced by a common cadence. Before a Solution reaches its users, it is checked or validated by a person other than its builder, accepted by the Team, and accepted by the Domain Owner who asked for it. AICC applies this way of working to itself and is the example of what it proposes to the Bank.

Authority and limits

AICC acts under the mandate of the Executive Sponsor, whom the Board names. It does not own or operate the AI Platform, own the business results of a Domain, set the rules of a Control Function, or validate its own work. The Control Functions stay independent and may stop a Solution, and internal audit gives assurance only. The AICC Lead reports each quarter, and the Board oversees AI through the Board Committee.

Organization Governance and oversight

Safeguards

Each Solution is assigned a Risk Tier, which sets the review, validation, human oversight, and speed of release that apply to it. The Bank accepts risk from AI where the benefit is measured and the controls are proportionate, and it does not accept a breach of law or regulation.

Values and principles

Three values guide the work: integrity, prudence, and respect for people. The principles of adoption, application, work, and delivery follow from them.
