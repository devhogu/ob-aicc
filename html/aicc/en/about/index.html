About AICC

AICC is the internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank for the adoption of artificial intelligence. It explores and proves the use of AI with the functions of the Bank, delivers measured outcomes, and sets the practice by which the Bank adopts AI, within the standards that the Bank sets, with accountability that stays with people.

Mission

To make artificial intelligence a trusted, everyday capability of the Bank: to prove its value with the functions that serve the customers of the Bank, to set the practice by which the Bank adopts it within the standards that the Bank sets, and to keep every use of it accountable to people.

Values and principles AICC Charter

What AICC is

AICC is an internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank. It explores, trials, and proves the use of AI with the functions, delivers the outcome, and proposes what the Bank should adopt at scale. It works as a consulting unit does: it engages with a function on a defined scope, delivers an outcome with its evidence, and keeps its commitments and its records in order.

Services Business Model

Purpose

AICC exists to enable the Bank to adopt AI as a governed capability that delivers measured value. The adoption improves the quality, speed, and consistency of the operations and services of the Bank, and serves its mission to improve people's lives with digital products and services. The direction is clear: from governed foundations, through assistants and embedded AI, to bounded AI agents under human oversight, with accountability for every Solution with a named individual.

Statement of Intent AICC Charter

Strategy

The top Strategic Priorities of the Bank for AI, from customer intelligence to software engineering, mark where AI contributes most to the strategy of the Bank; each rests on a governed foundation of people, data, and a shared AI Platform. Each advances at its own pace through five Maturity Levels, and a level is reached when its Measures are met. AICC shapes the AI adoption strategy within them as Proposals on which the Bank decides.

Approach

A function brings a need to AICC. AICC takes it in as run-rate work or as an Initiative, probes an Initiative first as a minimum viable product, measures it against defined success Measures, and extends it only on that evidence. The work is visible, limited in progress, and paced by a common cadence. Before a Solution reaches its users, it is checked by a person other than its builder, or validated by the Control Function Contacts, in proportion to its Risk Tier; then it is accepted by the product owner, the AICC Lead, and the Domain Owner who asked for it. AICC applies this way of working to itself and is the example of what it proposes to the Bank.

How to engage Portfolio Delivery

Authority and limits

AICC acts under the mandate of the Executive Sponsor, whom the Board names. It does not own or operate the AI Platform, own the business results of a Domain, set the rules of a Control Function, or validate its own work, and it is not a Control Function or a platform team of the Bank and does not operate a Solution at the scale of the Bank. It reports to the Executive Sponsor, who may bring its reports, findings, strategy, and Proposals to the Board Committee or the Board. The Control Functions stay independent and may stop a Solution, and internal audit gives assurance only. The AICC Lead reports to the Executive Sponsor each quarter. The Board oversees AI through the Board Committee, which the Executive Sponsor tells at once of a major AI Incident and of a risk accepted beyond the AI Risk Appetite Statement.

Organization Governance

Safeguards

Each Solution is assigned a Risk Tier, which sets the review, validation, human oversight, and speed of release that apply to it. The Bank accepts risk from AI where the benefit is measured and the controls are proportionate, and it does not accept a breach of law or regulation.
