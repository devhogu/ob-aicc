Candidate Project Charter — Card Dispute Progress Support

Status: candidate for selection and completion

Parent proposal: Customer Intelligence–Enabled Service Resolution

Technical profile: Card Dispute Progress Support

Parent proposal version/reference at approval: [document version or commit]

Decision requested

Approve a controlled employee-assist pilot for one dispute type and servicing queue. Authorize the named business team and capacity to establish the baseline, complete control decisions, operate the pilot, and return with evidence for scale, refinement, or closure.

Project intent

Reduce avoidable status contacts and customer uncertainty during card disputes by presenting the servicing employee with the authoritative dispute stage, completed actions, outstanding evidence, required communication, and approved next step.

Common delivery commitments

This charter incorporates the parent proposal's delivery workflow, process integration, governance and RACI, control coverage, operational workflow, dependencies, and continuing-ownership requirements. The journey-specific boundaries and acceptance conditions below apply where they are more restrictive. A material change to customer cohort, dispute type, data purpose, user, channel, decision authority, or automation level requires reassessment and approval.

Problem and selected scope

Included: one dispute type and service queue; authoritative dispute case state; prior contacts and approved communications; evidence requested and received; applicable milestones, deadlines, and escalation conditions.

Excluded: dispute adjudication by the capability, fraud determination, eligibility or reimbursement decisions, alteration of statutory or scheme deadlines, legal interpretation, and customer communication without employee approval.

Evidence and control-limit schedule required before live use

The completed charter records eligibility and exclusions, metric formulas and denominators, baseline/comparison design, observation period, state and exception coverage, live-workflow sample, and outcome sample or directional-evidence rationale. Each control limit records its metric, source, owner, baseline-informed tolerance, review frequency, and suspension threshold.

Acceptance rule

The Sponsor may approve scale when the primary customer outcome and one operational benefit meet their confirmed targets, all mandatory quality and control-limit criteria pass, Disputes Operations accepts the stage, evidence, deadline, and action interpretation, and the Measurement Owner confirms that the evidence supports that decision.

Directional evidence may authorize only a defined extension or further measurement. Refine applies when quality and controls pass but benefit evidence is inconclusive. Stop applies when dispute state or deadlines cannot be represented reliably or customer dispute rights could be impaired.

Risk, compliance, conduct, and privacy position

  • Employee-assistive use remains within the existing dispute-servicing process; the employee verifies the evidence and owns the response.
  • Dispute stage, evidence, deadlines, and communication remain grounded in authoritative records and approved procedures.
  • The approved purpose and minimum necessary data fields are recorded before use.
  • Customer communication, required notices, transparency, accessibility, and vulnerable-customer handling follow approved dispute-service rules.
  • Recommendations, source evidence, employee decisions, corrections, and outcomes are traceable and retained under an approved rule.
  • Incidents, suspension authority, restart approval, and material-change triggers are agreed before live use.
  • Adjudication, fraud determination, reimbursement, and regulated decision obligations remain in existing governed processes.
  • Missing or conflicting evidence and any deadline uncertainty route to a specialist.
