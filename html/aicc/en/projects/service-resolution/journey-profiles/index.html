Customer Intelligence–Enabled Service Resolution — Journey Technical Profiles

Purpose

The three candidate journeys use the common Technical Pilot Blueprint while retaining distinct banking systems, information boundaries, knowledge, tools, critical risks and validation requirements. Bank-specific products, APIs, data platforms and AI runtimes remain discovery decisions.

Each profile states:

  • which banking capabilities and authoritative information are required;
  • how that information is used by deterministic services and GenAI;
  • which knowledge and tools are permitted;
  • which information and decisions remain outside the AI-assisted workflow;
  • how the journey degrades or narrows when a capability is unavailable;
  • what IT must demonstrate before controlled live use.

Actual system names, owners, interfaces, volumes, service expectations, and selected products are completed during bank capability discovery.

Shared architecture and journey-specific boundaries

The common platform stays stable. Each overlay replaces the journey system, state model, information boundary, knowledge corpus, permitted tools, critical errors, and outcome definition.

Profile completion rule

Before a profile becomes implementation-ready, IT and the Journey Process Owner record:

Required decision What must be established
Authoritative sources named system or governed service for every displayed state, deadline, document/evidence status and outcome
Identity and linkage identifiers and matching rules connecting customer, journey object, case and contacts; quarantine behavior for uncertainty
Integration path existing API/event, controlled façade, or governed extract; freshness, supported use and owner
Context contract allow-listed fields, provenance, classifications, exclusions, freshness and retention
State interpretation recognized process states, transitions, blockers, exceptions and permitted customer-facing language
Knowledge product approved sources, owners, effective dates, access, withdrawal and retrieval tests
Tool authority read and write tools, prerequisites, limits, confirmation, idempotency, evidence and prohibited actions
Evaluation profile representative states, material exceptions, critical-error definitions, locked cases and live sampling
Operating dependency expected availability, latency, volume, fallback, support owner and suspension path appropriate to the pilot

If an authoritative source, safe information boundary, or supported live interface cannot be established, the journey remains historical or silent-evaluation only or is narrowed to the states that can be supported. GenAI must not compensate for a missing source of truth.

Payment Issue Resolution profile

Business charter: Payment Issue Resolution

Technical intent and boundary

Support one domestic retail-payment type with failed, pending, or reversed states for which the bank can obtain an authoritative status and an established servicing response. Unrecognized payments, fraud, disputes/chargebacks, sanctions or AML holds, cross-border/correspondent ambiguity, reimbursement decisions, and funds movement remain outside the profile.

Required bank capabilities and use

Required capability How the profile uses it If the capability is unavailable or unreliable
Customer/contact source identify the customer request and related contacts restrict to historical linked cases or stop live use where linkage cannot be trusted
Payment source of truth obtain payment identifier, type, timestamps and authoritative failed/pending/reversed state exclude the unsupported state or payment type; do not infer state from contact notes
Case or investigation workflow obtain owner, completed/pending service step and resolution; create an approved referral or note operate read-only and retain the existing manual write process
Complaint/escalation record identify formal complaint or specialist handling already in progress route possible complaints to the existing complaint check before advice
Payment-service knowledge ground state explanations, expected process and permitted action restrict output to verified facts and manual procedure lookup until the corpus is approved
Outcome reporting identify repeat contact, progression, reopen, complaint and handling effort run technical and quality validation only; do not claim customer benefit until outcome evidence is available

Functional allocation

Function Realization
Deterministic customer/payment/case linkage, authoritative status, eligibility, state-code mapping, freshness, permitted-action filtering
GenAI contact-history interpretation, concise timeline, likely service blocker, procedure-supported explanation and draft next step
Knowledge retrieval (RAG) current payment-state guidance, servicing procedure, communication wording, referral and escalation rules
Permitted tools read payment/case/contact state; record feedback/outcome; draft note; create an employee-approved referral where an existing API supports it
Critical exclusions state change, funds movement, fraud/dispute decision, reimbursement, regulated hold interpretation, autonomous customer communication

Journey-specific technical validation

  • Payment, customer, contact and case records link correctly or are quarantined.
  • The displayed payment state always comes from the authoritative source and carries freshness.
  • Each supported state maps to a Journey Process Owner-approved explanation and action set.
  • Conflicting status, missing investigation ownership, fraud/dispute indicators and excluded payment types route to the correct manual or specialist path.
  • Historical, silent and live evaluation separately measure state accuracy, action accuracy, unsupported explanation, employee correction and repeat-contact outcome.

Card Dispute Progress Support profile

Business charter: Card Dispute Progress Support

Technical intent and boundary

Support one dispute type and service queue by explaining authoritative dispute stage, completed actions, evidence state, responsible owner, applicable deadline, and permitted next service step. Adjudication, fraud determination, eligibility, reimbursement, legal interpretation, or alteration of scheme/statutory obligations remain outside the profile.

Required bank capabilities and use

Required capability How the profile uses it If the capability is unavailable or unreliable
Dispute system of record obtain dispute identifier, type, stage, milestones, owner and authoritative deadline data exclude states lacking authoritative stage/deadline information; retain specialist handling
Transaction reference connect the dispute to the correct card transaction without deciding fraud quarantine ambiguous linkage and route to existing verification
Evidence metadata show evidence type, request, receipt and validation status without unnecessary document content limit the profile to stage/status explanation; do not generate evidence requests from incomplete metadata
Communication record identify notices already sent and required servicing communications require manual confirmation against the dispute system before communication
Effective dispute knowledge retrieve the applicable internal and scheme-approved servicing procedure remain in silent evaluation for next-step generation until authority and effective dates are controlled
Outcome and deadline monitoring measure status contacts, evidence rework, handoff, complaint and missed-deadline events restrict the decision to quality/workflow learning; no scale decision on customer benefit

Functional allocation

Function Realization
Deterministic dispute/customer/transaction linkage, authoritative stage, deadline calculation or source value, evidence status, required-notice and permitted-action rules
GenAI prior-contact summarization, status explanation, identification of repeated/conflicting evidence requests, procedure-grounded next-step draft
Knowledge retrieval (RAG) effective dispute-servicing procedure, evidence guidance, required communication and escalation conditions
Permitted tools read dispute/evidence/communication state; record feedback and outcome; draft evidence request; create employee-approved specialist referral where supported
Critical exclusions adjudication, fraud finding, eligibility, reimbursement, deadline modification, legal interpretation or impairment of customer rights

Journey-specific technical validation

  • Dispute, transaction, customer and contact linkage is correct or quarantined.
  • Stage, evidence status, owner and deadline are authoritative, current and replayable.
  • No generated interpretation changes a deadline, customer right, adjudication state or reimbursement decision.
  • Any deadline, scheme-rule or evidence ambiguity triggers specialist fallback before customer communication.
  • Evaluation explicitly covers every common stage, evidence state, deadline boundary, prior-notice condition and material exception available for the selected dispute type.

Onboarding/KYC Progress Support profile

Business charter: Onboarding/KYC Progress Support

Technical intent and boundary

Support one customer segment and onboarding path using an approved customer-servicing metadata projection. Explain only established service state, permitted outstanding information, responsible queue and approved next step. The service resolution solution must not receive or infer restricted financial-crime rationale.

Required bank capabilities and use

Required capability How the profile uses it If the capability is unavailable or unreliable
Onboarding/KYC source of truth supply application identifier and authoritative customer-service state restrict to historical analysis; do not derive state from employee notes or document contents
Approved metadata projection expose only check completion, document type/status, approved reason code, responsible queue and safe timestamps live GenAI use is blocked until the projection or equivalent allow-listed service exists
Customer-facing reason-code catalogue translate permitted service state into consistent, approved language display status only and route explanation to a trained specialist
Prior approved communication prevent repeated or conflicting customer requests employee verifies prior communication manually where it cannot be obtained safely
Servicing knowledge corpus provide customer-facing onboarding procedure distinct from restricted KYC policy/intelligence restrict generation to source-linked status facts and manual specialist guidance
Outcome and cohort reporting measure repeat request, progression, abandonment, complaint and materially different treatment restrict claims to technical quality until sufficient outcome and cohort evidence is available

Functional allocation

Function Realization
Deterministic application/customer linkage, allow-listed field projection, completion state, document status, approved reason code, queue and permitted-action rules
GenAI approved communication-history summary, safe service-state explanation, consolidation of permitted information requests and next-step draft
Knowledge retrieval (RAG) customer-facing onboarding support procedure, permitted document guidance, routing and escalation; restricted financial-crime content remains separate
Permitted tools read the approved metadata projection and prior communications; record feedback/outcome; draft a permitted request; create employee-approved routing/referral
Critical exclusions documents/images, analyst notes, risk scores, screening hits, suspicious-activity information, approval/rejection, risk-rating change, control bypass or restricted rationale

Journey-specific technical validation

  • Architecture tests demonstrate that excluded restricted fields and content have no path into context, retrieval, prompts, traces or employee output.
  • The displayed service state, permitted reason code, document status and queue come from the approved projection and carry source/freshness evidence.
  • The capability cannot infer, expose or imply a regulated reason for delay, approval or rejection.
  • Inconsistent state, missing permitted reason, vulnerability, potential discriminatory treatment or financial-crime concern routes to a specialist.
  • Evaluation covers common onboarding states, repeated requests, reason-code gaps, restricted-data injection attempts and relevant cohort outcomes.

This division is the practical platform test. A second journey should reuse the common interfaces while replacing its overlay. Where reuse requires copying or bypassing the common services, the platform contract has not yet been proven.
