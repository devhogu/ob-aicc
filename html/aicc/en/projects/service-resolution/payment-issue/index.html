Candidate Project Charter — Payment Issue Resolution

Status: candidate for selection and completion

Parent proposal: Customer Intelligence–Enabled Service Resolution

Technical profile: Payment Issue Resolution

Parent proposal version/reference at approval: [document version or commit]

Decision requested

Approve a controlled employee-assist pilot for one domestic retail-payment type and servicing queue. Authorize the named business team and capacity to establish the baseline, complete control decisions, operate the pilot, and return with evidence for scale, refinement, or closure.

Project intent

Reduce repeated customer contact about failed, pending, or reversed payments by giving the servicing employee one evidence-linked account of the payment state, prior service history, responsible owner, and approved next action.

Common delivery commitments

This charter incorporates the parent proposal's delivery workflow, process integration, governance and RACI, control coverage, operational workflow, dependencies, and continuing-ownership requirements. The journey-specific boundaries and acceptance conditions below apply where they are more restrictive. A material change to customer cohort, payment type, data purpose, user, channel, decision authority, or automation level requires reassessment and approval.

Problem and selected scope

Included: one defined domestic retail-payment type and service queue; failed, pending, and reversed states for which an authoritative status and approved resolution action exist; related customer contacts, cases, investigation status, and approved communications.

Explicitly outside the pilot:

Excluded matter Reason for exclusion
unrecognized payments or suspected fraud requires fraud investigation, customer-protection rights, and separate decision authority
disputes and chargebacks governed by scheme/legal deadlines, evidence rules, and adjudication processes
sanctions or AML holds involves confidential regulated assessment and restricted information
cross-border or correspondent payments status may depend on multiple institutions and cannot always be established authoritatively
reimbursement or compensation decisions determines customer entitlement and financial outcome
movement, reversal, or correction of funds by the capability exceeds employee-assist interpretation and requires transaction controls
customer communication without employee approval exceeds the pilot's controlled operating model

Targets are confirmed after baseline; the percentages below are planning hypotheses rather than pre-approved claims.

Evidence and control-limit schedule required before live use

The completed charter records eligibility and exclusions, metric formulas and denominators, baseline/comparison design, observation period, state and exception coverage, live-workflow sample, and outcome sample or directional-evidence rationale. Each control limit records its metric, source, owner, baseline-informed tolerance, review frequency, and suspension threshold.

Acceptance rule

The Sponsor may approve scale when the primary customer outcome and one operational benefit meet their confirmed targets, all mandatory quality and control-limit criteria pass, Payments Operations accepts the interpretation and action workflow, and the Measurement Owner confirms that the evidence supports that decision.

Directional evidence may authorize only a defined extension or further measurement. Refine applies when quality and controls pass but benefit evidence is inconclusive. Stop applies when payment state cannot be established reliably, the workflow creates material additional effort, or a control limit cannot be maintained.

Risk, compliance, conduct, and privacy position

  • Employee-assistive use remains within the existing Payments servicing process; the employee verifies the evidence and owns the response.
  • Payment status and next step remain grounded in authoritative records and actions approved by the Journey Process Owner.
  • The approved purpose and minimum necessary data fields are recorded before use.
  • Customer communication, transparency, accessibility, and vulnerable-customer handling follow approved service rules.
  • Recommendations, source evidence, employee decisions, corrections, and outcomes are traceable and retained under an approved rule.
  • Incidents, suspension authority, restart approval, and material-change triggers are agreed before live use.
  • Fraud, disputes, AML, sanctions, authorization, reimbursement, compensation, and funds movement remain in their existing governed processes.
  • Conflicting or incomplete payment records route to manual investigation.
