Proposal
Project proposal · Three selectable charters

Customer Intelligence–Enabled Service Resolution

Customers should receive one accurate explanation and a clear next step even when their history spans several banking systems. The proposed capability assembles authoritative service context, uses governed GenAI to interpret what happened, keeps employees accountable for communication and action, and records the outcome as reusable customer intelligence.

Fewer repeat contacts Faster confirmed resolution Employee-approved action Measured customer outcome Reusable customer intelligence

Expected value

Customer

One coherent explanation, less repetition, and a resolution or committed next step.

Bank operations

Less investigation effort, fewer avoidable handoffs, clearer ownership and visible process failures.

Strategic capability

Governed customer context, a controlled AI workflow, approved knowledge and outcome learning that can be reused across journeys.

Candidate pilot journeys

Payment Issue Resolution · Lower complexityFailed, pending or reversed domestic payments with authoritative status and an approved resolution action. Card Dispute Progress Support · MediumDispute stage, evidence, deadlines and permitted customer-status communication. Onboarding/KYC Progress Support · Medium–highSafe service-state and permitted information guidance through a restricted metadata boundary.
