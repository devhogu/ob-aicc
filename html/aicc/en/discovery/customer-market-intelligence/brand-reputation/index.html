Brand & reputation

Brand and reputation tracks the Bank's standing in public, regulatory, and customer perception — the intangible asset that determines customer trust, employee attraction, and the regulatory goodwill that shapes supervisory discretion. Reputational risk is commonly categorized as a material risk in supervisory frameworks, and conduct-related reputational events carry a direct supervisory consequence. Brand trust in banking is fragile: a single high-profile conduct failure or a social-media amplified service breakdown can move customer acquisition and retention metrics materially within days. GenAI enables continuous reputation monitoring across all public signals — from social media to regulatory filings — surfacing reputational threats before they escalate beyond containment.

Net promoter & brand equity tracking

Tracking NPS, brand-recall, and brand-equity scores over time — the structured measurement of how customers perceive and recommend the Bank. Brand equity is a composite measure of awareness, association, loyalty, and differentiation; NPS is the single-metric proxy most commonly used in financial-services board reporting.

Reputational risk early warning

A composite early-warning signal for reputational risk — combining social-media sentiment trends, complaint-escalation patterns, regulatory communication volume, and media-tone shifts into a single leading indicator for the board and senior leadership. Reputational risk is distinct from the underlying event that causes it: the early-warning signal is not the event itself, but the pattern of signals that precede material reputational damage. Supervisory frameworks commonly classify reputational risk formally and expect the board to oversee a systematic reputational-risk monitoring process.

Social & media sentiment

Continuous monitoring of brand mentions, sentiment scores, and narrative themes across social-media platforms and news sources — the real-time signal channel for brand perception. Social and media sentiment leads NPS: a viral negative event on social media typically precedes a survey-measured NPS decline by four to six weeks. In many markets, messaging platforms and local social networks are the primary social channels for banking discussion; monitoring these platforms requires local-language NLP capabilities.

Sentiment Crisis Threshold Alert

The AI agent monitors social-sentiment trend velocity in real time, triggers an escalation alert to the communications leadership team when sentiment decline exceeds a crisis-threshold velocity, and pre-stages the relevant crisis-communication brief.

M
Problem to solve

The daily social-media monitoring brief identifies emerging negative themes but does not have a formal escalation mechanism tied to sentiment velocity. Communications leadership identifies a sentiment decline as a potential crisis when it is already visible in the aggregate score, typically two to three days after the threshold at which pre-emptive response would be most effective. Conduct requirements commonly expect a documented response to material reputational events within defined windows.

Solution

The AI agent monitors social-sentiment velocity in real time, compares the current decline rate against a predefined crisis-threshold parameter, and triggers an escalation alert to communications leadership the moment the threshold is crossed. The alert includes the current sentiment trajectory, the emergent theme driving the decline, and a link to the relevant pre-staged crisis-communication brief from the library.

Crisis communication readiness

Pre-staging response frameworks, key-message libraries, and stakeholder communication templates for the most probable reputational-crisis scenarios — the preparation that determines whether the Bank communicates in hours or days when an event occurs. Crisis scenarios in banking include: a data breach, a major service outage, a regulatory enforcement action, an employee fraud or misconduct case, and a viral social-media complaint. Supervisory guidance commonly expects regulated institutions to maintain documented crisis-communication plans.

Stakeholder Communication Readiness Check

The AI agent runs a quarterly readiness check of crisis-communication contact lists, escalation sequences, and channel access credentials, producing a gap report for the communications and business-continuity teams.

S
Problem to solve

Crisis-communication readiness depends on accurate stakeholder contact lists, escalation sequences, and channel access credentials — all of which decay over time as personnel change, channel platforms update their access controls, and regulatory contact points rotate. A quarterly readiness-gap report is produced for the communications and business-continuity teams; the communications team confirms the gaps, which are corrected at the quarterly check rather than discovered during a live event.

Product Complaint Concentration Analysis

The AI agent analyzes complaint volume relative to active product book size by product category, identifying which products have disproportionate complaint concentration as a trigger for conduct-risk review.

S
Problem to solve

Complaint volume by product is reported in absolute terms. A product with 150 complaints from a 50,000-customer base has a materially different conduct-risk profile from a product with 150 complaints from a 5,000-customer base, but both appear equally in the volume table. In conduct supervision, complaint rate relative to book size is commonly the metric that triggers conduct-risk escalation, not absolute complaint count.

Solution

The AI agent reads complaint volumes by product category and active-product-holder counts monthly, computes complaint rate per thousand active customers by product, and flags products above a threshold complaint rate for conduct-risk review. Compliance and the CRO receive the concentration analysis monthly; products with elevated complaint rates are escalated for root-cause investigation ahead of regulator inquiry.

OKR

Compliance and the CRO receive a monthly concentration analysis showing complaint rate per thousand active customers by product, with products above the threshold rate flagged for conduct-risk review.

Adoption

The AI agent produces the concentration analysis for ≥95% of scheduled monthly cycles for ≥12 consecutive months post go-live, covering all product categories.

Acceptance

≥80% of flagged products confirmed as warranting root-cause investigation by compliance.

Cycle

Complaint reporting moved from absolute volumes to a monthly rate-based analysis within 5 business days of month close.
