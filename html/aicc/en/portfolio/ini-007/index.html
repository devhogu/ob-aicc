INI-007 · Discovery: Scoping

Retail credit: credit lines, loans, and mortgages

If the Competence Center and the function discuss in full what AI can do for credit lines, loans, and mortgages and how to do it, then the function can start with the work that is most useful and controllable, beginning with the analysis of why mortgages are rejected.

Planned MVP

Minimum viable product: The approach to the analysis of mortgage rejections, agreed, and a first analysis where the data and the controls permit.

The MVP starts only after the business case is cleared by the Control Function Contacts, approved, and pulled within the limit.

  1. Hold working sessions with the function on credit lines, loans, and mortgages end to end: the process, the routine work, the knowledge it relies on, and its pain points.
  2. Identify and rank what AI can do and how, including the routine work of the day and the knowledge base (INI-008).
  3. Discover the analysis of mortgage rejections: what rejection data and reasons exist, where, and in what form; the questions the function wants answered; how AI can consolidate and analyze the reasoning behind rejections.
  4. Define the approach, the data, the output, and the controls, with the Control Function Contacts.
  5. Run a first analysis where the data is available and the controls permit.
  6. Agree the next steps with the function.

Out of scope: Any decision or recommendation on an individual application; any change to credit policy; model-based scoring. A person always decides on a mortgage, and AI only consolidates and analyzes.

Risk and clearances

Expected Risk Tier: Risk Tier 2 is expected.

Control Functions to clear the business case: Data protection, model risk, information security, and compliance.

An expected Risk Tier is not an assigned tier, and it does not mean that the business case is cleared or validated.
