Standing Initiative: Advise and formulate

For functions with small, repeatable needs in advise and formulate, a Standing Initiative gives each request a recorded owner, priority, acceptance criteria, and outcome while keeping delivery within one Iteration. The intended benefit is prompt, controlled support without a separate Initiative Brief and Service Agreement for every request.
