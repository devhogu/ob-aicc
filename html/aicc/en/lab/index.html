Concept

CloudLab

CloudLab is a concept for a controlled environment in which to test AI hypotheses before committing to wider use. An experiment starts with a business question, a bounded scope and evidence that could confirm or reject the hypothesis.

Approach

The environment supports exploration while keeping each experiment accountable: a named owner, permitted data, limited access, a time and cost budget, reproducible evaluation and an explicit decision at the end.

Hosting and model choices follow the requirements of the experiment and the approved conditions for data processing. The concept does not select a cloud provider or establish an operational environment.

Experiment workflow

  1. Define: state the question, intended outcome, current baseline and limits of the experiment.
  2. Prepare: establish the environment, permissions, permitted datasets and evaluation cases.
  3. Build: implement a bounded workflow with observable actions and a manual stop.
  4. Evaluate: compare quality, benefit, cost and failure behaviour against the agreed baseline.
  5. Decide: record the findings and recommend further work, a revised hypothesis or closure.

Evidence from an experiment supports a subsequent decision. It does not by itself authorise production access or live deployment.

Guidance to develop

The operating guidance will cover environment and access management, data admission and disposal, model and tool permissions, evaluation and reproducibility, incident handling, suspension, closure and handover.

The shared environment may support successive experiments. Each experiment has its own scope, time limit, evidence and outcome.
