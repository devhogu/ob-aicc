    Industry body of knowledge

    The charter of AICC is its own, and it draws on the common body of knowledge of the field: the frameworks for managing the risk of AI, the standards for managing it as a system, the principles that governments have agreed, and the practice of model risk, service management, and agile and lean delivery. This page lists the sources that matter to the Bank, in one line each, with where the charter uses them.

  • Страница

    Regulators and acts

    The Bank operates in the Kyrgyz Republic, within a region and a field where the rules for AI and data are set by many bodies. This page is the index of the regulators, the acts, the standards, and the frameworks that the Bank is aware of, by jurisdiction, with a page for each: who they are, what they set, whom they reach, why they matter to the Bank, and how the charter relates to them. The pages are for orientation; they quote no provision, and an entry here does not mean that the instrument binds the Bank. The Knowledge base page Acts and compliance states what the applicable ones require of a use of AI at the Bank.

Regulators, acts, standards, and frameworks

One page for each body or instrument the Bank is aware of, by jurisdiction: what it is, what it sets, whom it reaches, why it matters to the Bank, and how the charter relates to it. The index is the page Regulators and acts.

  • Kyrgyz Republic: National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic, Kyrgyz Republic: the Law on Personal Information and the authorized body for personal data, Kyrgyz Republic: the ministry responsible for digital development and the acts on digitalization, Kyrgyz Republic: the financial intelligence body and the legislation against money laundering and the financing of terrorism
  • Kazakhstan: Kazakhstan: the Law on Artificial Intelligence and the national concept for AI, Kazakhstan: the Law on Personal Data and Their Protection, Kazakhstan: the National Bank and the Agency for Regulation and Development of the Financial Market, Astana International Financial Centre
  • Russian Federation: Russian Federation: the Federal Law on Personal Data (152-FZ), Russian Federation: the National Strategy for the Development of AI and the experimental legal regimes, Bank of Russia on artificial intelligence in the financial market, Russian Federation: the Code of Ethics in the Field of AI
  • European Union: EU Artificial Intelligence Act, EU General Data Protection Regulation, EU Digital Operational Resilience Act and the guidance of the European Banking Authority
  • United States: NIST AI Risk Management Framework, United States supervisory guidance on model risk and on AI in credit, United States federal and state AI policy
  • Global: Lean portfolio management practice, OECD Principles on Artificial Intelligence, UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence, Council of Europe Framework Convention on Artificial Intelligence, G7 Hiroshima AI Process, ISO/IEC standards on AI: 42001, 23894, 22989, OWASP Top 10 for Large Language Model Applications, The financial standard-setters on AI: FSB, BCBS, BIS
