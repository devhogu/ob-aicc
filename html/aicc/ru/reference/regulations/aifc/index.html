Astana International Financial Centre

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

A financial centre in Kazakhstan with its own jurisdiction, courts, and regulator, built on common-law principles, whose rules on financial services, data protection, and technology, and whose fintech sandbox, shape practice in the region.

1. Identity

Item Entry
Kind Financial centre with its own regulatory framework
Issued or overseen by The AIFC and its Financial Services Authority
Jurisdiction Kazakhstan
Status Established 2018; its own rules on financial services, data, and technology; a regulatory sandbox for fintech

2. What it sets

¶2.1. Licensing and conduct rules for the participants of the centre; data protection regulations; a regulatory sandbox for fintech and for the testing of new services; and guidance on technology and digital assets.

3. Whom it reaches

¶3.1. The participants of the centre and the persons dealing with them.

4. Relevance to the Bank

¶4.1. A regional centre whose rules and sandbox are a reference for fintech practice and for how regulated experimentation can be organized; a possible venue for partners of the Bank.

5. How the charter relates to it

¶5.1. The Experiment workflow and the Lab of AICC follow the idea of bounded trial that a regulatory sandbox embodies.

    Kazakhstan: the National Bank and the Agency for Regulation and Development of the Financial Market

    The monetary authority and the financial supervisor of Kazakhstan. The agency regulates banks and other financial organizations, including their use of technology, data, and third parties, and has developed its approach to digital financial services and to the use of data and AI by the sector.
