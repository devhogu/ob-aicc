Lean portfolio management practice

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

The body of practice that treats a portfolio as a flow to be managed rather than a list of projects to be approved: strategic themes with investment envelopes and guardrails, a portfolio Kanban with limits on work in progress, a lean business case probed by a minimum viable product, and governance that is continuous and light. Its best-known codification is the Lean Portfolio Management competency of the Scaled Agile Framework.

1. Identity

Item Entry
Kind Industry practice and framework
Issued or overseen by Scaled Agile, Inc., for the Scaled Agile Framework; the wider lean and agile community for the practice
Jurisdiction Global
Status Current; the framework is revised periodically

2. What it sets

¶2.1. Three dimensions that interlock: strategy and investment funding, which translates strategic themes into investment and sets guardrails so that decisions can be local; agile portfolio operations, which coordinates the flow of large items through a portfolio Kanban with a regular sync and limits on work in progress; and lean governance, which carries compliance, audit, and forecasting continuously through the business case, the measures, and the review. The devices: the epic hypothesis statement and lean business case, the minimum viable product with a persevere or pivot decision, weighted shortest job first for ranking, participatory budgeting across value streams, and guardrails by investment horizon, capacity allocation, and approval thresholds.

3. Whom it reaches

¶3.1. Any organization that adopts it. It binds no one.

4. Relevance to the Bank

¶4.1. It is the practice that the Portfolio of AICC is built on, adapted to a small unit in a bank: a yearly frame instead of a rolling one, a single Team instead of many, and the clearances of the Control Functions written into the business case.

5. How the charter relates to it

¶5.1. The strategic loop with its Envelopes and Guardrails is the first dimension; the portfolio sync and the backlog care with the Kanban are the second; the portfolio review with the clearances and the measures is the third. The Portfolio Management Model states the model in the Bank's own terms and names none of the framework's vocabulary, by design.

  • Справочный материал

    OECD Principles on Artificial Intelligence

    The first intergovernmental standard on AI, adopted by the OECD members and adhering countries and taken up by the G20. It states what trustworthy AI is and what governments should do to foster it, and it supplies the definition of an AI system that most later instruments adopted.

  • Справочный материал

    UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence

    The global normative instrument on the ethics of AI, adopted by the member states of UNESCO. It frames AI in terms of human rights, human dignity, the environment, diversity, and peaceful societies, and it asks states to act through policy, assessment, and education.

  • Справочный материал

    Council of Europe Framework Convention on Artificial Intelligence

    The first international treaty on AI. It commits the states that ratify it to ensure that the activities within the life cycle of AI systems are consistent with human rights, democracy, and the rule of law, in the public sector and, as each state decides, in the private sector.

  • Справочный материал

    G7 Hiroshima AI Process

    The initiative of the Group of Seven on advanced AI. It produced guiding principles and a voluntary code of conduct for the organizations that develop the most capable models, on safety testing, transparency, incident reporting, and the responsible release of those models.

  • Справочный материал

    ISO/IEC standards on AI: 42001, 23894, 22989

    The international standards that give AI governance the form that organizations already know from quality, security, and service management: a management system with policy, roles, risk assessment, controls, monitoring, and improvement, together with guidance on AI risk management and the vocabulary of the field.

  • Справочный материал

    OWASP Top 10 for Large Language Model Applications

    The security community's list of the ten most important risks of applications built on large language models, maintained by the project that gave the web its best-known security list. It is the reference for the security test of an AI Solution.
