Bank of Russia on artificial intelligence in the financial market

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

The view of the central bank and financial supervisor of the Russian Federation on AI in finance: its benefits, its risks, and the approach to its regulation, developed in consultation with the market and leaning on soft law and codes before binding rules.

1. Identity

Item Entry
Kind Reports and guidance of a central bank and financial supervisor
Issued or overseen by Bank of Russia
Jurisdiction Russian Federation
Status Consultation and reports on the application of AI in the financial market from 2023; guidance and codes developed with the market

2. What it sets

¶2.1. The themes: the responsible use of AI by financial organizations, model risk, data, consumer protection and explainability, the risks of concentration and of large providers, and the balance between innovation and stability.

3. Whom it reaches

¶3.1. The financial sector of the Russian Federation.

4. Relevance to the Bank

¶4.1. The supervisory practice of the largest neighbouring market, and a likely influence on the supervisors of the region.

5. How the charter relates to it

¶5.1. The AI Risk Appetite Statement, the Risk Tiers, and the reporting to the Board Committee address the themes the Bank of Russia names.

6. Related pages

  • The financial standard-setters on AI
  • Regulators and acts

    The personal data law of the Russian Federation, notable for its requirement that the personal data of Russian citizens be recorded and stored in databases located in the Russian Federation and for its conditions on transfers abroad.

  • Справочный материал

    Russian Federation: the National Strategy for the Development of AI and the experimental legal regimes

    The direction of the Russian Federation on AI: a national strategy with goals to 2030, and a mechanism of experimental legal regimes under which AI and other digital innovations are trialled with temporary exemptions from ordinary rules, in defined places and sectors.

  • Справочный материал

    Russian Federation: the Code of Ethics in the Field of AI

    A voluntary code of ethics for the development and use of AI, drafted by the industry alliance and signed by a wide range of organizations in the Russian Federation. It states the ethical commitments that signatories undertake and creates a commission that interprets them.
