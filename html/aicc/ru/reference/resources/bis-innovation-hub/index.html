Bank for International Settlements and its Innovation Hub

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

The institution whose research central banks and supervisors read, and whose Innovation Hub builds working prototypes of supervisory and central-bank technology, including AI, with the central banks of its members. Together they are the most credible public source on what AI does in regulated finance and what supervisors make of it.

1. Identity

Item Entry
Kind International financial institution; research and applied technology projects
Who The Bank for International Settlements, the bank of the central banks; its Monetary and Economic Department for research; its Innovation Hub with centres in several financial centres
Access Free; publications and project reports on its site

2. What it publishes

¶2.1. Working papers and bulletins on AI, machine learning, data, and digital finance; the annual economic report, with chapters on AI and finance; the Innovation Hub's project reports, each a worked example of a technology applied to a supervisory or central-bank problem, with its design, its results, and its lessons; and the Basel Committee's publications, hosted on the same site, on the supervisory expectations of banks.

3. Why it matters to the Bank

¶3.1. What the BIS writes becomes, in time, what a supervisor asks. The Innovation Hub's projects show what regulated institutions can do with AI under supervision, which is the question AICC answers for the Bank. Its work on the risks of AI in finance, concentration, model risk, data, operational resilience, is the source behind the themes of the financial standard-setters page.

4. How to take it, and for what

¶4.1. For the supervisory view, read the bulletins and the annual report chapters first; they are short and written for decision-makers. For worked examples, read the Innovation Hub project reports in the areas the Bank works in: data and reporting, supervision technology, payments. For the risk themes, read the Basel Committee's papers on AI and on operational resilience. The AICC Lead reads the new publications each quarter for the watch; a Control Function Contact reads them for the themes of the risk check.

5. Cautions

¶5.1. The work is written for central banks; a project of the Innovation Hub is a prototype under supervisory conditions, not a product. Nothing of the Bank is entered into any tool or form of the site.

  • Справочный материал

    International Monetary Fund and the World Bank on fintech and AI

    The institutions that advise the governments and the central banks of the region on digital finance, inclusion, and, increasingly, AI. Their fintech notes, working papers, and country work set the policy context in which the Bank operates, and their inclusion agenda is one the Bank shares.

  • Справочный материал

    World Economic Forum on AI governance and financial services

    The forum where industry, regulators, and civil society state shared positions on AI and on the future of financial services, in reports written to be read by boards. Its AI Governance Alliance publishes frameworks on responsible generative AI; its financial services work publishes on the transformation of banking.

  • Справочный материал

    Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI: the AI Index

    The yearly AI Index is the reference for the data of the field: how much research, which models, at what cost, adopted where, regulated how, and what the public thinks. It is neutral, sourced, and updated each year, and it is the number to cite when a paper of the Bank needs one.

  • Справочный материал

    McKinsey, the McKinsey Global Institute, and QuantumBlack

    The most-cited source on the adoption and the value of AI in business: a yearly state-of-AI survey, the Global Institute's estimates of the economic potential of generative AI by function and industry, and a steady stream of pieces on AI in banking. It sets much of the vocabulary executives use, and it is read as the view of a firm that sells what it describes.
