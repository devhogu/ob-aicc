Strategy

The strategy of AICC is the strategy of the Bank applied to artificial intelligence. It starts from the mission and the Strategic Pillars of the Bank, is stated as seven Strategic Priorities, is carried through the way AICC invests, engages, delivers, and governs, and is measured along a road of five Maturity Levels. This page brings the strands together in one place; each points to the document that governs it.

The strategy of the Bank

The mission of the Bank is to improve people's lives with digital products and services. Its strategy rests on four Strategic Pillars. The adoption of AI serves each of them, as the Statement of Intent states, and the Strategic Priorities below are the themes through which it does so.

Strategic PillarContribution of AI adoptionCarried by
Innovative leadershipAI capability for every employee and for software development, and the use of AI and data to understand customersExpertise at the point of work; Software engineering; Customer intelligence
Financial inclusionDigital onboarding and identification, service in the Kyrgyz and Russian languages, and responsible lending decisions that extend access to financial servicesCustomer intelligence; AI in banking operations and systems
Shareholder value and dividendsHigher productivity, lower cost of routine operations, better decisions, and better information for investors and the Board, each measured against a baselineBusiness intelligence; Adoption within Domains; Information technology operations and service lifecycle
ESG goalsResponsible lending, fair treatment of customers, protection of data, and governance practice consistent with international standardsThe Principles of Application and the AI Policy, across every priority

Source: Statement of Intent 3

The AI adoption strategy: the Strategic Priorities

The Bank pursues seven Strategic Priorities, stated in order, each resting on a governed foundation of people, data, and a shared AI Platform. They are the strategic themes of the Portfolio: the Executive Sponsor sets them with the Board, each with an Investment Envelope, and every Initiative that AICC takes in fits one of them.

#Strategic PriorityObjectiveIntended outcome
1Customer intelligenceTo develop an end-to-end understanding of each customer across every service and product of the Bank.Risk-aware service, earlier detection of problems in customer journeys, and proactive support to customers.
2Business intelligenceTo provide AI-supported analysis to those who direct the business.Better-founded and faster decisions, and consistent communication with investors.
3Adoption within DomainsTo enable each Domain to use AI in support of its own processes.Time released for higher-value work and more consistent output.
4Expertise at the point of workTo make the Bank's current and compliant expertise available to each employee and, where appropriate, to each customer, at the point where it is needed.Consistent and compliant service and decisions, faster proficiency of new employees, retention of institutional knowledge, and a reliable basis for later AI agents.
5AI in banking operations and systemsTo embed AI in the flow of banking operations and in the banking systems that carry them, with decisions remaining with qualified persons.Shorter cycle times and fewer errors, with accountability retained.
6Information technology operations and service lifecycleTo apply AI to the daily operation of technology, separately from the development of software.Greater stability, faster recovery, and reduced manual effort in the operation of systems.
7Software engineeringTo apply AI throughout the software engineering lifecycle.Faster and safer delivery of the Bank's own Solutions.

Source: Statement of Intent 9; Statement of Intent 8

The strategy across the aspects of AICC

One strategy runs through every aspect of how AICC works. The table states, for each aspect, the strategic choice that the charter makes, who decides it, and where it is written.

The road: the Maturity Roadmap

The Bank advances the adoption of AI through five Maturity Levels. Each Strategic Priority advances at its own pace, and a level is reached for a priority when its Measures are met. The timing and the scope of advancement are set by the Executive Sponsor in the Priorities, not in the charter.

УровеньNameCapabilityUse of AI
1FoundationAI use is governed before it is extendedControlled experiments, and one approved assistant for a pilot group
2Controlled adoptionEmployees have secure access to AI, and knowledge capture beginsApproved assistants for employees, first knowledge bases, and first governed pilots in the priorities
3EmbeddedAI operates within selected processes and systems, under human decisionAI proposes and prepares in operations, in customer and business intelligence, and in technology operations, with persons deciding
4ScaledOne governed AI Platform is reused across DomainsAI is standard practice in every Strategic Priority, on shared gateways, the AI Registry, and Platform Guardrails, with data separated by classification
5AgenticBounded tasks are delegated to agents under continuous evaluationAgents act within limits and permissions set by Risk Tier, with human oversight on channels that the agent cannot influence

Source: Statement of Intent 11; Measures: Statement of Intent 11.3

How the strategy is set and kept

The strategy is set once a year and kept every quarter. At the yearly Steering, in the first two weeks of December, the Executive Sponsor sets the Strategic Priorities, the Investment Envelopes, and the Investment Guardrails from the strategy of the Bank and the priorities of the Domains, reads the benefit against each Envelope and the results against each priority, and reviews the documents and the AI Risk Appetite Statement (Portfolio Management Model 4.2). At the quarterly Steering the Executive Sponsor confirms the Roadmap and the mix of Initiatives within the Envelopes, decides on each Active Initiative, and issues the Quarterly Report to the Board Committee (Portfolio Management Model 4.3, AICC Charter 7.2). The Statement of Intent is reviewed at least annually and on a material change in the use of AI, in a principal provider, or in regulation (Statement of Intent 13.3). The Strategic Priorities in force, with their Envelopes, are recorded in the Priorities Record of the Registry; this site states the strategy, and the Registry states its current state.

    What we do

    AICC is the internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank for artificial intelligence. It advises and formulates, builds and runs, enables, and assures, across strategy, programs, solutions, and ways of working. It is a small delivery unit that proves the case and relies on the platform teams and the IT functions of the Bank to scale.

  • Страница

    How we work

    AICC works as a consulting unit works. A function brings a need; AICC takes it in, studies it, commits to it, proves it, builds it, and supports it, and each step leaves a record and a decision that the function can see. The Portfolio decides what AICC works on. Delivery builds it in small steps on a common cadence, with quality and control inside the flow rather than after it.
