Explore AICC

The charter is the body of knowledge of the AI Competence Center. It states how the unit is mandated, governed, and operated, and it is the source of truth for what AICC is, what it produces, and how it keeps its records. This page shows how the charter is organized on this site and where to start.

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

The charter in four parts

The charter has four kinds of content. Each answers one question, and each has its place on this site. The Registry and the Portfolio hold the records of the work; they are outside the charter and are not on this site.

PartAnswersOn this site
DocumentsWhat are the rules?About AICC, Responsible AI, Services, Portfolio, Delivery, Governance and oversight, and Organization, each document in the section of its subject; long documents are split into parts
WorkflowsHow does the work flow, and on which events?Beside the documents that they illustrate, in Responsible AI, Services, Delivery, and Governance and oversight
GuidesWho does what, when, and what is left on record?Beside their workflows, as companion guides
TemplatesWhat is the form of each record?Knowledge base, with the guides, the acts and compliance, and the publications
Registry and PortfolioWhat is the state of the work, and what is the evidence? Which Solutions exist, and in what state?Outside this site, on the corporate folder; the page Records and systems states where each record is kept: Records and systems

The sections of this site

О центре AICC

AICC is the internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank for the adoption of artificial intelligence.

  • Statement of Intent · Документ — The intent, values, principles, and strategy of the Bank for AI
  • Strategy · Страница — The strategy of the Bank, the Strategic Priorities, the strategic choices across every aspect of AICC, and the road of Maturity Levels
  • Charter · Документ — Mission, authority, funding, risk appetite, offer, and measures of AICC
  • What we do · Страница — AICC is the internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank for artificial intelligence. It researches and advises, proves and builds, runs what is worth running, teaches, and oversees, across strategy, programs, solutions, and ways of working. It is a small delivery unit that proves the case and relies on the platform teams and the IT functions of the Bank to scale.
  • How we work · Страница — A function brings a need; AICC takes it in, studies it, commits to it, proves it, builds it, and supports it, with a record and a decision at each step. The Portfolio decides what AICC works on; Delivery builds it in small steps on a common cadence, with quality and control inside the flow.
  • Values and principles · Страница — The values and the principles of adoption, application, work, and delivery, and what each applies to
  • Explore AICC · Страница — How the charter is organized on this site, where each part is, and where to start

Услуги

AICC serves the functions and units of the Bank as their internal consulting and innovation lab.

  • Strategy and governance office · Service line — What AICC built for itself it builds for any unit of the Bank: a strategy, a charter, an operating and governance model, a process set with its templates, and a portal and repository that hold them, developed with AI in weeks and maintained with it afterwards. AICC acts here as the strategy office and the governance office of the function, and the function keeps the ownership.
  • Normative documents and processes · Service line — Every function writes and keeps normative documents: policies, procedures, regulations, instructions, runbooks, and the process descriptions behind them. AICC helps HR, legal, accounting, compliance, operations, technology, and any other function draft, align, and maintain them with AI, in the style of the Bank and in one voice, under the ownership of the function.
  • Knowledge services · Service line — The documents of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, turned into a governed knowledge base that it can ask. Legal, compliance, accounting, operations, and any function with a corpus get an assistant that answers from approved, current, cited sources, for its own people and, where the function decides, for its clients.
  • Workplace automation · Service line — The routine work of a function done by assistants under human validation: routing requests, reading and filling forms, processing reports, consolidating inputs, and producing documents from templates. The person validates and promotes; the assistant prepares.
  • Information and decision support · Service line — Facts from many sources prepared, consolidated, and presented so that a function decides on data: the pipelines that collect and transform, the dashboards that show, and the analytical and research tooling that supports the work.
  • Content and document engines · Service line — Public, investor, and management material generated from governed data and templates, pre-filled for people to finish: periodic reports, disclosures, investor material, standard letters and documents, in the languages the Bank uses.
  • Enablement at the workplace · Service line — Each person able to use AI for their own routine, within the rules: training by role, coaching at the desk, Domain Experts who carry adoption in their function, prompt and skill libraries per line of work, and communities of practice.
  • Assurance and governance support · Service line — A function's use of AI made compliant without the function becoming an expert in the rules: the Risk Tier assessed, the AI Registry entry made, the provider assessed, the tool evaluated and tested before use, the Acceptance Checklist prepared, and the Adopted Solutions of others overseen under the same rules.
  • Watch, research, and partnering · Service line — The function knows what exists, what works, and what the rules require, before it invests. AICC watches the models, the platforms, the methods, and the regulation; digests the changes for the functions concerned; tests what matters in trials; and works with other organizations and with providers where the Bank gains from it.
  • The service model · Страница — A service of AICC is not a promise in the air. It is composed of defined parts, it has a life with states and gates, it is operated under named practices, and it leaves records. This page states the model that every service of AICC follows, whatever its line. It is the template; the services in operation are its instances and are kept in the Portfolio.
  • How to engage · Страница — A function does not need a form, a budget line, or a project to start with AICC. It brings a need. From there the Engagement follows six steps, each with a record and a decision that the function can see.
  • Service catalog: the form · Страница — The catalog of AICC lists what AICC runs, has delivered, is proving, and can re-deploy. This page states the form of the catalog: the two kinds of entry, the fields of each, and the states an entry passes through. The live catalog is an instance of this form, kept in the Portfolio of AICC and presented in the live portal; this site states the form only. One entry of each kind is shown as an illustration.
  • Business Model · Документ — What AICC is, whom it serves, what it offers, how it commits, and how it tracks value and flow
  • Engagement workflow · Процесс — The top-level workflow between AICC and the rest of the Bank: how AICC works as an internal consulting unit, from the first contact to the follow-on, with its Service Agreement, Outcome Report, and support levels
  • Guide: Engagement · Руководство — How AICC engages a function, commits to it, delivers, and reports

Портфель

The Portfolio decides what AICC works on.

  • Portfolio Management Model · Документ — How AICC decides which business initiatives to take in, fund, continue, defer, or reject: the strategic inputs, the four portfolio loops, the portfolio Kanban, the business case, and the MVP
  • The service lines in the Portfolio · Страница — Every service of AICC enters through the same front door and the same Portfolio. What differs is the lane: run-rate work is small and repeatable and flows in days; a program is an Initiative with a business case and an MVP and flows through the portfolio loops. This page states how the nine lines map to the two lanes, how an item is screened, and what the Portfolio shows at a glance.

Поставка

AICC delivers in small steps on a common cadence: the Capabilities and Features of an Initiative are built in Iterations of one month within Program Increments of one quarter, the work is visible and limited in progress, and a Solution is checked or validated, accepted by the Team, and accepted by its Domain Owner before it reaches its users.

  • Solution Lifecycle Model · Документ — How AICC delivers: the principles, the flow of value, the backlogs and the Program Board, the states, the cadence and its loops, verification and release, the life cycle, and the measures
  • The Experiment workflow: the Lab · Страница — Before the Bank invests, AICC proves. An Experiment is a time-boxed trial of a Solution, a platform, or a concept, run on governed data in a bounded environment, measured against leading indicators, and ended with a decision: promote to delivery, hand over to a Receiver, or shelve with its lessons. This page states the workflow of an Experiment and the rules of the environment in which it runs, the Lab of AICC. The workflow is stated on this site and is proposed for the Solution Lifecycle Model 7.
  • The life of a Service · Страница — A Service that AICC runs lives after its release. The Solution Lifecycle Model gives it three Stages, Operate, Evolve, and Retire; this page refines them into seven states, each with the question that is asked at it, the signals that answer it, the action that follows, and the gate to the next state. The refinement is stated on this site and is proposed for the Solution Lifecycle Model 8.
  • Service operations · Страница — The Business Model states that the support of a Service is managed in Service Management and that requests and incidents come to the queue of AICC. This page states the practices behind that sentence, sized to a small unit: how a request, an incident, a problem, a change, and a question are handled for a Service that AICC runs, what the service levels are, and what each practice leaves on record. It is the run-book template of a Service of AICC; each Service fills it in its Service Agreement and its Solution Definition.
  • Portfolio and service delivery workflow · Процесс — The value stream from a business need to a retired solution: the portfolio Kanban (funnel, review, analysis, backlog, MVP, and the decision after it), the Capabilities and Features, execution, deployment, operation, and the life cycle with its change, retirement, and measures
  • Guide: Service delivery · Руководство — How an item moves from a need to a retired Solution, and who decides on the way
  • Cadence · Процесс — The events of the loops by week, Iteration, and PI, without dates
  • Guide: Cadence · Руководство — The rhythm of the events, the calendar, and what each beat leaves on record
  • Collaboration tooling · Процесс — The tools and the portals that AICC uses, for what, and in which workflows

База знаний

The working knowledge of AICC: the thirteen templates that give the form of every record, the guides that explain the workflows, the acts and policies that the use of AI answers to and how AICC complies, and the publications of AICC for the Bank.

  • Initiative Brief, Service Agreement, Solution Definition, Acceptance Checklist, Control Sign-Off, Decision Record, Steering Summary, Outcome Report, AI Incident Review, Registry Snapshot, Quarterly Report, Appointments Record, Proposal
  • Package Definition (draft template) — A draft template, proposed for the templates of the charter. A package is the reusable asset that an Engagement leaves: a method, a kit, an engine, a catalog of automations, or a template set, which the next Engagement takes in days where the first took weeks. The Package Definition is the record of one package. Until it is adopted into the charter by a decision record, this page is the draft form.
  • Guides — The five guides in one place: who does what, when, and what is left on record, each beside its workflow
  • Acts and compliance — The use of AI at the Bank answers to law, to regulation, and to the policies of the Bank. This page states, for each act or policy that applies, what it requires of a Solution and where the AI Policy and the controls of AICC answer it. It is a working reference kept with the Control Function Contacts; it states no rule of its own, and the acts and the policies prevail.
  • Publications — What AICC learns it writes down and makes available to the Bank: the documents of the charter, the Proposals that the Bank has decided on, the method notes and playbooks from the Engagements, and the lessons of what did not work. This page is the list.

  • Vocabulary · Справочный материал — The defined terms of the charter and its style

  • Change history · Справочный материал — The revision history of the documents

  • Industry body of knowledge · Страница — The charter of AICC is its own, and it draws on the common body of knowledge of the field: the frameworks for managing the risk of AI, the standards for managing it as a system, the principles that governments have agreed, and the practice of model risk, service management, and agile and lean delivery. This page lists the sources that matter to the Bank, in one line each, with where the charter uses them.
  • Regulators and acts · Страница — The Bank operates in the Kyrgyz Republic, within a region and a field where the rules for AI and data are set by many bodies. This page lists the regulators and the acts that the Bank is aware of, by jurisdiction: who they are, what they set, whom they reach, and why they matter to the Bank. The Knowledge base page Acts and compliance states what the applicable ones require and how AICC complies.

How the charter is kept

Every page of this site is generated from the documents of the charter at the baseline revision stated in the footer; where a page and a document differ, the document prevails. The Document Catalog states the status, the revision, and the life cycle of each document and template, and the AICC Lead activates them. Each document carries its change history, shown at the end of its page. The controls that can be tested are listed in the Operating Model, section 8, and the evidence of each is held in the Registry.
