Explore AICC

The charter is the body of knowledge of the AI Competence Center. It states how the unit is mandated, governed, and operated, and it is the source of truth for what AICC is, what it produces, and how it keeps its records. This page shows how the charter is organized on this site and where to start.

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

The charter in four parts

The charter has four kinds of content. Each answers one question, and each has its place on this site. The Registry and the Portfolio hold the records of the work; they are outside the charter and are not on this site.

PartAnswersOn this site
DocumentsWhat are the rules?About AICC, What AICC does, How AICC works, Organization, Responsible AI, and Governance and oversight, each document in the section of its subject; long documents are split into parts
WorkflowsHow does the work flow, and on which events?Beside the documents that they illustrate, in What AICC does, How AICC works, Responsible AI, and Governance and oversight
GuidesWho does what, when, and what is left on record?Beside their workflows, as companion guides
TemplatesWhat is the form of each record?Library
Registry and PortfolioWhat is the state of the work, and what is the evidence? Which Solutions exist, and in what state?Outside this site, on the corporate folder; the page Records and systems states where each record is kept: Records and systems

О центре AICC

AICC is the internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank for the adoption of artificial intelligence.

  • Statement of Intent · Документ — The intent, values, principles, and strategy of the Bank for AI
  • Charter · Документ — Mission, authority, funding, risk appetite, offer, and measures of AICC
  • Values and principles · Страница — The values and the principles of adoption, application, work, and delivery, and what each applies to
  • Explore AICC · Страница — How the charter is organized on this site, where each part is, and where to start

Что делает AICC

Раздел описывает услуги, которые AICC предоставляет функциям Банка, решения, которые он создаёт, и порядок обращения функции в AICC.

  • Business Model · Документ — What AICC is, whom it serves, what it offers, how it commits, and how it tracks value and flow
  • Engagement workflow · Процесс — The top-level workflow between AICC and the rest of the Bank: how AICC works as an internal consulting unit, from the first contact to the follow-on, with its Service Agreement, Outcome Report, and support levels
  • Guide: Engagement · Руководство — How AICC engages a function, commits to it, delivers, and reports

Как работает AICC

Раздел излагает метод AICC: как принимаются и решаются инициативы, как создаются, выпускаются и эксплуатируются решения, как организован ритм работы и как она измеряется.

  • Portfolio Management Model · Документ — How AICC decides which business initiatives to take in, fund, continue, defer, or reject: the strategic inputs, the four portfolio loops, the portfolio Kanban, the business case, and the MVP
  • Solution Lifecycle Model · Документ — How AICC delivers: the principles, the flow of value, the backlogs and the Program Board, the states, the cadence and its loops, verification and release, the life cycle, and the measures
  • Portfolio and service delivery workflow · Процесс — The value stream from a business need to a retired solution: the portfolio Kanban (funnel, review, analysis, backlog, MVP, and the decision after it), the Capabilities and Features, execution, deployment, operation, and the life cycle with its change, retirement, and measures
  • Guide: Service delivery · Руководство — How an item moves from a need to a retired Solution, and who decides on the way
  • Cadence · Процесс — The events of the loops by week, Iteration, and PI, without dates
  • Guide: Cadence · Руководство — The rhythm of the events, the calendar, and what each beat leaves on record
  • Collaboration tooling · Процесс — The tools and the portals that AICC uses, for what, and in which workflows

How the charter is kept

Every page of this site is generated from the documents of the charter at the baseline revision stated in the footer; where a page and a document differ, the document prevails. The Document Catalog states the status, the revision, and the life cycle of each document and template, and the AICC Lead activates them. Each document carries its change history, shown at the end of its page. The controls that can be tested are listed in the Operating Model, section 8, and the evidence of each is held in the Registry.
