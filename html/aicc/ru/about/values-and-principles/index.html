Values and principles

The values and principles of AICC follow the values of the Bank and the Statement of Intent on the adoption of artificial intelligence. They state what AICC stands for, how it decides, and how it delivers. AICC holds itself to them first, and asks the same of every Solution it proposes to the Bank.

Mission

To make artificial intelligence a trusted, everyday capability of the Bank: to prove its value with the functions that serve our customers, to set the standard by which the Bank adopts it, and to keep every use of it accountable to people, so that the Bank serves its customers better, works with greater confidence, and leads with care.

Values

Three values guide every decision of AICC: integrity, prudence, and respect for people. They are the values on which the Bank adopts AI, and AICC is their first keeper. Where speed and value compete with them, the values prevail.

  1. Integrity requires compliance with law and policy without exception.
  2. Prudence requires that the pace of adoption follows the level of risk.
  3. Respect for people requires that AI supports employees and customers, and that employees are trained and involved.

Source: Statement of Intent 4

Principles of adoption

AICC adopts AI where it earns its place. These principles decide where AI is introduced, where it is extended, and where it is withdrawn, so that the Bank invests in what works and learns from what does not.

  1. Value. AI is adopted where it delivers measured value to customers, employees, or the Bank, and its use is discontinued where it does not.
  2. Evidence. Each Solution is piloted and measured against defined success Measures, and is extended only on the basis of that evidence.
  3. Institutional knowledge. AI responses are based on approved, current, and traceable sources, which are cited.
  4. Reuse. Shared AI Platform components, knowledge, and standards take precedence over individual Solutions.
  5. People. AI augments the work of employees. Employees receive training before use, and specialists of each Domain take part in shaping the adoption of AI in that Domain.
  6. Shared standards. Principles and minimum standards are set for the whole Bank. Each Domain applies them within the regulation and the responsibility that apply to it.

Source: Statement of Intent 5

Principles of application

Every use of AI in the Bank answers to these principles, whether AICC built it or not. They are the commitment the Bank makes to its customers, its employees, its regulators, and its auditors: accountable, fair, explainable, private, secure, overseen by people, and compliant.

  1. Accountability. A named individual is accountable for each Solution, its outcomes, and its risks. AI does not hold accountability. The Bank is answerable for AI that it procures to the same extent as for AI that it develops. The Board oversees AI through regular reporting.
  2. Fairness. AI treats customers and employees fairly and in the interest of the customer. It is tested for bias before release and monitored in use, to the extent that the AI Policy requires for the Risk Tier of the Solution.
  3. Transparency and explainability. Outputs and decisions can be explained to customers, employees, regulators, and auditors. Individuals are informed when they interact with AI, to the extent that the AI Policy requires for the Risk Tier of the Solution.
  4. Privacy and data protection. AI uses only the data that it requires, retains that data within the boundaries of the Bank and its data classification rules, and protects it.
  5. Security and reliability. AI is tested, evaluated, and monitored, and is protected against attacks specific to AI. Critical AI services and providers are subject to fallback and exit arrangements.
  6. Human oversight and contestability. Humans oversee AI in proportion to risk and are able to stop or override it. Customers are able to reach a person and to contest a decision taken with the assistance of AI.
  7. Compliance and proportionate control. AI complies with applicable law, regulatory expectations, and Bank policy before release. Controls are proportionate to the Risk Tier. A control requirement is not bypassed. An Exception to it is decided by the Control Function concerned, is recorded in the Risks and Issues Record, and is limited in time.

Source: Statement of Intent 6

Principles of work

This is how AICC runs itself: close to the facts, quick to reverse, light on process, and measured by outcomes. AICC is the example of the way of working that it proposes to the Bank.

  1. Decide where the facts are. The person closest to the work decides, on evidence.
  2. Keep decisions reversible where possible, and take reversible decisions quickly.
  3. Deliver at product speed within bank-grade control.
  4. Keep only the process and the records that someone uses, and remove the rest.
  5. Put outcomes before outputs, and collaboration before negotiation. The plan changes and the intent holds.

Source: Operating Model 3

Principles of delivery

This is how the Teams of AICC build: visible, in small steps, value first, with quality and control built into the flow rather than added after it. Working Solutions and evidence count more than documents and opinion.

AICC delivers on the values of the Statement of Intent: integrity, prudence, and respect for people. In delivery they mean working Solutions over documents, collaboration with the function over negotiation, response to change over adherence to a fixed plan, and evidence over opinion.

  1. Make work visible, limit work in progress, and pull work when the Limits on Work in Progress allow.
  2. Deliver in small steps: probe with a minimum viable product, measure against the success Measures, then scale.
  3. Put value first: rank work by value and urgency relative to effort.
  4. Plan on a cadence. Work is paced by Program Increments and Iterations that start and end on fixed dates, and the plan of a Program Increment states intent and direction and not a promise of scope.
  5. Build quality in. The check or the validation and the controls of the AI Policy are steps of the flow, and not a stage after it.
  6. Decide where the facts are. The Team decides how the work is built, the product owner decides whether a Feature or a Capability is accepted during development, and the Domain Owner decides whether the Solution is accepted.
  7. Make dependencies visible, and manage them as work.
  8. Improve continuously. Each Iteration and each Program Increment ends with a review and an improvement.

Source: Solution Lifecycle Model 2

    What we do

    AICC is the internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank for artificial intelligence. It advises and formulates, builds and runs, enables, and assures, across strategy, programs, solutions, and ways of working. It is a small delivery unit that proves the case and relies on the platform teams and the IT functions of the Bank to scale.

  • Страница

    How we work

    AICC works as a consulting unit works. A function brings a need; AICC takes it in, studies it, commits to it, proves it, builds it, and supports it, and each step leaves a record and a decision that the function can see. The Portfolio decides what AICC works on. Delivery builds it in small steps on a common cadence, with quality and control inside the flow rather than after it.
