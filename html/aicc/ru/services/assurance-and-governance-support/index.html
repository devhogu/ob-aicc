Assurance and governance support

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

A function's use of AI made compliant without the function becoming an expert in the rules: the Risk Tier assessed, the AI Registry entry made, the provider assessed, the tool evaluated and tested before use, the Acceptance Checklist prepared, and the Adopted Solutions of others overseen under the same rules.

1. What it is

¶1.1. The line covers the Risk Tier assessment of a use and its entry in the AI Registry; the assessment of a provider of models or services with the Control Function Contacts; the evaluation and the testing of a tool against the data and the cases of the function before use; the preparation of the gates before use and of the Acceptance Checklist; and the oversight of the Adopted Solutions that others deliver and run.

¶1.2. AICC helps a function comply; it does not replace the Control Functions, which validate, may stop, and keep their own remit.

2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the line, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

  • A Risk Tier assessed and an AI Registry entry made for a use
  • A provider assessed with the Control Function Contacts
  • A tool evaluated against the cases of the function before use
  • The gates before use made explicit and the Acceptance Checklist prepared
  • An Adopted Solution recorded, reviewed, and reported
  • A proven prototype packaged for a platform team or IT to run

3. What the function receives

¶2.1. For the function: a Risk Tier with its reasons, an AI Registry entry, the evaluation results of the tool, the provider assessment, the gates made explicit, and a prepared Acceptance Checklist. For the Bank: one register of every AI use, built or bought, under one set of rules, and a place for each in the Quarterly Report.

4. How it runs

¶3.1. Run-rate: an assessment takes days to one Iteration. A new tool is evaluated against an evaluation set built with the Domain Expert. An Adopted Solution enters the Portfolio when a function declares it or when a Receiver accepts the Handover of an Experiment, and AICC follows its review, its incidents, and its changes through the Registry.

5. What it leads to

¶4.1. Functions that adopt AI at the speed the rules allow, providers the Bank can use with confidence, and a Portfolio that shows the whole use of AI in the Bank.

6. The reusable package

¶5.1. The evaluation harness, the assessment checklists, and the register.

7. Run-rate or program

¶6.1. Run-rate.

8. Who decides

¶7.1. The AICC Lead assigns the Risk Tier. The Control Function Contacts validate, clear providers, and may stop. The Domain Owner owns the Adopted Solution and its results.

9. Rule source

AI Policy 2, 3, and 4; Business Model 2.3; Solution Lifecycle Model 7 and 8; Operating Model 7.

Read further

  • Service line

    Information and decision support

    Facts from many sources prepared, consolidated, and presented so that a function decides on data: the pipelines that collect and transform, the dashboards that show, and the analytical and research tooling that supports the work.

  • Service line

    Content and document engines

    Public, investor, and management material generated from governed data and templates, pre-filled for people to finish: periodic reports, disclosures, investor material, standard letters and documents, in the languages the Bank uses.

  • Service line

    Enablement at the workplace

    Each person able to use AI for their own routine, within the rules: training by role, coaching at the desk, Domain Experts who carry adoption in their function, prompt and skill libraries per line of work, and communities of practice.

  • Service line

    Watch, research, and partnering

    The function knows what exists, what works, and what the rules require, before it invests. AICC watches the models, the platforms, the methods, and the regulation; digests the changes for the functions concerned; tests what matters in trials; and works with other organizations and with providers where the Bank gains from it.

  • Страница

    The service model

    A service of AICC is not a promise in the air. It is composed of defined parts, it has a life with states and gates, it is operated under named practices, and it leaves records. This page states the model that every service of AICC follows, whatever its line. It is the template; the services in operation are its instances and are kept in the Portfolio.

  • Страница

    How to engage

    A function does not need a form, a budget line, or a project to start with AICC. It brings a need. From there the Engagement follows six steps, each with a record and a decision that the function can see.
