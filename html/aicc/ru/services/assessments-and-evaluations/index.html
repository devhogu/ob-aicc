Assessments and evaluations

Policies, controls, criteriaAssessments and evaluationsRisk tieringOversight

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

Before a tool, a provider, or a Solution is used, it is assessed and evaluated: does it do what it claims, on the cases of the function, within the rules, at an acceptable cost? AICC runs these assessments with the function and the Control Function Contacts, with an evaluation harness that tests a tool against the cases of the Bank, so that the decision to use rests on evidence.

1. What it is

¶1.1. The category covers the evaluation of a tool, a model, or a Solution against an evaluation set built from the cases of the function, with its accuracy, its failure modes, and its cost; the assessment of a provider of models or services with the Control Function Contacts, where data is processed and kept, whether the provider may train on it, the contractual terms, the fallback and the exit; the readiness assessment of a Domain in people, data, and process; benchmarking against the current way of working and its cost; and the check or the validation of a Solution before its first real users, prepared for the Checker or the Control Function Contacts.

¶1.2. The person who evaluates is not the person who built; AICC does not validate its own work.

2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the category, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

  • A tool or a model evaluated against the cases of a function before use
  • A provider assessed with the Control Function Contacts of information security, data protection, and legal
  • A readiness assessment of a Domain in people, data, and process
  • A benchmark of a Solution against the current way of working and its cost
  • A check or a validation prepared for a Solution before its first real users, with the evidence the Checker or the Control Function Contacts need
  • A post-implementation review of a Solution against its business case

3. What the function receives

¶3.1. An evaluation report with the results, the failure modes, and the cost; a provider assessment the Control Function Contacts have cleared; a readiness report; a benchmark; and the evidence pack for a check or a validation.

4. How it runs

¶4.1. Run-rate: an evaluation takes days to one Iteration with the Domain Expert; a provider assessment follows the AI Policy and the Control Function Contacts; a readiness assessment is one Iteration. The evaluation set stays with the Solution and grows with its use.

5. What it leads to

¶5.1. Tools and providers the Bank can use with confidence, Solutions that pass their gates on evidence, and a growing library of evaluation sets by Domain.

6. The reusable package

¶6.1. The evaluation harness, the provider assessment checklist, the readiness assessment, and the evaluation sets by Domain.

7. Run-rate or program

¶7.1. Run-rate.

8. Who decides

¶8.1. The AICC Lead decides whether a check or a validation is needed; the Checker checks for Risk Tier 1; the Control Function Contacts validate for Risk Tier 2 and 3, clear providers, and may stop; the Domain Owner decides on the use.

9. Rule source

AI Policy 3 and 4; Solution Lifecycle Model 7; Statement of Intent 7.3 and 10.5.

  • Service category · 4 частей

    Strategy and governance

    What AICC built for itself it builds for any unit of the Bank: a strategy, a charter and mandate, an operating and governance model with its roles, decisions, control loops, and controls, and the portal and repository that publish them, developed with AI in weeks and maintained with it afterwards. AICC acts here as the strategy office and the governance office of the function, and the function keeps the ownership of what is decided.

  • Service category · 5 частей

    Knowledge services

    The documents of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, turned into a governed knowledge base that it can ask. Legal, compliance, accounting, operations, and any function with a corpus get an assistant that answers from approved, current, cited sources, for its own people and, where the function decides, for its clients.

  • Service category · 2 частей

    Training and knowledge sharing

    AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches them at the desk, builds the prompt and skill libraries for each line of work, runs the communities of practice, and publishes what it learns so that the next function starts further ahead.

    How to engage

    A function does not need a form, a budget line, or a project to start with AICC. It brings a need. From there the Engagement follows six steps, each with a record and a decision that the function can see.
