Услуги

AICC serves the functions and units of the Bank as their internal consulting and innovation lab. Its services run along nine lines in three families: advise and found, build and run, enable and assure. Each line is a direction with examples; each Engagement follows one service model and leaves a reusable package. This section states the lines, how a function engages AICC, the service model, and the form of the catalog.

Service lines

Nine lines in three families. A line is a direction along which AICC provides services, with examples on its page; what AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement. Run-rate lines are asked for through the front door and done in days to one Iteration; programs start with a study and a business case.

Advise and found

The strategy, governance, and process office of the Bank for AI: the direction, the documents, the processes, and the knowledge of the rules.

  • Service line

    Strategy and governance office

    What AICC built for itself it builds for any unit of the Bank: a strategy, a charter, an operating and governance model, a process set with its templates, and a portal and repository that hold them, developed with AI in weeks and maintained with it afterwards. AICC acts here as the strategy office and the governance office of the function, and the function keeps the ownership.

  • Service line

    Normative documents and processes

    Every function writes and keeps normative documents: policies, procedures, regulations, instructions, runbooks, and the process descriptions behind them. AICC helps HR, legal, accounting, compliance, operations, technology, and any other function draft, align, and maintain them with AI, in the style of the Bank and in one voice, under the ownership of the function.

  • Service line

    Watch, research, and partnering

    The function knows what exists, what works, and what the rules require, before it invests. AICC watches the models, the platforms, the methods, and the regulation; digests the changes for the functions concerned; tests what matters in trials; and works with other organizations and with providers where the Bank gains from it.

Build and run

The solutions: knowledge a function can ask, routine work done by assistants, facts prepared for decisions, and material generated for people to finish.

  • Service line

    Knowledge services

    The documents of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, turned into a governed knowledge base that it can ask. Legal, compliance, accounting, operations, and any function with a corpus get an assistant that answers from approved, current, cited sources, for its own people and, where the function decides, for its clients.

  • Service line

    Workplace automation

    The routine work of a function done by assistants under human validation: routing requests, reading and filling forms, processing reports, consolidating inputs, and producing documents from templates. The person validates and promotes; the assistant prepares.

  • Service line

    Information and decision support

    Facts from many sources prepared, consolidated, and presented so that a function decides on data: the pipelines that collect and transform, the dashboards that show, and the analytical and research tooling that supports the work.

  • Service line

    Content and document engines

    Public, investor, and management material generated from governed data and templates, pre-filled for people to finish: periodic reports, disclosures, investor material, standard letters and documents, in the languages the Bank uses.

Enable and assure

The people and the compliance: each person able to use AI for their own work, and each use of AI kept within the rules.

  • Service line

    Enablement at the workplace

    Each person able to use AI for their own routine, within the rules: training by role, coaching at the desk, Domain Experts who carry adoption in their function, prompt and skill libraries per line of work, and communities of practice.

  • Service line

    Assurance and governance support

    A function's use of AI made compliant without the function becoming an expert in the rules: the Risk Tier assessed, the AI Registry entry made, the provider assessed, the tool evaluated and tested before use, the Acceptance Checklist prepared, and the Adopted Solutions of others overseen under the same rules.

How to engage

A function brings a need. The Engagement follows six steps, each with a record and a decision the function can see; AICC commits in a Service Agreement on a best-effort basis, and the function commits to nothing.

  1. 1ContactA head of function raises a need, in any form
  2. 2StudyThe need is scoped and the business case written and cleared
  3. 3Service AgreementAICC states what it commits to and the support level
  4. 4DeliveryMVP first, then build, verify, and deploy to the first users
  5. 5Outcome ReportThe outcome with its evidence, accepted by the Domain Owner
  6. 6SupportAt the agreed level, with a check-in at each Iteration

What AICC does not do

AICC does not own or operate the AI Platform, own the business results of a Domain, set the rules of a Control Function, or validate its own work. It does not deliver at scale: it proves and builds, and it relies on the platform teams and the IT functions of the Bank to run what the Bank adopts (AICC Charter 3.2).

The service model and the catalog

Every service of AICC follows one model: its composition, its life, its operation, and its records. The catalog has a defined form for a Solution and for a package. This site states the model and the form; the live catalog is an instance kept in the Portfolio and presented in the live portal of AICC.

  • Страница

    The service model

    A service of AICC is not a promise in the air. It is composed of defined parts, it has a life with states and gates, it is operated under named practices, and it leaves records. This page states the model that every service of AICC follows, whatever its line. It is the template; the services in operation are its instances and are kept in the Portfolio.

  • Страница

    Service catalog: the form

    The catalog of AICC lists what AICC runs, has delivered, is proving, and can re-deploy. This page states the form of the catalog: the two kinds of entry, the fields of each, and the states an entry passes through. The live catalog is an instance of this form, kept in the Portfolio of AICC and presented in the live portal; this site states the form only. One entry of each kind is shown as an illustration.
