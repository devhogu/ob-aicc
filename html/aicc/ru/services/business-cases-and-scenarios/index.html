Business cases and scenarios

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

Before the Bank invests in a use of AI, it needs to know what the use is, what it would take, what it would return, and what it would risk. AICC does this work with the function: it finds and shapes the use cases, writes each as a scenario with its problem, its solution, and its measures, assesses the readiness of the function and its sources, and writes the business case that the Portfolio decides on.

1. What it is

¶1.1. The category covers use-case discovery with a function; the shaping of each use case as a scenario, with the problem to solve, the proposed solution, the people who validate, the leading indicators, and the size; the readiness and source audit of the function, what documents, systems, and owners it has, and what that allows; the business case of an Initiative, with its run cost, its leading indicators, and its expected Risk Tier; and the screening and ranking of a backlog of scenarios by lens, size, and value.

¶1.2. A scenario is the unit of the use-case backlog of the Bank. It is written before any build, it is the hypothesis of the MVP, and it is kept with its results so that the Bank learns from what did and did not work.

2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the category, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

  • A use-case discovery with a function, ending in a ranked backlog of scenarios
  • A scenario written for one use case: problem, solution, people who validate, leading indicators, size
  • A readiness and source audit of a function
  • An Initiative Brief: the business case with its run cost, its leading indicators, and its expected Risk Tier, cleared by the Control Function Contacts where Risk Tier 2 or 3 is expected
  • A roadmap for a Domain: its scenarios ranked by value and effort and mapped to the Strategic Priorities and the Maturity Levels, as a Proposal

3. What the function receives

¶3.1. A scoped need with its intended outcome and its success Measures; the scenarios of the function in one form; an Initiative Brief ready for the approval of the Domain Owner, or of the Executive Sponsor above a guardrail, across Domains, or for enabling work; and a recommendation: continue to a proof, adopt what exists, or stop.

4. How it runs

¶4.1. A head of function brings the need; the AICC Lead takes it in and issues a Service Agreement that covers the study. The study runs in one Iteration for a single scenario and in two to three for a Domain, with the Domain Owner and a Domain Expert. The business case goes to the approver; an approved Initiative goes on to its MVP.

5. What it leads to

¶5.1. Decisions taken on evidence, a use-case backlog by Domain that feeds the funnel of the Portfolio, and a Bank that invests in what works and learns from what does not.

6. The reusable package

¶6.1. The scenario form, the readiness audit, the Initiative Brief template, and the screening lenses of the Portfolio.

7. Run-rate or program

¶7.1. Run-rate for a single scenario or an audit; a program for a Domain roadmap. The study itself is the first phase of every program.

8. Who decides

¶8.1. The AICC Lead takes the need in. The Domain Owner approves the business case; the Executive Sponsor approves it above a guardrail, across Domains, or for enabling work. The Control Function Contacts clear it when Risk Tier 2 or 3 is expected.

9. Rule source

Business Model 3 and 4.1; Portfolio Management Model 2, 5, and 6; AI Policy 3; the Engagement workflow and guide.

    Advise and formulate

    The strategy, governance, and process office of the Bank for AI: the direction and the mandate of a unit, its normative documents and processes, the watch on the field, and the business cases and scenarios that the Portfolio decides on.

  • Service category · 5 частей

    Knowledge services

    The documents of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, turned into a governed knowledge base that it can ask. Legal, compliance, accounting, operations, and any function with a corpus get an assistant that answers from approved, current, cited sources, for its own people and, where the function decides, for its clients.

  • Service category · 2 частей

    Training and knowledge sharing

    AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches them at the desk, builds the prompt and skill libraries for each line of work, runs the communities of practice, and publishes what it learns so that the next function starts further ahead.

  • Service category · 4 частей

    Policies, controls, criteria

    The use of AI in a function answers to the AI Policy of the Bank, to the controls of AICC, and to criteria that say when a Solution is good enough and safe enough to use. AICC helps a function state its own rules of use within the policy, map the controls that apply to its Solutions, and define the acceptance and evaluation criteria before any build, so that compliance is designed in and not inspected in.

  • Страница · 3 частей

    How to engage

    A function does not need a form, a budget line, or a project to start with AICC. It brings a need. From there the Engagement follows six steps, each with a record and a decision that the function can see.

The live catalog is an instance of this form, kept in the Portfolio of AICC and presented in the live portal; this site states the form only. One entry of each kind is shown as an illustration.
