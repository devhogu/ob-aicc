Knowledge services

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

The documents of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, turned into a governed knowledge base that it can ask. Legal, compliance, accounting, operations, and any function with a corpus get an assistant that answers from approved, current, cited sources, for its own people and, where the function decides, for its clients.

1. What it is

¶1.1. The category covers the curation of a corpus with named owners and review cycles; the ingestion of the documents of the function and of the acts, regulations, and circulars of the bodies it answers to; a question-and-answer assistant over it with citations; search and query tooling; and the governance of the sources so that the answers stay current and traceable.

¶1.2. A knowledge base is built for one function and designed for reuse across functions: the same kit, the same source governance, a different corpus. Cross-unit knowledge bases are built where several functions share a body of documents.

2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the category, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

  • A knowledge base over the procedures and the regulator documents of legal, compliance, or accounting, with citations
  • A cross-unit knowledge base where functions share a corpus
  • A supervisory query response base for regulatory reporting
  • A knowledge assist for a contact center agent at the moment of the call
  • Query and search tooling over the corpus of a function, and the source governance that keeps it current

3. What the function receives

¶3.1. A curated corpus, an assistant that cites its sources, access by data class, an evaluation set that shows how well it answers, and the source governance that keeps it current. For a function that serves clients: the same assistant on the material the function chooses to expose, under the rules of the Bank.

4. How it runs

¶4.1. A study of one Iteration maps the corpus, its owners, and its data classes. The MVP builds the base on a first set and measures it against the evaluation set with the Domain Expert. The function then owns the sources; AICC runs or hands over the assistant as the Service Agreement states.

5. What it leads to

¶5.1. Expertise at the point of work: consistent and compliant answers, faster proficiency of new staff, and institutional knowledge that stays when people move. It is the foundation that later agents stand on.

6. The reusable package

¶6.1. The knowledge-base kit: ingestion, source governance, retrieval, citation, and evaluation; re-deployed for each function with its own corpus.

7. Run-rate or program

¶7.1. A program for a new knowledge base; run-rate for adding a source set to an existing one.

8. Who decides

¶8.1. The Domain Owner decides the corpus and the audience; the Control Function Contacts clear the data classes; the AICC Lead assigns the Risk Tier.

9. Rule source

Statement of Intent 5.3, 9.5, and 10.2; AI Policy 2 and 3; Business Model 4.

  • Service category

    Normatives and processes

    Every function writes and keeps normative documents: policies, procedures, regulations, instructions, runbooks, and the process descriptions behind them. AICC helps HR, legal, accounting, compliance, operations, technology, and any other function draft, align, and maintain them with AI, in the style of the Bank and in one voice, under the ownership of the function.

  • Service category

    Research and exploration

    AICC watches the field so that the Bank does not have to: the models, the platforms, the methods, and the rules that change every quarter. It digests the changes for the functions concerned, explores what matters in trials, keeps the reference of the industry body of knowledge and of the regulators and acts, and works with other organizations, with providers, and with the professional bodies where the Bank gains from it.

  • Service category

    Business cases and scenarios

    Before the Bank invests in a use of AI, it needs to know what the use is, what it would take, what it would return, and what it would risk. AICC does this work with the function: it finds and shapes the use cases, writes each as a scenario with its problem, its solution, and its measures, assesses the readiness of the function and its sources, and writes the business case that the Portfolio decides on.

  • Service category

    Workplace automation

    The routine work of a function done by assistants under human validation: routing requests, reading and filling forms, processing reports, consolidating inputs, and producing documents from templates. The person validates and promotes; the assistant prepares.

  • Service category

    Analytics and decision support

    Facts from many sources prepared, consolidated, and presented so that a function decides on data: the pipelines that collect and transform, the dashboards that show, the analyses that explain, and the research tooling that supports the work.

  • Service category

    Content management

    Public, investor, and management material generated from governed data and templates, pre-filled for people to finish, and the content behind it kept in order: the templates, the editions, the versions, the languages, and the approval that precedes publication.
