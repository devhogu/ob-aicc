AI services run by AICC

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

Some of what AICC builds it also runs: portals, engines, pipelines, and knowledge services that serve a function or the Bank for as long as they are worth running. These are the Services of AICC. Each has a business case that states its run cost and its sunset rule, a Domain Owner who accepts and reviews it, and support through Service Management.

1. What it is

¶1.1. A Service is a Solution that AICC operates for its whole life: it is released, operated, evolved through new Capabilities and Features, and retired under its sunset rule. A Service that serves many Domains is accepted and reviewed by the Executive Sponsor.

¶1.2. The Services in operation, with their type, state, and receiver, are listed in the Service catalog, which is drawn from the Portfolio at each build of this site.

2. What the client receives

¶2.1. A running Service at the response targets of its Service Agreement, with requests and incidents handled through the Service Management queue of AICC, a review of the live Service by its Domain Owner at each Iteration Review and Demo, and new features through the Program Backlog.

¶2.2. The response targets are targets and not guarantees; AICC runs its Services on a best-effort basis within the capability it has.

3. How it runs

¶3.1. Requests and questions come through Service Management and are triaged by class of service. An outage or failure goes to the incident management of the Bank, and an AI Incident is reviewed as the AI Policy states. A change or a new feature enters the Program Backlog as a Feature and is verified and deployed as any Feature is.

4. What it leads to

¶4.1. A Service runs while it is worth running. When its sunset rule applies, or its Domain Owner decides, it is retired and its AI Registry entry is closed. A Service that the Bank should run at scale is proposed for adoption by a platform team or an IT function.

5. Records and templates

¶5.1. The Solution Definition; the Service Agreement; the AI Registry entry; the Service Management records; the AI Incident Review where one occurs.

6. Who decides

¶6.1. The Domain Owner accepts and reviews a Service of its Domain, and decides its retirement; the Executive Sponsor does so for a Service across Domains. The AICC Lead decides whether a change needs a new check or validation.

7. Rule source

Business Model 4.2; Solution Lifecycle Model 8.1, 8.4, and 8.5; AI Policy 5; the Engagement guide 6.

  • Service line

    Advisory

    AICC advises a function of the Bank on where and how to use AI: which needs are worth an Initiative, what a Solution would take, what it would cost to run, which Risk Tier it would carry, and which provider or platform fits. Advisory is the study phase of an Engagement, and it is how most work with AICC starts.

  • Service line

    Proof and prototyping

    Before the Bank invests in a Solution, AICC proves it. A proof is a probe: an Experiment, a proof of concept, a minimum viable product, or a trial of a platform or a concept, run for a stated number of Iterations against leading indicators, and ending in evidence and a decision.

  • Service line

    Solution delivery

    AICC builds Solutions for the functions of the Bank: knowledge bases on governed sources, assistants for a team, and the automation of routine work that a function does by hand today. A Solution of this kind is a Product: a version built for one consumer, handed over, and supported on demand.

  • Service line

    Enablement

    AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches the Domain Experts who carry adoption in their Domains, runs the communities of practice, and keeps the playbooks and the reusable assets of its Engagements so that the next function starts further ahead.

  • Service line

    Oversight of Adopted Solutions

    Not every AI Solution of the Bank is built by AICC. When a Proposal is adopted and a Domain or a platform team delivers it, or when a function brings in a Solution of its own, AICC oversees it: it keeps it in the AI Registry with its Risk Tier, follows its checks and its incidents, and reports on it with the rest of the Portfolio.

  • Service line

    Research and partnering

    AICC watches the field so the Bank does not have to: the models, the platforms, the methods, and the rules that change every quarter. It tests what matters in trials, keeps the Bank's reference of the industry body of knowledge and of the regulators and acts, and works with other organizations and with providers where the Bank gains from it.
