Solution delivery

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

AICC builds Solutions for the functions of the Bank: knowledge bases on governed sources, assistants for a team, and the automation of routine work that a function does by hand today. A Solution of this kind is a Product: a version built for one consumer, handed over, and supported on demand.

1. What it is

¶1.1. Solution delivery is the build and the release of a Solution after the decision to continue: the Capabilities and Features of the Initiative, built in the Iterations of the Program Increment, verified, accepted, and released to the users of the function.

¶1.2. AICC is a small delivery unit. It builds what proves the case and what a function needs for its own work, and it relies on the platform teams and the IT functions of the Bank to run at scale. A Solution that is to serve many Domains, or to carry the processes of the Bank, is proposed to the Bank for adoption and delivered by the Domains and the platform teams, with AICC overseeing it.

2. What the client receives

¶2.1. A released Solution with its Solution Definition, its Risk Tier, its entry in the AI Registry, its check or validation, and the acceptance of its Domain Owner; a handover with the knowledge to use and to keep it; and support at the level that the Service Agreement states.

3. How it runs

¶3.1. The Team selects the Features at Iteration Planning and builds them within the Limits on Work in Progress. The product owner accepts each Feature at the Iteration Review and Demo. Before the Solution reaches its first users, a person other than its builder checks or validates it, the AICC Lead gives the final acceptance of the Team, and the Domain Owner accepts the working Solution as the requester. Release beyond the first users is a separate decision, with the Acceptance Checklist signed.

¶3.2. Production deployment and later changes go through the change management of the Bank.

4. What it leads to

¶4.1. A Product in use by its consumer, supported on demand, revised through the Portfolio Backlog when a new version is needed, and retired for that consumer when it is no longer used. A Product with many consumers or recurring requests becomes a Service through a business case.

5. Records and templates

¶5.1. The Solution Definition with its release block; the Acceptance Checklist; the Control Sign-Off; the Outcome Report; the change ticket and test reference of each deployment.

6. Who decides

¶6.1. The Team decides how the work is built. The product owner accepts Features and Capabilities. The AICC Lead gives the final acceptance of the Team. The Domain Owner accepts the Solution and decides its release; the Executive Sponsor decides the release of a Risk Tier 3 Solution and of a Solution that the AICC Lead built.

7. Rule source

Business Model 4.3; Solution Lifecycle Model 3, 4, 7, and 8; AICC Charter 3.2; the Service delivery workflow and guide.

  • Service line

    Advisory

    AICC advises a function of the Bank on where and how to use AI: which needs are worth an Initiative, what a Solution would take, what it would cost to run, which Risk Tier it would carry, and which provider or platform fits. Advisory is the study phase of an Engagement, and it is how most work with AICC starts.

  • Service line

    Proof and prototyping

    Before the Bank invests in a Solution, AICC proves it. A proof is a probe: an Experiment, a proof of concept, a minimum viable product, or a trial of a platform or a concept, run for a stated number of Iterations against leading indicators, and ending in evidence and a decision.

  • Service line

    AI services run by AICC

    Some of what AICC builds it also runs: portals, engines, pipelines, and knowledge services that serve a function or the Bank for as long as they are worth running. These are the Services of AICC. Each has a business case that states its run cost and its sunset rule, a Domain Owner who accepts and reviews it, and support through Service Management.

  • Service line

    Enablement

    AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches the Domain Experts who carry adoption in their Domains, runs the communities of practice, and keeps the playbooks and the reusable assets of its Engagements so that the next function starts further ahead.

  • Service line

    Oversight of Adopted Solutions

    Not every AI Solution of the Bank is built by AICC. When a Proposal is adopted and a Domain or a platform team delivers it, or when a function brings in a Solution of its own, AICC oversees it: it keeps it in the AI Registry with its Risk Tier, follows its checks and its incidents, and reports on it with the rest of the Portfolio.

  • Service line

    Research and partnering

    AICC watches the field so the Bank does not have to: the models, the platforms, the methods, and the rules that change every quarter. It tests what matters in trials, keeps the Bank's reference of the industry body of knowledge and of the regulators and acts, and works with other organizations and with providers where the Bank gains from it.
