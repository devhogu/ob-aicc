The service model

The form of the catalogThe service model

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

A service of AICC is not a promise in the air. It is composed of defined parts, it has a life with service steps and gates, it is operated under named practices, and it leaves records. This page states the model that every service of AICC follows, whatever its area and category. It is the template; the services in operation are its instances and are kept in the Portfolio.

1. The composition of a service

¶1.1. Every service of AICC, whatever its category and whether run-rate work or an Initiative, is described by the same fields.

Field Meaning Where it is set
Area and category One of the fifteen categories in the four areas (advise and formulate; build and run; enablement; assurance) The Services section
Mode Run-rate work, a small, repeatable request done within one Iteration as a Feature under the Standing Initiative of its service area; or an Initiative, with a business case and an MVP Proposed by the category and confirmed by the AICC Lead at intake (Business Model 7.2)
Client The function, represented by its Domain Owner; the Executive Sponsor for enabling work Service Agreement; the Feature for run-rate work
Solution type Experiment, Product, or Service, which sets the life after delivery Solution Definition
Support level None, on demand, agreed response targets, or run by AICC; on demand only for run-rate work Service Agreement
Risk Tier 1, 2, or 3, which sets the review, the validation, the human oversight, and the speed of release Assigned by the AICC Lead, or by the Executive Sponsor for a Solution that the AICC Lead built; AI Registry
Owner The Domain Owner for the results; the Solution Engineer for the build, and for the run of a Service that AICC runs; the product owner for acceptance during development Appointments Record; Solution Definition
Package The Package the service leaves, where one results, by its Package Definition The catalog, in the Portfolio
Records The Initiative Brief, the Service Agreement, the Solution Definition, the Control Sign-Off, the Acceptance Checklist, the Outcome Report, and the Service Management records, as the mode and the type require The Registry

2. The life of a service

¶2.1. A service is born as a need at the front door and lives through the phases of an Engagement: study, proof, delivery, support. A Service that AICC runs then passes through the service steps that the page The life of a Service describes: Admitted, Catalogued, In service, Improving, Transition planned, Migrating, and Handed over or Retired, each with its question, its signals, its action, and its gate. The service steps refine the Stages Operate, Evolve, and Retire and are not states: the Service stays Active until it is Closed.

¶2.2. A Product lives in the hands of its consumer, supported on demand and revised through the Portfolio Backlog. An Experiment lives for its time-box and then goes to review: it is accepted with its lessons and closed, a Proposal is made, or it is rejected; when a Receiver accepts its Handover, it is closed and AICC oversees the Adopted Solution.

3. The operation of a service

¶3.1. A Service that AICC runs is operated under the practices that the page Service operations describes: request and incident handling by class of service, problem management, change enablement, knowledge, service levels against the Service Agreement, the run cost against the business case, and the fallback and the exit of each supplier, each in proportion to the Risk Tier of the Service.

¶3.2. The health of a Service is read at each Iteration Review and Demo in four signals, service levels, incidents, use, and cost, with the notices of its providers, by the Domain Owner, or the Executive Sponsor for a Service across Domains. The signals feed the sunset rule of the Service and the Quarterly Report.

4. The records of a service

¶4.1. The Solution Engineer enters each service step and the date on which it was reached in the Solution Definition and the AI Registry, and each decision leaves its record in the form of the templates of the Knowledge base. The site states the forms; the Registry and the Portfolio hold the instances; the Quarterly Report reads them.

5. The catalog

¶5.1. The catalog of AICC has two parts: the Solutions, each by its Solution Definition, and the Packages, each by its Package Definition. The page Service catalog states the form of each entry. The live catalog is kept in the Portfolio (Business Model 4.4).

6. Rule source

Business Model 4 and 5; Solution Lifecycle Model 7 and 8, with the service steps and the practices in 8.8 to 8.12; AI Policy 3; Operating Model 7.

  • Страница · 5 частей

    Advise and formulate

    The strategy, governance, and process work that AICC drafts with AI for a function, which owns the substance: the direction and the mandate of a unit and its normative documents and processes; with them the watch on the field, and the business cases and scenarios that the Portfolio decides on.

  • Service category · 4 частей

    Strategy and governance

    What AICC built for itself it builds for any unit of the Bank: a strategy, a charter and mandate, an operating and governance model with its roles, decisions, control loops, and controls, and the portal and repository that publish them, developed with AI in weeks and maintained with it afterwards. AICC drafts them for the function, and the function owns their substance; AICC decides none of it (Business Model 4.10).

  • Service category · 5 частей

    Knowledge services

    The documents of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, turned into a governed knowledge base that it can ask. Legal, compliance, accounting, operations, and any function with a corpus get an assistant that answers from approved, current, cited sources, for its own people and, where the function decides, for its clients.

  • Service category · 2 частей

    Training and knowledge sharing

    AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches them at the workplace, builds the prompt and skill libraries for each line of work, runs the communities of practice, and publishes what it learns so that the next function starts further ahead.

  • Service category · 4 частей

    Policies, controls, criteria

    The use of AI in a function answers to the AI Policy of the Bank, to the controls of AICC, and to criteria that say when a Solution is good enough and safe enough to use. AICC helps a function state its own rules of use within the policy, map the controls that apply to its Solutions, and define the acceptance and evaluation criteria before any build, so that compliance is designed in and not inspected in.

  • Страница · 3 частей

    How to engage

    A function does not need a form, funding of its own, or a plan to start with AICC. It brings a need. From there the Engagement follows six steps, each with a record and a decision that the function can see.
