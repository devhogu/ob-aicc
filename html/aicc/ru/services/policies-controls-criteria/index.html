Policies, controls, criteria

Текст этой страницы пока доступен только на английском языке. Русский перевод ещё не готов.

The use of AI in a function answers to the AI Policy of the Bank, to the controls of AICC, and to criteria that say when a Solution is good enough and safe enough to use. AICC helps a function state its own rules of use within the policy, map the controls that apply to its Solutions, and define the acceptance and evaluation criteria before any build, so that compliance is designed in and not inspected in.

1. What it is

¶1.1. The category covers the rules of use of a function within the AI Policy, which data classes, which tools, which uses, and which human review; the mapping of the controls of the Operating Model and of the Control Functions to the Solutions of the function; the acceptance criteria of a Solution, what it must do and must not do, and the Acceptance Checklist prepared from them; the evaluation criteria and the evaluation set against which a tool or a Solution is tested; and the guardrails of a Solution in operation, grounding, limits, logging, escalation.

¶1.2. AICC helps the function state these; the Control Functions set the rules of their remit, validate, and may stop. AICC does not set the rules of a Control Function.

2. Examples of what a function asks for

The following are examples that illustrate the category, not a list of commitments. What AICC commits to is stated in the Service Agreement of each Engagement.

  • The rules of use of a function within the AI Policy, for its data classes and its tools
  • A control map for the Solutions of a function: which control applies, who owns it, what evidence it leaves
  • Acceptance criteria for a Solution, and the Acceptance Checklist prepared from them
  • Evaluation criteria and an evaluation set built with the Domain Expert
  • The guardrails of a Solution in operation: grounding, limits, logging, escalation
  • A control self-assessment prepared for internal audit

3. What the function receives

¶3.1. Rules of use the function can apply, a control map its auditors can test, acceptance and evaluation criteria written before the build, and guardrails stated in the Solution Definition.

4. How it runs

¶4.1. Run-rate: the criteria and the control map are written in the study of an Engagement or in one Iteration for an existing Solution, with the Domain Expert and the Control Function Contacts concerned.

5. What it leads to

¶5.1. Solutions that are built to pass their gates, functions that know their rules, and audits that find the evidence where the control map says it is.

6. The reusable package

¶6.1. The assessment checklists, the acceptance criteria patterns by Solution type and Risk Tier, and the control map template.

7. Run-rate or program

¶7.1. Run-rate.

8. Who decides

¶8.1. The Domain Owner approves the rules of use and the acceptance criteria of its Solutions; the Control Function concerned sets and validates what is in its remit; the AICC Lead owns the control catalogue of AICC.

9. Rule source

AI Policy 2 and 3; Operating Model 8; Solution Lifecycle Model 7; the Acceptance Checklist template.

  • Service category · 4 частей

    Strategy and governance

    What AICC built for itself it builds for any unit of the Bank: a strategy, a charter and mandate, an operating and governance model with its roles, decisions, control loops, and controls, and the portal and repository that publish them, developed with AI in weeks and maintained with it afterwards. AICC acts here as the strategy office and the governance office of the function, and the function keeps the ownership of what is decided.

  • Service category · 5 частей

    Knowledge services

    The documents of a function, and the state and regulator documents it works with, turned into a governed knowledge base that it can ask. Legal, compliance, accounting, operations, and any function with a corpus get an assistant that answers from approved, current, cited sources, for its own people and, where the function decides, for its clients.

  • Service category · 2 частей

    Training and knowledge sharing

    AI reaches the Bank through its people. AICC trains employees before they use AI, coaches them at the desk, builds the prompt and skill libraries for each line of work, runs the communities of practice, and publishes what it learns so that the next function starts further ahead.

  • Страница · 3 частей

    How to engage

    A function does not need a form, a budget line, or a project to start with AICC. It brings a need. From there the Engagement follows six steps, each with a record and a decision that the function can see.

  • Страница · 2 частей

    Service catalog

    The catalog of AICC lists what AICC runs, has delivered, is proving, and can re-deploy. This page states the form of the catalog: the two kinds of entry, the fields of each, and the states an entry passes through. The live catalog is an instance of this form, kept in the Portfolio of AICC and presented in the live portal; this site states the form only. One entry of each kind is shown as an illustration.
