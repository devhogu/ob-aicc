1. Scope

¶1.1. This site is the charter portal of the AI Competence Center ("AICC") of O!Bank ("the Bank"). It presents the charter of AICC, its intent, its mandate, its services, its method, its organization, and its controls, and it explains them in courses, guides, and references written for the people of the Bank. These terms state the conditions under which the site is made available. They are internal rules of the Bank.

¶1.2. Access to the site is restricted to the persons whom the Bank authorizes, through the access gateway of the Bank. The use of the site is subject to the policies of the Bank on information security, the classification of information, and the acceptable use of its systems.

2. Information of the Bank

¶2.1. The content of the site is information of the Bank and is for internal use. It shall not be disclosed, copied, or distributed outside the Bank unless the Bank authorizes it under its rules for information. The Statement of Intent is made available outside the Bank only as the Executive Sponsor decides.

¶2.2. The Bank owns the content of the site, including the documents, the diagrams, the courses, and the design. Third-party fonts and components are used under their own licenses, which are included with the site.

3. Standing of the pages

¶3.1. The site holds two kinds of page. The documents of the charter, the workflows, the guides, and the templates are generated from the charter at the revision stated in the footer; where a page and a document differ, the document prevails. The courses, the service pages, the learning paths, the questions, the acts and compliance page, and the Reference are written for the site to explain the charter and to orient the reader; they state no rule of their own, and the charter prevails over them.

¶3.2. The pages on regulators, acts, standards, and external sources summarize those instruments and sources in general terms for orientation. They quote no provision, they are not legal advice, and a listing is not an endorsement. What applies to the Bank is confirmed by the Control Function Contacts of compliance and legal, and the Knowledge base page Acts and compliance is kept with them.

¶3.3. The site is a reference. The live records of AICC, among them the decisions, the appointments, the risks, the AI Registry, the Portfolio, and the backlogs, are kept in the Registry, Jira, Confluence, and Service Management, as the page Records and systems states, and the site links to the Registry and the charter on the corporate share. A page that states the form of a catalog, a measure, or a package is the form; the instance is in the Registry or the Portfolio.

¶3.4. The site is provided for information, as it is. A page does not by itself create a right, an obligation, an approval, or a commitment of AICC to a function. Those arise from the documents, the Records, the Service Agreements, and the decisions of the persons whom the Operating Model names.

4. Use of the site

¶4.1. The site is used for the work of the Bank. A user shall not attempt to circumvent the access gateway, to alter the site, or to extract its content for a purpose outside the Bank.

¶4.2. The external sources named in the Reference are visited under the rules of the Bank for the use of its systems. No data of the Bank, no customer or employee data, and no internal document is entered into an external site, tool, form, or model found through this site; a model or a service found through it enters the Bank only through the provider check of the AI Policy. Registration with a work address is made only where the rules of the Bank allow it.

¶4.3. The use of AI tools at the Bank is governed by the AI Policy, which the site explains and does not replace: only Solutions approved for the data class and the purpose and recorded in the AI Registry are used, and a person reviews AI output before relying on it.

¶4.4. A user who finds an error on a page reports it through the feedback link, with the identifier of the page.

5. How the site is kept

¶5.1. The AICC Lead keeps the site, as the keeper of the tools of AICC, and is accountable for its content under the Operating Model. The documents on it change only by the life cycle of the charter, from the baseline by a decision record; the pages written for the site are revised by the AICC Lead and, where they concern the acts and the regulators, with the Control Function Contacts. The site is reviewed with the yearly review of the documents, and its access is part of the quarterly access review of the tools of AICC.

6. Changes and contact

¶6.1. The Bank may change the site and these terms. The revision in effect is the one published. A question about these terms goes to the contact address in the footer. The handling of information about users is stated in the Privacy statement.
