A template gives the form of a record that AICC produces; its rules are in the documents. There are thirteen, used along the life of an Engagement and on the cycle of the unit, and a fourteenth is proposed. This page says which form is used when, who fills it and who signs it, and where the record is kept; each form has its own page with a copy button.

1. Which form when

Before the work: the start of an Engagement

Form Used when Filled by Signed or approved by Kept in
Initiative Brief A need becomes an Initiative: the one-page business case The AICC Lead with the Domain Owner The Domain Owner, or the Executive Sponsor above a guardrail, across Domains, or for enabling work; cleared by the Control Function Contacts for Risk Tier 2 and 3 The Registry, with the Initiative
Service Agreement The study of an Engagement starts; amended when the business case is approved The AICC Lead Issued by the AICC Lead; the function is notified The Registry, with the Initiative

During the work: the Solution and its gates

Form Used when Filled by Signed or approved by Kept in
Solution Definition A Solution is defined, in the MVP; kept current for its life The Solution Engineer The Domain Owner approves; the AICC Lead assigns the Risk Tier; the release block records the acceptances and the release The Portfolio
Decision Record A Decision needs a record: the approval of a business case, the decision after the MVP, a release of Risk Tier 3, a risk beyond appetite, a hard-to-reverse decision The AICC Lead, or the decider The decider The Registry, decisions
Control Sign-Off A Control Function Contact clears a business case, validates, checks a provider, grants an Exception, or stops The Control Function Contact The Control Function Contact The Registry, sign-offs
Acceptance Checklist AICC hands a ready Solution to a Domain, before its release beyond the first users The AICC Lead Each party within its remit: the Domain Owner, the Solution Engineer, the AICC Lead, the Checker, the Control Functions concerned, the IT function with the Platform Owner The Registry, checklists

After the work: the outcome

Form Used when Filled by Signed or approved by Kept in
Outcome Report An Engagement ends The AICC Lead The Domain Owner accepts, or the Executive Sponsor for enabling work The Registry, with the Initiative
Proposal A Solution is proposed for adoption at scale; the yearly Proposal of the AI adoption strategy The AICC Lead The owners and the Executive Sponsor decide on a Solution; the Bank on the strategy The Registry, proposals

The cycle of the unit: governance and oversight

Quarterly Report Each quarter: the report to the Board Committee The AICC Lead The Executive Sponsor approves and issues The Registry, reports
Registry Snapshot The close of an Iteration and of a Program Increment, and at the cutover The AICC Lead None; it is the evidence of the living records at a date The Registry, snapshots
AI Incident Review After the post-incident review of an AI Incident The AICC Lead with the function and the Control Function Contacts The Control Function Contacts concerned The Registry, incident reviews
Appointments Record An appointment, acting designation, change, or relief; the Holders, deputies, assigned persons, training, and access The AICC Lead The appointer of each Role The Registry, appointments

Proposed

Form Used when Status
Package Definition A reusable package is planned, produced, or available A draft on this site, proposed as the fourteenth template; adopted by a decision record

2. How a form is used

¶2.1. A form is copied from its page with the copy button, or from the charter folder, and its metadata block is omitted. The record it becomes is kept where the table says, under the rules of the Registry: closed and dated when it is evidence, corrected only by a new dated entry. A figure of the Bank stays in its source system, and the record points to it. The Document Catalog states the life cycle of a template; a template changes by a decision from the baseline.

3. Rule source

Document Catalog 6; Operating Model 7; the templates README of the charter.
