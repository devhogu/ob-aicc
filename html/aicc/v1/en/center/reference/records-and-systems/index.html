Records and systems

This site is static. It states the rules and the forms of the Competence Center and holds no live record. The table lists each record by its template, with the place where it is kept and the controls that it evidences.

Systems

RegistryDecisions, appointments, priorities, standards, risks and issues, the AI Registry, the controls, and the evidence records as closed and dated extracts. Kept on the corporate folder: \\10.128.20.244\aicc\governance\registry
PortfolioThe catalog of Solutions and Packages and the working state: the Discovery catalog, the Portfolio and Program Backlogs and Kanbans with the Initiative Briefs, the Roadmap, the Calendar, the Program Increments, the Dashboard, and the projects. Kept on the corporate folder: \\10.128.20.244\aicc\portfolio
Jira and ConfluenceThe daily work: the Work Items of the Teams and a mirror of the Initiatives, Capabilities, and Features; guidance and dashboards by reference
Service ManagementRequests and incidents, including AI Incidents
