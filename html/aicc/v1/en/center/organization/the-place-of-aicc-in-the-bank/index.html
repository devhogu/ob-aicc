The place of the Competence Center in the Bank

OverviewPlace in the BankRolesWho does whatAppointmentsGrowth

The Competence Center is an internal consulting and innovation lab of the Bank, mandated by and reporting to the Executive Sponsor whom the Board names, serving the functions of the Bank as its clients, and executing through the Domains. This part states where it sits, what it is, and what it is not.

1. Mandate and reporting line

¶1.1. The Competence Center acts under the mandate of the Executive Sponsor, whom the Board names, and whose appointment of the Competence Center Lead and whose decision reference are entered in the Appointments Record. The Competence Center Lead is accountable for carrying out the commitments of the Statement of Intent that are for the Competence Center, and the Executive Sponsor for its annual review and its communication. The Competence Center reports to the Executive Sponsor: reporting runs from the Teams to the Competence Center Lead and to the Steering, which the Executive Sponsor chairs. The Executive Sponsor may bring the Quarterly Report, findings, the AI adoption strategy, or a Proposal at the scale of the Bank to the Board Committee or the Board, when the Executive Sponsor judges it useful or the Board asks. The Executive Sponsor always tells the Board Committee of a major AI Incident and of a risk accepted beyond the AI Risk Appetite Statement without waiting for any report.

2. What the Competence Center is

¶2.1. A joint team: the Competence Center Lead and the people whom the functions assign to it, who stay in their own reporting line, with the consent of their line managers and a stated time allocation. The Competence Center directs, guides, and may supply Solution Engineers to build and run Solutions; the Domains execute. The Competence Center owns a Service that it runs, and nothing else of the business results of the Bank.

3. What the Competence Center is not

¶3.1. The Competence Center does not own or operate the AI Platform, which the Platform Owner provides and operates outside the Competence Center to the requirements the Competence Center states. It does not own the business results of a Domain, which stay with the Domain Owner. It is not a platform team of the Bank, and it does not operate a Solution at the scale of the Bank. It does not set the rules of a Control Function, and it is not one: the Control Functions are independent of it and keep their own accountability for their remit. It does not validate its own work. It does not decide a matter that the regulation of the Bank reserves to the Board, to the management, or to a Control Function. And it has no administrative line over the people assigned to it or over the partners from the functions.

4. Whom it works with

Who Relation to the Competence Center
The functions of the Bank The clients: a function brings a need, its Domain Owner represents it, its Domain Expert works with the Competence Center
The Domains Execute: adopt AI in their own processes with governed knowledge; put forward their priorities to the Portfolio
The Control Functions Beside, independent: clear, validate, decide Exceptions, may stop
The Platform Owner and the IT functions Provide and operate the AI Platform and the environments; run at scale what the Bank adopts
The AI Steering Committee Advises the Executive Sponsor on the Portfolio and on conflicts between Domains
The Board Committee Oversees AI for the Board; receives what the Executive Sponsor brings to it, and is told of a major AI Incident and of a risk accepted beyond appetite
Internal audit Independent assurance over the Portfolio and over the Competence Center

5. Rule source

AI Competence Center Charter 3; Business Model 2; Operating Model 2 and 6.10; Statement of Intent 7.3 to 7.6; the Organization guide 2.
