# Teams

The Teams that deliver. A Team is a Solution Engineer with the Domain Expert, the Domain Owner, and the product owner. While a Team has up to three people the Competence Center Lead is its product owner (Solution Lifecycle Model 7.3). The business acceptor is the Domain Owner, or the Executive Sponsor for enabling work, who accepts the Solutions of the Team. One person may be in several Teams.
