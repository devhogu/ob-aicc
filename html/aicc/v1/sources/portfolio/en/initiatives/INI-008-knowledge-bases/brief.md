## 1. Hypothesis

If knowledge bases are built on one common approach across functions, then answers are consistent, cited, and reusable, and each function keeps its knowledge current through a named owner.

Today each function holds knowledge that its people search for or ask about every day, in documents that are not governed as sources. Answers differ, and knowledge leaves with people.

## 3. Scope and the minimum viable product

The steps below are the plan of the MVP. They start when the business case is approved and cleared and the Initiative is taken from the Portfolio Backlog into work (Portfolio Management Model 5).

The Competence Center does the following.

1. Agree a common approach for every knowledge base: its sources, a named owner and a review date for each source, access by data class, and answers that cite their source (Standards ARC-001).
2. List with each function the knowledge that its people need daily. Candidates: legal (policies, contracts, regulations), HR (policies and procedures), the commercial functions (products and services), retail credit (credit policies and procedures), and compliance.
3. Rank the candidates by use and feasibility.
4. Build the first knowledge base with legal, and start the second.
5. Record what is learned for the next bases.

Out of scope: Publishing knowledge outside the Bank; sources without an owner.

Minimum viable product: The first knowledge base, for legal, in use with owned and dated sources.
