# Portfolio Backlog

The Portfolio Backlog: the ranked list of Initiatives, which are business programs. It flows through the Portfolio Kanban (board.md). Scores are 1 to 5: value and urgency (5 is highest), risk reduction or opportunity (5 is highest), and effort (5 is largest). The rank of an Initiative is blank until the Competence Center Lead sets it, in the order of the goals of the first 100 days and not by the scores (Portfolio Management Model 6.5). An Engagement is an Initiative that has a client function. The Service Agreements are issued at the start of the study. The limit on the Initiatives that are Active is set by the Competence Center Lead, within the mix that the Executive Sponsor sets, and is reviewed at the monthly Steering (Portfolio Management Model 5.4).

The four Standing Initiatives are Proposed and tracked separately from the ranked portfolio Kanban. Their first Feature has not yet been admitted, so they are not Active. They share the Team limits in board.md and do not count against the limit on Active ordinary Initiatives.

Every ordinary Initiative is Proposed in the Funnel and has no rank. INI-013 comes from the existing service-resolution project proposal; its entry is not an intake screening, a business-case approval or a delivery admission. It is unscored; the scores of the other six Initiatives are kept.
