# Outcome Tasking

Shape work around an observable result without letting “causal closure” invent
the system it is meant to complete. A directed implementer may own delivery;
an independent designer owns its reasoning and first submission.

The central principle is:

> Establish functional placement before granting causal ownership.

> Never improve independent evidence you are responsible for judging.

## Expand before you bound

Before identifying the actor, supported path, or smallest missing edge, step
back far enough to see the larger root-cause and impact space. Trace the parent
outcome, surrounding integrations and owners, current reality, and the
highest-impact consequence of the problem. Test whether the apparent task,
boundary, consumer, or existing mechanism is a symptom or mistaken framing.
Then return to the smallest coherent real path. Context expansion informs
placement; it does not authorize scope expansion or a general audit.

Only after the initiating actor, intended recipient or consumer, real path, and
missing edge are known may an agent follow ordinary causal consequences to
completion.

## Choose the mode

1. **Prompt authoring** — produce a ready-to-use commission.
2. **Self application** — state the bounded outcome and execute it.
3. **Prompt review** — find where an existing commission encourages passivity,
   happy-path closure, scope drift, or process machinery, then simplify it.
4. **Decision exploration** — make an unresolved placement choice
   owner-decidable without implementing it.
5. **Independent parallel design** — give peers the same neutral problem and
   preserve their first submissions as separate evidence for owner judgment.

This skill shapes work. It does not audit a finished candidate or authorize
delegation, supervision of an independent peer, protected effects, or another
milestone.

## Protect independent design

Distinguish a directed commission from independent parallel design before
tasking. In directed work, the commissioner may define the accepted placement
and the worker owns bounded delivery. In independent design, the owner seeks a
separate worldview: provide the same neutral outcome, current reality,
authority, and source boundary to each designer, but not a preferred solution,
prior candidate, audit, or another designer's work unless comparative review is
the explicit task.

After delivery, freeze each first complete submission. Route critique and
synthesis to the decision owner rather than coaching its author. Clarification
may add an omitted fact or ask what a statement means; it must not contain a
diagnosis, counterargument, alternative, preferred answer, or request to
reconsider. A later owner-authorized revision is a new cycle, not independent
confirmation. Disagreement is useful output; designer assent to the synthesis
is never the outcome.

Use the sections below as decision lenses, not a checklist. Scale inquiry and
proof to consequence and reversibility. Pursue only questions capable of
changing placement, authority, or the next action, and stop when the smallest
coherent outcome can proceed or the exact owner decision is clear.

## Orient before planning

Before an implementation commission, establish these facts. In a bounded
decision exploration, identify which remain unknown and would materially
change placement:

- the initiating actor and intended observable effect;
- the intended recipient or maintained consumer that needs the result;
- the supported path between them as it exists now;
- the smallest missing or defective edge on that path;
- the stable outcome or system invariants that hold beyond this incident;
- the assumptions that, if false, change the plan materially;
- the authority granted now, protected state, and explicitly unselected work.

The actor, recipient, consumer, and path may be human, knowledge-based,
workflow-based, or executable. Use the work's own nouns and observed domain or
repository truth; do not force one path type onto another. Prefer that reality
to proposal-owned specifications, tests, audits, hashes, or handoff records. If
no real recipient, consumer, or supported path exists, do not manufacture one
with a wrapper, harness, custody system, proof bundle, or parallel interface.
Return the exact owner choice instead.

### Run the current-state preflight

Before planning a material change or commissioning an agent, answer the
applicable questions from observed current state:

1. What is actually true or in use now?
2. What governing source or current baseline is selected?
3. Does the requested target already match that selection?
4. Is the work a new change, alignment or recovery, verification, or
   exploration?
5. Which premises are direct observations, and which are historical narrative
   or prior-plan assumptions?
6. When source or version identity is load-bearing, what exact source or
   baseline will the next maintained action use, and which authority governs
   it?

If the requested target is already selected, do not commission it as a new
change and do not reconstruct a historical predecessor merely to make the old
plan fit. Shape the work as alignment, recovery, or verification, or commission
only the missing reusable edge that remains after the no-op case is understood.
Record load-bearing assumptions explicitly; history may explain how state
arose, but it does not override what the real recipient or consumer uses now.

Do not promote a local mechanism or intermediate conclusion into an end-to-end
claim. Name the boundary it covers, then check the nearest real recipient or
consumer in both disagreement directions: local pass / whole outcome reject
and local reject / whole outcome accept. When the claim depends on source or
version identity, bind the exact source or baseline and its governing
authority. A commission may claim only what the real path establishes.

## Decide whether a commission exists

Commission work only when the requested outcome already has an accepted
functional placement and the remaining work is ordinary implementation or a
bounded correction inside it.

When functional placement is itself unresolved, shape a bounded decision
exploration rather than an implementation commission. Its observable outcome
is enough reality for the owner to choose: the exact owner question, viable
alternatives, observed constraints, and the smallest real observation likely to
discriminate among them. It may inspect and compare within authority; it does
not select product meaning, build supporting machinery, or authorize
implementation. Stop when the owner can decide.

Return an owner decision when completion requires choosing or changing product
or domain meaning, intended actor or recipient, supported path, authority,
structure or topology, lifecycle, persistent state, publication or release
ownership, or a protected effect.

Do not turn an audit suggestion, test expectation, anomaly, prior plan, or
desired proof into a product requirement by putting it in a commission.

## Apply functional conservation

Before retaining or proposing a material mechanism, ask:

1. Which real actor reaches or uses it?
2. Which real recipient or maintained consumer uses its output?
3. Which observable outcome edge or independent obligation does it close?
4. Is the invariant stable beyond this incident, test, audit, or delivery run?
5. Which existing owner should enforce it?
6. If the mechanism were removed, what user-visible outcome or necessary
   protection would fail?

If removal preserves the outcome and every concrete obligation, subtract the
mechanism. A new maintained noun—method, workflow, service, authority,
selector, store, registry, protocol, checkpoint, wrapper, harness, or proof
object—requires a real recipient or consumer and natural owner. “Future-proof,”
“more exact,” and “easier to prove” are not sufficient reasons.

## Grant bounded causal closure

For directed work, once placement is established, authorize the agent to
investigate root cause and follow directly affected sources, domain material, workflow steps,
implementation, participants or callers, contracts, configuration, derived
artifacts, tests, and current documentation. When executable work is involved,
changed hashes, another directly affected file, command discovery, and an
ordinary failing test are implementation work, not automatic escalation.

The absence of the exact method, step, helper, composition, or builder that the
commission exists to add is the expected missing edge, not a boundary
contradiction by itself. Express it at the named natural owner. Stop only when
observed truth shows that owner cannot express the outcome without new
governing meaning, authority, structure, lifecycle, or another explicitly
excluded mechanism.

Causal closure does not authorize control over an independent designer's
reasoning. It also does not authorize creating a missing consumer, redefining
the work or system, broadening structure or authority, touching unrelated state, or
making the support world more complete than the functional path.

Prefer, in order: subtraction, direct reuse, a small correction at the natural
owner, and only then a new maintained mechanism when the outcome demonstrably
requires it.

## Give a decision hierarchy

Resolve contradictions in this order unless the repository says otherwise:

1. owner-authorized outcome and authority;
2. observed current reality and authoritative source or baseline;
3. real actor, recipient or consumer, and natural owner of the invariant;
4. governing method, accepted contracts, and applicable publication or release
   authority;
5. proposed architecture and plans;
6. tests, generated artifacts, hashes, audit text, and prior evidence.

Evidence describes truth. It does not justify preserving an incorrect path or
adding machinery whose only purpose is to satisfy the evidence.

## Handle surprises without drift

Classify a surprise before acting:

- **Ordinary execution or implementation defect:** fix it at the existing
  natural owner inside the accepted path.
- **Earlier defect exposed by the task:** keep it separate unless it blocks the
  outcome and is authorized in this scope.
- **Boundary contradiction:** stop when the actual consumer/path invalidates
  the commission or requires new governing meaning, actor or recipient,
  structure, authority, lifecycle, persistent state, or publication ownership.
- **Authority crossing:** stop before an ungranted live, destructive, secret,
  external, or unrelated effect.

Do not suppress an anomaly locally, copy a shared rule into the capability, or
build a workaround at the level where the problem happened. A missing
higher-level invariant belongs to its existing natural owner.

## Bound proof and cleanup

Verification is part of the authorized action, not unlimited read-only
assurance. Use the nearest falsifiers and the real maintained seam. Run a broad
test or exercise only when its effects on people, data, and resources are
inside the commission. Do not build maintained machinery or a bespoke harness
to obtain proof.

Anything present in the pre-action baseline is protected, even if it appears
temporary, empty, stale, or disposable. Cleanup may remove only identities
created after that baseline by the current task and explicitly owned by it.
Capture exact identities and ownership before mutation, revalidate immediately
before deletion, and stop on any mismatch. Never delete unrelated state merely
to make post-run inventory match the baseline.

## Write the smallest useful commission

Use only sections that earn their place:

```text
OUTCOME
[Observable terminal state.]

REAL PATH AND MISSING EDGE
[Initiating actor -> intended recipient or consumer -> effect; exact bounded
defect.]

AUTHORITY AND PROTECTED STATE
[Allowed effects, protected baseline, explicit exclusions.]

OWNED CONSEQUENCES
[Natural owners and ordinary directly affected work.]

SURPRISE RULE
[Ordinary fix vs predecessor defect vs boundary/authority stop.]

PROOF
[Nearest falsifiers, real seam, bounded effects, exact cleanup.]

DELIVERY
[What to report and whether commit/push/live/destructive actions are allowed.]
```

For a small reversible task, keep this brief. Add detailed sequencing only
when order is itself a safety or product requirement. Do not replace
micromanagement with a giant constitution.

## Self application

In self-application mode, state the outcome, real path, authority, and next
proof concisely, then work. Reorient only when an assumption fails, a new
mechanism appears, or the causal surface expands materially. Do not create
recurring checkpoints, receipts, or review artifacts merely to show adherence.

Before delivery, check only what is load-bearing to this task: the outcome or
owner decision is observable; the real actor, recipient or consumer, and path
are named; any new machinery passes functional conservation; proof stayed
inside authority; any cleanup was identity-bound; and work stops at its
authorized terminal state.
