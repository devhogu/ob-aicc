Brand and visual foundations

Identity

Use the original O! mark with a readable portal name. Preserve its geometry, proportions and speech tail. Provider/application logos identify their subjects beside names; they do not change the portal's theme or establish ownership. Use a suitable original variant or a neutral brand plate when a logo needs a contrasting background.

The supplied internal examples support a burgundy-to-near-black header and restrained magenta emphasis. Large magenta/cyan waves belong on a cover or a small outer edge. Keep working surfaces opaque and quiet.

Colour roles

The shared tokens define semantic roles. Use --text, --surface, --action, --link, --border-control and --focus instead of per-page literal colours. Light/dark surfaces are two presentations of one application.

Role Use
Brand magenta Identity, selection marker and restrained emphasis
Action / on-action Main actionable control and its text
Surface / surface-subtle Main content and supporting groups
Surface-selected Selected item, paired with a bar, underline or explicit state
Text / text-muted Primary and secondary reading hierarchy
Border / border-control Quiet dividers / necessary control boundaries
Focus Visible keyboard location, independent of selection
Success / warning / error / info Explicit feedback meanings with words and icons
Cyan Optional decorative edge; no implied operational meaning

Brand magenta against white is not our normal small-text action pairing. The inherited accessible action/link variants are used instead. Every chart or status must remain understandable without colour alone. Unknown and proposed are textual qualifications, not automatically amber or green scores.

Typography

TT Norms Pro: headings, weights 500 and 700. Golos Text: body, UI, tables and forms, weights 400–700. Keep the same font pair across all portals. Source files come from the existing curated asset kit; screenshot font identity was not inferred.

Use Default size / line height
Page title 28–32 / 36–40px
Section 22–24 / 30–32px
Subheading 18 / 26px
Reading text 16 / 24px
Navigation, table and essential metadata 14 / 20px
Short auxiliary label 12 / 16px

Use sentence case, normal tracking, real heading levels and tabular numerals for measures. Keep important qualifications at least 14px. Russian/English wording may differ in length; stable IDs and layout semantics do not. Reading width is about 68 characters; diagrams/tables may use the whole canvas.

Icons and assets

Use the supplied Lucide outline family: 24px view box and 2px stroke. Display at 20–24px for controls. Inline SVG can inherit currentColor; an external image cannot. Pair unfamiliar icons with words. Icon-only controls require accessible names. Do not mix unrelated icon styles in one toolbar.

O! and provider assets are contextual identities. Golos and Lucide retain their licence files. TT Norms Pro is authorized for these internal portals under the existing practitioner guidance; retain the internal-use restriction with any shared kit.

Space, corners and density

Use 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 and 48px spacing steps. Controls use 8px corners; outer workspace surfaces 16–20px. Prefer section spacing and dividers to nested cards. Shadows identify floating menus/dialogs, not every content block.

Default control height is 44px. Comfortable lists use approximately 48–56px rows; compact desktop tables can use 36–40px rows while preserving readable text and suitable targets. Density changes spacing, not information or qualifications.
