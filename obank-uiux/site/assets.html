Assets and implementation kit

All downloads are generated from the existing curated source assets. The selected O! identity, Golos/TT Norms Pro fonts and Lucide icons appear below. Provider logos keep their original colours and required background.

The implementation kit contains the resolved tokens, common workspace CSS, reusable primitives, selected assets/licences, all seven examples, the Cloud LAB flow-first recipe and the adoption instructions. Keep its version with a consuming portal. Update shared style at its canonical source, then rebuild the kit; do not independently modify copies and call them the same version.

Internal distribution: the included TT Norms Pro files follow the existing internal-use authorization. The kit includes the font-use note and Golos/Lucide licences. O! and provider marks remain identifiers and do not confer rights to represent another entity.
